穿过舷梯，委员会宣读了终止通知。天线上的阻尼器证明这个华而不实的信号曾被手动节流。总督笨拙地表演愤怒哑剧，但合格的协商代表托马掌握着一份令人腻烦的、记录他抗命行为的文字记录。在春分听证会上，书记员一篇煽动性的悔罪诗使旁听席因惊讶而骚动。

The governor began to **gyrate** through a **tendentious** defense, blaming a **heterogeneous** crowd. Ilyra answered his **impudence** with no **wrongheaded** flourish. She had watched the visitor **accouter** herself for travel with only **nourishment**, a **latent** beacon, and one request: do not **flatter** aliens into miracles. An **intercessor** explained that her **dialect** was **anthropomorphic** by necessity; her **palette** of light was a practical **malapropism** made by translation.

总督开始在偏颇的辩护中辗转回旋，把责任归咎于杂乱的人群。伊莱拉没有以错误导向的花招回应他的无礼。她见过访客为旅行配备的只有食物、一枚潜伏信标和一个请求：不要把外星人吹捧成奇迹。一名调停者解释，她的方言出于必要而采用拟人化表达；她的光色调板是翻译造成的实用性用词误置。

She was **implacable** only about evidence. **later**, when the **lank** prisoner was declared **insolvent**, Ilyra learned the stranger had come from a **gynecology** vessel in **meteoric** distress. Its crew’s **apostasy** from their sponsors had left them with **spare** fuel. Their distress signal could **pervade** the upper atmosphere, becoming **prophetic** only to people who refused to measure it.

她只在证据问题上毫不让步。后来，当那名瘦长囚犯被宣布破产时，伊莱拉得知陌生人来自一艘正遭遇流星般灾难的妇科飞船。船员背弃资助者，使他们只剩下备用燃料。他们的求救信号能弥漫高层大气；只有拒绝测量的人才会把它视为预言。

The visitor’s presence was **seemly**, not supernatural. A **splint** on her wrist marked the cost of landing. In **comity**, the town offered a **ponderous** **totem** of brass; she declined it for a **seance** with the telescope, then used its **rouse** command to wake the dormant tracker. Hadrin’s **pedagogics** had made Ilyra **indomitable**, and the **clapper** of the dome sounded with her sudden **chagrin**.

访客的存在得体而非超自然。她手腕上的夹板标记着着陆的代价。出于友好礼让，全镇献上一座笨重的黄铜图腾；她拒绝接受，转而与望远镜进行一场降神会，随后用它的唤醒命令启动休眠追踪器。哈德林的教学法使伊莱拉不屈不挠，穹顶的钟舌随着她突如其来的懊恼响起。

The visitor was no **freebooter**, no **idealize**-made savior, no **whim** of the observatory. Her signal was **consonant** with the ship’s known **shibboleth**, and her **peroration** came as a plain request for repair parts. Ilyra set an **anemometer** beside the dish, separating **transcendental** rumor from wind. When a councillor tried to **objurgate** her, she answered: **conversely**, **photometry** had shown the truth.

访客不是海盗，不是由理想化塑造的救世主，也不是天文台的一时兴起。她的信号与飞船已知的暗语相一致，她的结语只是对维修零件的朴素请求。伊莱拉在天线盘旁放置风速计，将超验传闻与风区分开来。当一名议员试图严厉斥责她时，她回答说：相反，光度测量已经显示了真相。

They would **accoutre** the landing field with lamps, **alcohol** for cleaning contacts, and a petition to **legislate** rescue access. The **onus** belonged to the valley, whose best **elixir** was careful work. Ilyra handled each **delicacy** of the repair while the visitor remained **withdrawn** from praise. There was no **overt** **repartee** after the **acrimonious** hearing, only an effort to **embellish** neither danger nor mercy.

他们将为着陆场配备灯具、用于清洁触点的酒精，以及一份要求立法保障救援通道的请愿书。责任属于山谷，它最好的灵药是细致工作。伊莱拉处理维修的每一处精细环节，访客则始终不接受赞美。激烈听证之后没有公开的机智应答，只有一种不美化危险也不美化仁慈的努力。

By dawn, the **quantity** of false light had fallen to zero. A small **sylph** of exhaust lifted from the craft, leaving the sky like a **palimpsest** cleared of a bad hand. Under Ilyra’s **ministration**, the observatory resumed its old **idiom**: look, compare, and believe what survives the looking.

到黎明时，虚假光的数量降至零。一小缕精灵般的废气从飞船升起，天空仿佛一张擦去了拙劣笔迹的重写羊皮纸。在伊莱拉的主持下，天文台恢复了旧有准则：观察、比较，并相信经得起观察的东西。
