## Story for Chunk 008 - Weather Museum

Celia Voss came to the Weather Museum before dawn to **reconstruct** a vanished forecast. Rain struck the tall windows on a **slant**, making the familiar building seem less **commonplace** than its guidebook promised. Someone had tried to **finagle** the archive lock, but a bent brass pin was the only **impediment** left behind. The attempt was so **barefaced** that Celia laughed once, then stopped: beyond the glass, the old parade ground lay **barren** beneath a greenish sky.

黎明前，西莉亚·沃斯来到气象博物馆，试图重建一份消失的预报。雨水斜着打在高窗上，让这座熟悉的建筑显得远没有指南中说的那么平凡。有人试图耍手段撬开档案室的锁，但留下的障碍只有一根弯曲的黄铜销。那次尝试赤裸得近乎可笑，西莉亚笑了一声便停下了：玻璃外，古老的阅兵场在泛绿的天空下荒芜一片。

Her **intention** had been modest: catalog the papers of Professor Edrin Voss, the museum's first director. The night guard, Dario, was **forthright** when she asked whether anyone else had entered. “No one,” he said, “unless a dead man can **suffocate** a building.” Celia chose to **retrace** his rounds through the Hall of Storms, past an **anthropology** display about rain rituals, until she came to **recognize** the museum's secret: the barometer needles all pointed inward.

她的打算很简单：整理博物馆首任馆长埃德林·沃斯教授的文件。她问夜班警卫达里奥是否还有别人进来过时，他直截了当地回答：“没有，”他说，“除非死人也能让一栋建筑窒息。”西莉亚决定沿着他的巡逻路线穿过风暴大厅，经过一处介绍雨祭仪式的人类学展柜，直到她认出博物馆的秘密：所有气压计的指针都朝向内部。

From the organ loft came a thin **cadenza**, played by no visible hand. Voss had been a **sedulous** observer of clouds and a reckless keeper of **combustible** experiments. His portrait showed a narrow-faced **hermit** who had sworn to **perpetuate** the weather records of the town at any cost. The admission fee, once **prohibitive**, had been paid by generations wanting to know why the professor's plaster **cranium** watched the ceiling.

管风琴阁楼传来一段纤细的华彩乐段，却看不见演奏者的手。沃斯曾是勤勉的云层观察者，也是鲁莽的可燃实验保管人。画像里的他面容狭长，像个隐士，曾发誓不惜一切代价延续小镇的气象记录。入场费曾经高得令人望而却步，但一代又一代人还是愿意支付，只为弄清教授的石膏头骨为何凝视着天花板。

The first thunderclap was **frightful**. It seemed to **pulverize** a pane in the Newspaper Gallery, where a yellowed **byline** named an **octogenarian** reporter who had challenged Voss's weather laws. Celia, trained in municipal **jurisprudence**, felt an instant **affinity** for the reporter's stubborn demand for **veracity**. A painted hostess appeared to **simper** from a mural of the old **borough**, her porcelain hand holding a packet of **desiccant**.

第一声雷鸣令人骇怕。它仿佛把报纸展厅的一块玻璃震成了粉末；一条发黄的署名提到一位八旬记者，曾挑战沃斯的气象定律。受过市政法学训练的西莉亚，立刻对这位记者固执地要求真实一事产生了亲近感。壁画中旧城区的一名女主人露出假笑，瓷器般的手里拿着一包干燥剂。

“Don't **hustle**,” Dario warned as drafts began to **encircle** them. “Voss's **annuity** funded the turbines.” Celia tried to **exhale**, but the air had become **turbid** with yellow dust. A corridor map promised to **facilitate** an exit through the roof observatory. It was not a **propitious** route, yet calling the plan **vituperable** would not open a door.

气流开始包围他们时，达里奥警告道：“别慌。”他说：“沃斯的年金资助了涡轮机。”西莉亚试图呼气，但空气已被黄色尘土搅得浑浊。走廊地图承诺可以帮助他们从屋顶观象台出去。那不是吉利的路线，可就算骂这个计划该死，也打不开一扇门。

In the Gallery of Forecasting, a **renaissance** astrolabe cast a moving **figure** across the floor. The old apparatus consumed gusts with **gluttonous** appetite, while a placard claimed it could prevent the **abduction** of whole seasons. Its copper vanes were **flexible**, but the building groaned like a **distressed** ship. Dario summoned the museum **constable**, who had been posted at the Victorian **cantonment** exhibit for the coming **ceremonial** opening.

预报展厅里，一台文艺复兴时期的星盘把移动的图形投射到地板上。旧仪器以贪婪的胃口吞噬阵风，旁边的牌子声称它能防止整个季节被掳走。铜制叶片很灵活，但建筑像一艘遇险的船般呻吟。达里奥叫来了博物馆警员；那人原本被派驻在维多利亚时代军营展区，负责即将举行的仪式性开幕。

The officer's pistol was a poor **deterrent** to air. Celia called the emergency mechanism **invalid** after reading Voss's hostile **polemic** against modern ventilation. “This place is **insalubrious**,” she said. The guard mentioned his small **stipend** and refused to abandon the collection. At higher **altitude**, an old **advert** for the museum flapped beside a locked **hatch**.

警员的手枪对空气毫无威慑力。西莉亚读过沃斯反对现代通风系统的敌意檄文后，认定应急装置无效。“这里不利于健康。”她说。警卫提到自己微薄的津贴，拒绝抛下藏品。在更高的海拔处，一张博物馆旧广告在锁住的舱口旁扑动。

Outside, the daylight was **unpropitious**, bruised and starless. A **fanciful** legend said Voss had **committed** his final forecast to a living cloud; Celia began to **retch** at the thought of its smell. Beneath the panel stood a weather **rig** whose coils showed a recent **mutation** of blue fire. The officer demanded Celia's **recognizance** before he would follow her through a door marked with a red **cockade**.

外面天光不祥，像被打伤一般昏暗，没有一颗星。一个异想天开的传说说，沃斯把最后一份预报托付给了一朵活云；想到它的气味，西莉亚开始作呕。面板下方放着一台气象装置，线圈上显出蓝色火焰最近发生的变异。警员要求西莉亚提供保证，才肯跟她穿过那扇标有红色帽徽的门。

They gained **clearance** from a faded staff ledger, but its pages were **deficient** in one year: 1907, the year Voss disappeared. Dario's stiff manner had seemed an **affectation** until Celia found a typed accusation naming him a **quisling** to the storm. A flash left her in a **daze**. The apparatus's wheel spun under tremendous **torque**, and Celia, **predisposed** to caution, stepped back as rain began to **bedraggle** the museum's **lustrous** banners.

他们从褪色的员工名册中获得了许可，但名册缺少一整年：1907年，也就是沃斯失踪的那一年。达里奥僵硬的举止原本像是矫揉造作，直到西莉亚发现一份打字指控，称他是风暴的卖国贼。一道闪光让她陷入恍惚。仪器的轮盘在巨大扭矩下旋转；天生谨慎的西莉亚向后退去，雨水开始把博物馆光泽夺目的旗幡打湿弄脏。

The laboratory's walls were painted **tan**, except where heat had left a black **char**. A diagram used a strange **locative** notation to mark winds inside rooms. Celia, usually **shrewd**, felt **lethargic** as she copied it. Dario, no longer **servile**, showed her a purple **welt** on his wrist and admitted he had read Voss's last **potboiler** of a pamphlet.

实验室的墙漆成黄褐色，只有受热处留下了黑色焦痕。一张图用奇怪的处所标记法标出房间内部的风。平时精明的西莉亚抄写时却感到懒怠。达里奥不再卑躬屈膝，他给她看手腕上的紫色肿痕，并承认自己读过沃斯最后那本粗制滥造的小册子。

“Someone tried to **pilfer** the key,” he said. The diagram predicted an **expansion** of sealed air, shaped like a fleeing **doe**. “We must **pass** the rotunda.” In its center, a black **dais** bore a glass globe. Celia was **anxious** to name the **sabotage**, but first saw the curator's silver **diadem** lying beside a case of **numismatic** medals and a school display on **natation**.

“有人试图偷走钥匙。”他说。图纸预示密封空气会膨胀，形状像一只逃跑的母鹿。“我们必须通过圆厅。”圆厅中央的黑色台座上放着一个玻璃球。西莉亚急于指出破坏行为，但先看见馆长的银色冠冕，正躺在一柜钱币奖章和一处游泳教学展览旁。

The missing curator, Celia Orm, was the likely **instigator**, according to a **parenthetical** note in her hand. Its despairing tone carried **dejection**, followed by a legal **proviso**: “Do not **reck** with the lower **furnace**.” That was the **crux**. The museum had once invited **inclusive** public experiments, and its coal engines were still running.

根据塞莉娅·奥姆亲笔写下的括号旁注，失踪的馆长很可能就是煽动者。字条语气绝望，带着沮丧，后面还有一条法律附带条件：“不要招惹下层炉膛。”这正是关键。博物馆曾经邀请公众参与包容性的实验，而它的燃煤发动机仍在运转。

An **imminent** collapse made every elegant theory **exceptionable**. In a case of **contemporaneous** field notes, Celia found dry **fodder** for the heater mixed with salt. Her face went **blanche** as she understood the **stake**: the machine was pulling moisture from the river and storing it above the roof. Even a **tyro** could read the instruction, joined by an elaborate **ampersand**: turn both valves.

迫在眉睫的坍塌让所有漂亮理论都变得难以接受。在一盒同期的现场笔记中，西莉亚发现加热器的干饲料里混着盐。她明白其中利害，脸色变得惨白：机器正在从河里抽取水分，并把它储存在屋顶上方。即使新手也能读懂那条指令，旁边还连着一个复杂的“与”号：转动两只阀门。

Voss had written “my dear” as an **endearment**, then added a **prompt** for a stage scene. The next case held a **vignette** of a flooded **acre**, an iron **tong**, and a long **digression** on the weather's **psyche**. It concluded that only **forcible** release could save the district. A **necrology** clipping recorded Voss's death; Celia, always **fastidious**, noticed the date had been altered.

沃斯写下“亲爱的”作为昵称，又添了一段舞台场景提示。下一个展柜里有一幅被洪水淹没的田地小景、一把铁钳，以及一篇关于天气心理的冗长离题文章。文章最后断定，只有强行释放压力才能拯救这个地区。一张讣告剪报记载了沃斯的死讯；一向细致的西莉亚发现日期被改过。

Celia was no mere **sophisticate** collecting oddities. She had meant to **incite** the machine, then make the storm a public **sentence** against the city council. Celia thought the motive less than **humane**: Voss's dream of scientific **autarchy** had made him believe weather could answer to one mind. An **expedient** valve repair, dismissed as **passe**, was now the only remedy.

塞莉娅并不是只会收集奇物的世故之人。她原本想启动机器，再让风暴成为对市议会的公开判决。西莉亚认为这个动机毫无人道：沃斯追求科学专制的梦想，让他相信天气可以服从一个人的意志。一项曾被视为过时的权宜阀门修理，如今成了唯一的补救办法。

They heard a **strident** voice from the upper **court**. Celia stood there, accusing the investors' **avarice** of turning Voss's research **inverse**, from warning system to weapon. She began to **imprecate** the officials while wind raised a ghostly **chorus** in the pipes. “I am his **plenipotentiary**,” she cried, throwing a printed **broadside** into the storm.

上层庭院传来刺耳的声音。塞莉娅站在那里，指责投资者的贪婪把沃斯的研究倒转了方向，从预警系统变成武器。她开始诅咒那些官员，风则在管道中掀起幽灵般的合唱。“我是他的全权代表！”她喊道，把一张印刷传单扔进风暴。

Celia tried to **ennoble** the professor's work without excusing it. “You have followed a **tangent**,” she said. “This engine will leave only **dross**.” Celia demanded the public's **suffrage** on whether to destroy it. Dario called that decision a future **chore**; in the present **austerity** of the emergency, the roof's eight-sided drum, an **octagon**, was shedding bolts.

西莉亚试图提升教授工作的意义，却不为它开脱。“你已经偏离正题了。”她说，“这台发动机最后只会留下废渣。”塞莉娅要求公众投票决定是否摧毁它。达里奥说那是将来的麻烦事；在眼下紧急状态的严酷局面中，屋顶那座八边形的鼓状结构正在掉落螺栓。

The warning was **salutary**, but Celia offered an **alibi**: she had only opened the archive. Dark cloud laid a **pall** across the skylight, seeming to **typify** the entire dreadful century. Around them, **rococo** lightning rods danced. **barring** the panel, Celia appealed to Celia's **empathy** and quoted Voss's final **apophthegm**: “A forecast is **veracious** only while it serves the living.”

这番警告发人深省，但塞莉娅提出了不在场证明：她只是打开了档案室。乌云在天窗上铺开一层阴影，仿佛象征着整个可怕的世纪。周围洛可可风格的避雷针跳动起来。西莉亚挡住面板，诉诸塞莉娅的同理心，并引用沃斯最后的格言：“预报只有在服务活人时才是真实的。”

The globe's **sphericity** began to split. Its **ductile** brass cage bent inward, and Celia could only **expostulate** as a shining **shoal** of droplets swam through the air. No **layman** could mistake the danger. Water had begun to **fester** in the sealed galleries, while the museum's **reverend** trustee, trapped in his office, clutched Voss's **tome** and begged them to **bestow** mercy on the collection.

玻璃球的球面性开始裂开。柔韧的黄铜笼向内弯曲，塞莉娅只能抗议着看一大群闪亮的水滴在空中游动。任何外行都不可能误判危险。封闭的展厅里开始积水腐败；博物馆那位牧师出身的受托人被困在办公室，紧抱着沃斯的巨著，哀求他们对藏品施以怜悯。

Celia tore a **diaphanous** curtain from the Meteor Theater and used it as a filter. At the valve she found an **ante**-dated maintenance ticket tucked beneath the **awning**. Its **application** required a royal **dispensation**, signed at the fatal **jucture** when patriotic **jingoism** made every invention seem **exuberant**. Celia began to **exult**, certain Voss had predicted this hour.

西莉亚从流星剧场撕下一幅轻薄透明的帘子，把它当作过滤器。在阀门处，她发现一张日期提前的维护票据，塞在遮篷下面。它的申请需要皇家特许，签署于那个致命的交汇点：爱国沙文主义让每项发明看起来都过分辉煌。塞莉娅开始欢呼，确信沃斯早已预言了这一刻。

But the instructions were **separable** from the professor's propaganda. The crucial **conjugation** of two levers had one clear **pitfall**: an **extension** pipe fed the roof tank. The brasswork looked **mediocre**, but it had been designed to **incriminate** anyone who moved it. Celia could **educe** no safer **opportunity**. “Voss was a **swindler**,” she said, refusing his final **dictate**.

但这些指令可以同教授的宣传分开理解。两根操纵杆的关键联动有一个明显陷阱：延伸管道把水送进屋顶水箱。黄铜构件看起来平庸，却是专门设计来嫁祸给任何移动它的人。西莉亚想不出更安全的机会。“沃斯是个骗子。”她说，拒绝服从他最后的命令。

Dario, a **fledgling** conservator before becoming guard, remembered an outbreak of mold called the museum **contagion**. It had spread through a **polygon**-shaped water chart recovered from a coastal **dune**. The curator's **raspy** laugh stopped when Celia read the engineer's **judgment**: Voss's **lineage** had no claim upon the skies. A city **cabal** had erased that verdict because of its political **connotation**.

达里奥在当警卫前只是个新手藏品修复员，他记得曾有一场霉菌暴发被称为博物馆的传染源。霉菌沿着一张从海岸沙丘找到的多边形水文图扩散。西莉亚读出工程师的判词时，馆长沙哑的笑声停了下来：沃斯的血统无权支配天空。城中的秘密集团因这句话的政治含义而抹去了判决。

The machine's **presumptive** master, Celia saw, was no man but pressure itself. The thought was almost **transcendent**, and fiercely **iconoclastic** in a house devoted to relics. Celia threatened to **arraign** them both for trespass, yet Dario made a **valiant** leap across the flooded **moor** tableau. He used a fountain-pen **nib** to bridge a switch.

西莉亚看出，这台机器表面上认定的主人不是某个人，而是压力本身。这个想法近乎超凡，在一座专事收藏遗物的房子里也格外具有破坏偶像的意味。塞莉娅威胁要以非法闯入罪起诉他们两人，可达里奥勇敢地跃过积水的荒原布景。他用钢笔尖搭接了一个开关。

The spark brought **modernity** into the old hall: screens woke with weather **static**, reciting a forecast in blank **pentameter**. There was not a **scintilla** of warmth left; even Celia sagged with **languor**. Celia found a **defensible** sequence: turn three wheels on **consecutive** breaths, before the moment was **fleeting**. A brass plate said the apparatus was inspected at **triennial** intervals.

火花把现代性带进了古老大厅：屏幕伴着气象静电亮起，用无韵的五步格念出一份预报。那里连一丝温暖都不剩，连塞莉娅也因倦怠而垂下肩膀。西莉亚找到了一个站得住脚的顺序：在连续的几次呼吸间转动三个轮子，趁时机稍纵即逝。一块黄铜牌写着，这台仪器每三年检查一次。

Behind a locked **cabinet**, they found the original report. Its **snobbish** author called the apparatus **antediluvian**, but the evidence was **germane**: an **encyclopedia** of cloud observations showed a pursuit between river level and air pressure. “We must **chase** neither,” Celia said. The report condemned a **shiftless** mechanic who had bypassed the drain beneath the east **precinct**.

在一只上锁的柜子后面，他们找到了原始报告。作者势利地称这台仪器古老得像洪水前的遗物，但证据切题：一本云层观测百科展示了河面高度与气压之间的追逐。“两者都不能盲目追赶。”西莉亚说。报告谴责了一名懒散的机械师，他绕过了东区下面的排水管。

Dario wrapped a **muffler** around his mouth as the air thinned. Celia had to **admonish** Celia, who kept reaching toward the **ethereal** light above them. They argued **hotly** over a ceiling map of the **zodiac**, then used a museum baker's demonstration to **knead** wax into the cracked seal. The model bore the **title** “Ascension of Rain,” a grand **apotheosis** spoiled by a spelling **solecism**.

空气变稀时，达里奥把围巾裹在嘴上。塞莉娅不断伸手去够头顶空灵的光，西莉亚不得不训诫她。两人围绕天花板上的黄道图激烈争论，随后借用博物馆面包师的演示，把蜡揉进裂开的封条。模型题为《雨的升腾》，这场宏大的神化却被一个拼写错误毁了。

In the basement, the water had begun to **ensconce** the old boilers. A **metallurgical** label repeated Voss's **admonition** that every copper pipe needed an outer **integument**. The gift-shop **vendor** had removed one to display its hidden **dimension**. That theft, not magic, had exposed the **ordnance** of valves to the wrong **mode** of pressure.

地下室里，积水已经包围了老锅炉。一张冶金标签重复着沃斯的告诫：每根铜管都需要外层包覆物。礼品店售货员曾拆掉其中一层，以展示隐藏的尺寸。正是这次盗拆，而不是魔法，让阀门的装备暴露在错误的压力模式下。

Celia picked up a ceramic **shard** and struck a **duet** against the pipe with Dario's wrench. The sound guided them through a **ruthless** current to a maintenance **scaffold**. Under it, the main lever rocked on a **fulcrum** beside a narrow **alcove**. “This is the **catastrophe**,” Celia said, choosing the blunt word over any **euphemism**.

西莉亚捡起一片陶瓷碎片，用它和达里奥的扳手在管道上敲出二重奏。声音引导他们穿过无情的急流，来到一座维修脚手架。脚手架下，主杠杆在狭窄壁龛旁的支点上摇晃。“这就是灾难。”塞莉娅说，她选择了直白的词，而不是任何委婉说法。

She offered to **rescind** the alarm, but Celia demanded a **reprise** of her earlier confession. Celia said the undertaking had begun in **felicity**, when the clear **welkin** seemed a promise available only to an **elitist** few. Then a drought made her family farm an **awful** place. Voss's weather **catechism** had given her a language for revenge.

塞莉娅提出撤销警报，但西莉亚要求她重述早先的供认。塞莉娅说，这项行动始于幸福的时光，那时澄澈的苍穹仿佛只向少数精英许诺未来。后来旱灾让她家的农场变成了可怕的地方。沃斯的气象教义问答给了她一套复仇的语言。

Celia answered with an **exposition** of the system, not with **hostility**. The deceased professor's first **investor** had expected a **pastoral** attraction, but years of **corrosion** made every failure **imaginable**. “We can still stop it,” Celia said, “but not **utterly** alone.” A school **syllabus** listed the required emergency **regimen**, filed by the coal **consignor** with a practical **rationale**.

西莉亚没有敌意地回应，而是解释了系统的原理。已故教授最初的投资者期待一个田园牧歌般的景点，但多年的腐蚀让任何故障都变得可以想象。“我们仍能阻止它，”西莉亚说，“但不能完全靠一个人。”一份学校课程表列出了所需的应急方案，由煤炭托运人以实用的理由归档。

The instructions called for wet **pollen** as a pressure marker, a steady **tenor** in the release bell, and no **disparity** between the paired gauges. Celia, suddenly **sedate**, translated a line of **metonymy**: the “eye” meant the valve, not the storm. So, **perforce**, they followed the **archaic** directions. Dario had to **cajole** the trustee away from the controls; the old man had swallowed an **emetic** from the infirmary and complained like a **gourmand** denied supper.

说明要求用湿花粉作为压力标记，让释放铃保持稳定的男高音，并确保成对仪表之间没有差异。塞莉娅突然平静下来，翻译了一句借代：“眼睛”指的是阀门，不是风暴。因此他们不得不遵循古老的指示。达里奥得哄劝受托人离开控制器；老人吞下了医务室的催吐剂，像一个被拒绝晚餐的美食家般抱怨。

At last the trustee produced a copper **amulet** used as the master key. It was **toxic** with green oxidation, and its **senescence** showed in the worn grooves. The museum's founding **entrepreneur** had built the lock with needless **inefficiency**; green **verdigris** clogged it, filling Celia with **lassitude**. Still, she rejected the **presumption** that old things deserved to survive merely because they had lasted.

最后，受托人拿出一枚铜护符，用作万能钥匙。它因绿色氧化物而有毒，磨损的凹槽显示出它的衰老。博物馆的创办企业家把锁造得毫无必要地低效；绿色铜锈堵住了锁孔，让西莉亚感到疲惫。然而她拒绝接受这种假定：旧物仅仅因为存在得久，就理应继续存活。

They braced the lever against a stone **buttress**. Celia watched the gauges, one eye fixed on each tiny **pore** in the intake grate. “Do not **desist**,” she told Dario, whose **prehension** on the slick handle was failing. Celia stopped making **hackneyed** speeches about destiny; her old **mannerism** had become a genuine **susceptibility** to fear. When the trustee called dismantling Voss's machine **blasphemous**, no one listened.

他们把杠杆抵在石制扶壁上。西莉亚盯着仪表，两只眼睛分别锁定进气格栅上的每个小孔。“不要停下。”她对达里奥说，他握住湿滑手柄的力量正在减弱。塞莉娅不再发表那些陈腐的宿命演说；她旧日的习惯姿态已经变成真正容易恐惧的表现。受托人称拆除沃斯的机器是亵渎时，没有人理会。

The release bell sounded a simple **melody**. A rubber hose went **flaccid**, and the room filled with clean rain. Celia, once a proud **philogynist** of storms, admitted that power was **transmissible** only through ordinary hands. Celia ordered an **excision** of the exposed pipe and asked the officer to **assess** the damage without **acrimony**.

释放铃奏出简单的旋律。一根橡胶软管瘪了，房间里下起干净的雨。塞莉娅曾是自豪的风暴爱好者，如今承认力量只能通过普通人的双手传递。西莉亚下令切除暴露的管道，并请警员不带怨恨地评估损失。

Under the city's emergency **auspices**, workers arrived with unexpected **generosity**. Yet **paranoia** gripped the crowd when a final blue flash made the glass globe seem **uncanny**. The source was a **subaquatic** relay below the flooded floor. Its removal proved **beneficial**, though Celia was **distraught** to see her hope reduced to wires.

在城市应急机构的支持下，工人带着意外的慷慨赶来。然而最后一道蓝光让玻璃球显得诡异，偏执情绪攫住了人群。光源来自淹水地板下的水下中继器。拆除它证明十分有益，尽管塞莉娅看到自己的希望只剩下一堆电线时悲痛欲绝。

The missing money had come from a local **tycoon**, whose taste was as **dull** as his loyalty was questionable. Records established Dario's **fidelity** to the museum. He emerged **hale** from the fumes, wearing an antique **legionary** rain cape that made him look absurdly **acquisitive**. Celia sent it to the **medieval** costume room and returned to the evidence.

失踪的钱来自一位当地大亨；他的品味和他的忠诚一样乏味而可疑。记录证明达里奥忠于博物馆。他从烟雾中健康地走出来，穿着古老军团士兵的雨披，显得荒唐地贪得无厌。西莉亚把雨披送到中世纪服装室，重新回到证据前。

There she traced the storm's **virulence** to Voss's letters, rich with **vitriol** against any critic who might **incur** his anger. The letters described a planned **depredation** of the city waterworks, marked with a black **plume**. Celia had inherited the **scheme** but changed its target from council hall to museum. A retired **nurse** had found the papers and reported their **inexplicable** instructions.

在那里，她从沃斯的信件中追溯到风暴的猛烈程度；信中充满了对任何可能招致他愤怒的批评者的刻毒言辞。信件描述了一场计划中的城市水厂掠夺，并用黑色羽饰作了标记。塞莉娅继承了这个计划，却把目标从议会大厅改成了博物馆。一名退休护士发现了这些文件，并报告了其中莫名其妙的指示。

By noon, a repaired **canopy** covered the entrance. The trustee's **egotistical** objections had gone **slack** after experts proved the laboratory was never truly **hermetic**. They drained the Roman **frigidarium** and added an **addendum** to every display: scientific ambition can turn **morbid** when nobody questions it. The board voted to **disband** Voss's private foundation.

到了中午，修好的遮棚覆盖了入口。专家证明实验室从未真正密封后，受托人自负的反对意见也软了下来。他们排空了罗马冷藏室，并给每个展览增添附录：无人质疑时，科学野心可能变得病态。董事会投票解散了沃斯的私人基金会。

At the public meeting, Celia chose to **commence** with the exact **occasion** of the failure. She would not **impute** every crime to dead engineers, nor erase Voss's **antislavery** work merely because it complicated his legacy. A temporary **trestle** let visitors cross the wet gallery; their tickets would **afford** repairs, not spectacle. Celia listened with an exhausted **visage** while the museum **broadcast** the findings.

在公众会议上，西莉亚选择从故障发生的确切时刻开始。她不会把每项罪行都归咎于已故工程师，也不会仅因沃斯的反奴隶制工作使他的遗产变复杂，就把它抹去。一座临时支架桥让访客穿过潮湿的展厅；门票收入将用于维修，而非制造景观。塞莉娅带着疲惫的面容聆听，博物馆则播报了调查结果。

One columnist tried to **carp** that the institution would **pack** away its scandal. Instead, Celia accepted a year of **probation** and helped write a **judicious** account for the archive. The shattered globe became a **souvenir** of responsibility, not a prize for a **malcontent**. The new exhibit described Voss's uneasy relation to **catholicism**, and the roof **cistern** where the danger had gathered.

一名专栏作家挑剔地声称，机构会把丑闻收拾起来藏掉。相反，塞莉娅接受了一年的察看期，并帮助为档案写下审慎的记录。破碎的玻璃球成了责任的纪念品，而不是不满者的奖品。新展览介绍了沃斯与天主教之间不安的关系，以及危险曾聚集的屋顶蓄水池。

Children later climbed a grassy **hummock** to watch a safe **calorific** balloon rise over the city. The lesson addressed the **zeitgeist** that had seemed to **preordain** conquest over nature, then asked whether anyone could **abolish** uncertainty. A small **choreography** of flags demonstrated fronts and pressure. Celia saw in Voss's obsession an **atavism**, a desire to rule by omen.

后来，孩子们爬上长满青草的小丘，观看一只安全的高热气球升过城市。课程讨论了那个似乎预先注定要征服自然的时代精神，又问是否有人能废除不确定性。一组小型旗帜编舞演示了锋面和压力。西莉亚在沃斯的执念中看见了一种返祖现象：凭借预兆进行统治的欲望。

No longer **accusatory**, Celia became a careful docent. She explained how a **vindictive** plan can **suckle** on real grief until it believes itself **entitled** to harm strangers. The opening day was **chaotic**, but no one concealed their **complicity** in the museum's silence. The old **armory** loaned pumps, and the trustee publicly chose to **acknowledge** Celia's **presentiment** that the first leak mattered.

塞莉娅不再咄咄逼人，成了一名谨慎的讲解员。她解释，一个报复性的计划如何吸食真实的悲痛，直到它相信自己有权伤害陌生人。开幕日一片混乱，但没人隐瞒自己曾参与博物馆的沉默。旧军械库借来了水泵，受托人也公开承认西莉亚的预感是对的：最初的泄漏至关重要。

Celia placed the bent brass pin beside a small **cog** in a new **seminar** case. Its label asked an **interrogatory** question: who owns a forecast? The answer led visitors through an **odyssey** of instruments and people. They would **commemorate** the disaster, she decided, without making it a **crutch** for fear. Knowledge was public **property**, not an exclusive **venison** of collectors.

西莉亚把弯曲的黄铜销放在新研讨展柜里一枚小齿轮旁。标签提出了一个质问式问题：谁拥有一份预报？答案带领访客踏上一场穿越仪器与人群的漫长旅程。她决定，人们应当纪念这场灾难，却不能把它变成恐惧的拐杖。知识属于公众，而不是收藏家的专享猎物。

The final **acquisition** was Voss's corrected notebook, full of careful **diacritical** marks. Reading it did not **mortify** Celia; it clarified how frost, or **rime**, had first inspired the engine. Voss had tried to **dogmatize** a changing sky, but under the museum's new **aegis**, his surviving **project** would **militate** for humility rather than control.

最后一件征集品是沃斯修订过的笔记本，里面满是仔细标注的变音符号。阅读它没有使西莉亚难堪，反而说明了霜或雾凇最初如何启发了这台发动机。沃斯曾试图把变化的天空教条化，但在博物馆新的庇护下，他遗留下来的项目将推动谦逊，而不是控制。

On the first quiet evening, a child's **spontaneity** gave Celia the needed **device** for the closing display: a hand pump that showed atmospheric **inconstancy** without pretending to command it. They sealed the old heater so it could never **putrefy** another dream, left a safe **vent** in the wall, and explained the **feudalism** of patronage that had kept Voss unchallenged.

第一个宁静的夜晚，一个孩子自发的举动给了西莉亚闭幕展览所需的装置：一台手泵，展示大气的不稳定，却不假装能够支配它。他们封死旧加热器，使它再也不能腐蚀另一个梦想；在墙上留下安全的通风口，并解释了曾让沃斯无人挑战的赞助封建主义。

Outside, Celia wore a plain coat rather than the curator's **negligee** costume. The gathered **laity** received a **bountiful** rain without fear. Celia removed the last **fetter** from the barometer and heard a brief **ejaculation** of steam as its needle found the storm's **crest**. The reading was **accurate**; the old machine, finally **immovable**, stood beneath a **tricolor** warning flag.

外面，塞莉娅穿着朴素的外套，而不是馆长那套睡衣式服装。聚集的平信徒毫无恐惧地迎接丰沛的雨水。西莉亚卸下气压计最后的束缚，听见指针找到风暴顶峰时蒸汽短促地喷出一声。读数准确；那台老机器终于稳固不动，矗立在三色警示旗下。

The next morning was **decorous** and bright. Workers would **retrench** the ruined wing and **disperse** the salvaged objects across the museum, keeping the **parapet** open to the weather. Celia believed that was truer **patriotism** than worshipping inventions. The new exhibit made public scrutiny **obligatory**, included a plain **glossary**, and welcomed even the most **dissentious** visitor to challenge its conclusions.

第二天早晨端庄而明亮。工人们将缩减那座毁坏的侧翼，把抢救出的物件分散到博物馆各处，同时让女儿墙继续向天气敞开。西莉亚相信，这比崇拜发明更是真正的爱国主义。新展览规定公众审查为义务，附有一份朴素的词汇表，也欢迎最爱挑剔的访客质疑它的结论。

Beside Voss's portrait, the final panel named no single villain. It recorded each **imputation**, the scientists' pencil marks in **graphite**, and the **reputed** miracle that had been only valves and pressure. No visitor, learned or **illiterate**, could miss the point when the old hall filled with a **tumultuous** recording of rain. In the **adjacent** gallery, the storm engine's harmless **orgies** of light would **accentuate** the lesson: no forecast is **everlasting**, and every machine can **frieze** a moment while human choices **spawn** its future.

沃斯肖像旁的最后一块展板没有指认唯一的恶人。它记录了每一项归罪、科学家在石墨上的铅笔痕迹，以及那场据称存在、其实只由阀门和压力造成的奇迹。古老大厅充满喧闹的雨声录音时，无论博学还是不识字的访客，都不会错过其中的要旨。在相邻展厅里，风暴发动机无害的绚丽灯光将进一步凸显这堂课：没有预报能够永恒，每台机器都能冻结一个瞬间，而人的选择会孕育它的未来。
