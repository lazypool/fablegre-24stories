## Story for Chunk 023 - Embassy Garden

At the river embassy, Sera Lin held the **nominal** rank of assistant secretary, though everyone knew that a street **peddler** had taught her more about people than the ministry had. Beyond the reception windows, the **willowy** acacias inclined over clipped paths. The ambassador asked Sera to **inform** the two delegations that no ceremonial expense would **exceed** the value of their old **fraternal** treaty. A flower-strewn **raft** waited at the water gate, intended to **surpass** last year's display. The host, a **sapient** woman with an **eternal** patience, had hired a resident **polymath** to plan the garden's restoration.

在河畔大使馆，塞拉·林名义上是助理秘书，但人人都知道街头小贩教她识人的本领胜过外交部。接待厅窗外，垂柳般的金合欢斜向修剪整齐的小径。大使请塞拉通知两方代表，礼仪开支不得超过旧日兄弟条约的价值。一只铺满鲜花的木筏停在水门，意在胜过去年的陈设。主人是一位睿智而耐心永恒的女士，聘请一名博学多才的常驻学者规划花园修复。

Before the toast, the island's northern province threatened to **secede**. An **errant** irrigation pipe had begun to **infest** the orchid beds with brackish water; brown **necrosis** spread through roots where gardeners had cut a **cross** trench. The chief gardener, ordinarily **dapper** even in rain, showed Sera a **striated** leaf and described the **grueling**, **cerebral** work of saving rare stock. News of the damage might cause an **outcry** and make the treaty's symbolic roses **wilt**. Sera promised to **exculpate** no one without evidence.

祝酒前，岛屿北部省份威胁脱离。一根失控的灌溉管开始用咸水侵害兰花床；园丁挖十字沟处的根部蔓延着褐色坏死。即使下雨也总是衣着整洁的首席园丁向塞拉展示一片条纹叶，讲述挽救稀有品种的艰苦而费脑的工作。损害消息可能引起公愤，让条约象征性的玫瑰凋萎。塞拉答应没有证据绝不为任何人开脱。

At eight, an **isochronous** fountain rhythm began, almost **heartrending** beside the empty beds. Lanterns served to **illuminate** the pergolas, while guards moved to **isolate** the affected terrace. A **spontaneous** chill passed through the guests; the minister's famous **moodiness** gave way to a demand for a report. Water could still **yield** clues. Sera noticed a **vagrant** boatman at the gate and recalled that neither a treaty nor a garden was **immutable**: each was **vincible** when neglected. A gray **courser** stamped near the stable wall as her **observant** eye caught a damp paper under its bridle.

八点，喷泉开始均匀的节奏，在空花床旁格外令人心碎。灯笼照亮凉亭，卫兵隔离受影响的平台。宾客间自发掠过一阵寒意；部长著名的喜怒无常变成索要报告。水仍可能提供线索。塞拉注意到门口一名流浪船夫，想起条约与花园都非不可改变，疏于照料便都能被征服。一匹灰色骏马在马厩墙边踏蹄，她敏锐的眼睛看见缰绳下潮湿的纸。

The paper was a furious **screed** against the accord, written in a **heterodox** hand by someone otherwise **congenial** at embassy dinners. It did not **bode** well. In the service court, cooks were serving **gruel** to laborers beside a marble **cameo** of the founding envoy. Sera began to **recast** the evening from celebration into inquiry. She could not let anybody **enamor** the delegates with theatrical suspicion, nor permit **sacrilege** against the old memorial to **undercut** their trust. A porter with a **limp** said the annual **convention** of gardeners might **tempt** a saboteur to hide in plain sight.

纸上是一篇愤怒抨击协议的檄文，笔迹异端，作者平日却在大使馆晚宴上颇为和善。这不是好兆头。勤务院里，厨师在建馆使节的大理石浮雕旁给劳工盛粥。塞拉开始把夜晚从庆典重塑为调查。她不能让任何人用戏剧化的怀疑迷惑代表，也不能容许对旧纪念碑的亵渎削弱他们的信任。一名跛脚搬运工说，园丁的年度大会可能诱使破坏者公然藏身。

"I **opine** the culprit used one grain at a time," he said, offering a blue **granule** from the drain. Its color was too **handsome** to be fertilizer. A delivery cart had begun to **careen** near the tea pavilion, where an envoy in a formal **kimono** discussed the **advent** of a new trade measure. Sera noted the fountain's stone **fiducial** point and trusted the gardener's **sapience**: mineral dye alone could not **implicate** a person. Yet a **turquoise** stain trailed toward the boathouse, with a curious **lag** between drops.

“我认为犯人一次只用一粒，”他说着递来从排水沟取出的蓝色颗粒。那颜色太漂亮，不像肥料。一辆送货车在茶亭旁开始横冲直撞，一名身着正式和服的使节正在谈论新贸易措施的到来。塞拉记下喷泉石头的基准点，并相信园丁的智慧：矿物染料本身不能牵连某人。然而一条绿松石色污痕通向船屋，滴点间隔异常。

The embassy's **intellectual** attaché dismissed the trail as a product of public **credulity**. Sera made him **crouch** beside it. "Do not **exaggerate**," she said, "but do not let a clever lie **supplant** your own **opinion**." The gardener hoped to **rehabilitate** the beds before dawn; the attaché said a public inquiry would **emasculate** the hosts. A burst of **hail** tapped the glass, enough to **peeve** the musicians and **hinder** the servants. In a reed basket Sera found river **jetsam**, including the seal of a known **recusant**.

大使馆的知识分子随员把痕迹斥为公众轻信的产物。塞拉让他蹲到旁边。“不要夸大，”她说，“但也别让聪明的谎言取代你的看法。”园丁希望黎明前复原花床；随员说公开调查会使主人难堪。一阵冰雹敲打玻璃，足以惹恼乐师并妨碍仆役。塞拉在一个芦苇篮中发现河上漂流物，包括一名知名拒服者的印章。

Its marks showed a **progression** of payments, then an offer of **atonement** if the plot succeeded. The garden seemed to **encompass** every interest in the dispute: the ceremonial **conservatory**, the border canal, and the revenue from its seeds. The note's claim that a leak was **risible** could **beget** carelessness, but its measurements had unsettling **accuracy**. A **coincident** footprint led to a **ferrous** tool chest. The guards had been **oblivious**; Sera's discovery drew their **incredulity**. She heard no **seismic** boom, only the delicate click of an **elaborate** valve.

印记显示付款的递进过程，继而提出若阴谋成功便赎罪。花园似乎囊括争端中的所有利益：仪式温室、边境运河和种子收益。便条称漏水可笑，这可能滋生大意，但其测量却准确得令人不安。一串恰巧吻合的脚印通向铁制工具箱。卫兵毫无察觉，塞拉的发现令他们难以置信。她没有听见地震般巨响，只听到精巧阀门轻微的咔嗒声。

No one wished to **condemn** a guest before proof. Still, the visiting minister complained of **gastritis** and asked to rest beneath the **cornice**. Her **multilingual** interpreter, an **introvert** with a love of legal **terminology**, suggested that the sabotage was a **hoax** staged for sympathy. Sera read the reception **script** and saw its **merit**: it scheduled every toast. To **philosophize** about motive was **naturally** easier than to test it. She followed a waiter in the **guise** of a florist, whom the sentries watched **askance**.

在证据出现前，没人愿意谴责宾客。不过来访部长抱怨胃炎，请求在檐口下休息。她那位多语种、内向且喜爱法律术语的口译员认为，破坏是为博取同情而策划的骗局。塞拉读了接待流程，看到其优点：每次祝酒都有安排。空谈动机自然比验证它容易。她跟踪一名伪装成花商的侍者，哨兵正怀疑地盯着他。

In his apron lay a folded **manifesto**, thick with **sapiential** quotations, and a map bearing the name of a **conspirator**. Under the map was a rubber **mat**, blackened by a **sinister** liquid. From the musicians' tent came a soft **croon**, then the **splenetic** voice of the regional delegate calling the damage **trivial**. Sera knew him as no common **drudge**. He kept a small bronze **reptile** as a paperweight, and when she named the chemical, his face made a brief **convulsion**.

他的围裙里有一张折叠的宣言，满是智慧格言，还有一张标有阴谋者姓名的地图。地图下是一块被阴险液体熏黑的橡胶垫。乐师帐篷传来低吟，随后地区代表以暴躁的声音称损害微不足道。塞拉知道他绝非普通苦工。他用一只小铜爬行动物作镇纸；她说出化学品名称时，他的脸短暂抽搐。

He belonged to a resentful **minority** that hoped scandal would **discountenance** the treaty in the public **forecourt**. His accomplices had hired a dancing **troupe**, a coastal **fishmonger**, and an **imperial** antiques dealer. Their arrival **preceded** the failure by forty minutes, Sera could **compute** from the fountain clock. The dye would **diffuse** quietly, leaving the breakaway province to blame the hosts. Their **renegade** doctrine mixed commerce with **polytheism** and had made its advocate unusually **pensive**. "I **reckon** you want an **episode** of panic," Sera told him, "not a **linear** investigation."

他属于一个心怀怨恨的少数派，希望丑闻在公共前庭毁掉条约声誉。他的同伙雇来一个舞团、海岸鱼贩和一名帝国古董商。塞拉从喷泉时钟算出，他们比故障早四十分钟到达。染料会悄然扩散，让分离省份归咎主人。他们的叛逆教义把商业与多神教混合，使其鼓吹者格外沉思。“我看你想要一场恐慌的插曲，”塞拉告诉他，“而不是一场线性调查。”

The accusation seemed to **stun** the room. The delegate answered with practiced **trickery**, blaming a young **hoyden** whose **acne** had made her hide among the hedge staff. Sera appealed instead to **amity**. The girl, from a **nomadic** family, had only carried a **moderate** crate across the dry **arroyo**. Her account matched the **semblance** of a trainee's uniform worn by the culprit. The actual **cadet** had made a **gamble**: he assumed an **unassuming** apprentice would be ignored.

指控仿佛震住全场。代表以熟练的诡计回答，把罪责推给一名因青春痘而躲在篱笆工人中的轻佻少女。塞拉转而诉诸友谊。这名出身游牧家庭的女孩，只是将一个适中的箱子穿过干涸溪沟。她的叙述与犯人穿着的学员制服外观相符。真正的学员赌了一把：他以为不起眼的学徒会被忽略。

Sera asked the apprentice not to **indulge** fear. He recited an **orison** learned from his grandmother, and the wet stones began to **coruscate** beneath fresh lamplight. Behind a hedge, someone began to **gnash** his teeth. The orchestra changed **timbre** as a retired **prospector** identified the stain as crushed mineral. "An old **apothegm** applies," he said: "Beautiful poison remains **carcinogenic**." His analysis was **workmanlike**, not **somniferous**, and it gave Sera reason to **fret** over one **pivotal** detail.

塞拉让学徒别纵容恐惧。他背诵祖母教的祷词，湿石在新灯光下开始闪耀。篱笆后有人咬牙切齿。乐团改变音色，一名退休探矿者认出污痕是压碎的矿物。“有句老格言适用，”他说，“美丽的毒物仍会致癌。”他的分析扎实而非催眠，让塞拉为一个关键细节忧虑。

The valve key bore the **meliorism** of the chief gardener before his **patronymic**, a cruel attempt to overturn his reforming ideals. The saboteur had hoped to **expend** his reputation and then **felicitate** himself as defender of the province. But the gardener had been **obdurate** about his records, preserving every **encyclical** from the horticultural society. A **peevish** clerk had initialed the key ledger. Sera asked him to **edify** the council rather than behave like a **charlatan**. He produced a clay **tablet** and accepted their **reprobation** for leaving the cabinet open.

阀门钥匙在首席园丁父姓前刻着其改良主义，这是一种残酷企图，要颠覆他的改革理想。破坏者希望耗尽其名誉，再自我祝贺为省份辩护。但园丁对记录十分固执，保存园艺协会的每份通函。一名易怒职员在钥匙账本上签了缩写。塞拉请他教益议会，而非表现得像江湖骗子。他拿出一块泥板，并接受因柜子未锁而受责难。

The record proved that the plotter meant to **pollute** the pool, **swamp** the treaty with rumor, and force each side to **retaliate**. His speeches had been **vitriolic**, but a rush of **vertigo** betrayed his fear when Sera named the **illicit** shipment. Its **precursor** was stored in sweet syrup meant for guests to **ingest**. A brass alarm began to **blare**. The apprentice was no criminal; he was a **surrogate** son of the gardener, and an **equestrian** courier could reach the infirmary. Sera ordered the reception kept **peaceful**.

记录证明策划者意图污染池水、用谣言淹没条约，并迫使双方报复。他的演说充满刻毒，但塞拉点出非法货物时，一阵眩晕暴露其恐惧。其前体储存在供宾客食用的甜糖浆中。一只铜制警报开始鸣响。学徒并非罪犯；他是园丁的代子，一名骑马信使可以赶到医务室。塞拉命令维持接待会平静。

From the **hinterland**, a magistrate arrived to conduct an **inquest**. On the way she passed a cotton **boll** display and silenced an **impertinent** reporter who tried to turn private **grief** into policy. Her **dogma** was simple: an **inversion** of evidence and rumor nourished **cynicism**. Someone had tried to **capsize** the ceremonial vessel, but a **furtive** witness, the garden **curator**, had seen the attempt. He offered a soil **gloss** made by the embassy's **vanguard** botanists, though a visiting **pedant** challenged its **affirmation**.

一名法官从内陆赶来主持调查。途中她经过棉铃展览，并让一名企图把私人悲痛变成政策的无礼记者闭嘴。她的信条很简单：颠倒证据与谣言会滋养愤世嫉俗。有人曾试图倾覆礼仪船只，但一名鬼祟的证人，即花园馆长，看到了企图。他提供大使馆先锋植物学家制作的土壤释义，尽管一名来访的迂夫子质疑其断言。

The keeper pointed to a border **tapestry** commissioned for the garden's **tercentenary**. It showed an ancient warning against **idolatry** of power. A shipping **magnate** had funded the reception; his heraldic lion's **mane** appeared beside the first **bellwether** rose. In the ledger **log**, Sera found a **portentous** invoice hidden amid the **patter** of caterers' orders. She sent a **dispatch** to both delegations. There would be no **ovation** for accusation, no appeal to **thearchy**, only a clear **profile** of the plot and an **inviolable** promise of fair hearing.

馆长指着为花园三百周年制作的边境挂毯。它展示反对权力偶像崇拜的古老警告。一名航运巨头资助接待会；他纹章狮子的鬃毛出现在第一朵风向标玫瑰旁。塞拉在账本日志中发现一张不祥发票，藏在餐饮订单的琐碎声中。她向双方代表团发出急件。不会为指控欢呼，不会诉诸神权政治，只有对阴谋的清晰概述和不可侵犯的公正听证承诺。

The accused delegate retained his **hauteur** as a string **rondo** drifted from the ballroom toward the river **quay**. He predicted the **decimation** of his opponents' dignity. Sera replied that **diplomatic** work was less theatrical than his favorite **periodical** suggested. The confiscated valve was a **prototype**, and the gardeners deserved **kudos** for recognizing it. The treaty delegates were **bound** by duty, she said, but no one should **generalize** guilt from origin. The flaw was **inherent** in the plan, not in a people. She kept **score** of evidence and asked each witness to **reiterate** only what they knew.

被告代表保持傲慢，弦乐回旋曲从舞厅飘向河码头。他预言对手尊严将遭大规模摧毁。塞拉答道，外交工作没有他最爱期刊所说的那样戏剧化。被没收的阀门是原型，园丁认出它值得称赞。她说条约代表受职责约束，但没人应从出身概括罪责。缺陷内在于计划，而不在于某个民族。她记录证据，只请每名证人重申所知之事。

Rain made the outer walk **dreary**, and a small **mischance** nearly upset the hearing: the **hierarchical** guard captain tried to **expel** the apprentice before testimony. Sera stopped him. The plot's financial **proceeds** were routed through a company named **de** Lorme, whose clerks could **secrete** invoices. Its director, not the gardener, was the **miscreant**. He had styled himself a **visionary** who would **codify** a separate state. His stated **intension** was peace, yet the **halcyon** language concealed coercion.

雨使外侧步道凄凉，一场小意外几乎扰乱听证：等级森严的卫队长试图在证词前驱逐学徒。塞拉阻止了他。阴谋的财务收益经由名为德·洛尔姆的公司流转，其职员能够藏匿发票。罪犯是公司董事，不是园丁。他自称将为独立国家编纂法典的幻想家。他声明的意图是和平，但宁静措辞掩盖强迫。

The magistrate asked for **abstinence** from further wine while the orchestra held a single **chord**. That restraint would **benefit** the testimony. Under questioning, the director watched his confidence **metamorphose** into a **quaint** request for **concurrence**. He claimed a foreign spy was responsible, a **chimera** Sera would not **laud**. His plan was to **persecute** the treaty's quiet supporters through a forged **iliad** of insults, attributed to a **hibernian** sailor who had actually seen a **cormorant** carrying nesting straw from the valve shed.

法官要求众人暂停饮酒，乐团维持一个和弦。这种克制有利于证词。受审问时，董事看着自信变形成一项古怪的请求，要人附和。他声称外国间谍负责，这是塞拉不会赞许的妄想。他计划用一部伪造的辱骂史诗迫害条约的沉默支持者，并归罪于一名爱尔兰水手；那名水手实际看见鸬鹚从阀门棚带走筑巢草。

The bird's habit provided a **chronological** clue. Sera, a **stickler** for time, showed that the shed's **inveterate** leak began before the sailor could **comport** himself into the garden. The witnesses reached **unanimity**. A **lateral** pipe had been opened from the music court, whose **mellifluous** entertainment had masked the machinery. The director's **buffoonery** at dinner had been an effort to **estrange** the two ministers. Instead, they voted to **institute** a joint inquiry and refused his **histrionic** claims about **nationality**.

鸟的习性提供时间线索。严守时间的塞拉证明，棚屋的老漏水在水手进入花园前就已开始。证人达成一致。音乐庭院方向一根侧管被打开，甜美的演出掩盖了机器声。董事在晚餐上的插科打诨，是要离间两名部长。相反，他们投票设立联合调查，并拒绝他关于国籍的夸张说法。

The elder minister asked all present to **hearken** to the gardeners. They would **replenish** the reservoirs from a small **skiff** at dawn. The director called this pessimistic talk, but even the **defeatist** could see the **mote** of dye near the intake. His **headstrong** assistant gave an **explicable** account of payments in gold **specie**. It also revealed the shipment's **venereal** poison, smuggled under a **specialty** perfume designation. No garden could **dilapidate** so rapidly from ordinary water, and the director's **shopworn** excuses failed.

年长部长请全体倾听园丁。他们将在黎明用一叶小艇补充水库。董事称这是悲观言论，但连失败主义者都能看到进水口附近的染料微粒。他固执的助手对黄金现货付款作出可解释说明，也揭露货物的性病毒物，冒充专用香水走私。没有花园会因普通水如此迅速破败，董事陈旧的借口失败了。

With **alacrity**, the magistrate found the receipts: their seals were **interchangeable**, but their dates could **awaken** any careful reader. The **chapel** bell rang **stern** and clear while the air grew **tense**. No **illiberal** order would punish a whole delegation, Sera told the **marshal**. The damaged pipe's **membrane** had been cut with a tool belonging to a **distiller**. Its **turgid** owner was **immune** from neither inquiry nor embarrassment. Sabotage had become a **vogue** among extremists who dressed greed in **pantheism**.

法官迅速找到收据：印章可互换，但日期足以唤醒任何细心读者。小教堂钟声严肃清晰，空气愈发紧张。塞拉告诉执法官，不会用狭隘命令惩罚整个代表团。受损管道的膜被一把属于蒸馏商的工具割破。其自负主人既不能免于调查，也不能免于难堪。破坏已成为极端分子的时尚，他们以泛神论装扮贪婪。

The director tried to **lacerate** the hearing with insults, then gave a brittle **chortle** and ran **headfirst** toward the service gate. He meant to **inaugurate** a fire alarm and send the gathering **awry**. The **ramification** would have been panic. A trained **parrot** in the birdhouse repeated the lock code, allowing the officer to **reintegrate** the sealed court. The garden gate was **permeable** only during deliveries; its **untimely** opening showed the director had tried to **wangle** an escape.

董事试图用侮辱撕裂听证，随后发出干涩的咯咯笑，头也不回冲向勤务门。他想启动火警，让集会失控。后果会是恐慌。鸟舍里一只受训鹦鹉重复锁码，使警官得以重新封闭庭院。花园门只在送货时可通行；其不合时宜的开启显示董事曾设法骗取逃脱。

At the gate, a trellis threatened to **collapse**. The director sought to **evade** capture by staging a fall, but his **stunt** fooled no one. The apprentice, once the apparent **underdog**, saw him **shuffle** a vial into his sleeve. "You **dastard**," he said, too plainly for protocol. A hedge worker had used **camouflage** to watch the route, and the officer refused to **hector** the child for speaking. The vial's **aural** alarm was a **humbug**; its contents were **adulterated** dye.

门口一架棚架眼看要坍塌。董事试图演一场跌倒来逃避抓捕，但无人受骗。曾是表面弱者的学徒看见他把小瓶塞进袖子。“你这个卑鄙小人，”他说得太直白，不合礼仪。一名篱笆工人用伪装监视路线，警官拒绝因孩子开口而训斥他。瓶子的声音警报是骗局，内容物是掺假的染料。

A visiting **professor** established that the intended **atrocity** was economic, not lethal. The treaty required **abidance** by its anti-smuggling rules, and the director's **indenture** of vulnerable boatmen made his offense worse. The embassy would **renovate** the ruined channels; no gardener would **shirk** the work. Sera proposed a **commonsensical** **criterion** for recovery: clean water at every **submersible** intake. The magistrate gave a **wry** nod. A **feral** cat had knocked a jar onto the pale **alabaster** steps, an **awkward** accident that nearly let the director **delude** them with a new **rationalization**.

来访教授确认，预谋暴行是经济性的，并非致命性的。条约要求遵守反走私规则，董事让脆弱船夫受契约束缚，使罪行更重。大使馆将翻修毁坏水道；没有园丁会逃避工作。塞拉提出常识性的恢复标准：每个水下进水口都有净水。法官苦笑着点头。一只野猫把罐子碰到淡白雪花石台阶上，这一尴尬事故几乎让董事以新说辞蒙骗他们。

One boatman, nursing a **hernia**, explained that the contraband crate had a **divertible** false bottom. His testimony survived every **jibe** from defense counsel. The director's theory was **simplistic**: he blamed an **inanimate** pipe for a human choice. Sera asked the ministers to **unfetter** their shared water commission. At the **confluence** of the two canals, each party made a **plea** to repair rather than punish. The recruit admitted an **infraction**, but a **hypothetical** punishment of exile would not heal the damage.

一名患疝气的船夫解释，违禁箱有可转移的假底。他的证词经受住辩护律师的每次嘲讽。董事的理论过于简单：他把人的选择归咎于无生命管道。塞拉请部长们解除共同水务委员会的束缚。在两条运河汇流处，各方请求修复而非惩罚。新招人员承认违规，但假设性的流放处罚无法治愈损害。

Its purpose was **vindicatory** only in the honest sense: to clear the innocent, not make the guilty into **prey**. Around them, the **efflorescent** beds formed a green **convolution** against the wall. The director's **lugubrious** counsel tried to **qualify** the findings by invoking an old **eponym**, but the evidence was too **effusive**, too **empirical**.

至午夜，催眠般细雨使花园安静。塞拉写出简洁报告，抵制每位使节都想发表的五项全能式演讲。其目的只是在诚实意义上为人洗冤：澄清无辜者，而非将罪人变成猎物。周围繁茂花床在墙边构成绿色卷曲。董事阴郁的律师援引一个旧名词，试图限制结论，但证据过于充沛，也过于实证。

The finding was clear: **inasmuch** as one province had suffered greater losses, it would receive no **dominance** over the recovery board. A new irrigation **line** would be laid during the **balmy** season under the joint **judicature**. 法官命令销毁伪造的债务契约绞索。她称董事的上诉幼稚，尽管双手微颤。结论明确：由于一省损失更大，它也不会在恢复委员会拥有支配地位。新灌溉线将在宜人季节、由联合司法机构主持铺设。董事谴责程序暴虐，随后对失踪大桶给出一堆含混搪塞。委员会将在公开场合进行审查。

Sera found that barrel behind the herb house. Its receipt exposed the final **inconsistency**: the alleged accident had been scheduled to **impend** precisely as the new treaty recipient arrived. The **grantee** was a cooperative, not an **exploitative** syndicate. The ministers asked the director to **introspect**, then invited him to **encourage** his followers to accept the verdict. He would not. A museum **musket** mounted above the garden office became the focus of brief **fervor**, but the officer prevented any **misbehavior** and had the guard act **autonomously** to secure it.

塞拉在香草屋后找到那只桶。收据暴露最后的不一致：所谓事故被安排得恰在新条约受益人抵达时即将发生。受赠者是合作社，不是剥削性财团。部长请董事反省，继而请他鼓励追随者接受裁决。他拒绝了。花园办公室上方陈列的一支博物馆火枪短暂成为狂热焦点，但警官阻止不当行为，并让卫兵自行处置以保障安全。

At last the director chose to **denounce** the gardeners' **phonology** of shared place-names, claiming that a **residual** difference made agreement impossible. His claim was **unintimating** in its blandness and burdened with **superfluity**. The elder minister answered that **conjugal** alliances and ordinary **terrestrial** labor had long connected the banks more securely than any **minatory** speech. The talks had felt **interminable**, yet Sera could only **beseech** them to finish with candor.

最后董事选择谴责园丁对共享地名的语音学，声称残余差异使协议不可能。他的主张平淡得毫无威慑，又充满冗余。年长部长回答，婚姻联盟和普通陆地劳动早已比任何威胁言辞更牢固地联结两岸。会谈令人感觉无休无止，塞拉却只能恳求他们坦诚结束。

The dawn delegation met beneath a **leonine** fountain while a cello provided quiet **accompaniment**. Even the most **churl** envoy accepted the gardeners' **faculty** for repair. They set a **semiannual** inspection, acknowledged the old **inequity** in water allotments, and placed no partisan **label** upon the sabotage. The magistrate would **expose** the director's accounts, then **consolidate** the canal offices. After a **postprandial** walk among the rose beds, the ministers felt the last **perturbation** leave their breathing and began to **respire** easily.

黎明代表团在狮子般喷泉下会面，大提琴提供安静伴奏。连最粗鲁的使节也承认园丁修复的能力。他们设定每半年检查，承认旧日配水不公，且不给破坏加上党派标签。法官将揭露董事账目，再整合运河办公室。饭后漫步玫瑰花床后，部长们感到最后的扰动离开呼吸，开始轻松喘息。

At the gate, a small **primate** in the ambassador's **pedagogical** menagerie watched the workers reset an **aquiline** stone at the fountain **helm**. Sera signed the treaty's final **rendition** beside the restored **sylvan** border. The joint commission proved **efficacious** at once: it would **obligate** both governments to share water records in plain **lingo**, however **complex** the dispute. The arrested director could no longer **philander** with competing loyalties; his scheme was **untenable**. In the bright **aviary**, the last **sodden** leaves dried. Sera did not **implore** applause. She simply **marked** the repaired valve, watched the clean water defeat the remaining **deviltry**, and listened to its quiet **trickle** through the garden.

门口，大使教育性动物园中的一只小灵长类看着工人将一块鹰状石头重新安在喷泉舵位。塞拉在修复的林地边界旁签署条约最终文本。联合委员会立刻证明有效：无论争端多复杂，它都将约束两国政府用通俗语言共享水务记录。被捕董事再不能与相互竞争的忠诚调情；他的计划站不住脚。明亮鸟舍里，最后的湿透叶片干了。塞拉没有祈求掌声。她只是标记修好的阀门，看着清水战胜剩余恶行，并聆听它在花园中安静滴流。
