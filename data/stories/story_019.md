## Story for Chunk 019 - Silent Fever

By Thursday morning, Dr. Mara Venn had begun to **vilify** herself for the **complaisance** that had brought her to Saint Orra. She had accepted the ministry's **sanction** for a trial **quarantine**, believing the island's **nefarious** rumors would be easier to **govern** than a real epidemic. Her **rash** confidence had met the first patient at the pier: a girl named Ilya who would **simper**, whose **hubris** of a smile remained fixed even while her neck bent **awry**. The ministry had declared a **moratorium** on ferries, leaving every **pedestrian** on the island trapped among the salt sheds and the abandoned **mosque**. Mara's assistant, Joren, tried to **invigorate** the waiting families with coffee, but the **intransigent** mayor would only **concur** that the **minatory** red welts made evacuation **mandatory**. Outside, the island's **momentum** toward panic gathered as a crowd began to **incite** itself, every **spectator** watching the girl's spasms and waiting for someone to **reciprocate** their fear. An **officious** fisherman called quarantine an **impediment**, while Mara wondered whether a **latent** poison, not a germ, lay beneath the sand.

到星期四早晨，玛拉·文医生已经开始因为把自己送到圣奥拉岛的那种顺从而责备自己。她接受了卫生部批准的试行隔离，以为岛上那些恶毒的传言总比真正的疫病容易治理。她轻率的自信在码头遇见了第一个病人：一个名叫伊莉娅、带着假笑的女孩。即使脖子歪向一边，她脸上的笑仍然僵在那里。卫生部宣布渡船暂停，使岛上每一个行人都被困在盐仓和那座废弃清真寺之间。玛拉的助手乔伦试图用咖啡给等候的家属打气，可那位不妥协的市长只肯同意，女孩身上的威胁性红色疹块使撤离成为强制命令。岛上走向恐慌的动力在外面聚集起来，人群开始彼此煽动，每个旁观者都盯着女孩的痉挛，等着别人回报他们的恐惧。一名爱多管闲事的渔夫说隔离是障碍，玛拉却怀疑沙土下面潜伏的不是细菌，而是某种毒物。

At noon, Mara found the mayor's **arrogance** less frightening than Ilya's **insubordinate** calm. The child sat with **phlegmatic** calm beneath a **hooked** iron lamp and described the island's **iniquity** without accusation. "Do not burn the wells," she said. "The fever is asking to be heard." Mara began to **beseech** her for details, though the girl's **preternatural** voice made the room feel colder. The mayor, a **conservative** man who kept an old **figurine** in his pocket, said the child's visions were a **hallucination** caused by **scurvy** and fear. He ordered Joren to **maintain** the stockade and told Mara to **repudiate** any diagnosis that did not fit the official **list**. His **deadpan** face and **jocund** little laugh were meant to show that reason would **prevail**, but the laugh began to lessen when Mara uncovered a patch of **unproductive** ground beneath the clinic. The soil was bare except for a **dulcet** vibration that made every **consonant** in the mayor's speech tremble. In the **opprobrium** that followed, he blamed a **limousine** visitor from the capital, a **riveting** but **sanctimonious** television doctor who had once called the island's history **macabre** and offered the usual **bromide** about cleanliness. Mara recognized the mayor's **guile**: he meant to make a homeless **derelict** the scapegoat for a **petty** shortage he had concealed. Without **compunction**, he sent the town **windbag** to make the charge sound official and **forestall** questions; the man's cheerfulness was a **damper** on no one's dread.

中午时，玛拉发现市长的傲慢还不如伊莉娅那种不服从权威的镇定令人害怕。女孩平静地坐在一盏带钩的铁灯下，不带指责地描述着岛上的不公。“不要烧掉水井，”她说，“发烧是在请求被听见。”玛拉开始恳求她说明细节，尽管女孩那超自然的声音让房间仿佛更冷了。市长是个保守的人，口袋里总放着一尊小雕像。他说女孩的幻象是坏血病和恐惧造成的幻觉。他命令乔伦守住栅栏，又告诉玛拉，凡是不符合官方名单的诊断都必须拒绝承认。他那张无表情的脸和欢乐的小笑声，是想表明理智终会获胜，可当玛拉在诊所下面发现一片不出产的土地时，笑声开始平息。那片土壤光秃秃的，只有一种悦耳的振动，而市长说出的每个辅音都在振动中颤抖。随之而来的辱骂声中，他把责任推给一个乘豪华轿车从首都来的访客，那是一个引人入胜却假装虔诚的电视医生，曾把这座岛的历史称作可怕，并用关于清洁的老套话敷衍众人。玛拉看出了市长的狡计：他想把一个无家可归的流浪汉变成替罪羊，来掩盖自己藏起来的一桩微不足道的短缺。他毫无愧疚地派镇上那个空话连篇的人去把指控说得像官方结论，好抢先堵住问题，而那人的快活也没能压低任何人的恐惧。

That evening, Ilya led Mara through the **purlieu** behind the mosque, where moonlight lay on the **topsoil** like powdered glass. A **green** stain spread around a sealed well, and a **characteristic** odor of iron rose from it. Joren, whose **candor** had always embarrassed Mara, admitted that the mayor had paid him to **blur** the clinic's records and **dissimulate** the first deaths as heatstroke. "I should have refused," he said, his **countenance** collapsing. "But the town is **needy**, and he promised medicine." Mara gave him an **analgesic** for the pain in his wrist after he suffered a **sprain** forcing the rusted cover. She told him that the sickness might be **contagious**, yet the still water was **static**, and the **sloth** of the ministry had left them no culture tests. The mayor arrived with a **musket** and an **eccentric** brass **talisman**, performing **bravado** before the well. He tried to **extort** Mara's silence and **exhort** Joren to kneel, but Ilya lifted an **elliptical** stone marked with a **hieroglyph**. The mark was **equivalent** to the diagram on the ministry's cargo sheet, a warning that the well's buried chamber contained a living colony. Mara used a needle to **embroider** the symbol on a strip of cloth, then pressed tree **gum** into the crack. The seal opened. A breath rose from below, warm and **full-bodied**, and the mayor's **levity** vanished. Above them, a ferry flag stayed **aloft** in the upper rigging, **inert** in the wind; its **shallow**, **wordy**, **redundant** safety notice was no help.

那天傍晚，伊莉娅带玛拉穿过清真寺后面的边缘地带，月光落在表层土上，像撒开的玻璃粉。一口封闭的井周围蔓延着绿色污痕，空气里升起铁锈特有的气味。乔伦一向坦率，这一点总让玛拉不自在，此刻他承认市长曾付钱让他弄模糊诊所记录，并把最初的死亡伪装成中暑。“我本该拒绝的，”他说，脸色垮了下来，“可镇上的人很贫困，他答应给药。”玛拉给了他一片止痛药，因为他在撬动生锈井盖时扭伤了手腕。她告诉乔伦，病症可能具有传染性，可井水静止不动，而卫生部的懒怠使他们连培养检测也没有。市长带着一支火枪和一枚古怪的黄铜护身符赶来，在井边表演虚张声势。他试图勒索玛拉，让她保持沉默，又劝诱乔伦跪下，但伊莉娅举起了一块椭圆形石头，上面刻着象形文字。这个标记与卫生部货单上的图示相同，意味着井下埋藏的密室里有一个活着的菌落。玛拉用针把这个符号绣在一条布带上，再把树胶压进裂缝。封口打开了。一股温暖而浓重的气息从下面升起，市长的轻浮神态消失了。他们头顶上，一面渡轮旗帜高挂在上层索具中，在风里毫无动静，旗上的肤浅、冗长而多余的安全告示没有任何帮助。

The colony was not a disease but a buried, breathing intelligence, and its first release had been **manifest** in the red welts. It could **homogenize** human speech into one low tone, Ilya explained, and that tone did not **mean** obedience; it meant recognition. The mayor tried to **balk**, then repudiate the evidence, but a wave of heat passed through the ground and made his old figurine split. Mara had to **gamble**: close the well and leave the island's people to a slow fever, or open it fully and risk a larger **exodus** into the mainland. She used the radio to send a **petition** for a medical **reprieve**, not for the mayor but for the quarantined families. Joren, now **resolute**, helped her **exert** the winch while the mayor watched with **disdain**. The cable descended; the colony answered with a dulcet note. Mara's **anticipation** of disaster gave way to a **rapt** attention as the note became an **ode** in a language no living mouth could speak. The island's people gathered in uneasy **solidarity**. A **bellwether** of the crowd, the outgoing baker, asked Mara whether they could **reconcile** fear with survival. She said they could try, but the answer would require **sedulous** testing, not faith. The mayor's sanctimonious authority was gone, and his opprobrium was now public, yet Mara felt no triumph. She could not **liken** the colony to a god or a cure. She could only maintain the open valve and wait. A **perspicacious** nurse used a **whet**stone to sharpen the cutter, while the mayor tried to **cadge** a place on the departing boat; Mara would not **commend** that mercy or let his old **accession** to office **trump** the children. She ordered him to surrender the **miscreant** ferryman he had bribed to break the moratorium.

这个菌落并不是疾病，而是一种埋在地下、会呼吸的智慧；伊莉娅解释说，它第一次释放时，红色疹块便已经清楚地显现出来。它能够把人类的语言均匀化成一个低沉的音调，但那个音调并不意味着服从，而意味着辨认。市长先是抗拒，随后又否认证据，可一股热浪穿过地面，使他那尊旧雕像裂开。玛拉必须冒险作出选择：关上水井，让岛上的人慢慢发烧，还是彻底打开井口，冒险让更大规模的逃亡涌入大陆。她用无线电提交了一份请求医疗暂缓执行的正式申请，申请对象不是市长，而是被隔离的家属们。乔伦此刻已经坚决起来，帮她用力拉动绞盘，市长则带着蔑视旁观。缆绳向下沉去，菌落以一个悦耳的音符回应。玛拉对灾难的预期让位于全神贯注的倾听，因为那个音符渐渐变成一首颂诗，用没有活人能够说出的语言吟唱。岛上的人们在不安中团结起来。带头的面包师问玛拉，他们能否让恐惧与生存彼此和解。她说可以尝试，但答案需要勤勉而持续的检测，而不是信仰。市长假装虔诚的权威已经消失，他的耻辱也公开呈现在众人面前，可玛拉没有感到胜利。她不能把这个菌落比作神，也不能把它比作疗法。她能做的只有维持阀门开启，然后等待。一名有洞察力的护士用磨刀石磨利切割器，市长则想讨一个登船位子，玛拉不肯赞同那份宽恕，也不肯让他昔日获得官位的经历压过孩子们。她命令他交出那个被他收买、曾破坏渡船暂停令的恶棍摆渡人。

Before dawn, the heat began to **subside** and the welts on Ilya's neck faded to **inconsequential** freckles. The ministry radio remained a **vapid** hiss, but Joren's **outgoing** voice carried across the yard as he organized water and **tender** food for the families. Mara found a **chameleon** asleep beside the well, its skin matching the stone, and realized the colony had not asked for worship. It had asked for a reprieve from burial. The mayor, no longer an **ideologue**, sat within the **stockade** and accepted the people's disdain without protest. When he finally spoke, his voice held no **asperity**. "I thought control was the only **viable** protection," he said. "I was wrong." Mara answered with candor that his arrogance had made the danger worse, but she also admitted her own **ignorance**. Ilya, **devoted** to the living warmth below, placed the marked stone back in its **position** and helped **undergird** the cover with fresh timber. The act was a small **rapprochement** between the island and the thing beneath it. Mara put an **earplug** in one ear to protect herself from the returning tone, leaving the other open. Through it she heard a final **peep** from the chamber, a sound that did not **confuse** her now. It was not a command, nor a **nonsense** warning. It was the colony breathing, and the people breathing back. The baker would **ruffle** the blankets, **instigate** a **jest**, and **embrace** the children while the old coronation crown, preserved for the island's ceremonial **coronation**, went aboard the evacuation ferry; Mara knew the **benign** morning might not **endure**, but she did not **waver**.

黎明之前，热度开始平息，伊莉娅脖子上的疹块也淡成了无关紧要的雀斑。卫生部的无线电仍然只发出乏味的嘶嘶声，可乔伦那爽朗外向的声音传遍院子，他正在为家属们安排饮水和柔嫩的食物。玛拉发现一只变色蜥蜴睡在井边，皮肤与石头融为一色，这时她明白，菌落并没有要求人们膜拜。它要求的是一场不被埋葬的暂缓。市长不再是一个意识形态狂热者，他坐在栅栏里，默默接受众人的蔑视。最后他开口时，声音里没有粗暴。“我以为控制是唯一可行的保护，”他说，“我错了。”玛拉坦率地回答，市长的傲慢使危险变得更糟，但她也承认自己的无知。伊莉娅忠于地下那股活着的温暖，把刻有标记的石头放回原来的位置，又用新木料帮助从底部加固井盖。这个动作成了岛屿与地下之物之间一次微小的和解。玛拉在一只耳朵里塞上耳塞，以保护自己免受重新响起的音调伤害，同时让另一只耳朵保持畅通。她从那里听见墓室传来的最后一声轻微的鸣响，而这一次，她不再困惑。那不是命令，也不是毫无意义的警告。那是菌落在呼吸，而人们也在回应它的呼吸。面包师会抖开毯子，煽动大家开个玩笑，并拥抱孩子们。那顶为岛上加冕仪式保存的旧王冠被送上撤离渡轮，玛拉知道这个温和的早晨也许不会持久，但她没有动摇。
