## Story for Chunk 014 - White Maelstrom

Martin Vey and Mara Quill crossed the **insular** ice shelf during a **sporadic** whiteout, hauling an **unpretentious** sled from the **seminary** where they trained polar weather observers. Martin, a **novice** field engineer, had a **naive** faith in the map; Mara, an **avid** glaciology **practitioner**, trusted the **tangible** tremor beneath her boots. A **gargantuan** pressure ridge had begun to **bulge**, its **precipitous** face shedding blue shards. “The pattern is **pertinent**,” Mara said in a **laconic** voice. “Those pulses of **irregularity** **portend** a slab release.” Martin checked their **fail-safe** beacon, the only **defender** against being lost in the storm, and saw its battery **deplete** faster than planned. The **baleful** sky and a low **sensation** in his ribs made him **muddle** the cable colors. Mara used old **mnemonics** to **concatenate** the readings, then told him to **suspend** the drill. Their choice was **deliberate**: **preempt** the slide by cutting a narrow release trench, or **traverse** the ridge and risk its **implacable** collapse.

马丁·维和玛拉·奎尔在零星的白障中穿越孤立的冰架，拖着一架朴素的雪橇，刚离开培养极地气象观测员的训练学院。马丁是一名新手野外工程师，天真地相信地图；作为冰川学从业者的玛拉则相信靴底下能感觉到的震动。一道巨大的压力脊正在鼓起，陡峭的冰壁不断落下蓝色碎片。“这种模式很相关，”玛拉简洁地说，“那些异常脉冲预示着雪板会滑落。”马丁检查了故障保护信标，那是防止他们在风暴中迷失的唯一保障，却发现电池消耗得比预计快。阴险的天空和肋骨间的异样感觉让他弄混了电缆颜色。玛拉用旧记忆法把读数连起来，随后叫他暂停钻机。他们慎重地选择：先切出一条狭窄的泄压沟来抢先引发滑落，或者横越山脊，冒着它无情崩塌的风险。

Mara took the **ductile** cutter while Martin used a **sling** to lower the charge into a **cramped** crevasse. A gust stripped snow from a **seamy** **covert** crack, exposing **antediluvian** ice full of black grit and **decay**. “No **flippancy** now,” Martin said; the task was **demanding**, and the wind could **exacerbate** every error. Mara gave a **mordant** smirk, then set the charge with **cunning** fingers. A distant **shriek** answered. From the drifting snow came a pale **ewe**, followed by a hungry snow leopard with a **prepossessing** coat and a **rapscallion**'s reckless gait. Martin raised the flare, but its flame would **desiccate** their last fuel cell. Mara found a **supple** hose, fitted it to the sled's canister, and made a **provisory** jet of vapor. The cat recoiled; the **somatic** shock left Martin **frail**; he began to **stammer** as he read the marker aloud. Still, he could **hack** it free from the ice, and the engraved **incantation** of an earlier expedition showed a route **away** from the unstable shelf.

玛拉拿起可延展的切割器，马丁则用吊索把炸药放进狭窄的冰缝。一阵风吹走一道隐秘而裂纹斑驳的缝隙上的雪，露出布满黑色砂砾和腐化痕迹的远古冰层。“现在别轻浮，”马丁说；这项工作要求很高，风会使每个失误恶化。玛拉尖刻地冷笑了一声，用灵巧的手指安放炸药。远处传来尖叫。飘雪中出现一只苍白的母羊，后面跟着一只饥饿的雪豹，它的皮毛讨人喜欢，步态却像个鲁莽的恶棍。马丁举起信号火焰，但火会耗干他们最后一块燃料电池。玛拉找到一根柔软的软管，把它接到雪橇气罐上，喷出一道临时的蒸汽。雪豹退开了；这次身体上的冲击使马丁虚弱，读出路标内容时开始结结巴巴。尽管如此，他仍能把路标从冰里砍出，上面刻着早期远征队的咒语，指示一条远离不稳定冰架的路线。

They followed the marker through a **sinuous** ravine where each footfall made snow **squelch**. Martin wanted to **douse** the flare, but Mara insisted its **lustrous** light could **deter** the predator. Her **epistemology** was practical: what they could measure mattered more than what they feared. Yet the leopard kept pace, **indifferent** to vapor and glare, while the ridge started to **disintegrate** behind them. Mara found a **rudimentary** repeater half buried in ice, an **on** switch stiff with frost, and a weak **periodical** signal that repeated too faintly to summon help. “We cannot **quibble**,” she said. “We must **outmaneuver** it.” Martin used the repeater's **probe** to **rein** the charge circuit, then let the line **mingle** with the beacon lead. The signal was barely **intelligible**, but it could **marshal** the sled's emergency rotor. The rotor's controller began to **babble** in bursts, a **verbose** electronic **peroration** of faults; Martin had to **mediate** between its competing safety locks. He refused to **abdicate** control, even as **somnolence** pulled at his eyelids and the **depressant** cold dulled his breathing.

他们沿着路标进入一条蜿蜒的峡谷，每一步都让积雪发出挤压声。马丁想浇灭火焰，但玛拉坚持说它有光泽的亮光能威慑捕食者。她的认识论很务实：能测量的事物比恐惧更重要。然而雪豹对蒸汽和强光毫不在意，始终跟着他们，山脊则在身后开始瓦解。玛拉发现一台半埋在冰中的初级中继器，结霜的开启开关僵住了，还有一道反复发出却微弱得无法呼救的周期信号。“不能吹毛求疵，”她说，“我们必须智胜它。”马丁用中继器的探针控制炸药回路，再把线路与信标导线接合。信号勉强可以理解，却足以调度雪橇的应急旋翼。旋翼控制器断续地胡言乱语，像一段冗长的故障结语；马丁必须在互相冲突的安全锁之间调停。他拒绝放弃控制，尽管困倦拉扯着眼皮，抑制性的严寒也使他的呼吸变得迟钝。

The rotor spun, then failed with a **break** that sent the sled sideways. Martin could **inveigh** at the **remiss** maintenance crew, but blame had the **pointlessness** of shouting into wind. Beneath a rock overhang, they found a shepherd sheltering with the pale ewe; he had watched the snow leopard's trail turn along the lee side of the ravine. Martin and Mara chose to **barter** their remaining flare for that information, gave it to the shepherd, and received a direction to a covered route past the animal. There they found an old **charlatan**'s tourist cache, **replete** with useless **lottery** tickets, but its metal case held a **sage** guide's emergency **protocol**. The **politic** instructions told them to **enact** a ground pulse, **quell** panic, and **restore** the rotor by warming its intake. Martin's **disinclination** vanished. He would not let a technical failure **destroy** them, nor let the predator **subvert** their escape. Mara, usually **reticent**, began an **oath** in a clear **paean** to their **amity**: each would carry the other through the **skirmish** ahead. That **altruism** gave Martin **relief**. He packed snow around the casing to **intensify** the pulse, then used the charge to **emancipate** the jammed fan. The leopard lunged; Mara made a **sneer** and a **taunt**, and Martin drove the rotor blast between them and the animal.

旋翼转动后又因一次断裂而失效，雪橇被甩向一旁。马丁本可以痛骂失职的维修人员，但责备如同对着风喊叫一样毫无意义。他们在岩石突檐下发现一名牧羊人正带着那只苍白的母羊避风，他看见雪豹的足迹转向峡谷背风的一侧。马丁和玛拉决定用剩下的信号火焰交换这条消息，把火焰交给牧羊人，并得到一条绕过雪豹的有遮蔽路线的指引。那里有一名骗子留下的游客储物箱，里面装满无用的彩票，但金属盒中存着一位贤明向导的紧急规程。精明的说明要求他们制造地面脉冲、平息恐慌，并通过加热进气口恢复旋翼。马丁的不情愿消失了。他不会让技术故障毁掉他们，也不会让捕食者破坏逃生。平日沉默的玛拉发出清晰的誓言，唱出赞颂他们友好的歌：两人都要带着对方穿过前方的小冲突。这份利他主义给了马丁宽慰。他在外壳周围堆雪来增强脉冲，再用炸药解放卡住的风扇。雪豹扑来；玛拉冷笑着嘲弄它，马丁则把旋翼气流打在他们与雪豹之间。

The pulse cracked the shelf, creating a safe gap before the main slide; Martin and Mara could **achieve** the far moraine as the leopard retreated. In the quiet after, he could **marvel** at Mara's **astute** timing, though she dismissed praise with a **stolid** nod. Their survival was no **punch line**, no **neologism** for an accident, and no tale of **ignominy**. It was a **content** conclusion earned by an **onerous** night. Martin used the last battery charge to **annul** the damaged drill's automatic restart, while Mara logged the **enormity** of the fault and the **depravity** of the contractor who had knowingly concealed its dangerous wiring. Their **provident** repairs would **nurture** a safer route for future travelers rather than **withhold** warning data. A **philistine** contractor tried to **depose** Martin from his repair assignment, but Mara's brief report proved his work was **integral**. The contractor's **autocracy** ended when the crew saw the evidence; nobody needed a **pedestal**, **derision**, or a grand **tribute**. Martin did not **dally** or **obsess** over blame. The **quandary** had taught him to **abide** by Mara's calls without a **jaunty** pose or **indulgent** optimism. A **gangly** radio mast carried their rescue signal to a **legion** of nearby camps, where a communications **maven** could **accelerate** the evacuation. Mara, a **neophyte** rescuer only two winters earlier, would not **embarrass** a frightened survivor; she planned against fuel **deprivation** instead. Under the returning **illumination** of the storm lamps, Martin and Mara made the beacon reliable again, ready for the next traveler who might need it.

脉冲震裂冰架，在主滑坡前形成安全缺口；雪豹退去时，马丁和玛拉得以抵达远处的冰碛。随后安静下来，马丁惊叹于玛拉敏锐的时机判断，尽管她只是木然地点头，不接受称赞。他们的生存不是一个笑点，不是为事故创造的新词，也不是耻辱故事。这是艰难一夜换来的满足结局。马丁用最后的电池电量取消受损钻机的自动重启，玛拉则记录故障的严重性，以及承包商明知线路危险却刻意隐瞒的邪恶行径。他们有远见的修理将为未来旅行者培育一条更安全的路线，而不是扣留警报数据。一名庸俗的承包商试图把马丁从维修任务中罢免，但玛拉简洁的报告证明他的工作不可或缺。工作人员看到证据后，承包商的独裁结束了；没人需要基座、嘲笑或盛大的颂辞。马丁没有拖延，也没有执迷于责备。这个困境教会他遵从玛拉的判断，不再摆出轻快的姿态或抱持放纵的乐观。一座细长的无线电桅杆把他们的求救信号传给附近众多营地，那里的一位通信行家能够加快撤离。仅在两个冬天前还是新手救援员的玛拉不会使惊恐的幸存者难堪，而是为燃料匮乏预作计划。在风暴灯重新亮起的照明下，马丁和玛拉让信标再次可靠，准备帮助下一位可能需要它的旅行者。
