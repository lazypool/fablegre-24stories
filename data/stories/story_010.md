## Story for Chunk 010 - Breathing Moon

Tomas Reed guided the lander through dust that seemed to hold a **lien** on every joint, a drifting veil they could not shake. Mira Sol, a formerly **dissolute** scavenger who had become a disciplined field engineer, sorted samples in non-**alphabetical** trays, while Inez Vale, a **virtuoso** exobiologist, asked the mission to **patronize** a patient survey rather than a hasty extraction. The engine's **muffler** softened its exhaust as they watched a valley of blue reeds **ebb** and rise with the sunless tide. Tomas could **demonstrate** that the reeds shared nutrients, but no **partisan** theory explained why their roots drew a star-shaped **doodle** around the lander. The **knotty** pattern included an **apostrophe**-like break. As a navigation **tyro**, Tomas first called it a **fallacy** of perspective; Inez corrected his **obtuse** angle. A dominant colony of translucent grazers had begun to **evict** smaller grazers from the basin. It was a **momentous** migration, and Mira stood **venerating** the quiet precision of it. Their drilling plan went into **abeyance** when Inez raised a red **flag**: the creatures were leaving a mineral **residue** that formed a living **yoke** around the reeds.

托马斯·里德驾驶着着陆器穿过一层仿佛对每个接缝都拥有留置权的尘雾，他们怎么也甩不掉它。米拉·索尔曾是放荡不羁的拾荒者，如今已成为严谨的现场工程师，她整理着不按字母顺序摆放的样本盒；外星生物学家伊内兹·维尔要求任务组资助耐心的勘测，而非仓促开采。引擎的消声器柔化了排气声，他们看见蓝色芦苇谷随着无日潮汐退落又升起。托马斯能证明芦苇彼此交换养分，却没有偏颇的理论能解释为何根系在着陆器周围画出星形涂鸦。这个棘手图案有一道像撇号的缺口。作为导航新手，他起初把它当成视角谬误；伊内兹纠正了他钝角般的判断。占优势的一群半透明食草生物正把较小的食草生物逐出盆地。这是一次重大的迁徙，米拉敬畏其安静的精确。伊内兹举起红旗后，他们暂停钻探：生物留下的矿物残渣正把芦苇连成一道活的轭。

Inez learned the organisms' chemical **argot** by tasting air through a filter. The warmth the organisms gave the surrounding ground was **unrequited** by the cold regolith, yet their **tenacity** kept the meadow alive. “Do not **construe** the retreat as panic,” she said. Her **determination** rested on a **simplicity** Tomas had missed: the colony moved **retrograde** across its own nutrient trail to feed larvae hidden below. Mira, normally **ascetic** about supplies, proposed that they **evacuate** the lander and make an **emendation** to the route. Tomas would not **forsake** the creatures, though the worsening dust began to **beleaguer** their seals. Years in flight had **inure**d him to alarms, but he ordered the team to **disperse** across a shallow **gully** and map the roots. “We do not **grovel** before an unknown ecosystem,” Mira said, beginning to **bedeck** her helmet. “We must **conciliate** it through care, not force.” Under their boots, a thin crust began to **dissolve**, revealing the organisms' **provenance**: a warm saline aquifer whose **instability** could turn its **porcelain** ceiling to shards.

伊内兹用过滤器品尝空气，从中学会了这些生物的化学行话。这些生物给周围土层的温暖得不到寒冷风化层的回应，但它们的韧性让草甸存活。“别把后撤解释为恐慌，”她说。她的决心建立在托马斯忽略的朴素事实之上：族群沿着自己的养分痕迹逆行，为地下幼体供食。平时对补给颇为克制的米拉建议撤出着陆器，并修订路线。托马斯不愿抛下这些生物，尽管加剧的尘暴不断围困舱体密封。多年的飞行已使他习惯了警报，但他仍命令众人分散到浅沟中绘制根系图。“我们不必向未知生态系统卑躬屈膝，”米拉说着扣紧装饰过的头盔，“我们必须以照料而非强迫来安抚它。”脚下薄壳开始溶解，露出它们的起源：一片温暖咸水层，失衡会令其瓷质顶板化为碎片。

Mira felt **chagrin** when her earlier core sample proved **dire**: its heat would **accumulate** enough pressure to kill the nursery. Tomas called himself a **simpleton** for accepting the **vacuous** mission **prescription** to extract first and observe later. Spore-feeders were **rife** beneath the **saddle** of rock, and Inez could **anticipate** their response: leave the aquifer untouched and the larvae would drown; vent it and the adult colony would lose its **autonomy** from the roots. No one offered **obeisance** to the mission schedule. Inez's **intuitive** solution was to use the lander's **tugboat** thrusters to lift one section of crust, but the model was **untenable** until Mira found a **flexible** membrane in the root web. Tomas gave her no **commendation**, only a careful **check** of the stresses. The pulse might **weaken** the cavern, make the grazers **misbehave**, and **taint** the first contact with harm. Still, the web's **resilience** answered a low **snarl** of vibration; the familiar **routine** of extraction now seemed a **glossy**, **labile** lie.

米拉因先前的岩芯样本十分凶险而懊恼：其中的热量会积聚成足以杀死育幼层的压力。托马斯觉得自己像个傻子，竟接受了那套空洞的任务规定，先开采、后观察。岩石鞍部下孢子食者极为繁盛；伊内兹预见到它们的反应：不碰含水层，幼体会淹死；泄压，成体族群就会失去相对于根系的自主性。没有人向任务日程低头。伊内兹凭直觉提出用着陆器如拖船般的推进器抬起一片地壳，但模型难以成立，直到米拉在根网中发现柔韧薄膜。托马斯没有表彰她，只仔细检查应力。脉冲可能削弱洞穴，让食草生物失控，并给这次初次接触留下伤害的污点。然而根网的恢复力回应着一声低沉咆哮，熟悉的开采流程此刻像一则光鲜而易变的谎言。

The **circular** chamber below them opened in a **windy** plume, exposing an **elephantine** brood-mother whose **thorny**, **lackluster** mantle protected thousands of larvae. She remained **sedate**, not hostile; her **cherubic** young made a **measured** clicking, like a distant **tambourine**. The **despotic** impulse to take command felt **feral** in Tomas. A sensor lure could **entice** the adults toward the surface, but their sudden **expansion** would be an **abomination** for the fragile meadow. Inez faced a final **puzzle** and the **obstreperous** tremor of the ceiling. “The **lank** roots are an **estimable** bridge,” she said. “The **anterior** chamber can fill. It will not **perturb** the brood.” Tomas resisted **avarice** for a dramatic discovery and used a slow, **innocuous** thermal **crescendo** instead. The **credulous** crew watched an **acre** of **serrate** roots lift like an **epaulet**. It was not **imprudent** luck but a calculation made by a **distraught** Tomas through **shale** and mineral ribs that seemed to **emboss** the cavern. They held **neutrality** between rescue and study, chose to **suppress** the drill, and accepted the **immutable** cost: no sample would test the web's **durability**. A green **tint** began to **accrete** on the **enamel** crust as Mira helped **prune** broken filaments. Their **success** was no **labyrinth** of claims, no **outlandish** tale to **prate** about. In the cave beginning to **glisten**, they did not **knead** a world into human use; they chose to **pack** their tools and continue the **expedition** on foot. The long **trudge** over **gravel** could **nauseate** them, but Mira shared **tangy** water. Tomas recorded no human **appellation** for the species, only to **assert** its **animus**: it had chosen to live. That was not **speculation**. The rescued web did not **discombobulate** their assumptions; it served to **highlight** Tomas's old **dyslexia** for living patterns. With **alacrity**, they chose to **renounce** extraction. The **grisly** fear that had been a constant **peeve** lost its **calorie** of power. Mira's **mirth** returned as they left no **salvage** operation, no plan to **augment** the site. Their **parlance** became observation, their promise to **preserve** distance. Behind them, the **moth-eaten** sky seemed to **braid** with the dawn. The dilemma had not been a **disaster** but a **ring** of dependence, a **paradox** the reeds could **loll** within. They would not **circumscribe** its future.

他们脚下的圆形空腔在多风的喷流中打开，露出一只巨大的育母。它多刺、暗淡的外套保护着数千幼体。她安静而非敌对；天使般的幼体发出有节拍的咔嗒声，如同遥远的小手鼓。托马斯感到一种野蛮而专横的冲动，想要接管局面。传感器诱饵能引诱成体上浮，但它们突然膨胀会对脆弱草甸造成可憎的破坏。伊内兹面对最后的谜题和喧闹的顶板震动说：“细长的根系是一座值得尊敬的桥，前方腔室可以进水而不扰乱育群。”托马斯压下对轰动发现的贪欲，改用温和、无害、渐强的热量。轻信的队员看着一英亩锯齿状根系像肩章一样抬起。这不是轻率的运气，而是心绪不宁的托马斯穿过页岩与仿佛压印在洞穴上的矿物肋所作的计算。他们在救援与研究间保持中立，关闭钻头，并接受不可改变的代价：没有样本能检验根网耐久性。米拉修剪断丝时，绿色色调开始在珐琅般地壳上积聚。成功不是可以吹嘘的离奇故事，也不是一座论断迷宫。在闪亮洞穴中，他们没有把世界揉成供人使用的形状，而是收拾工具，徒步继续探险。碎石上的长途跋涉令人反胃，米拉便分了些辛香的水。托马斯没有为物种记下人类命名，只写下一个断言：它有求生的意志。这不是推测。获救的根网没有打乱他们的理解，反而凸显托马斯对活体图案的阅读障碍。他们迅速放弃开采。那种可怕恐惧曾是不断的烦恼，如今失去供给它的能量。米拉重新欢笑，他们没有展开打捞，也没有扩张此地的计划。三人的交流变成观察，他们承诺保持距离。身后虫蛀般的天空似乎与晨曦编织在一起。这个两难不是灾难，而是一圈相互依存，芦苇得以在其中悠然起伏的悖论。他们不会限制它的未来。
