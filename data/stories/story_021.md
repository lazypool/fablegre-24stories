## Story for Chunk 021 - Salt Horizon

Dr. Leda Voss, a desert **podiatrist** who could read a blister the way other doctors read an X-ray, had been three hours into a rescue flight over the Kharif **savanna** when the radio began to **fluctuate**, dissolving into crackle and long silences. Below, a **galaxy** of salt pans caught the dying light with false **grandeur**, and she tried to recall which side of them the survey camp stood. The distress call had been brief: Arman, the cartographer, had collapsed after his **hike** along the irrigation line. Her **supposition** was heatstroke—simple, treatable, familiar. But the **fateful** decision to set down was made for her when Noor, the **svelte**, **saturnine** **pilot** who never once smiled, pointed at a ribbon of smoke leaking from the engine cowling. "We can **divert** to the ranger shed," Noor said in her **terse** way, and Leda only nodded as a **whiff** of hot oil entered the cabin. The engine answered with a **jarring** cough.

莉达·沃斯医生是一名沙漠足病医生，读一个水泡就像别的医生读一张X光片。她乘救援直升机飞越哈里夫热带草原已经三个小时，无线电开始波动，化作噼啪杂音与长长的静默。下方，一片片盐田在迟暮的光中铺开，带着一种虚假的壮观，她努力回想勘测营地位于哪一边。求救讯息很简短：制图师阿尔曼沿灌溉渠徒步之后倒下了。她的猜测是中暑，简单，可治，熟悉。但降落这个决定命运的选择早已不由她做主：努尔，那个身材纤细、神情阴郁、从没笑过的飞行员，指着发动机罩缝隙漏出的一缕烟。"我们可以绕到护林员的小屋，"努尔以惯常的简洁说。莉达只是点了点头。一股热油味钻进机舱，发动机随即发出一声刺耳的咳嗽。

She could **infer** neither their height nor the machine's remaining will; she only watched the salt flats **coalesce** into a pale, **uncanny** map beneath them. They put down beside the shed at dusk, in a gust of dust. Its **perimeter** was a drift of **sawdust**, fish bones, and empty fuel tins, and a **rodent** fled through a gap in the sandbag **rampart** as the rotors died. Arman lay under an awning, his clothes in **disarray**, his bootlaces **slack**, the **epidermis** of his heel split clean open. He admitted, with a thin apology of a smile, that he had tried to **malinger** through the injury because the expedition leader called every show of caution a **farce** and mocked him to his face as a **dolt**. Leda thought of the man she had met once at the camp gate—a man of **considerable** girth, fond of his own voice—and pressed her lips together. Behind them the water tower's **gaudy** paint flaked off its small **turret**, and she felt how far they were from any help. "Don't touch it yet," she said, more to herself than to Noor, though the sight of Arman's foot had already turned her stomach.

她既无法推断自己的高度，也无法判断机器还剩多少意志，只看着盐地汇合成一张苍白而离奇的地图。黄昏时，他们在小屋旁降落，卷起一片尘土。小屋周界散着锯屑、鱼骨和空燃料罐，旋翼停转时，一只啮齿动物从沙袋壁垒的缝隙里逃走了。阿尔曼躺在遮棚下，衣服凌乱，靴带松弛，脚跟的表皮裂开一道口子。他带着一个勉强称得上微笑的表情承认，自己一直想假装伤势不重，硬撑下去，因为探险队领队把每次谨慎都称作闹剧，还当面骂他是个笨蛋。莉达想起那位领队，她在营地门口见过他一次，体形相当庞大，爱听自己的声音，于是抿紧了嘴唇。他们身后，水塔俗丽的漆片从被风压弯的小塔楼上剥落，她感到这里离任何救援都太远了。"别碰它，"她说，这话更像是说给自己听，虽然阿尔曼脚上的情形已经让她胃里翻搅。

"That leader is a **libertine** with a compass," Noor said, but her **raillery** was too sharp to **mollify** anyone, least of all Arman, who had heard worse. The wound's **malodor** made Leda **bristle**; this was far beyond any blister she had read in a clinic. She could **identify** a long thorn buried below the **superficial** cut, yet its **protean** **shadow** on the portable scanner began to **vex** her, shifting shape with every sweep. Arman offered an **anecdote** about a snakebite he had once survived, then tried to **dupe** them into believing the pain was ordinary, though the effort did not **irritate** him for long—he flinched when Noor laid a hand near the wound. A jar of water on the sill held three **tadpole** larvae and an **aquatic** beetle, which proved, Leda told herself, that the irrigation channel still ran somewhere close. It was a small comfort, and she would need every bit of it.

"那个领队是个拿着罗盘的放荡鬼，"努尔说，但她的戏谑太尖锐，谁也安慰不了，尤其是阿尔曼，他听过更难听的话。伤口的恶臭让莉达浑身发紧，这早已超出她诊所里读过的任何水泡。她能辨认出有一根长刺嵌在浅表伤口之下，可它在便携扫描仪上的变形阴影开始让她烦恼，每扫一遍就换个形状。阿尔曼讲了一段自己曾被蛇咬的轶事，随后又想骗她们相信这疼痛没什么了不起，不过这种努力没能让他坚持多久，努尔把手靠近伤口时，他缩了一下。窗台上一个水罐里有三只蝌蚪和一只水生甲虫，莉达对自己说，这说明灌溉渠仍在附近某处流淌。这是小小的安慰，而她需要它。

While Noor used a towing strap to **hitch** the damaged helicopter to a ring bolt in the wall, Leda took the **crucial** step: she would **extract** the thorn before the swelling could **inflame** the whole foot. Then the rain began—**inclement**, **incessant**, drumming a **barrage** against the corrugated roof. She heated forceps over a stove and used **bracing** water to **irrigate** the wound, while Arman's pain rose into **vociferous** **fury** that she did not yield to, because she could not. "Hold him," she told Noor, and Noor held him without a word. A **conspicuous** black thread emerged from the cut, and Leda understood the scanner's **discrepancy**: this was no thorn at all but the root of a poisonous grass, entered beside the bone and curving **up** toward an artery. Her hand stayed steady, but her mind raced. She had a **penchant**, she knew, for doubting her own instruments at the worst possible moment.

努尔用牵引带把损坏的直升机拴在墙上的环栓上时，莉达采取了至关重要的一步：在肿胀把整只脚引向发炎之前，她要取出那根刺。接着雨落了下来，天气严酷，雨声不绝，密密的雨点像一阵弹雨敲打着波纹铁皮屋顶。她在炉子上加热钳子，又用清凉提神的水冲洗伤口，阿尔曼的疼痛化作喧闹的狂怒，她没有让步，因为不能让步。"按住他，"她对努尔说。努尔一句话不说地按住了他。一根醒目的黑丝从伤口中露出，莉达这才明白扫描仪上那处不一致的原因：这根本不是刺，而是一种有毒草的根，从骨旁钻入，正向上弯向一条动脉。她的手很稳，心里却翻江倒海。她知道自己有个癖好，总在最要命的时刻怀疑自己的仪器。

Noor, who kept a **tenacious** respect for procedure even amid chaos, found a field **syllabus** in the emergency kit. Its **figurative** drawings of muscles and veins were useless until she noticed the actual **order** of the blood vessels printed in a margin. "I can **decode** the image," she said, tracing the line with a fingertip. Leda had an **inkling**, cold and certain, that the grass was the same species Arman had been sent to **discover**, and that the leader's **caprice** had sent a sick man out alone to **victimize** him further. She gave a **cogent** instruction to hold still, used a **backhanded** pull, and drew out a **prodigious** root, black as a **grotesque** bristle. Arman did not **respire** for one terrible beat. Then he coughed, and the shed's stale **effluvium** gave way to the smell of rain.

即使在混乱中，努尔对规程仍怀有顽强的敬重，她在急救包里找到一份野外课程提纲。其中那些比喻性的肌肉与血管示意图毫无用处，直到她注意到页边印着血管真正的排列顺序。"我能解读这张图，"她说，用指尖沿着那条线划过去。莉达有一种又冷又确定的感觉：这种草正是阿尔曼被派去发现的物种，而领队的一时任性把一个病人单独派出去，进一步迫害了他。她发出一句条理分明的指令叫阿尔曼别动，用反手一拉，拔出一条长得惊人的根，黑得像一根怪诞的硬毛。阿尔曼有一拍没有呼吸。随后他咳了起来，小屋里陈旧的浊气让位给雨的气息。

By morning, the antidote kit was empty except for a **cameo** vial of mineral paste. Leda could **procure** more only by reaching the abandoned pumping station two kilometers away, where the channel crossed a **mire**. Arman asked them to leave him, but that was the old **delusion** of a useful man, and she said so, gently. Noor listed **scads** of reasons to stay: the storm had torn the map, damp cloth had failed to **insulate** the engine, and a **pitiless** flash flood had erased the track. Still, a **perceptible** blue gleam pulsed through the grass—a **harbinger**, Leda thought, of water moving in the drainage ditch. She made an **inventory** of bandages, rope, and the **restorative** tablets that remained, then told Arman, with more **tact** than warmth, that he must sip, not **nibble**, the hard biscuit. "You cannot malinger your way out of this," Noor said, and laughed at the wrongness of her own joke.

到早晨，解毒包几乎空了，只剩一小瓶浮雕般精致的矿物膏。莉达只有走到两公里外废弃的泵站才能设法弄到更多药物，水渠在那里穿过一片泥沼。阿尔曼叫她们留下他，但那只是一个自认有用之人的旧日错觉，她温和地把这话说了出来。努尔数出许多多得不胜枚举的理由留在原地：暴雨撕碎了地图，湿布没能给发动机隔热，残酷的山洪冲掉了小径。然而草丛里有一抹可察觉的蓝光在跳动，莉达想，那是排水沟里水流的先兆。她清点了剩下的绷带、绳索和恢复体力的药片，又以多于温情的机智告诉阿尔曼，他必须小口啜饮，不能只啃硬饼干。"你不能靠装病走出这个困境，"努尔说，说完又因自己玩笑的不妥而笑了。

The three of them took the **detour** along the rampart, where the flood had begun to **grind** shells into the mud. At the pumping station they found no antidote but a locked cabinet and the survey leader's **prologue** to a failed paper, in which he had tried to **vaunt** himself as a **visionary** who would **vanquish** the desert. Leda felt no wish to **indict** a man absent from the plain; she had seen how easily a **juggernaut** of ambition could **abet** harm without a **knave** at its center. The leader had ordered the workers to **repeal** rest breaks, called a **sapient** technician a **sluggard**, and demanded **perjury** on the injury forms; his **aggressive** **reign** had left **untold** harm behind it. "This is who signs our paychecks," Noor said, and for once her voice held something like bitterness.

三人沿着沙袋壁垒绕路前行，洪水已经开始把贝壳磨进泥里。泵站里没有解毒剂，却有一个上锁的柜子，还有勘测队领队一篇失败论文的前言，他在其中自夸是能征服沙漠的远见者。莉达不想指控一个不在这片平原上的人；她见过野心这股不可阻挡的力量多么容易助长伤害，即使它的核心并没有一个恶棍。领队曾命令工人废除休息时间，把一位明智的技术员叫作懒汉，还要求他们在伤情表上作伪证。他那咄咄逼人的统治留下了无法尽述的伤害。"这就是给我们发薪水的人，"努尔说，这一回她的声音里难得地带了一丝苦涩。

Yet the cabinet held a hand pump, and Noor managed to **doff** its rusted cover, **hone** the valve with a file, and **amalgamate** two broken tubes into one. The water they raised was bitter but **tasty** after a day of salt, and its **plasticity** in the clay seal proved the well still had pressure. Leda mixed the mineral paste with charcoal for **detoxification** and watched the swelling recede as a **clarion** call of cranes crossed the brightening flats. Arman whispered a **valediction** to the map he had ruined, then asked to **emigrate** from fieldwork—not from fear, he said, but because he wanted a life that did not mistake pain for proof. Noor said nothing **acrimonious**; she simply started the repaired engine. In a faded **chiaroscuro** mural on the wall, an old **prude** from the camp's first **melodrama** held a pump lever above a painted **skeleton**, and the **portentous** image seemed to **reveal** nothing but the artist's fear.

可柜子里有一台手泵。努尔设法卸下生锈的盖子，用锉刀把阀门磨利，又把两根断管合并成一根。抽上来的水很苦，但在经历一天盐味之后却算得上可口，黏土封口处水的可塑性证明井里仍有压力。莉达把矿物膏与木炭混合用于解毒，看着肿胀渐渐消退，一群鹤发出清亮的鸣叫飞过逐渐明亮的盐地。阿尔曼低声向他毁掉的地图告别，然后说他想离开野外工作，不是出于恐惧，他说，而是因为他想要一种不把疼痛误当成证明的生活。努尔没有说任何尖刻的话，只是启动了修好的发动机。墙上褪色的明暗对照壁画里，营地第一出情节剧中的一位老古板握着泵杆，站在画出的骷髅上方，那幅不祥的画似乎只显露出画家的恐惧。

Leda did not **abominate** the leader, nor did she **decry** Arman's wish to leave; **esteem** for work could not excuse a **repugnant** system. Noor used the **lathe** in the shed to cut a **wax** plug for the fuel line, then told Leda with **concision** that the weather was **propitious**.

莉达并不憎恶那位领队，也不谴责阿尔曼想离开的愿望；对工作的敬重不能为一个令人厌恶的制度开脱。努尔用小屋里的车床切出一枚蜡制塞子补住燃油管，然后以简洁的口吻告诉莉达，天气正适合起飞。

As the rotors lifted them above the salt pans, Leda did not **hallow** survival or preach a **sermon** about it. She used the clean back of the syllabus to **jot** one line: "Never let **fervor** **supersede** **probity**, **objectivity**, or **equality**." She would **instill** that rule in every trainee who came after her, not as **mesmerism** and not as a **fad**, but as a way to **discern** what no forecast could **predict**. Arman, still pale, watched the flats slide beneath them and said nothing; he seemed already to be someone else. Leda thought of the rain, of the root, of the ledger of a man who had never once set foot in a clinic, and decided that some distances could not be crossed by any vehicle. The engine hummed on, and the salt horizon unrolled beyond the glass, empty of judgment and full of light.

旋翼把他们带离盐田时，莉达没有把幸存神圣化，也没有就此说教。她只用课程提纲干净的背面匆匆写下一句："绝不能让热忱取代正直、客观或平等。"她会把这条规则灌输给每一位后来的受训者，不是当作催眠术，也不是当作一时的风尚，而是作为一种辨明任何预报都无法预测之事的方法。阿尔曼仍然苍白，望着盐地在身下滑过，什么也没说；他似乎已经变成了另一个人。莉达想起那场雨，那根毒根，那个从未踏进过诊所的人的账目，然后明白，有些距离是任何交通工具都跨越不了的。发动机继续嗡嗡作响，盐的地平线在玻璃窗外不断展开，没有评判，只有光。
