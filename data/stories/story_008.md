## Story for Chunk 008 - Drowned Compass

Adrian Vale found the **concave** mouth of the sandstone shaft at noon, its walls painted with a procession that seemed to **mime** a caravan's last climb. Mara Chen would **vouch** for the survey, but Ivo chose to **flout** the heat warning and start the excavation machine. Its diesel cough began to **fulminate** beneath the ridge. Mara used a filter to **decant** muddy seepage into their last **stock** of canteens. Adrian wanted to **eschew** Ivo's **badinage**, yet the jokes helped **buoy** them while the **raffish** mechanic tightened a belt. A faint **movement** stirred dust over the **fallow** basin. In the **foreground**, wind worried the tarps and began to **harass** their eyes. None of them would **desert** the buried chamber after coming so far. Adrian asked for basic **propriety** around the carvings, but Ivo continued to **lionize** the machine and claimed its teeth would **collude** with the rock in destroying the blockage. Mara's **veneration** for the site made her notice an **unwonted** line of wet sand. Their water was **scant**, and the first metallic crack seemed to **sunder** the afternoon.

正午时，阿德里安·维尔找到砂岩竖井内凹的入口，墙上的行列图案仿佛在模仿一支商队最后的攀登。玛拉·陈愿意为勘测担保，但伊沃无视高温警告，启动了挖掘机器。柴油机在山脊下发出爆裂般的轰鸣。玛拉把浑浊渗水过滤后倒入他们仅剩的水壶。阿德里安本想避开伊沃的玩笑，可这些笑话在那位不拘小节的机械师调整皮带时仍给他们一点支撑。休眠盆地上方的尘土微微移动，近处的风扯动篷布，开始折磨他们的眼睛。既然走到这里，谁也不肯放弃埋藏的石室。阿德里安要求尊重这些雕刻，伊沃却一味吹捧机器，说它的齿会与岩石合谋毁掉堵塞物。玛拉对遗址的敬意让她留意到一条反常的湿沙线。饮水不足，随后第一声金属裂响仿佛把下午劈成两半。

Adrian stayed **steadfast** as the drill carriage tipped, telling Mara to **accommodate** the injured Ivo in the narrow alcove. The old rig began to **dilapidate** with terrifying speed; its promised safety now looked merely **verisimilar**, though the warning placard was **authentic**. Ivo had been **stingy** with spare bolts, so they had to **retrench** their plan to one escape route. It was not **seemly** to blame him while stone fell. Adrian watched Ivo **fumble** for the shutdown lever until Mara's **deft** fingers found the emergency **design** etched beside it. The **truth** was immediate: the machine had drilled through a clay plug. Mara did not **solicit** forgiveness or excuse Ivo's **misuse** of the rig. Adrian would not **postulate** that the tremor had ended; in a **sober** voice he ordered them uphill. There was no **acclaim**, only the roar of water. Mara tried to **refine** their route, refusing to **disregard** a channel cut in **immemorial** floods. The rising wall did not **daunt** Ivo. His **affinity** for engines gave way to a **discursive** stream of directions, but he would not **resign** himself to dying as a **sybarite** of easy camps.

阿德里安坚定不移，看着钻车倾斜，叫玛拉把受伤的伊沃安置进狭窄壁龛。老旧设备迅速崩坏，原本的安全承诺看起来只是貌似可信，尽管警告牌确是真的。伊沃舍不得使用备用螺栓，于是他们只能把计划缩减为一条逃生路线。石块不断落下，此时责怪他并不合适。伊沃摸索停机杆时，玛拉灵巧地找到了旁边刻着的紧急构造。真相立刻显现：机器钻穿了黏土塞。玛拉没有请求原谅，也没有为伊沃错误使用钻机辩解。阿德里安没有假定震动已经结束，而是冷静地命令大家往高处走。没有喝彩，只有水声轰鸣。玛拉调整路线，不肯忽视远古洪水切出的水道。上涨的洪水没有吓倒伊沃。他对机器的偏爱变成一连串离题的指令，但他不愿像一个贪图舒适的营地客那样坐等死亡。

Mara remained **vigilant** where a **comely** shelf of red stone crossed the wadi. Ivo's **jovial** face hardened when the flood exposed his deliberate decision to ignore the radio warning as **perfidy** toward the team. The channel became **dreary** under brown water, and his expensive **sartorial** jacket turned **transparent** with rain. Adrian had to **arrest** their panic. Mara's sharp **remonstrance** stopped Ivo from trying to **pillory** himself with apologies. “Do not **stonewall** the fear,” she said. “Use it.” The storm's **inscrutability** made every echo dangerous. Adrian measured the **circumference** of a half-buried stone wheel, then saw that a fallen boom could **mangle** their only rope. Ivo used the winch **brake** before its **dormant** motor slid free, though the effort brought him near **exasperation**. A tin of **herbicide** from the supply crate floated past beside cold **dew** washed from the rock; then a wadi **carp** surged from a sudden pool. The absurd sight earned no **panegyric**, but Ivo called it a **votary** of survival. Mara rejected the **mockery** and tied the rope with quiet **bonhomie**. As the water began to **cavort** around their knees, Adrian did not **advertise** bravery or **squander** breath. He helped disconnect the snagged cable to **defuse** the battery hazard, knowing one **incendiary** spark could end them. The carved wheel received a brief **homage** as their anchor.

玛拉在一道秀丽的红色岩台上保持警觉，岩台横跨干涸河谷。洪水揭露伊沃先前故意无视无线电警报的决定，是对团队的背信之举；他脸上的欢快消失了。褐水让水道变得阴沉，他昂贵的外套被雨淋得透亮。阿德里安必须阻止恐慌。玛拉严厉的规劝阻止伊沃用不断道歉来公开羞辱自己。她说，不要拒绝承认恐惧，要利用它。暴雨的不可预测让每道回声都很危险。阿德里安量出半埋石轮的周长，发现倒下的吊臂会毁掉他们唯一的绳子。伊沃拉住绞盘刹车，避免沉睡的马达滑脱，努力几乎让他暴怒。补给箱里的一罐除草剂漂过，岩石上的冷露也被冲走，接着一条沙漠鲤鱼从突现的水洼跃出。这荒谬画面得不到颂扬，伊沃却称它为生存的信徒。玛拉没有理会嘲弄，平和地系好绳索。洪水在膝边翻腾时，阿德里安没有夸耀勇气，也不浪费呼吸。他帮忙断开缠住的电缆，以消除电池火花可能引燃他们的危险。那只石轮作为锚点，得到片刻敬意。

Their **affection** for one another became **palpable** once the rope held; no **trite** promise could make the climb easier. The high ledge did not **enamor** Adrian, but its grooves let Mara **decipher** the route of an ancient spillway. Ivo made one **brash** jump, and Adrian became the **sentinel** who caught his harness. That rescue was the only **inducement** Ivo needed to stop trying to **abase** himself. Beyond the ledge, a sand-buried **galley** carving marked the **exigent** turn toward a **fluvial** cut in the plateau. The **irascible** storm forced each **posture** to be deliberate as rain began to **suffuse** the ravine. The footing was **treacherous**, but Adrian dictated a field **affidavit** for the record: the flood had made ascent their only defensible choice. Mara gave an **imperious** order toward the **pinnacle**, away from the collapsing **landfill** of spoil. Her **subtlety** was a simple **compliment** to Ivo's hands, enough to turn his old **fanatic** devotion to machinery into care for people. He carried each **batch** of tools so the load was not **lopsided**. At the summit they used dried dates to **leaven** a **rambunctious** silence. The emergency tent was barely **habitable**, yet Ivo began to **atone** by sharing the last batteries. Adrian made the **smart** choice to **shun** the ruined rig, its **spartan** shelter, and any **submissive** wish to return for samples. They would **abstain** from reopening the shaft. In the **congenial** dawn, Ivo did not **prevaricate**: a single radio **chord** in the emergency jingle had warned him about the storm, and he had made the **hasty** choice to **disavow** it. He would **curb** that pride. Mara said the only **appropriate** memorial was to leave the chamber in its **sepulchral** darkness, without an **unctuous** speech, **penalty**, or **crutch** of excuses. The flood receded through a stone **mesh** while a guide's **vernacular** call crossed the **interregnum** after thunder. Adrian marked the map with a **caret**, a **seminal** correction he would never **tout**. A bright **speck** of sun followed a **circuitous** path over the ridge. Mara, always **savvy**, refused a **turgid** account of the ordeal. Their clothes were **glutinous** with clay, but the old **unfamiliarity** among them was gone. Ivo's **lissome** repair of the radio completed their **fusion** as a team. They walked out **askew** but alive, beyond **censure**, past the site's **wickedness** of chance, ready to **contemplate** the rescue without a **boycott** of fear or a need to **exculpate** anyone.

绳子承重后，他们彼此的情感变得清晰可感，任何陈词滥调的保证都无法让攀爬更轻松。高岩台没有让阿德里安忘乎所以，但上面的沟槽让玛拉解读出古老泄洪道的走向。伊沃鲁莽地一跳，阿德里安像哨兵一样接住了他的安全带。这次救援足以让伊沃停止自我贬低。岩台之外，一幅被沙掩埋的战船刻画标出通往高原河道缺口的紧急转向。暴躁的风暴迫使他们谨慎摆放每个姿势，雨水开始灌满峡谷。脚下极不可靠，但阿德里安口述了一份现场宣誓陈述以存档，洪水已使向上攀爬成为唯一站得住脚的选择。玛拉强硬地命令大家奔向峰顶，远离正在坍塌的废土堆。她巧妙地赞扬伊沃的手艺，让他对机器近乎狂热的迷恋转变为对人的照顾。他分批携带工具，使负重保持平衡。登顶后，他们吃干枣，给喧闹后的沉默一点松弛。应急帐篷勉强能住，伊沃却把最后的电池分享出来，开始弥补过错。阿德里安明智地避开毁坏的设备、简陋的掩体，以及任何回去取样的顺从念头。他们决定不再打开竖井。融洽的黎明中，伊沃不再含糊其辞：紧急提示音里的一段无线电和弦曾警告过暴雨，他却仓促地否认了它。他会克制这种骄傲。玛拉说，唯一适当的纪念是让石室留在墓穴般的黑暗中，不需要油滑的演说、惩罚或借口作拐杖。洪水穿过石网退去，雷声停歇的间隙，一名向导用当地话呼喊。阿德里安用校正符在地图上作下重要更正，绝不会拿它夸耀。一点阳光沿曲折路径越过山脊。玛拉一向精明，拒绝把这场磨难写成冗长浮夸的故事。他们的衣服沾满黏土，但彼此曾有的陌生感已经消失。伊沃灵巧地修好无线电，完成三人作为团队的融合。他们步伐歪斜却活着走出峡谷，超越责难，穿过命运的恶意，准备思考这场救援，不再抵制恐惧，也不需要为任何人开脱。
