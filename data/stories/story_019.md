## Story for Chunk 019 - Silent Fever

By Thursday morning, Dr. Mara Venn had begun to **vilify** herself for the **complaisance** that had brought her to Saint Orra. She had accepted the ministry's **sanction** for a trial **quarantine**, believing the island's **nefarious** rumors would be easier to **govern** than a real epidemic. Her **rash** confidence had met its first patient at the pier: a girl named Ilya who would **simper** as though nothing were wrong, her **hubris** of a smile fixed even while her neck bent **awry**. The ministry had declared a **moratorium** on ferries, leaving every **pedestrian** on the island trapped among the salt sheds and the abandoned **mosque**. Mara's assistant, Joren, tried to **invigorate** the waiting families with coffee, but the steam did nothing to soften the fear.

到星期四早晨，玛拉·文医生已经开始因为把自己送到圣奥拉岛的那种顺从而责备自己。她接受了卫生部批准的试行隔离，以为岛上那些恶毒的传言总比真正的疫病容易治理。她轻率的自信在码头遇见了第一个病人：一个名叫伊莉娅的女孩，她总是假笑着，仿佛什么都没发生，那张傲慢而僵硬的微笑即使在脖子歪向一边时也依然挂在那里。卫生部宣布渡船暂停，使岛上每一个行人都被困在盐仓和那座废弃清真寺之间。玛拉的助手乔伦试图用咖啡给等候的家属们打气，可那热气丝毫没能冲淡恐惧。

The **intransigent** mayor would only **concur** that the **minatory** red welts made evacuation **mandatory**. Outside, the island's **momentum** toward panic gathered as the crowd began to **incite** itself, every **spectator** watching the girl's spasms and waiting for someone to **reciprocate** their fear. An **officious** fisherman called the quarantine an **impediment** and shook his knife at the stockade. Mara let the noise pass over her; she kept wondering whether a **latent** poison, not a germ, lay beneath the sand. At noon she found the mayor's **arrogance** less frightening than Ilya's **insubordinate** calm. The child sat with **phlegmatic** composure beneath an old iron lamp, watching the doorway as if she had been waiting all her life for a doctor who would listen.

那位不妥协的市长只肯同意，威胁性的红色疹块使撤离成为强制命令。外面，岛上走向恐慌的势头不断积聚，人群开始互相煽动，每个旁观者都盯着女孩的痉挛，等着别人回报他们的恐惧。一名爱管闲事的渔夫说隔离是障碍，朝栅栏晃着刀。玛拉任由那喧闹从头顶掠过，她仍在怀疑沙土下面潜伏的不是细菌，而是某种毒物。中午时，她发现市长的傲慢还不如伊莉娅那种不服从权威的镇定令人生畏。女孩带着一种平静的冷漠坐在一盏旧铁灯下，望着门口，仿佛这一生都在等一个愿意倾听的医生。

The child sat beneath a **hooked** iron lamp and described the island's **iniquity** without accusation, her voice low and level. "Do not burn the wells," she said. "The fever is asking to be heard." Mara began to **beseech** her for details, though the girl's **preternatural** voice made the room feel colder than the sea wind. The mayor, a **conservative** man who kept an old **figurine** in his pocket for luck, said the child's visions were a **hallucination** caused by **scurvy** and fear. He ordered Joren to **maintain** the stockade and told Mara to **repudiate** any diagnosis that did not fit the official **list**. His **deadpan** face and **jocund** little laugh were meant to prove that reason would **prevail**, but the laugh began to falter when Mara knelt and pressed her palm to the bare ground.

女孩坐在一盏带钩的铁灯下，不带指责地描述着岛上的不公，声音低而平稳。"不要烧掉水井，"她说，"发烧是在请求被听见。"玛拉开始恳求她说出细节，尽管女孩那超自然的声音让房间比海风更冷。市长是个保守的人，口袋里总揣着一尊小雕像图个吉利。他说女孩的幻象是坏血病和恐惧造成的幻觉。他命令乔伦守住栅栏，又告诉玛拉，凡是不符合官方名单的诊断都必须否认。他那张无表情的脸和欢乐的小笑声本是想证明理智终会获胜，可当玛拉跪下、把掌心按在光秃秃的地面上时，那笑声开始动摇。

Mara uncovered a patch of **unproductive** ground beneath the clinic, soil bare except for a **dulcet** vibration that ran up through the bone and made every **consonant** in the mayor's speech tremble. In the **opprobrium** that followed, he blamed a **limousine** visitor from the capital, a **riveting** but **sanctimonious** television doctor who had once called the island's history **macabre** and offered the usual **bromide** about cleanliness. Mara recognized the mayor's **guile**: he meant to make a homeless **derelict** the scapegoat for a **petty** shortage he had concealed. Without **compunction**, he sent the town **windbag** to make the charge sound official.

玛拉在诊所下面发现一片不出产的土地，土壤光秃秃的，只有一种悦耳的振动顺着骨头往上爬，令市长说出的每个辅音都在颤抖。随之而来的辱骂声中，他把责任推给一名从首都坐豪华轿车来的访客，那是个引人入胜却假装虔诚的电视医生，曾把这座岛的历史称作可怕，并用那套老掉牙的清洁说辞敷衍众人。玛拉看穿了市长的狡计：他想把一个无家可归的流浪汉变成替罪羊，来掩盖自己藏下的一桩微不足道的短缺。他毫无愧疚地派镇上那个空话连篇的人去把指控说得像官方结论。

Joren's cheerfulness was a **damper** on no one's dread, and questions still mounted despite the mayor's attempt to **forestall** them. That evening, Ilya led Mara through the **purlieu** behind the mosque, where moonlight lay on the **topsoil** like powdered glass. A **green** stain spread around a sealed well, and a **characteristic** odor of iron rose from it. Joren, whose **candor** had always embarrassed Mara, admitted that the mayor had paid him to **blur** the clinic's records and **dissimulate** the first deaths as heatstroke. "I should have refused," he said, his **countenance** collapsing. "But the town is **needy**, and he promised medicine." Mara gave him an **analgesic** for the pain in his wrist after he suffered a **sprain** forcing the rusted cover, and told him the sickness might be **contagious**.

乔伦的快活没能压低任何人的恐惧，尽管市长想抢先堵住问题，疑问还是越来越多。那天傍晚，伊莉娅带玛拉穿过清真寺后面的边缘地带，月光落在表层土上，像撒开的玻璃粉。一口封闭的井周围蔓延着绿色污痕，空气里升起铁锈特有的气味。乔伦一向坦率，这一点总让玛拉不自在；此刻他承认，市长曾付钱让他弄模糊诊所记录，并把最初的死亡伪装成中暑。"我本该拒绝的，"他说，脸色垮了下来，"可镇上的人很贫困，他答应给药。"玛拉给了他一粒止痛药，他撬生锈井盖时扭伤了手腕，并告诉他，这病也许具有传染性。

Yet the water in the well was **static**, and the **sloth** of the ministry had left them no culture tests to settle it. The mayor arrived with a **musket** and an **eccentric** brass **talisman**, performing **bravado** before the well. He tried to **extort** Mara's silence and **exhort** Joren to kneel, but Ilya lifted an **elliptical** stone marked with a **hieroglyph**. The mark was **equivalent** to the diagram on the ministry's cargo sheet, a warning that the buried chamber held a living colony. Mara used a needle to **embroider** the symbol on a strip of cloth, then pressed tree **gum** into the crack. The seal opened, and a breath rose from below, warm and **full-bodied**.

可井水是静止的，卫生部的懒怠又让他们连培养检测都没有，无从断定。市长带着一支火枪和一枚古怪的黄铜护身符赶来，在井边表演虚张声势。他试图勒索玛拉的沉默，又劝乔伦跪下，但伊莉娅举起一块椭圆形的石头，上面刻着象形文字。这个标记与卫生部货单上的图示相同，意味着地下密室中有一个活着的菌落。玛拉用针把这个符号绣在一条布带上，再把树胶压进裂缝。封口打开了，一股温暖而浓重的气息从下面升起。

The mayor's **levity** vanished with that breath. Above them, a ferry flag stayed **aloft** in the upper rigging, **inert** in the still air, its **shallow**, **wordy**, **redundant** safety notice no help at all. The colony was not a disease but a buried, breathing intelligence, and its first release had been **manifest** in the red welts. It could **homogenize** human speech into one low tone, Ilya explained, and that tone did not **mean** obedience; it meant recognition. The mayor tried to **balk**, then repudiate the evidence, but a wave of heat passed through the ground and cracked his old figurine. Mara had to **gamble**: close the well and leave the island to a slow fever, or open it fully and risk a larger **exodus** to the mainland. She used the radio to send a **petition** for a medical **reprieve**, not for the mayor but for the quarantined families.

那股气息升上来，市长的轻浮神态消失了。头顶上，一面渡轮旗帜高挂在上层索具中，在静止的空气里毫无动静，旗上那张肤浅、冗长又多余的安全告示毫无用处。这个菌落并非疾病，而是一种埋在地下、会呼吸的智慧；伊莉娅解释说，它第一次释放时，红色疹块便是明证。它能把人类的语言均匀化成一个低沉的音调，而那个音调并不意味着服从，而是意味着辨认。市长先是抗拒，随后又否认证据，可一股热浪穿过地面，把他那尊旧雕像震裂了。玛拉必须赌一把：关上水井，让岛上的人慢慢发烧，还是彻底打开，冒险让更大规模的逃亡涌入大陆。她通过无线电递交了一份医疗暂缓申请，对象不是市长，而是被隔离的家属们。

Joren, now **resolute**, helped her **exert** the winch while the mayor watched with **disdain**. The cable descended; the colony answered with a dulcet note. Mara's **anticipation** of disaster gave way to **rapt** attention as the note became an **ode** in a language no living mouth could speak, and the islanders gathered in uneasy **solidarity**. A **bellwether** of the crowd, the outgoing baker, asked whether they could **reconcile** fear with survival. She said they could try, but the answer would require **sedulous** testing, not faith. She could not **liken** the colony to a god or a cure, so she simply maintained the open valve and waited. A **perspicacious** nurse used a **whet**stone to sharpen the cutter, while the mayor tried to **cadge** a place on the departing boat.

乔伦此刻已经坚决起来，帮着她用力拉动绞盘，市长则带着蔑视旁观。缆绳向下沉去，菌落以一个悦耳的音符回应。玛拉对灾难的预期让位于全神贯注的倾听，因为那音符渐渐变成一首颂诗，用一种没有活人能够说出的语言吟唱，岛上的人也在不安中聚成一体。人群中带头的那位即将离职的面包师问，他们能否让恐惧与生存彼此和解。她说可以尝试，但答案需要勤勉的检测，而不是信仰。她不能把这个菌落比作神明或疗法，于是只维持阀门开启，静静等待。一名有洞察力的护士用磨刀石磨快切割器，市长则想讨一个登船的位置。

Mara would not **commend** that mercy, nor let his old **accession** to office **trump** the children's safety. She ordered him to surrender the **miscreant** ferryman he had bribed to break the moratorium. Before dawn, the heat began to **subside**, and the welts on Ilya's neck faded to **inconsequential** freckles. The ministry radio remained a **vapid** hiss, but Joren's **outgoing** voice carried across the yard as he organized water and **tender** food for the families. Mara found a **chameleon** asleep beside the well, its skin matching the stone, and understood that the colony had never asked for worship. The mayor, no longer an **ideologue**, sat inside the **stockade** and accepted the people's disdain without protest. When he finally spoke, his voice held no **asperity**. "I thought control was the only **viable** protection," he said. "I was wrong." Mara answered honestly that his arrogance had made the danger worse, but she admitted the coming day might not be **benign**.

玛拉不肯赞同那份宽恕，也不肯让他昔日登上官位的经历压过孩子们的安全。她命令他交出那个被他收买、曾破坏渡船暂停令的恶棍摆渡人。黎明前，热度开始消退，伊莉娅脖子上的疹块淡成无关紧要的雀斑。卫生部的无线电仍只发出乏味的嘶嘶声，可乔伦那爽朗的声音传遍院子，他正在为家属们安排饮水和柔嫩的食物。玛拉发现一只变色蜥蜴睡在井边，皮肤与石头融为一色，这时她明白，菌落从未要求膜拜。市长不再是意识形态狂热者，他坐在栅栏里，默默接受众人的蔑视。最后开口时，他声音里已没有粗暴。"我以为控制是唯一可行的保护，"他说，"我错了。"玛拉坦率地回答，他的傲慢让危险变得更深，但她承认，即将到来的早晨也许并不温和。

She also admitted her own **ignorance**. Ilya, **devoted** to the living warmth below, placed the marked stone back in its **position** and helped **undergird** the cover with fresh timber, a small **rapprochement** between the island and the thing beneath it. Mara put an **earplug** in one ear to guard against the returning tone, leaving the other open, and through it she heard a final **peep** from the chamber, a sound that did not **confuse** her now. It was not a command, nor a **nonsense** warning. It was the colony breathing, and the people breathing back. The baker would **ruffle** the blankets, **instigate** a **jest**, and **embrace** the children while the old crown, saved for the island's ceremonial **coronation**, went aboard the evacuation ferry. Mara knew the gentle morning might not **endure**, but she did not **waver**.

她也承认了自己的无知。伊莉娅忠于地下那股活着的温暖，把刻有标记的石头放回原位，又用新木料从底部加固井盖，这是岛屿与地下之物之间一次微小的和解。玛拉在一只耳朵里塞上耳塞，护住自己不受重新响起的音调侵扰，另一只耳朵保持畅通。她从那里听见墓室传来最后一声轻微的鸣响，这一次，她不再困惑。那不是命令，也不是毫无意义的警告。那是菌落在呼吸，而人们也在回应它的呼吸。面包师会抖开毯子，煽动大家开个玩笑，并拥抱孩子们；那顶为岛上加冕仪式保存的旧王冠则被送上撤离渡轮。玛拉知道，这个温和的早晨也许不会持久，但她没有动摇。
