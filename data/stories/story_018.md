## Story for Chunk 018 - Salt Witness

At the **outset** of the second survey day, Mara Venn, the ship's **curator** of biological samples, watched a lone **pixel** pulse on the sonar screen where no reef should be. The **magnitude** of the growth was absurd: a black mat had climbed the intake pipe overnight, adding meters of living tissue while everyone slept. Beyond the glass, a **panoramic**, **pellucid** dawn opened over the **archipelago**, every island drawn in clean ink. Captain Ivo Rusk answered her **salutation** with a **stiff** nod. A former **plantation** manager, he disliked laboratory **jargon**, and he found Mara's **chary** request to hold position simply intolerable. The charter **contract**, he said, allowed no delay, and bad weather might **hamper** their return to the mainland.

第二次勘测日伊始，生物样本馆员玛拉·文恩望着声呐屏幕上本不该有礁石的地方，一个孤零零的像素在闪烁。那片生长物的规模荒谬得惊人：一夜之间，黑色菌毯沿进水管爬升了数米，在所有人沉睡时长出了活组织。玻璃窗外，清澈而全景式的黎明在群岛上展开，每一座岛屿都用干净的黑线勾勒。船长伊沃·拉斯克用一个僵硬的点头回应她的问候。他从前管理过种植园，讨厌实验室行话，也完全无法容忍玛拉谨慎地要求原地待命。他说包船合同不允许耽搁，坏天气可能妨碍他们返回大陆。

His **sanguine** certainty did not **deject** her. The mat's return had clearly **miff**ed him, and he told the cook to **mince** a sample and be done with it. Mara opened the intake **hatch** and found translucent, **mite**-sized animals beginning to **knit** themselves into a living **compound** around the valve. Their smell was **acrid**, their threads almost **edible** in appearance; they could **slake** neither hunger nor thirst, yet they turned seawater brine into a corrosive **preservative**. She used a torch to **singe** one strand, then tried to **solder** a sensor lead above it so the deck could read what was happening.

他那种乐观的笃定没有使她沮丧。菌毯的再度出现显然令他恼火，他叫厨师把样本切碎了事。玛拉打开进水口的舱盖，发现一群半透明、大小如螨虫的生物正把自己编织成包裹阀门的活体复合物。它们散发着刺鼻的气味，丝状物看上去几乎可以食用；它们既不能解渴也不能充饥，却会把海水盐卤变成腐蚀性的防腐剂。她用喷灯烧焦一根丝，又试着把一根传感器引线焊在上方，好让甲板上的人能读到正在发生的事。

The colony recoiled from the flame, gave a dry **quack** through the pipe, and multiplied. By the **exactitude** of its salinity readings, Mara could **deduce** that the ballast tanks already held larvae. This was no harmless **anomaly**. It could **bypass** the filters, **circumvent** every quarantine routine, and make every fish in the next port the **quarry** hunted by an invasive species that had no predator of its own. Its **mercurial** behavior defeated every **orthodox** test they could run, and its one reliable **feature** was a salt shell it could **sculpt** and reshape at will. She asked Rusk to **confine** the evidence to the lab and **deposit** one routine sample at the island station; he answered that she must not **disseminate** panic.

那群生物从火焰前缩回去，隔着管道发出一声干涩的鸭叫，然后繁殖起来。凭着盐度读数的精确性，玛拉能推断压载舱里已经有了幼体。这不是无害的反常现象。它能够绕过过滤器，规避每一道隔离程序，让下一座港口里的每条鱼都沦为这种入侵物种捕食的猎物，这种物种本身没有天敌。它变幻莫测的行为令每一种正统检测都失效，而它唯一可靠的特性，是能随心所欲塑造并重塑的盐制外壳。她请拉斯克把证据限制在实验舱内，只在岛上站点存放一份例行样本；他回答她，不得传播恐慌。

He was already beginning to **slur** after a drink from his flask, and he held the bottle like a talisman. Mara saw the **imperial** crest on his old ring and understood his private **apostasy**: years of obedience had made him resent any rule that diminished him. He offered an **amicable** bargain first, then turned **adamant** when she refused. The station's **doyen**, Dr. Sato, asked by radio whether they could **ratify** a quiet transfer of the samples. Rusk tried to **adulate** Sato, to **extol** her judgment, to **paraphrase** Mara's warning into harmless **circumlocution**. When that failed, he called Mara **callous** and **insensitive**, and began to **lambast** her caution as professional vanity.

他喝过保温瓶里的酒后已经开始口齿不清，把那瓶子攥得像护身符。玛拉看到他旧戒指上的帝国徽章，明白了他私下的背离：多年服从使他痛恨任何会削弱自己的规矩。他先提出一个友好交易，被她拒绝后又变得固执。站点的老前辈佐藤博士从无线电里问，他们能否批准一次低调的样本转运。拉斯克试图谄媚佐藤，颂扬她的判断，把玛拉的警告改述成无害的迂回说法。这一套行不通后，他便称玛拉冷酷又麻木不仁，开始痛斥她的谨慎是职业虚荣。

His temper mounted until he was ready to **lambaste** Sato for **cronyism**, called containment **therapeutic** nonsense, and drew an **invidious** comparison between Mara and the island women he thought he knew. Her **kindred**, he said, would reward obedience with an **encomium**, while stubbornness earned only blame. The rule had long been **resented** by Rusk, and his anger turned **vindictive**. Mara set the alarm to an **apocalyptic** pitch and broadcast the raw sensor feed to the bridge. "I **aver** that this vessel is contaminated," she said, refusing his order to **recant**. He moved to **dismiss** her and erase the recording, but she locked the bridge controls by laying a **stencil** over the emergency keypad and entering the override sequence she had found in his notebook while repairing his **tonsorial** razor.

他的火气一路攀升，终于准备痛骂佐藤任人唯亲，把隔离称为所谓治疗性的胡言乱语，还在玛拉和他自以为认识的岛上女人之间作了惹人生厌的比较。他说，她的亲族会用一篇颂词奖赏服从，而固执只能招来责难。这条规矩早已让拉斯克积怨在心，他的怒气也随之变得恶毒。玛拉把警报调到近乎末日般的尖锐，把原始传感器数据播到驾驶台。"我郑重断言，这艘船已受污染，"她说，拒绝服从他收回声明的要求。他要解雇她并抹掉录音，但她把一张模板压在紧急键盘上，输入了她修理他那把理发剃刀时从笔记本里发现的覆盖序列，锁住了驾驶台控制系统。

The engine's **soporific** hum could not quiet her. Rusk lunged, **clumsy** with drink, and she used the emergency **breach** gate to **repulse** him from the ballast corridor. Then she began to **dismantle** the intake pump; the work **rumple**d her gloves and scorched her wrist, and it would **truncate** the voyage, but it would **prevent** the larvae from ever reaching shore. Rusk accused her of sabotage, then **vacillate**d as the pressure reading began to **balloon** toward the red zone. To **limit** the flood she followed the emergency **blueprint**, a sequence of **bracket**ed orders written for exactly this failure, while he tried to **expostulate** and then to argue.

发动机催眠般的低鸣无法让她安静下来。拉斯克扑上来，醉得动作笨拙，她借紧急破口闸门把他从压载走廊击退，随即动手拆卸进水泵。这项工作弄皱了她的手套，灼伤了她的手腕，也会缩短航程，却能阻止幼体抵达海岸。拉斯克指控她破坏船只，随后在压力读数向红区不断膨胀时犹豫起来。为了限制进水，她遵循紧急蓝图里一串用括号标明的指令，那份蓝图正是为这种故障而写，而他一再劝阻，接着又表示异议。

He could have helped; instead he watched her open the sea valve and flood the machinery room, offering no **demur** as the water rose around his boots. By evening the ship lay quiet beside the outer **levee**, its passengers transferred to Sato's launch, and neither Mara nor Rusk could **ignore** the cost. The company would **accuse** her of causing a **catastrophe**, and its **mendacious** **advert** would call the voyage a mere mechanical inconvenience. Sato, a **veracious** woman, would send the recordings but would not **endorse** Mara until the cultures returned. Rusk made a small **jibe** about her heroic **trophy**, the burned valve in a jar, then fell silent. Mara did not **impugn** his fear or offer him a **homily**. She simply watched the **parallel** lights of the island come on one by one.

他本可以帮忙，却只是看着她打开海阀，让机器舱进水，当水漫上靴子时，他一句异议也没有。傍晚时分，船静静泊在外侧堤坝旁，乘客已转移到佐藤的小艇上，玛拉和拉斯克都无法忽视代价。公司会指控她造成一场灾难，而它那则虚假的广告会把这次航程称作一次单纯的机械故障。佐藤是个诚实的女人，她会寄送录音，但在培养结果出来之前不会替玛拉背书。拉斯克拿她装在罐子里的烧坏阀门开了个小小的玩笑，称它为她的英雄奖杯，随后沉默。玛拉没有质疑他的恐惧，也没有给他讲什么道理。她只是看着岛上平行的灯火一盏盏亮起。

Looking out at those lights, she could **ruminate** without panic; the sea had taken the valve, the larvae, and her easy future, and in return it left a hard **tranquility**. At dawn Sato brought tea, and Mara drank slowly, **abstemious** even with comfort, while a **flock** of terns crossed the water and **linger**ed nowhere. Sato would **delve** into the cultures with **conscientious** care, refusing to **plagiarize** Mara's method or **parrot** Rusk's **tendentious** account. Her report might **allude** to his **domination** of the bridge, to his **scurrilous** insults, and to the **wretchedness** of choosing one vessel over a whole coast, but it would not **satirize** him.

望着那些灯火，她终于能不带惊慌地沉思：大海带走了阀门、幼体和她原本轻松的前程，作为交换，留下了一份坚硬的宁静。黎明时佐藤送来茶，玛拉慢慢喝着，连安慰也保持节制；一群燕鸥飞过水面，不在任何地方停留。佐藤会以尽责的细心钻研培养物，拒绝剽窃玛拉的方法，也拒绝鹦鹉学舌般复述拉斯克偏颇的说法。她的报告也许会暗示他对驾驶台的支配、他粗鄙的侮辱，以及在一艘船和整片海岸之间作出抉择的苦难，但不会讽刺他。

Mara could not **outdo** the sea or **eliminate** all risk, but she could **abjure** the easy lie, **dampen** panic with evidence, and accept a **truce** with her own fear. The **footloose** terns carried more **vim** than anyone aboard; their old **epithet**, "white needles," came to her as a **pun**, not a **hymn**. She wondered whether the company's **divestiture** of the research program had rested on a **conjecture** that science could be automated, whether **meteorology** would vindicate Rusk's delay, and whether the **chaff** of his excuses concealed a tidy **syllogism** about blame.

玛拉无法胜过大海，也无法消除一切风险，但她可以公开放弃轻易的谎言，用证据缓和恐慌，并和自己的恐惧达成休战。那些自由来去的燕鸥比船上任何一人都更有活力；它们那句老绰号"白针"在她听来像一个双关语，而不是一首圣歌。她想，公司剥离研究项目，是否建立在"科学可以被自动化"的猜想之上；气象学是否会证明拉斯克的延误情有可原；而他那些借口的谷壳里，又是否藏着一个关于责任归属的整齐三段论。

Yet no **cavil**, no **impertinent** rebuke, no **bumptious** claim could restore the trip. In the **tempestuous** night Rusk had tried to **proselytize** her into silence; now, **pertinacious** as a barnacle, she would keep the sample. It was a trace of proof, but it gave her work **parity** with his rank. The board might **slight** her, call the salvage **stodgy**, or call it an unfair breach. Still, the **mural** of salt on the lab wall seemed a **veritable** **resurgence**, not hope exactly, but a record of what she had refused to let through. Its least **adherent** auditor, some mischievous **imp** with a political **slant**, might dispute that record. Mara would let the data answer him.

然而，任何吹毛求疵、任何无礼的责难、任何傲慢的宣称都无法挽回这次航程。在那场狂暴的夜里，拉斯克曾试图劝她皈依沉默；如今她会像藤壶一样执拗，保住那份样本。那只是一点证据，却让她的工作与他的职衔平起平坐。董事会或许会怠慢她，称这场抢救陈旧，或称之为一次不公的违规。即便如此，实验舱墙上的盐画仍像一场名副其实的再现，不算希望，却是一份她拒绝放行之物的记录。它最不忠实的听众，某个带政治偏见、顽皮如小鬼的审计员，也许会同这份记录争执。玛拉会让数据回答他。
