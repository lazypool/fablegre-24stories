## Story for Chunk 016 - Glass Brood

On the first noon of her field **sabbatical**, Nera Vale worked alone in an insectary above Drybone **creek**, hired to **conserve** a desert moth whose larvae could **propagate** only in the roots of blue **flax**. She was a **scrupulous**, **diligent** entomologist, able to **discriminate** one wing vein from another by touch, but **vacillation** overcame her whenever care demanded harm. The moths made an **asymmetrical** **display**, their **pigment** silver and rust, their antennae **spiny**, and her **compendium** lay open beside them.

休假第一天的正午，内拉·维尔独自在枯骨溪上方的昆虫饲养室工作。她受雇保护一种沙漠蛾，这种蛾的幼虫只能在蓝亚麻根部繁殖。她是一位一丝不苟、勤勉的昆虫学家，能凭触觉分辨每一条翅脉，可每当保护需要伤害时，犹豫不决便会压倒她。蛾子构成不对称的陈列，色素呈银锈两色，触角多刺，她的概要就摊开在它们旁边。

Her **immaculate** tools sat unused because Seven was **robust**, **pliant**, and covered in a **myriad** of pores that exhaled **viscid** mist, which made the light **diffuse** and almost **insensible**. Its **racy** scent altered the usual **bouquet** of the soil. "This is no **piddling** anomaly," Nera murmured, and her measurements verified fertile eggs; a **resonant** click came from the cage, like a **husky** throat beginning to **rouse**. She barely noticed Milo arriving in his **tawdry** scarf.

她一尘不染的工具闲置着，因为七号标本强健而柔韧，身上有无数微孔，正呼出黏稠的雾气，雾气让光线散开，几乎难以察觉。它浓郁的气味改变了泥土原有的香气。"这不是微不足道的异常，"内拉低语道。她的测量证实了有生育力的卵；笼中传出洪亮的咔嗒声，像一副沙哑的喉咙开始苏醒。她几乎没注意到米洛戴着那条俗丽的围巾回来了。

Milo Quist came from the weather mast in that scarf, an **affable**, **consummate** technician who was **ham-handed** with forceps but proud of his **foolproof** irrigation **gadget** and its tidy **construct**. "Your miracle has a **fault**, Nera," he said in a slow **drawl**, adding a **facetious** salute. A **tornado** had swept through, leaving **debris**, a **spate** of dust, and one **permeable** cracked panel; the wind could carry eggs past the mesh toward the pasture, where a herder's **cow** would eat the flax. The mesh's broken **tether** no longer held it to the frame.

米洛·奎斯特从气象塔回来，围着那条围巾。他是位和蔼可亲、技艺高超的技术员，拿镊子却笨手笨脚，却为那套万无一失的灌溉小装置及其整洁的构造感到自豪。"你的奇迹有个故障，内拉，"他拖着慢吞吞的腔调说，随即开个玩笑般敬礼。一场龙卷风扫过，留下残骸、大量尘土和一块可渗透的裂面板；风会把卵吹向牧场，牧民的母牛会吃掉亚麻。系着网罩的系绳已断，不再把它固定在框架上。

Nera's **languor** vanished. Though the wind would **wane** by dusk, a **transient** gust could still do the damage, and Milo urged her to **defer** action until morning. She saw a **substantial** breach: the brood's **puissance** was not **absolute**, but it could **impede** every plan. "Do we **cosset** it because it is rare?" he asked. By night the larvae made a **welter** of roots, pausing to **masticate** the flax with **voracious** patience; one of them gave Milo a **lash** across the hand, and Nera bound a **tourniquet** above the cut.

内拉的倦怠一扫而空。风到黄昏会减弱，但一阵短暂的阵风仍足以造成破坏，米洛劝她推迟到明早再说。她看到一次实质性的泄漏：这窝生物的力量并非绝对，却能阻碍所有计划。"我们是因为它稀有就溺爱它吗？"他问。夜里，幼虫把根系搅成一片混乱，贪婪而耐心地咀嚼亚麻；其中一只在米洛手上抽出一道伤痕，内拉在伤口上方绑了止血带。

She had him **splice** the mesh while she began to **excavate** trays, and his **feeble** apology made her **testy** — though she did not **excoriate** him. Their **mobility** allowed a sudden **dart** across the floor, and their skins began to **mottle** the tiles; the manual's **lacunae** offered no answer. Milo, a **gourmand** even over canned beans, proposed fire, but Nera would not **violate** the **obligatory** rule whose **foundation** was the desert's **purity**, a belief she had never once tested.

她让他接合网罩，自己开始挖出托盘，他无力的道歉令她烦躁，尽管她没有严厉责骂他。它们惊人的移动性使其能突然猛蹿，皮肤把地板染得斑驳；手册中的空缺没有给出答案。米洛即使面对罐装豆子也是个美食家，他提议放火，内拉却不肯违反那条强制规则，它的根基是沙漠的纯净，而这种信念她从未检验过。

An **addendum** to the manual described a chamber built to **subjugate** breeding stock; they could **fabricate** a hose from **limber** tubing and **deploy** nitrogen, though they could not trust its controls. At dawn a **stentorian** alarm brought no **retinue**, only the **abiding** brood, and Milo remembered how his **kinfolks** had farmed here. A **devout** neighbor would call the moth **impiety** made flesh; his **diehard** grandmother would call its death a **sin**. The larvae had reached the chamber's **hub**.

手册的补遗说明有一间专门用来制服繁殖种群的舱室；他们可以用柔软的管材造一根软管，部署氮气，却不能信任它的控制装置。黎明时，洪亮的警报没有带来任何随从，只有持续不息的幼虫群；米洛想起自己的亲属曾在这里务农。一位虔诚的邻居会说这蛾是人间不敬神明的化身，他顽固守旧的祖母会说杀死它是罪。幼虫已经逼近舱室的核心。

Nera could not **adopt** either answer. Release vapor and risk the strain, or let the uncontained swarm's dazzling display **ravish** the valley cattle until they wandered into poison. Milo's **persiflage** ceased. "Your **qualification** is knowing them," he said. "Mine is knowing this station." A **discretionary** bypass could **catalyze** a controlled cooling reaction and offer Seven **succor**, and Nera rejected the **illusion** of a merciful choice; she told him to **broach** the vent, and together they began the **travail**. She chose containment over **liberation**, made a **contrite** admission that she had delayed too long, and noted the repairs would exceed her **honorarium** — yet they could **reap** several days for quarantine.

内拉无法接受任一种答案。释放蒸汽就可能危及这一支系，不放则会让失控虫群炫目的展示迷住山谷里的牛群，直到它们走进毒亚麻。米洛的插科打诨停止了。"你的资格是了解它们，"他说，"我的资格是了解这个站点。"一条可自行启用的旁路能催化受控的冷却反应，或许能给七号以救援；内拉拒绝把任何选择称作仁慈的幻觉。她让他钻开通风口，两人开始辛劳。她选择隔离而非解放，悔悟地承认自己拖延太久，还注意到维修费会超过她的酬金，但他们仍能争取几天隔离时间。

The extra days would not **alienate** herders whose **wealth** was forage; she would **cede** no specimen and **divulge** no location until quarantine could **qualify** it. Her **abstract** would **verify** that the damage did not **incriminate** Milo, and she would not **profess** any **logicism** or write a **lachrymose** **elegy**. At the county hearing, opponents had used a **filibuster** to delay the vote on emergency containment, concealing the **barbarity** of easy fire.

多出来的几天不会疏远那些以牧草为财富的牧民；在隔离团队确认合格之前，她不会割让标本，也不会泄露地点。她的摘要会证实损坏不能证明米洛有罪，她不会宣称什么逻辑主义，也不会写一首伤感的挽歌。在县里的听证会上，对手曾用冗长演说阻挠紧急隔离的表决，掩盖轻易放火的残酷。

A **stalwart** Milo used an axe to **hew** a **twig**-sized wedge from a crate and brace the bypass; the valve's **strength** failed, but the wedge held the line. They would not **enfranchise** Seven by releasing it into certain ruin, nor **feign** mercy in a **masquerade**. A **rabble** gathered at the gate, but Nera's **imperturbable** stare sent them away, and she felt no **envy** for their certainty. She would **pore** over data until her **contention** earned a defensible review **score**, then let the station **rejoice**.

坚强的米洛用斧头从木箱上砍下一块细枝大小的楔块，撑住旁路；阀门的强度失效了，楔块却守住了管线。他们不会以授予自由的名义把七号放归必毁之境，也不会在伪装里假装仁慈。一群人聚到门口，内拉镇定自若的一瞥把他们送走；她不嫉妒他们的确信。她会仔细研读数据，直到论点在评审中获得站得住脚的评分，再让站点欢庆。

New filters would **distill** the mist and keep it from **encumber**ing the vents. A **mischievous** larva gave one final **leer** from its tray; Milo gave the praise a **snub**, then asked with **wit** whether a moth was an **underdog**. "A **puerile** question," Nera said, though no answer could **tantalize** her now. Their **camaraderie** was hard won. Outside, the **preeminent** female **kangaroo** rat — the dominant female of the local pair — passed beneath the flax, and Nera watched its shadow **forge** a line across the sand. She felt no need to **sanctify** what they had saved, only to **honor** the boundary she had nearly failed to keep.

新的过滤器会蒸馏雾气，让它不再堵塞通风口。一只调皮的幼虫从托盘上投来最后一眼斜睨；米洛冷落了夸奖，接着机智地问一只蛾算不算弱者。"幼稚的问题，"内拉说，尽管此刻已没有答案能再吊她的胃口。他们的情谊得来不易。外面，当地繁殖配对中公认占主导地位的雌性袋鼠鼠从亚麻下经过，内拉看着它挖洞时影子在沙上划出一条线。她不需要把他们救下的一切神圣化，只需要尊重那条自己险些没能守住的界限。
