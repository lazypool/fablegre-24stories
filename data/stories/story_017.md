## Story for Chunk 017 - Alpine Rescue

At dusk, rescue leader Rhea Venn heard Professor Halden **insist** that the storm was a mere inconvenience. The expedition had crossed the glacier to test a solar drill, a **novelty** even in the high Alps. Their cook had packed every **nutrient** the body needed, but no food could outlast a buried tent. Halden, a minor **viscount** with a scholar's vanity, had financed the attempt. His nephew Ivo liked to **satirize** the mountain rules at supper. The funding contract carried a hidden **codicil** about exclusive mineral rights. Rhea warned that greed could **undervalue** a human life. A steel **pendent** hung from Ivo's neck, stamped with the drill company's mark.

黄昏时，救援队长瑞娅·文恩听见哈尔登教授坚持说，暴风雪不过是点小麻烦。 这支探险队穿越冰川测试太阳能钻机，即使在高阿尔卑斯山中那也是新奇事物。 厨师带足了人体所需的各类营养物质，但再多食物也撑不过被雪埋住的帐篷。 哈尔登是一位带着学者虚荣心的小子爵，资助了这次行动。 他的侄子伊沃喜欢在晚饭时讽刺山上的规矩。 资助合同里藏着一项关于矿产专有权的附加条款。 瑞娅警告说，贪婪会低估人的生命价值。 伊沃脖子上挂着一枚印有钻探公司标记的钢制吊坠。

He drank enough brandy to **inebriate** himself before watch began. The brass instruments looked **anachronistic** beside their satellite beacon. Rhea had ordered a winter **revamp** of the old refuge radio. She trusted its signal with hard-earned **confidence**. At first light she would **dispense** the final weather warning. Someone had left a tube of **dentifrice** beside the sabotaged battery. It was a small child's **toy** compass that revealed the torn pack. Rhea refused to **minimize** the clue.

值守开始前，他喝了足以让自己醉倒的白兰地。 那些黄铜仪器放在卫星信标旁显得不合时代。 瑞娅安排在冬季彻底翻新旧避难所的无线电。 她对它的信号怀有来之不易的信心。 天一亮，她就会发布最后的天气警报。 有人在遭破坏的电池旁留下了一管牙膏。 是一只小孩的玩具指南针揭露了被撕破的背包。 瑞娅拒绝轻视这条线索。

The cable's **radix** lay exposed where a knife had cut its insulation. Even panic required **decorum**, she told the gathered climbers. Snow began to **suffuse** the windows with a blue, blind light. No one mistook Halden's **asceticism** for courage anymore. Ivo took **miff** at Rhea's questions and turned away. Halden answered with a **theological** argument about providence. Outside, the wind was **pitiless**. Rhea's **absorption** in the broken transmitter kept her from sleeping.

电缆的根部在刀割开绝缘层的地方暴露了出来。 她告诉聚集的登山者，即使恐慌也应当保持体面。 积雪开始用蓝色而刺眼的光笼罩窗户。 再也没有人把哈尔登的苦行作风误当成勇气。 伊沃因瑞娅的提问而恼火，转过身去。 哈尔登以一番关于天意的神学论证作答。 外面的风毫不留情。 瑞娅全神贯注于坏掉的发报机，因而无法入睡。

The sliced wire was **evidential** proof of intent. Below the ridge, an avalanche made a **propulsive** roar. The radio's old valve was too **finicky** to restart in damp air. The cut matched a **similar** nick in the emergency flare box. An injured geologist began to **doze** from cold. Then a red beacon blinked through the **emergence** of cloud. Rhea found a **promissory** note promising Ivo a share of the claim. Its implication lay beyond the **ken** of the exhausted students.

被切断的电线是意图明确的证据。 山脊下方，雪崩发出强劲推进般的轰鸣。 无线电的旧阀门太过娇气，无法在潮湿空气中重启。 这道切痕与紧急信号弹盒上相似的缺口相吻合。 一名受伤的地质学家因寒冷开始打瞌睡。 随后，一盏红色信标在云层散开的空隙中闪烁。 瑞娅找到一张承诺给伊沃一份矿权收益的本票。 其中的含义超出了疲惫学生们的理解范围。

The visible damage was only **superficial** on the battery case. The slope beyond camp was **underexposed** beneath a moonless sky. Halden grew **obstinate** when Rhea ordered him indoors. Wind seemed to **sibilate** through the seams of the refuge. Her radio operator, Sera, was a **puissant** climber with steady hands. She flexed her injured **extensor** tendon and took the rope. Rhea followed the buried marker **unerringly**. The avalanche report was **graphic** enough to silence Ivo.

电池外壳上可见的损伤只是表面的。 在无月的天空下，营地外的坡面昏暗得看不清。 瑞娅命令哈尔登进屋时，他变得固执起来。 风仿佛从避难所的缝隙中发出嘶嘶声。 她的报务员塞拉是一位强健、双手沉稳的攀登者。 她屈伸受伤的伸肌腱，接过了绳索。 瑞娅毫无偏差地循着被埋住的标记前进。 雪崩报告的细节足以让伊沃闭嘴。

Summer grasses would **fertilize** this scarred slope one day. For now, a whiteout made every decision a **muddle**. Rhea had no patience for an **intolerant** man blaming the weakest member. Ivo's souvenir mask was **anthropomorphous**, carved in a snarling shape. It looked briefly **maleficent** in the lantern light. His plan had begun as a lodge **bacchanalia** boast, Sera guessed. It would **behoove** him to tell the truth before another slide came. The promise of ore had given him a **tawdry** ambition.

总有一天，夏草会让这片伤痕累累的坡地恢复肥力。 眼下，白茫茫的风雪让每个决定都乱成一团。 瑞娅无法容忍一个偏狭的人责怪最弱的队员。 伊沃的纪念面具具有人形特征，刻成龇牙咆哮的模样。 它在灯光下短暂地显得邪恶。 塞拉猜想，他的计划起初只是山舍狂欢中的吹嘘。 在下一场滑坡到来前，他最好说出真相。 矿石的许诺让他生出庸俗的野心。

His footprints led toward a **peripheral** equipment shed. There Rhea found maps marked for corporate **espionage**. Ivo had planned his uncle's **defection** from a rival firm. The storm made retreat a **retrograde** crawl through snow. An old **tenant** of the refuge had once mapped a safer gully. Rhea called that knowledge **kismet** rather than luck. Inside the shed lay Halden's missing **medallion**. A **wisp** of blue wire clung to its clasp.

他的脚印通向一间边缘地带的器材棚。 瑞娅在那里发现了标有企业间谍活动内容的地图。 伊沃策划让他的叔叔从竞争公司倒戈。 暴风雪使撤退变成在雪中艰难倒退的爬行。 避难所的一位老租客曾绘制过一条更安全的沟谷路线。 瑞娅把这份知识称为命中注定，而非好运。 棚内放着哈尔登失踪的奖章。 一缕蓝色细线缠在它的扣环上。

Rhea rejected Ivo's cold **utilitarianism**. No profit could excuse this **misfortune**. The traitor suddenly looked **insecure**, not formidable. His joke carried a **barb** meant for Halden. He tried to **deceive** them by claiming the wire was old. The luxurious scent of his cologne seemed absurdly **sensual** there. Sera's wrist had a deep **lesion** from the cable. Rhea checked every piece of their **equipage**.

瑞娅拒绝伊沃冷酷的功利主义。 任何利润都不能为这场不幸开脱。 这个叛徒突然显得不安，而不再可怕。 他的玩笑带着一根针对哈尔登的刺。 他声称那根电线很旧，试图欺骗他们。 他古龙水奢华而感官化的气味在此处显得荒谬。 塞拉的手腕被电缆割出很深的伤口。 瑞娅检查了他们的每一件装备。

Ivo's nervous **volubility** filled the shed. Rhea did not **accuse** him until she found the knife. The canvas roof was barely **penetrable** beneath the drift. Another crack made them **accelerate** their return. A **piebald** mountain dog appeared beside the trail. Sera used a flare to **douse** the animal's fear. Halden called Ivo's act a **demerit** against the whole team. Rhea's **provident** cache of dry gloves saved the geologist's fingers.

伊沃紧张而滔滔不绝的话充满了棚子。 直到找到那把刀，瑞娅才指控他。 帆布屋顶在积雪下几乎无法穿透。 又一声裂响让他们加快返程。 一只花斑山犬出现在小径旁。 塞拉用信号弹消除那只动物的恐惧。 哈尔登称伊沃的行为是对整个团队的过失。 瑞娅有先见之明地储备的干手套保住了地质学家的手指。

She reserved her **censure** until everyone was safe. The guide used resin to **calk** a split boot sole. The climb back was **taxing** beyond speech. Each ice axe strike seemed to **resonate** under the crust. An artillery-like **salvo** of thunder rolled over the pass. Ivo, once a lodge **sybarite**, shivered without complaint. Rhea chose to **embed** a locator in Sera's pack. The youngest student, an **aspirant** alpinist, carried the spare oxygen.

她把谴责留到所有人安全之后。 向导用树脂填补裂开的靴底。 返程攀登艰难得无法言说。 每一次冰镐敲击似乎都在冰壳下回响。 一阵炮火般的雷鸣滚过山口。 曾是山舍享乐主义者的伊沃不发怨言地发抖。 瑞娅决定把定位器嵌入塞拉的背包。 最年轻的学生是一名有志登山者，背着备用氧气。

Far below, spring **tilth** waited under frozen fields. Rhea tightened a **tourniquet** above Sera's bleeding wrist. The ridge became a **spectral** wall in the snow. At the **inception** of the rescue, she had promised nobody would be abandoned. The drill had been designed by a private **collector** of patents. Halden began to **expatiate** on the geology, then stopped at Rhea's stare. The valley **coroner** would have no work tonight, she decided. They had to **modify** their route before the cornice failed.

远在下方，春季可耕的土壤正等待着从冻田下苏醒。 瑞娅在塞拉流血的手腕上方扎紧止血带。 山脊在雪中变成一堵幽灵般的墙。 救援开始时，她曾承诺不会抛下任何人。 这台钻机由一名私人专利收藏家设计。 哈尔登开始详谈地质情况，却在瑞娅的瞪视下停住了。 她决定，山谷验尸官今晚不会有活干。 他们必须在雪檐坍塌前修改路线。

Her laminated **prescript** said to descend only by fixed anchors. In the food bag she found a frozen **gourd** soup flask. Pain seized Sera in a **paroxysm** when she moved her hand. Ivo muttered like a **bigot** about who deserved the rope. That cruelty ended Rhea's **quandary**. She spoke with a **pleasant** calm that frightened him more than anger. The first sun would **gild** the far summit, if they lasted. Sera still had the **flair** to tie knots one-handed.

她的覆膜指令写着只能沿固定锚点下降。 她在食物袋里发现一个冻住的葫芦汤壶。 塞拉一动手，剧痛便突然发作。 伊沃像个偏执者般嘟囔着谁配得上用绳索。 这份残酷终结了瑞娅的两难。 她以一种和悦的平静说话，比愤怒更令他害怕。 若他们能撑到那时，初升的太阳会为远处的山顶镀金。 塞拉依然有单手打结的天赋。

Rhea watched the wound for a **septic** stain. Ivo finally admitted his **connivance** with the drill agent. Halden answered with a short **ode** to foolish sons. The agent had offered Ivo a **miniature** transmitter. Greed had helped **motivate** the betrayal. The hidden ore was **albite**, pale as the storm itself. Ivo's excuse was **ineffectual**. The students' first relief became wild **ebullience** when the beacon answered.

瑞娅留意伤口是否出现感染的污痕。 伊沃终于承认自己与钻探代理人共谋。 哈尔登以一首写给愚蠢儿子的短颂回应。 代理人曾给伊沃一台微型发报机。 贪婪促成了这场背叛。 隐藏的矿石是钠长石，苍白得如同暴风雪本身。 伊沃的借口毫无说服力。 信标回应时，学生们最初的如释重负变成了狂喜。

Sera protected her **osseous** wrist with a splint. Halden's speech turned **pharisaical** until Rhea cut him off. The corporate seal was an **ersatz** copy on cheap paper. At its center was a **demoniac** red emblem. The refuge's **timeworn** bell rang as the wind shifted. What had been a survey **excursion** became a night of reckoning. The storm imposed its own **limitation** on pride. They lowered the geologist onto a canvas **pallet**.

塞拉用夹板保护她的骨性手腕。 哈尔登的讲话变得伪善，直到瑞娅打断他。 公司印章是印在劣质纸上的仿制品。 中心有一个如恶魔般的红色徽记。 风向变化时，避难所年久的钟响了。 原本的勘测远足成了清算之夜。 暴风雪以自己的方式限制了傲慢。 他们把地质学家放到帆布担架上。

Halden's research **bibliography** had concealed the agent's name. Lightning made the valley **phantasmagorical**. The youngest student felt **naive** for trusting Ivo. Rhea dismissed Halden's **sententious** apology. She felt a private **pang** for the man he had been. Ivo had spread an **aspersion** against Sera to isolate her. Halden began to **inveigh** against his nephew. Rhea called family loyalty an **oxymoron** when it protected harm.

哈尔登的研究参考书目掩盖了代理人的名字。 闪电让山谷变得如幻似真。 最年轻的学生因信任伊沃而觉得自己天真。 瑞娅不理会哈尔登说教式的道歉。 想到他曾经的模样，她心里隐隐作痛。 伊沃散布对塞拉的诽谤，想孤立她。 哈尔登开始猛烈谴责自己的侄子。 瑞娅说，保护伤害的家族忠诚是自相矛盾的说法。

Sera remembered her **maternal** aunt teaching her knots. The refuge floor was cracked **linoleum** over stone. Ivo's **condescending** smile vanished at the sight of handcuffs. The radio emitted a **shrill** burst from the rescue base. Halden's **obsequious** apology did not soften Rhea. Ivo had treated the hired porter as a **minion**. The coming **centenary** of the refuge would remember this night differently. Rhea had once been an **itinerant** medic before leading rescues.

塞拉想起母系的姨妈教她打结。 避难所石地上铺着开裂的油毡。 一看见手铐，伊沃居高临下的笑容就消失了。 无线电传来救援基地尖厉的一阵呼叫。 哈尔登谄媚的道歉没有软化瑞娅的态度。 伊沃把受雇的搬运工当作奴才。 避难所即将到来的百年纪念会以不同方式记住这一夜。 在领导救援前，瑞娅曾是一名流动医护员。

For her, cowardice was the **antonym** of leadership. She tried to **pacify** the student who feared another slide. The **principal** danger was now the fractured ridge. The forecast had been **imprecise**, but the sabotage had not. Their remaining **fare** was hard bread and tea. A sprig of **herbaceous** thyme scented the pack. Halden's title made him seem **noble** only at banquets. Rhea would **nominate** Sera for the service medal.

对她而言，怯懦是领导力的反义词。 她试图安抚害怕再次滑坡的学生。 现在最主要的危险是破裂的山脊。 预报并不精确，但破坏行为却明明白白。 他们剩下的食物只有硬面包和茶。 一枝草本百里香为背包带来香气。 哈尔登的头衔只会在宴会上让他显得高贵。 瑞娅将提名塞拉获得服务奖章。

The dry air held a brief **freshness** after the gale. Ivo looked **depressed** as the consequences settled on him. Rhea prayed the anchors would **forfend** a fall. Every **mountaineer** knows that a mountain collects unpaid errors. With deliberate **apophasis**, Rhea declined to mention Ivo's weeping. The stove **smelt** of scorched fuel. One bright **facet** of ice reflected the beacon. Ivo continued to **annoy** the group with objections.

大风过后，干燥的空气带着短暂的清新。 后果落到身上时，伊沃看起来很沮丧。 瑞娅祈祷锚点能防止坠落。 每个登山者都知道，山会收取尚未付清的错误代价。 瑞娅有意避而不谈伊沃的哭泣。 炉子散发出焦糊燃料的气味。 冰的一块明亮晶面反射着信标。 伊沃继续以异议惹恼众人。

Rhea used her knife to **whittle** a plug for the leaking flask. Frostbite left the geologist **susceptible** to infection. An **ambulance** waited at the valley station, six hours away. Ivo proved a **chauvinist** by questioning Sera's command. Rhea called that claim a **travesty** of what they had survived. No legal **technicality** could erase the cut cable. The lower track lay **awash** in slush. Their route crossed **heteromorphic** layers of twisted ice.

瑞娅用刀削出一个塞子，堵住漏水的瓶子。 冻伤使地质学家容易感染。 六小时路程外的山谷车站有一辆救护车等着。 伊沃质疑塞拉的指挥，证明自己是个沙文主义者。 瑞娅称那种说法是对他们共同经历的歪曲。 没有法律技术细节能抹去被切断的电缆。 下方小径淹没在雪泥中。 他们的路线穿过形态各异的扭曲冰层。

The black flag would **connote** danger to the approaching helicopter. Sera saw a rope end **wiggle** under powder. Rhea began to **sculpt** steps down the drift. The dog had a **pachyderm** patience with their slow march. An **indentation** in snow showed where the missing porter had fallen. Rhea **fell** to her knees and listened. She made a rescue **table** from two skis and a tarp. The expedition could not **acclimate** to catastrophe in one night.

黑旗将向接近的直升机表示危险。 塞拉看见一截绳头在粉雪下蠕动。 瑞娅开始在雪堆中凿出向下的台阶。 那只狗对他们缓慢的行进有着大象般的耐心。 雪中的凹痕显示了失踪搬运工跌落的地方。 瑞娅跪倒在地，侧耳倾听。 她用两副滑雪板和一张篷布做成救援担架。 探险队无法在一夜之间适应灾难。

The agent's papers promised a **voluptuous** holiday reward. Below, a trapped voice cried out, **frantic** but alive. Rhea gave the students warm **oatmeal** before descent; **incidentally**, the porter had carried the real transmitter. His contract contained a **covenant** requiring the company to protect him. Halden tore an **epaulette** from his ceremonial coat to pad the splint. Rhea chose to **debar** Ivo from the rope team. The dog made a **mischievous** dash toward the voice. The porter was the **descendant** of the refuge's first keeper.

代理人的文件承诺一场奢华享乐的假期奖赏。 下方一个被困的声音喊叫，惊惶却还活着。 下撤前，瑞娅给学生们吃热燕麦粥；顺带一提，搬运工带着真正的发报机。 他的合同中有一项要求公司保护他的契约。 哈尔登从礼服上撕下肩章，为夹板作衬垫。 瑞娅决定禁止伊沃加入绳索队。 那只狗顽皮地冲向声音传来的方向。 搬运工是避难所第一任看守人的后代。

A **statutory** inquiry would follow, Rhea said. The rescue **criteria** were simple: reach him before dawn. They began to **trudge** along the wind-carved shelf. The **prospect** of an open crevasse slowed everyone. Sera's **callus** split under her glove. Rhea made an **audacious** traverse around a hanging serac. The porter later repeated her dry **epigram** about fear being useful. Ivo tried to **sequestrate** the agent's papers in his coat.

瑞娅说，随后将进行法定调查。 救援标准很简单：黎明前抵达他身边。 他们开始沿着被风雕出的横坡艰难跋涉。 想到前方可能有张开的冰裂缝，所有人都慢了下来。 塞拉手套下的老茧裂开了。 瑞娅大胆地横越悬冰川周围。 后来，搬运工重复了她那句关于恐惧有用的冷峻警句。 伊沃试图把代理人的文件藏在自己的外套里。

A frozen **tadpole** shape in the ice marked a meltwater channel. Rhea had no **clairvoyance**, only maps and judgment. They found the porter below a narrow **fissure**. His **menial** job had made him the first target of the scheme. Rescue work was Rhea's **avocation** before it became her profession. Sera proved fully **competent** despite her wound.
