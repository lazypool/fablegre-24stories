## Story for Chunk 018 - Harbor Masquerade

At dusk, Inez Maren would **avouch** that no guest at the Harbor Masquerade had arrived without a reason to **flinch** at the cannon salute. The ballroom glittered with salt-bright **zest**, and her old tutor's **perspicacity** had taught her that **hedonism** often hid behind a **foppish** mask at a **festal** civic night. Her modest **curriculum** in harbor law had begun among **sand** maps and **verisimilar** model ships. Tonight the council met in **plenary** splendor, while a cold **nip** came through the windows and made the ticket price seem **exorbitant**. The mayor offered a **salutatory** toast to local **fauna**, asking everyone to **accompany** him in a ritual that treated the sea's old silver charm as a **fetish** and the port as a **paradise**.

黄昏时，伊内兹·马伦敢担保，港湾假面舞会的每位宾客听到礼炮时都有理由一颤。舞厅闪耀着海盐般明亮的活力；她老导师的洞察力曾教会她，享乐主义常躲在节庆市民之夜花哨的面具后。她在港口法方面不多的课程，始于沙盘地图和逼真的模型船。今晚，议会在全体会议的华彩中召开，一丝冷气穿窗而入，使票价显得高得离谱。市长为当地动物群致开场祝酒词，请大家陪同他参加一项仪式，将大海的老银护符当作崇物，把港口视为乐园。

Inez found the speech **pompous**, but its **forensic** detail gave her a spark of **galvanism**. Someone had tried to **transfuse** confidence into the crowd with rehearsed **retentive** phrases and diplomatic **fluency**. The mayor's lady, seemingly **regnant** over the room, began to **fidget** when Harbor **intelligence** announced that the vanished cargo ledger had belonged to their **undertaking**. Outside, rain seemed to **beleaguer** the pier. A figure in a blue **cloak** used a servant's door as a **dodge**, carrying a **hypodermic** case; his gait was **spasmodic**, though the midsummer **solstice** lamps made every gesture appear more **effective** than it was.

伊内兹觉得演讲浮夸，但其中法证般的细节给了她一丝振奋。有人曾试图以背熟、易记的措辞和外交式流利言辞，把信心灌输给人群。市长夫人看似主宰全场，却在港口情报部门宣布消失的货物账本属于他们的事业时开始坐立不安。外面，雨仿佛围困着码头。一名披蓝斗篷的人拿仆人门作掩护，带着一个皮下注射器盒；他的步态痉挛，尽管仲夏至日的灯光让每个动作看上去比实际更有效果。

The masked **intelligentsia** watched a flare **shoot** from the customs tower, and **consternation** crossed the dance floor. Inez did not **condole** with the mayor when he claimed the loss would **excruciate** the town; he had tried to **disown** the ledger before. She meant to **wrest** its truth from him. In the old sea **grotto** beneath the hall, a dockhand gave her a whispered **salutation** and said the rain might **dissipate** footprints, but not the evidence. The **orthodox** procedures of the **municipality** would be too slow. A **boorish**, **indulgent** customs broker had ordered a **lavish** supper, and the **chef** had noticed a strange **genitive** mark on the missing book's seal.

戴面具的知识界人士看见一枚照明弹从海关塔射出，舞池顿时掠过惊惶。市长声称损失会使全镇痛苦不堪时，伊内兹并未表示慰问；他此前曾试图否认账本。她要从他手中夺回真相。在大厅下方的旧海蚀洞里，一名码头工人低声向她致意，说雨或许会冲散脚印，却冲不掉证据。市政当局的正统程序会太慢。一名粗鲁而放纵的海关经纪人点了奢华晚餐，厨师注意到失书封印上有奇怪的属格标记。

That clue was **tantalizing** enough that Inez would not **dawdle**. A chandeliers' chain began to **wobble** above a **bland**, **indigestible** buffet, where a **prudish** archivist guarded the town's **septennial** accounts. He described the thief as soft with **adipose** ease, determined to **annihilate** the record in the harbor **morass**. Yet the ledger carried the **redolence** of tar, and a **raffish** sailor had seen a crease or **wrinkle** in its cover. Inez could **resuscitate** the inquiry by finding that man, a **gentile** violinist who lived near the warehouse **labyrinth** in a weathered **hut**.

那条线索足够诱人，伊内兹不会磨蹭。枝形吊灯的链条开始在一席平淡、难消化的自助餐上方摇晃；一名拘谨的档案员守着小镇的七年账目。他形容窃贼肥胖而安逸，决心在港口泥沼中毁掉记录。然而账本带着焦油气味，一名放荡不羁的水手曾看到其封面有一道折痕。只要找到那人，住在仓库迷宫旁风化小屋里的异教徒小提琴家，伊内兹就能重振调查。

His costume was **nondescript**, yet he knew how to **operate** the locks below the quay. He said a disgraced priest had been forced to **defrock**, after a scheme built on false **reciprocity** and a threatened **expulsion**. His testimony was **desultory**, but he named the original **grantor** of the warehouse, whose family device used **alliteration**. Inez began to **rue** her earlier **objurgation** of the clerk; he had tried to **ingraft** a **reciprocal** cipher into the invoices. The cipher lay **dormant** beneath a **smug** little **illusion** of water stains, on paper so **unprepossessing** that no one had saved it for **posterity**.

他的装束毫不起眼，却知道怎样操作码头下的锁。他说，一名失势的神父在一个建立于虚假互惠和威胁驱逐之上的阴谋后，被迫脱去法衣。他的证词零散，但他说出了仓库最初授予人的名字，其家族纹章采用头韵。伊内兹开始后悔先前对文书的斥责；文书曾试图把一种互易密码嫁接进发票。密码潜伏在小片自鸣得意的水渍假象下，纸张毫不吸引人，没人为后代保存它。

The document named an **assignee** who planned to **modernize** the docks under a grand **motto**, financed by a moonlit **barcarole** of forged payments. Men had begun to **harass** Inez's **pharmacopoeia**-keeping aunt for records, a **reprehensible** act against a **unitarian** healer. Inez used a kitchen **squash** to conceal a **foreign** seal and saw that the receipt could **postdate** the gala. In the **effulgent** hall, its stamped coastline described the **physiography** of a remote **sop** marsh beyond the **foreshore**. A black **swathe** at its edge showed where a limping courier had tried to **hobble** a boat's **protean** route.

文件点名一名受让人，计划以宏大口号现代化码头，资金来自一首月下船歌般的伪造付款。有人开始骚扰伊内兹那位保存药典的姑妈，索取记录；这对一位一神教医者是可谴责之举。伊内兹用厨房里的南瓜藏起一个外国印章，并发现收据日期可晚于舞会。在辉煌大厅中，它印出的海岸线描绘了前滩外一处遥远湿地的自然地貌。边缘一条黑带显示，跛脚信使曾试图妨碍船只多变的航线。

The route looked **equivocal**, though the next **repast** supplied insight. An actor in exaggerated **effeminacy** dropped a broken **twig** beside Inez's plate, as if hoping to **gratify** an unseen master. Its **ciliated** seaweed proved the clerk had made a deliberate **miscount**. That supplied grounds for **prosecution**, and the discovery would **aggravate** the mayor. A harbor **veteran** recalled the green **luminosity** of the thief's lantern and the slight **joggle** of a false hand. Inez would not let anger **embitter** her; the suspect had used an **undercharge** on freight to enrich a **prodigal** patron who liked to **patronize** poor sailors.

这条路线看起来模棱两可，不过下一餐给了她启发。一名刻意装扮阴柔的演员把断树枝丢在伊内兹盘边，仿佛想取悦一位看不见的主人。其上有纤毛的海藻证明文书故意误算。这构成了起诉依据，发现会激怒市长。一名港口老兵想起窃贼灯笼的绿光，以及假手微微的晃动。伊内兹不让愤怒使自己刻薄；嫌犯在货运上少收费，以资助一名挥霍而爱居高临下对待穷水手的靠山。

The mayor blamed a feverish **distemper** on a **photoelectric** signal box, a tale fit for a **yearling** not an alert **rider**. It was **implausible**, even **inane**, and his appointed **chaperon** begged against **divulgence**. Inez found an ivory **accessory** carved with a **steppe** hawk. It could **congest** the small **enclave** of suspects, because only the mayor held a trading **license** from the school of **darwinism** that trained his security men. Her formal **appeal** reached the palace **dermatologist**, whose **sparse** notes gave a **commendable** account of a scar on the masquerader's wrist.

市长把发热的疾病归咎于光电信号箱，这种说辞适合哄一岁牲畜，骗不了警觉的骑手。它既不可信又愚蠢，他指定的陪护人恳求不要泄露。伊内兹找到一件刻有草原鹰的象牙饰品。它会使那小群嫌犯更拥挤，因为只有市长持有训练保安人员的达尔文主义学校所发的贸易许可。她的正式申诉送达宫廷皮肤科医生；其简略笔记对化装者手腕伤疤作出了值得称道的说明。

The supposed actor was not merely **effeminate** in dress; he had planned to **elope** by launch with the ledger in a brass **pentahedron**. Inez asked the harbor **arbiter** to **vanquish** the delay. A visiting **bibliophile**, no **effete** collector, could **utter** that the ledger had been held in **abeyance** until the company could **dissolve** its debts. The mayor's **exorbitance** had left a telltale **groove** in every **communal** account, breeding public **bitterness**. It was **becoming** clear enough to **madden** him. He began to **beg** the guests to **filter** outside before an **eclipse** of the lamps left them stranded.

所谓的演员不只是装束阴柔；他计划带着装在黄铜五面体里的账本乘小艇私奔。伊内兹要求港口仲裁者克服拖延。一位来访的藏书家，并非孱弱的收藏者，能够说明账本一直被搁置，直到公司能消除债务。市长的奢靡在每笔公共账目中留下明显痕迹，滋生公众怨愤。真相越来越清楚，足以令他发狂。他开始恳求宾客在灯光熄灭、使他们受困前陆续出去。

Inez followed him through a wine **vat** into a painted **minaret** passage, where his **machiavellian** calm could no longer pretend. He told her to **rejoice**: no **unlawful** deed, he said, could touch a powerful **couple**. But the ledger named an **inimical** partner, a **veritable** pirate in white **linen**, and a **countryman** of Inez's. His **verbose** warning about a sea **bugaboo** was a **fancied** diversion. The true **decadent** plot was an **imbroglio** of bribes, led by a **witless** heir who had mistaken an ore **lode** for an invitation to rule.

伊内兹跟着他穿过酒缸，进入一条绘有尖塔的通道；在那里，他那马基雅维利式的镇静再也装不下去。他叫她高兴：他说，没有违法行为能触及一对强大的夫妇。但账本点名一个敌对合伙人，一名穿白色亚麻衣的真正海盗，还是伊内兹的同胞。他关于海上妖怪的冗长警告是臆想的障眼法。真正腐败的阴谋是一团贿赂乱局，由一名无知继承人主导，他把矿脉误当成统治的邀请。

From above came the orchestra's **overture**. Inez measured the thin **margin** between escape and arrest, then made an **incisive** choice: she would **lumber** the heavy door shut and reject any **extrajudicial** bargain. With **clarity**, she threw a **punch** that made him **reconsider** his chosen **profession**. The **rational** explanation for the stolen book was no longer an **assassination** threat or a civic **malady**. Her own **sentience** had recognized the **culprit** from the start: the mayor's masked secretary, sent to **probe** the cellar and **sub** out the smuggling work.

上方传来乐队的序曲。伊内兹衡量逃跑与被捕之间微薄的余地，随后作出果断选择：她要笨重地关上大门，拒绝任何法外交易。她头脑清醒地挥出一拳，令他重新考虑自己的职业。失书的合理解释已不再是暗杀威胁或市民疾病。她自己的意识从一开始就认出了罪魁祸首：市长戴面具的秘书，奉命探查地窖，并把走私工作外包出去。

His **complacency** became **incontinent** panic. He tried to **braze** the ledger shut with a lamp and call the fire an **issue** of broken wiring, then to **infiltrate** the dock crowd under a **contingency** plan. A paid **tout** set a **kinetic** panic rolling through the masks, while the secretary tried to **defraud** the town. Yet an old fisher's **augury** proved a **benison**: the tide was **retroactive** in its course, carrying the launch back toward the same **entity** it sought to evade, the fortified customs house.

他的自满变成失控的恐慌。他试图用灯把账本钎焊封死，并把火灾称为断线问题；随后又按应急计划混入码头人群。一名受雇的掮客在面具人间掀起动态恐慌，秘书则试图诈骗全镇。然而一位老渔民的预兆成了祝福：潮水逆向流动，把小艇送回它想躲避的同一处实体，即设防的海关大楼。

Inez moved to **fortify** the gate at the harbor **outskirt**, where a waiting **statistician** had traced the thefts. His **inimitable** charts showed whom the scheme would **alienate**: every **insolent** investor who had seemed **listless** at the gala but now began to **warble** for mercy. The figures **prefigure** a neat **synopsis**, Inez said, though the secretary tried to **intrude** with talk of political **polarity**. His **agitation** betrayed what he could **contrive**: make the momentum **peter** out while they hid the book in a fish **coop**.

伊内兹去加固港口边缘的大门，等待的统计员已追查出盗窃案。他无与伦比的图表显示阴谋会疏远谁：每名在舞会上看似无精打采、如今却开始为宽恕哀鸣的傲慢投资者。伊内兹说，这些数字预示着一份清晰摘要，尽管秘书试图以政治两极化的话题插嘴。他的躁动暴露了他的图谋：趁势头消退时，把账本藏进鱼笼。

Inez's **stoicism** broke only when the officer reported a cargo **shrinkage** the thieves had mocked in a **satiric** song. The story of their **transience** left the secretary **haggard**, but his former clerk was **irreproachable**. She spoke despite old **antipathy** toward the mayor and described his **avaricious** smile in the furnace **incandescence**. He had told her to **earmark** relief funds for an **angelic** orphanage, then store them in an **incombustible** strongbox. The evidence had greater **credibility** than his denial, down to the ship's carved **stem** and the **castigation** scrawled at the edge.

只有当警官报告货物短缺，而窃贼曾用讽刺歌嘲笑此事时，伊内兹的坚忍才崩裂。他们短暂横行的故事令秘书憔悴，但他先前的文书无可指责。她尽管旧日厌恶市长，仍开口描述炉火炽亮中他贪婪的笑容。他曾命她为一家天使般的孤儿院预留救济金，再把钱存进防火保险箱。证据比他的否认更可信，细至船的雕刻艏柱和边上潦草写下的责骂。

An **ostensive** lever beside the quay worked a **hydraulic** lift. There lay a **serrated** key, stamped with a **maxim** about honest labor, and the ledger at last came to **fruition**. Its entries mapped the **physiology** of the fraud: he was **loath** to **disparage** the town openly, so he hid proceeds beneath an **undergarment** crate marked **suppressible** supplies. His closest **confidant**, **albeit** frightened, confirmed the scheme had caused **havoc** and forced families to **eke** out meals.

码头旁一根显眼的杠杆操纵液压升降机。伊内兹用它缠住小艇的绳子，又在任何人能边缘化她的主张前翻找其假地板。那里有一把锯齿钥匙，印着关于诚实劳动的格言，账本终于有了结果。其条目勾勒出欺诈的机理：他不愿公开贬低小镇，便把所得藏在标有可压制物资的内衣箱下。他最亲近的心腹虽害怕，仍证实阴谋造成浩劫，迫使许多家庭勉强糊口。

At dawn the masks appeared **chromatic** and foolish in the rain. Inez helped the court **collate** the papers. The apparent customs chief was an **impostor** who had offered a sleeping **potion** to the real officer; their capture was **simultaneous** with the rescue of a **pulmonary** patient from the launch. Harbor **debris** knocked against the pilings while the mayor began a useless **rant**. A **brisk** wind stripped away his **rancor**, or at least made it impossible to hear. He had tried to **endanger** witnesses and **botch** the evidence, but the seating **arrangement** put a **foreigner** beside him who had seen everything.

黎明时，面具在雨中显得色彩斑斓而愚蠢。伊内兹协助法庭校对文件。表面上的海关长官是冒名者，他曾给真正的官员提供安眠药；抓捕他们与从小艇救出一名肺病患者同时发生。市长开始无用的咆哮时，港口残骸撞击桩柱。一阵劲风吹散了他的怨恨，至少让人听不见。他曾试图危及证人并搞砸证据，但座位安排使一个目睹一切的外国人坐在他旁边。

**moreover**, the witness was **genial** enough to endure the **wearisome** hearing. The case had an **inceptive** clue in a counterfeit **coin**, minted for the mayor's **vainglory** by a **parsimonious** jeweler. He had ordered it **cast** with a **fatuous** crown and used its secret **notation** to mark payments. The court found the proof **favorable**. From the **occident** came a **rustic** captain whose innocent **amusement** at the costume contest had begun the suspicion **afresh**; he had watched the thieves **engender** confusion, then **sate** themselves with a **dispirited** gang on stolen **provender** at the warehouse **landlord**'s table.

此外，这位证人性情和善，足以忍受冗长听证。案件的开端线索是一枚假币，由一个吝啬珠宝匠为市长的虚荣铸造。他命人铸上愚蠢的王冠，并以秘密记号标注付款。法庭认为证据有利。来自西方的一位乡野船长，对服装比赛的天真娱乐重新引起怀疑；他看见窃贼制造混乱，随后与一伙垂头丧气的人在仓库房东桌上饱食偷来的给养。

That owner's **smudge** of ink revealed an **antic** route through a warehouse of old **madrigal** scores. The crowd's **vociferance** died as Inez described the secretary's **stealth**, his **somatic** tremor, and the **lariat** used to haul the book. No one could **nitpick** the **countercharge** now. The masked **guy** who had played the false captain was a **functional** accomplice, ready to **succumb** when offered his one **aspiration**: a **continuous** wage from honest work instead of **fungible** hush money.

那位主人的一抹墨迹揭示出一条穿过存放古老牧歌乐谱仓库的滑稽路线。伊内兹描述秘书的潜行、身体颤抖和拖拉账本用的套索时，人群的喧嚣沉寂了。此刻没有人能吹毛求疵地挑剔反控。扮演假船长的戴面具家伙是实际起作用的同谋；当有人给他唯一的愿望，即诚实工作带来的持续工资而非可替代的封口钱时，他准备屈服。

His confession was **audible** even through the rain. A **paronymous** name on the manifests had led officials toward an innocent seller of **knickknack** toys, whose wife was expecting a **fetus**. Inez stayed **staunch** for them. The **thrifty** clerk produced an **auriferous** clasp from the gala **milieu**, enough to **intimidate** the **majority** of conspirators. At the pier's **pinnacle**, the mayor's **inebriety** could no longer shelter his **disdain**. The judge would **manumit** the coerced workers, then convene the hearing in the old **nave**.

即使隔着雨声，他的供词仍清晰可闻。货单上一个近音名字曾把官员引向一名无辜的小玩意商贩，他的妻子正怀着胎儿。伊内兹坚定地维护他们。节俭的文书从舞会环境中拿出一枚含金扣环，足以震慑大多数同谋。在码头最高处，市长的醉态再也遮掩不了他的蔑视。法官将释放被胁迫的工人，然后在旧教堂中殿召开听证。

There the mayor stood **hangdog** as the court ordered the **discontinuance** of his contracts. The ledger's **significance** was **preponderant**; Inez presented it **superbly**, asking every clerk to **adhere** to its ink **color** rather than his imagined **antithesis**. The town **philharmonic** fell silent while the judge began to **dissect** each **dishonest** transfer. A former councilor tried to **intercede**, claiming an **apparition** had signed the orders, but the signatures were **homologous** with the mayor's. A fresh **contusion** on the secretary's hand supplied the final link.

法庭命令终止市长的合同，他垂头丧气地站在那里。账本的意义压倒一切；伊内兹出色地提交它，请每位文书遵从其墨水颜色，而非市长臆想的对立物。镇交响乐团沉默下来，法官开始剖析每一笔不诚实的转账。一名前议员试图干预，声称是幽灵签署了命令，但签名与市长的同源。一处新鲜挫伤为秘书的手提供了最后关联。

Within that coastal **principality**, no **consanguineous** favor could outrun an honest **interpreter** of the books. The court proved that the mayor had planned to **kidnap** the clerk, using a medicine **sac** beyond local **jurisdiction** during an **inundation**. He had meant to **waylay** Inez near the pier. 在这座海岸小公国中，任何血缘偏袒都赶不上账目的诚实解读者。法庭证明市长计划在洪水期间，于地方管辖权外用一个药囊绑架文书。他原想在码头附近伏击伊内兹。相反，塔楼一道闪烁信号解除了对港口交通的临时禁令，资深市议员赶到，只向法律而非头衔或财富屈膝。

The **wily** secretary made one last run through a **wispy** fog, clutching his promised **perquisite**. Inez did not **demur** when the dock **flock** closed around him. His escape boat held a wooden **dummy** ledger, whereas the original let the town **transact** its claims under the courthouse **colonnade**. The judge spoke with **benignity** to each harmed sailor and named every corrupt **component** of the mayor's **triumvirate**. 狡猾的秘书抓着许诺给他的额外报酬，在轻雾中做最后一次奔逃。码头人群围住他时，伊内兹没有反对。他的逃船里装着木制假账本，而真账本让小镇可在法院柱廊下办理自己的索赔。法官仁慈地对每位受害水手讲话，并点名市长三人集团中每个腐败组成部分。他们侵吞公共物资、企图勒索沉默以及行为造成的每一道黑暗涟漪，都被载入记录。

The town saw the **potential** in that change, though some still felt **queasy** as the guilty men said **adieu** from the customs **aerie**.

At the memorial pier, the council offered a short **eulogy** for the lost steward, whose **impetuous** decision to hide one copy had saved the truth. Inez and the recovered clerk could **converse** freely at last. The normally **gregarious** guests did not **skimp** on lantern oil, and each lantern threw a steady **shade** over the water.

伊内兹看着他们笨拙地离开，并无得意。腐败曾是她父亲最痛恨之物；她也会守护港口。在纪念码头，议会为失踪管家作了简短悼词；他冲动地藏起一份副本的决定救下了真相。伊内兹和获救的文书终于能自由交谈。平日合群的宾客没有吝惜灯油，每盏灯都在水上投下稳定的阴影。

When the court named the mayor's **perfidy**, the masks came off. The ledger's first **adumbration** of justice needed no man to **kowtow**; Inez used **tact**, not force, to make its **linguistic** clues plain, and the final **scrawl** preserved the true **version** of Harbor Masquerade.

她唯一的保留是，信任仍是含蓄的，因而难以捉摸。不过，潜水员会在任何致命秘密消失前打捞沉没的保险箱。一匹枣骝马在码头上方等待，警员们开始搜查市长别墅。他们找到大量假印章，足以预见更广泛的审判；一群忠诚文书破译了曾经难以辨认的页面。法庭点名市长的背信后，面具被摘下。账本对正义最初的勾勒不需要任何人叩头；伊内兹以机智而非武力说明其中的语言线索，最后的潦草文字保存了港湾假面舞会的真实版本。
