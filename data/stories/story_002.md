## Story for Chunk 002 - Broken Silence

Marguerite had arrived at the monastery on a **sweltering** July afternoon, the road winding up through a **grove** of olive trees whose silver leaves shimmered with an **ethereal** light. She was no longer the **flamboyant** singer who had once filled opera houses across Europe; age and **bitterness** had begun to **attenuate** her voice to a whisper, and the **pulchritude** that critics once praised had faded into something **homely**, **mundane**, and ordinary. A **rivulet** of sweat traced the **convex** curve of her cheek as she paused to catch her breath beside a gate post worn smooth by time.

玛格丽特在一个闷热的七月午后抵达修道院，道路蜿蜒穿过一片橄榄树林，银亮的叶子在飘渺的光里闪烁。她不再是那个曾让欧洲各大歌剧院座无虚席、华丽夺目的歌手；岁月与苦涩已经把她的嗓音削弱成低语，评论家曾赞美的美貌也褪色成家常、平凡、不起眼的模样。一滴汗沿她凸起的脸颊轮廓淌下，她在一根被岁月磨得光滑的门柱旁停下喘气。

She leaned against an ancient stone **pillar**, its surface scarred by a deep **notch** where centuries of hands had steadied themselves. The monastery's **cordon** of cypress trees stood like sentinels, and from within came the faint **murmur** of the evening **choir**, their **metrical** chant drifting through the warm air. She had come to **confront** her daughter Elena, who three years ago had chosen this place over the stage—a decision that continued to **grieve** her with a pain that could **scorch** the breath from her lungs. Yet she told herself the visit was merely **inquisitive**, a test of whether their bond had become **evanescent** enough to vanish like the last **bait** of light that lures the eye before dusk. "If she is happy here," Marguerite whispered to no one, "then I will learn to bear it." It was the most **quotidian** sort of lie she had told herself in years.

她靠上一根古老的石柱，柱身有一道深深的刻痕，是几个世纪以来人们扶稳自己时留下的。修道院外围的柏树哨兵线如卫兵般矗立，从里面飘来晚祷唱诗班的低语，他们合着韵律的吟唱在温热的空气里流动。她此行是为了面对女儿埃琳娜：三年前，埃琳娜选择了这个地方而不是舞台，这个决定至今仍让她心痛，痛得几乎灼焦肺腑。可她告诉自己，这次探访不过是出于好奇，想试试她们之间的纽带是不是已经脆弱得快要消散，像黄昏前最后那一点诱人的光。她自言自语道："如果她在这里过得开心，那我就学着忍受。"这是她这些年来对自己说过的最平凡不过的谎话。

Father Aldo, the monastery's abbot, greeted her at the gate with the **civility** of a man who had spent decades smoothing the rough edges of human desire. His face was **resplendent** in the golden light, deeply lined but gentle, and he moved with the **stoic** calm of someone who had long ago chosen to **withdraw** from the world's **volatile** passions. "Your daughter is in the **grotto**," he said, his voice carrying no **disapprobation**, only a kind of weary **insouciance** that Marguerite found herself envying. She admitted that she had tried to **wheedle** the truth from Elena in letters, to **cajole** her into returning home, but every attempt had been **bootless**. The **strident** tone of her final letter, she confessed, had made Elena stop replying altogether.

修道院院长阿尔多神父在门口迎接她，那份礼貌，来自一个用几十年光阴磨平人类欲望棱角的人。他的脸在金色的光里显得灿烂，皱纹很深，却温和；他走路的姿态带着一种早已抽身离开世间反复无常之热情的人所有的淡泊沉静。"你女儿在洞室里。"他说，声音里没有一丝非难，只有一种玛格丽特发现自己竟有些羡慕的、倦怠的漫不经心。她承认，她曾在信里想用甜言蜜语从埃琳娜那里套出真话，想哄她回家，但每一次都徒劳无功。她坦承，自己最后一封信里那种尖刻的口气，让埃琳娜干脆不再回信。

The abbot led her through a courtyard where **mortar** and ancient brick seemed to **efface** the boundary between building and cliff, and a **tarpaulin** stretched over a workbench suggested ongoing efforts to **remodel** the crumbling east wing. "She is **amenable** to seeing you," he said, though a **caveat** followed: "she has found a peace here that I urge you not to **desecrate** with old grievances." Marguerite's expression must have shown her **dubious** feelings, for the old monk added, "I began to **canvass** the sisters on this matter, and there is **trepidation** among them. But a visit from her mother would **galvanize** Elena, and I do not think she will **stint** in her welcome."

院长领她穿过一个庭院，灰泥与古砖几乎抹掉了建筑与悬崖之间的界线；工作台上盖着柏油帆布，暗示东翼那面塌陷的墙正在重新修缮。"她愿意见你。"他说，但随即跟上一个告诫："她在这里找到了一份平静，我恳求你不要拿旧日的怨怼去亵渎它。"玛格丽特的表情大概泄露了她的疑虑，因为老修士又补了一句："我曾就此事向众修女征询过意见，她们中间有些惶惶不安。不过母亲来探望，会鼓舞埃琳娜的，我想她不会吝啬她的欢迎。"

Elena was sitting on a stone bench when Marguerite found her, and the sight of her daughter nearly made the old woman **limp** with shock. Elena seemed to **transfigure** before her eyes: her face no longer **contentious** but smoothed into something serene, her hair cropped **fast** beneath a linen veil. "You look like a **mirage**," Marguerite said, surprised by her own voice. Elena's lips curved into a **wry** smile. "And you look like someone who came to **intimidate**," she replied, with no venom, only a **dispassionate** observation. They sat in **disjunctive** silence, each enclosed in her own thoughts, the space between them charged like a **reactant** awaiting its catalyst. Then Elena spoke of having played **understudy** to Marguerite's grand ambitions, and of each childhood **spat** that had been left to fester.

玛格丽特找到埃琳娜时，她正坐在一条石凳上。女儿的模样几乎让老妇人惊得两腿发软。埃琳娜像是变了个人：脸上不再有争强好斗的神色，而是舒展开的安详；头发在亚麻面纱下剪得很短。"你看起来像海市蜃楼。"玛格丽特说，被自己的声音吓了一跳。埃琳娜嘴角浮起一丝苦笑。"而你看起来像是来威慑谁的。"她答道，没有恶意，只有一番不动感情的观察。两人各自困在断裂般的沉默里，中间那段距离像等待催化剂的反应物一样绷紧。随后埃琳娜提起，自己曾怎样给玛格丽特的宏大抱负当替补，童年时每一次口角又如何被放着不管，任它溃烂。

Those old fights, Elena said, had begun to **solidify** into a wall of **mendacity** and unspoken **guilt**. "You wanted me to be your **model**, your continuation, your **promoter** on stage," she said quietly, "but I was only ever a **dilettante** in your world, and your **adulation**—your hungry need to be adored—was reserved for yourself alone." Marguerite felt her **temper** flare; the old **dissonance** between them refused to **expire**, each accusation serving as a **pseudonym** for deeper wounds neither could name. Elena continued, her voice measured: "Every **blunder** you ever made, you blamed on someone else—the conductor, the pianist, me. It did not **signify** to you who it was. Then the next morning you would **resuscitate** the whole ugly cycle as though nothing had happened."

埃琳娜说，那些旧日争执早已凝固成一堵由谎言和无言罪疚砌成的墙。"你想让我成为你的样板，你的延续，你在舞台上替我吆喝的推广人。"她轻声说，"可在你的世界里，我从来只是个半吊子；而你对崇拜的渴望，那种想被爱慕的饥渴，只留给你自己。"玛格丽特觉得怒气上涌；她们之间那套旧的不和谐拒绝消散，每一次指控都像更深处伤口的化名，谁也叫不出名字。埃琳娜继续说下去，声音克制："你犯的每一个大错都怪在别人头上：指挥、钢琴师、我，对你来说是谁都一样。第二天早上，你又会把整套丑陋的循环重新搬出来，仿佛什么都没发生。"

"You **abhor** what I am," Marguerite shot back, her voice—once capable of filling a **symphony** hall—now carrying a **quaver** that seemed to **grate** against the ancient walls. "But you have your own **flaw**: you **spurn** everything that does not fit your quiet **theocracy** of silence." Elena sat **plumb**, still and upright, her expression unreadable. "I do not **ridicule** your **religion**," Marguerite continued, struggling to **summon** the composure she had always used to **defend** her choices, "but your **self-abasement** is **tantamount** to cowardice: you **splint** your wounded pride with humility and **preach** about virtue while hiding from the world."

"你憎恶我这样的人。"玛格丽特回击道，她的声音，曾经能填满整个交响乐厅，如今带着颤音，仿佛在刮擦古老的墙壁。"可你自己也有缺陷：你唾弃一切不符合你那套沉默神权统治的东西。"埃琳娜笔直地坐着，一动不动，表情难以捉摸。"我不会嘲笑你的信仰。"玛格丽特继续说，努力唤起她从前用来捍卫自己选择的镇定，"可你的自我贬抑等于懦弱：你用谦卑当夹板，固定住受伤的骄傲，又向人宣讲美德，自己却躲开整个世界。"

Elena's eyes narrowed. "Your **insincerity** fueled years of **bombast**," she said. "Every **ostentatious** gesture was designed to **accentuate** the legend of Marguerite Valois, while backstage you gave **hilarious** imitations and were cruel to anyone who dared **dissent**." Marguerite opened her mouth to retract, but no words came. She could not deny that Elena's account, stripped of all ornament, was a description she recognized all too well.

埃琳娜眯起了眼睛。"你的虚伪喂大了好多年的夸夸其谈。"她说，"每一个铺张的手势都是为了放大玛格丽特·瓦卢瓦的传奇，而到了后台，你拿别人开滑稽的玩笑，又对每一个敢于持异议的人残忍。"玛格丽特张嘴想反驳，却一个字也说不出来。她无法否认，埃琳娜的描述剥去所有装饰之后，正是她自己再熟悉不过的样子。

At last she managed to **retract** the accusation she had not even finished making, because Elena's words were **true**, and the **ineffable** weight of that recognition settled between them like dust. The silence that followed was not **peremptory** but necessary. Beyond the garden wall, **salmon** flashed in the stream as dusk fell. Neither woman needed to continue their lifelong **polemic**, and Marguerite began to see that their conflict had always been **incongruent** with the love buried beneath it. That evening they walked together through the monastery garden, where the last light of day began to **taper** into purple dusk, and Marguerite could **detect** in Elena's posture a new **symmetry**—the rigidity of old hurt replaced by a kind of **pliable** grace. "I came here because my doctor said I needed to **recuperate**," she admitted.

她终于勉强收回了那句甚至还没说完的指责，因为埃琳娜的话是对的，那份领悟沉甸甸、难以言说地落在两人之间，像灰尘一样。随后的沉默不是命令式的，而是必需的。花园墙外，暮色降临，鲑鱼在溪流里闪过。两个女人都不需要再继续她们这场终身的论战，玛格丽特开始明白，她们的冲突从来就与埋藏其下的爱格格不入。那天傍晚，她们一起穿过修道院的花园，白昼最后的光渐渐收细成紫色的薄暮；玛格丽特能从埃琳娜的姿态里察觉出一种新的匀称，旧日伤痛造成的僵硬，换成了某种柔韧的优雅。"我来这里，是因为医生说我要好好休养。"她承认道。

"But the real **liability** was never my health," Marguerite went on. "It was the **falsehood** that I needed no one." Elena placed her hand on her mother's arm, a gesture so **arresting** in its simplicity that Marguerite felt tears **trickle** down her face. "When I was a **nomad** on tour," she whispered, "I used to **reckon** that fame was the **balm** for every wound, but it was a **banal** lie, as **transitory** as the applause." The effort to **quench** that old thirst for recognition had left her hollow, but here she could feel something new taking root. Elena spoke of her **regimen** of morning prayer and evening song, of how she had learned to **improvise** a life from what remained once the noise had been stripped away.

"可真正的负累从来不是我的身体。"玛格丽特接着说，"而是'我不需要任何人'这个谎言。"埃琳娜把手放在母亲的手臂上，这个手势简单得引人注目，玛格丽特感到眼泪顺着脸颊滴下来。"我巡回演出、像个流浪者的时候，"她低语，"我总以为名声是敷在所有伤口上的镇痛膏，可那是一个平庸的谎言，和掌声一样转瞬即逝。"她为熄灭那股求认可的旧渴耗尽了自己，变得空空荡荡；可在这里，她能感到新的东西正在生根。埃琳娜说起自己晨祷晚唱的养生法，说起在把噪音剥除之后，她怎样学会用剩下的东西即兴搭建一种生活。

"You were always the **iconoclast**," Elena said with a **swagger** that recalled her mother's younger self, "and I was always **sedentary**—content to stay put." They both laughed, the sound **elate** and wild, and Marguerite understood at last that the **exalting** force she had sought in music had been here all along, in the simple **symbiosis** of mother and daughter learning to **meld** their broken pieces into something whole. The next morning she woke to sunlight flooding her cell and found Father Aldo in the refectory, where he told her the monastery had once served the local **aristocracy** before becoming a refuge for those seeking silence. "Each elder here becomes a kind of **testator**," he said, "leaving wisdom rather than wealth to those who follow." His words were gentle and **apposite**: "Your daughter's love would never **vitrify** into something rigid. It remains **malleable**, still capable of warmth."

"你一直是那个打破偶像的人。"埃琳娜说，带着一种让人想起母亲年轻时模样的得意劲，"而我总是安于久坐，乐于待着不动。"两人都笑了，那笑声高昂而狂放。玛格丽特终于明白，她曾在音乐里寻找的那股让人升腾的力量，其实一直都在这里，就藏在这对母女学会把破碎的部分融成一个整体的简单共生里。第二天早上，她醒来时阳光灌满小屋，她在食堂找到阿尔多神父。神父告诉她，这座修道院在成为寻求沉默之人的避难所之前，曾服务过当地的贵族阶层。"这里的每一位长者都会成为某种立遗嘱的人。"他说，"留给后来者的不是财富，而是智慧。"他的话温和而贴切："你女儿的爱不会凝固成僵硬的东西，它仍是可塑的，仍然能带来温暖。"

His **judgment** had been right, Marguerite told herself as she walked **out** to the garden, where Elena waited by the gate with a **lavish** bouquet of wildflowers she had gathered at dawn. "I don't want this visit to be merely a **coda** to our estrangement," Marguerite said, and Elena replied, "It won't **discommode** me to keep trying; let this be a beginning." They sat together on a low wall where roots had cracked the old **cement**, and Marguerite spoke of her **enthusiasm** for a future in which neither woman would try to **oust** the other's choices. She had **eliminated** so much from her own life without noticing—her ambition, her fear, even her voice—but she would not eliminate this. The story of their reconciliation was no simple **amalgamation** of easy fixes but a slow, honest work. A **rider** passed on the distant road, and Marguerite felt the rough stone **abrade** her palm as she steadied herself: a small, grounding discomfort that reminded her she was here, now, and that some things did not need to be magnificent to be real.

玛格丽特告诉自己，他的判断是对的。她走到花园里，埃琳娜在门口等着，手里捧着一大束黎明时采的野花，丰盛而慷慨。"我不希望这次探访只是我们疏远的一个尾声。"玛格丽特说。埃琳娜答道："继续努力不会让我觉得不便；就让这成为一个开始吧。"她们一起坐在一堵矮墙上，树根已经撑裂了旧水泥。玛格丽特谈起自己对一个未来的热忱：在那个未来里，谁也不必把对方的抉择挤走。她曾在不知不觉中从自己的生活里剔除掉太多东西，野心、恐惧，甚至自己的声音，但这一次她不会剔除眼前这份。她们和解的故事不是把种种轻巧的修补拼成简单的混合体，而是一段缓慢、诚实的劳作。一个骑手从远处路上经过，玛格丽特扶稳自己时感到粗糙的石头磨着手掌：这细小、踏实的不适提醒她，她此刻就在这里，而且有些东西并不需要辉煌才算真实。
