## Story for Chunk 015 - Salt Oracle

Mara Venn, a **lexicographer** hired to name the new organisms found by the survey ship, had once believed the sea's **allure** lay in its refusal to be named. At forty she worked with **methodical** patience in a cabin no wider than a freight elevator, cataloging clicks from the abyssal receiver while the vessel lay above a **chasm** west of Madeira. Her only colleague was Ivo, a professional **instrumentalist** who kept the hydrophones tuned and wandered the decks like a **peripatetic** **hermit**. The ship's **nondescript** hull carried a brass **relic** from its first voyage, green with **patina**, and a child-size **scooter** strapped beside the lifeboat as an absurd emergency **accessory**; one loose **spoke** tapped its rim in the swell. Their **affluent** sponsors had made the expedition possible, and they expected results.

玛拉·文恩是一名受雇为调查船发现的新生物命名的词典编纂者，她曾相信，大海的魅力正在于它拒绝被命名。四十岁的她在一间宽度不及货运电梯的舱房里，以井然有序的耐心整理深渊接收器传来的咔嗒声，船只停在马德拉岛以西的一道深渊上方。她唯一的同事是伊沃，一位职业器乐演奏家，负责调校水听器，像个四处步行的隐士般在甲板上游荡。这艘外形平淡的船，船体上装着首次航行留下的一件黄铜遗物，覆着绿色铜锈，救生艇旁还绑着一辆儿童踏板车，作为荒谬的应急附件；一根松动的轮辐随浪涌轻敲轮圈。他们富裕的赞助人让这次远航成为可能，也盼着早日看到成果。

The sponsors had even sent her a formal **outfit** for ceremony days, though Mara preferred to **dabble** in practical repairs and to shrug off their description of her as a young **polymath**; she tried, politely, to **deprecate** the label. The captain, meanwhile, had used drills to **indoctrinate** the crew into obeying alarms without question, making fear precede thought, and Mara's own **proclivity** was to resist exactly that habit. Her **punctilious** notes began to **evince** a troubling **variance**: every midnight the receiver repeated an old sailors' **overture** on a **harp**, then a woman's **cryptic** sentence. "Return what the water remembers." Ivo delivered the recording with a **nonchalant** shrug that could not hide his **pensive** face.

赞助人还为她送来一套正式的服装，供典礼日穿着，可玛拉更喜欢涉猎实际的维修活计，并尽量淡化他们给她贴的"年轻博学者"标签，礼貌地表示不敢当。与此同时，船长用反复演练向船员灌输不加质疑地服从警报的观念，让恐惧先于思考，而玛拉自己的倾向恰恰是抗拒这种习惯。她一丝不苟的笔记开始显示出令人不安的差异：每天午夜，接收器都会重复一段老水手的竖琴前奏，随后是一句女人含义隐晦的话："归还水所记得的东西。"伊沃把录音带给她时漫不经心地耸耸肩，却掩不住沉思的神情。

A low **convulsion** rolled through the ship; the deck took a **tilt**, and seawater burst from a **cleft** beside the engine room. The leak was **inadvertent** — a corroded intake valve — yet its **untoward** timing seemed to **augur** more than a mechanical fault. Ivo wanted to jettison the receiver, calling its absent designer a **combustible** **goldbrick** and its data **nugatory**. Mara refused; her audio **catalog** showed the melody **predate**d the ship by three centuries and had been **attributed** to a drowned navigator of considerable **repute**. "That is **hyperbole** dressed as science," Ivo said.

一阵低沉的痉挛穿过船身，甲板开始倾斜，海水从机舱旁的一道裂缝喷涌而出。漏水本是一个意外，腐蚀的进水阀所致，但它不祥的时机仿佛预示着远不止机械故障。伊沃想把接收器抛进海里，骂那位缺席的设计师是块易燃的假金砖，说它的数据毫无价值。玛拉拒绝了；她的音频目录显示，那段旋律比船早了三个世纪，且被归于一位颇有名望、溺亡的航海家。"那是披着科学外衣的夸张，"伊沃说。

Ivo had a **catholic** taste in music but an **intemperate** impatience with mystery, and Mara, who had watched wealthy sponsors **censor** inconvenient findings for years, heard in him the old **prejudice**: only the **gullible**, he thought, took unmeasured things seriously. She did not **boast** of her **acumen**, yet she knew the message had changed the moment the valve failed, as though the sea could feel the ship's weakened **integrity**. The captain's **timeworn** safety **rite** required them to **promulgate** an alarm; Ivo's **naysaying** made Mara **upbraid** him until she spotted a **typo** in the valve code.

伊沃的音乐口味很广，却对神秘事物怀着不加节制的不耐烦；玛拉这些年来见过有钱的赞助人审查掉不便示人的发现，她从他话里听出了那种旧偏见：凡是不能测量的东西，都是轻信者的胡话。她没有夸耀自己的敏锐，却知道阀门损坏的那一刻消息已经变了，仿佛大海能感到船体完整性的削弱。船长那套陈旧的安全仪式要求他们发布警报；伊沃的唱反调惹得玛拉斥责他，直到她发现阀门编号里一处印刷错误。

Once the **relative** risk was clear, the work became grim: by dawn the bilge was **fetid** and the pumps **balky**. Repair was **taxing**; Ivo, **maladroit** with a wrench despite **dexterous** hands at the strings, bruised his **elbow**, so Mara had to **tackle** the valve herself, **compress** a **brittle** gasket, and **buttress** the pipe with a steel brace. In a moment of **torpor** Ivo stared at the rising water, but the **consequence** of waiting was obvious, and the receiver began to **exhibit** bursts of light.

一旦相对的危险变得清楚，工作便沉重起来：到黎明时分，舱底污水恶臭，水泵也顽固地不肯正常运转。维修十分吃力；伊沃弹弦时双手灵巧，拿扳手却笨手笨脚，撞伤了手肘，玛拉只得亲自处理阀门，压紧一枚脆弱的垫圈，用钢支架撑住管道。伊沃一度陷入麻木，盯着上涨的水，可等待的后果显而易见，接收器也开始闪现脉冲之光。

The bursts were **copious**: first **monochromatic** green on the screen, then **refulgent** white, and no ration of water could **assuage** the thirst their work raised. Between the flashes came a **nautical** chart, not an **epic** prophecy but a **plausible** route toward a submerged **pachyderm** carcass that the current had pushed against the intake. The creature's **corporeal** bulk had torn the valve, and a **sodden** length of fishing net hung from its mouth. At the **inception** of the program, an engineer whose **psychology** had been shaped by grief for stranded animals recorded the distress and programmed it as a **precursor** warning loop. Ivo, a **glutton** for improbable stories, laughed.

脉冲密集得很：屏幕上先是单色绿，继而灿烂的白，而无论喝下多少配给的水，都缓解不了劳作带来的干渴。闪烁间隙出现一张航海图，不是史诗式的预言，而是一条看似合理的路线，通向一具被水流推到进水口的沉没厚皮动物尸体。那具动物有形的庞大躯体撕裂了阀门，一段浸透海水的渔网挂在它嘴边。项目刚开始时，一名因困在海上的动物而悲痛万分的工程师录下了它的求救，把它编成由压力触发的前兆警报循环。伊沃一向贪恋离奇故事，笑了。

Then he choked on the air and began to **drivel** apologies, and Mara would not **mollycoddle** him. "Help me cut the net, or we lose power. Do not **ingest** the bilge, and use the crowbar to **pry** the grille." The evidence joined two **disparate** causes — animal and machinery — into one **hideous** emergency; an **inept** response would only make it worse. A **meteoric** flash crossed the clouds, and the machine's **sensitive** screen showed one final instruction. Mara would not try to **propitiate** the rescue service with a story, and she was no **craven**; their radio had enough charge for one short **run**, and the authority's night **quota** made a reply uncertain. Still, she was no **martinet** about orders.

接着他被空气呛住，开始胡言乱语地道歉，而玛拉不肯娇惯他。"帮我割开渔网，否则我们会失去电力。别吞下舱底水，用撬棍撬开格栅。"证据把动物与机械这两个根本不同的原因连成一场可怕的紧急事故，笨拙无能的应对只会雪上加霜。一道流星般的闪光划过云层，机器灵敏的屏幕显示出最后一条指令。玛拉不会用戏剧化的说辞去安抚救援部门，她也不是个胆怯的人；无线电只剩一次短暂传送的电力，救援机构的夜间出航限额也让回应难以确定。不过，她并非严守规章的教条主义者。

"A motion for **cloture**, then," she said, ending the debate before it could **protract** and **aggravate** the leak. Ivo's **mulish** silence did not **disgruntle** her; she read the ship's emergency **agenda** and chose a third course: use the last electrical current to **swerve** toward the shallow shelf, where the hull could **abut** a rock spur without sinking. Ivo wanted to **quash** the plan, but Mara spoke as an **advocate**, and her calm voice held. No **omniscient** rescue service would find them from the radio static; at this **nadir**, even a **sere** islet looked better than their **puny** raft.

"那就提出终止辩论的动议，"她说，在争论拖长并加剧漏水之前结束了它。伊沃执拗的沉默没有让她不快；她读了船上的应急议程，选择第三条路：用最后一点电流驱动侧推器，急转向浅滩，让船体靠上岩石突脊而不至沉没。伊沃想否决这个计划，玛拉却以倡议者的口吻讲话，平静的声音撑住了局面。没有任何全知的救援部门能从无线电杂音里辨出他们的需要；在这样的最低点，一座干枯的小岛也比他们弱小的救生筏更令人向往。

The sonar's **impassive** readout gave no comfort, and they drank **tepid** coffee, **imbibe**d no courage from it, and let anger and worry **commingle** under the strain. His hands began to **falter**; she told him that **fatigue** was not cowardice. "My **preoccupation** is always the word for fear," she said. "Today it is **mettle**." The **moribund** battery left no **prospect** of a second call, and it seemed **providential** that the shelf lay only a mile away. The hard mechanics felt less like **alchemy** than a test of whether they could stay **stouthearted**. At noon Mara **perforate**d the scored diaphragm, and the sea entered with a **colossal** roar.

声呐冷漠的读数给不了任何安慰，他们喝着微温的咖啡，没有从中汲取到勇气，任由愤怒与牵挂互相交织。他的双手开始发颤迟疑，她告诉他，疲惫不是怯懦。"我总是执迷于找出恐惧的词，"她说，"今天，它叫勇气。"那块濒于耗尽的电池让第二次呼叫不再有希望；幸运的是，浅滩就在一英里外。艰难的机械操作不像炼金术，更像一场对能否保持勇敢的考验。正午时玛拉刺穿了带刻痕的膜片，海水带着巨大的轰鸣涌入。

The ship gave a **rupture** like a broken chord, and they watched the carcass of a creature already near **senescence** slide away, its hide beginning to **wizen** and grow **flaccid**. The instruction had flooded the lower compartments, enough to **depreciate** a repairable survey ship to a written-off wreck; Ivo's earlier **ruse** would have avoided that, but Mara had no patience for it now. In the silence the receiver played the old melody once more, stripped of its **prophetic** allure, and Mara understood its **correlative** lesson: every name was a promise to look closely, not an omen that excused it. She would **deplore** the animal's death and **lament** the damage, but she would not invent a **curse** to make either grander. Ivo, formerly so **timid** of embarrassment, **accost**ed her with a sheepish grin and called his own suggestion a **misdemeanor** against language; her smile had **verve**. Before the rescue launch arrived, she logged a new term: "rescue, not possession," and left the next definition blank, a small act of **fidelity** to the water itself.

船身发出一声如同和弦断裂般的破裂声，他们看着一具死时已近衰老的动物尸体滑走，它的皮开始干瘪，变得松弛。那条指令淹没了下层舱室，足以让一艘本可维修的调查船贬值成报废的残骸；伊沃先前的计策本可免去这一切，但玛拉现在没有心情理会。寂静中，接收器又播放了一遍旧旋律，褪去了预言性的诱惑；玛拉明白了其中相互关联的教训：每一个名字都是要人仔细观看的承诺，而不是替观看免责的预兆。她会为动物的死痛惜，也会为船的损伤哀叹，却不会编一个诅咒让任一件事显得更宏大。先前那么怕出丑的伊沃，带着羞怯的笑容凑过来，把自己的提议称作对语言的轻罪；她的笑容充满活力。救援艇抵达前，她在日志里记下一个新词条："救援，不是占有"，并把下一条释义留空，这是对水本身一点小小的忠诚。
