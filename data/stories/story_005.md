## Story for Chunk 005 - Clockbound Mill

Bracken Mill stood beyond the last paved road, and everyone agreed that Mira Vale had a **knack** for hearing which wheel needed oil long before the groan reached the courtyard. After a night of rain, the lower rooms smelled **septic**, a sour odor that clung to the grain sacks no amount of sweeping could remove. A **plump** moon hung above the sluice, and her father, Tomas, would **don** his miller's coat only after sunset, when an **amorphous** stain moved beneath the millpond ice like a bruise surfacing in water. Mira's **industriousness** kept the flour white and dry, but the village's **licentious** old rite treated a childless woman as property, and it demanded that she wind the clock. Len, the quiet **chef** at the communal oven, came to **entreat** her before the bells began, his apron still white with dust. Their strokes were too **harmonious** to be mechanical; each **sound** seemed to speak Mira's name in a different key. Tomas **explained** that the **torrential** water in the millrace would **presage** a death unless the clock received a keeper, and soon.

布拉肯磨坊坐落在最后一条铺装道路之外，人人都说米拉·维尔有一种诀窍，能在哀鸣传到院子之前就听出哪只水轮需要上油。一夜雨过后，低层房间便散发腐败的酸气，那股气味钻进粮袋，怎么打扫也去不掉。圆满的月亮悬在闸门上方，她的父亲托马斯要到日落后才穿上磨坊主的外套，那时冰封的磨塘下会有一片无定形的污影，像水面上浮起的淤青般游动。米拉的勤勉让面粉保持洁白干燥，可村庄放荡的古老仪式把无子的女人当作财产，并要求她去上发条。集体烤炉的沉默厨师伦在钟声响起前赶来恳求她，围裙上还沾着白色的面粉。钟声的节拍和谐得不像是机械发出的，每一响似乎都用不同的调子念着米拉的名字。托马斯解释说，磨渠里暴涨的水会预示一场死亡，除非时钟尽快迎来一位看守者。

Mira understood, in that instant, what the **provisional** hour meant. She did not **berate** him; what good would blame do, she thought, when the gears had already chosen her name? She could only **crouch** beneath the great wheels as the pendulum began to **teeter** over the black water. The clock was said to **beget** a single dry harvest for every keeper it swallowed, and Len, usually **fussy** about his dough, now opened its **impenetrable** casing with a **convoluted** bone key that had hung for years behind the oven. No one had the **authority** to **parse** those teeth except a Vale, and Mira found that every notch named a season she must **fulfill**. Beneath the bell tower lay a **squalid** chamber, and there Tomas gave **curt** instructions for the **arduous** vigil that waited.

米拉在那个瞬间明白了那临时时辰的含义。她没有责骂他；她想，当齿轮已经选中她的名字时，责备又有什么用呢？她只能蹲在大轮下方，看着摆锤在黑水上方开始摇晃。传说时钟每吞下一位看守者，就会带来一次干燥的丰收。平日对面团格外挑剔的伦，此时却用一把悬挂在烤炉后多年的盘绕骨钥匙打开了不可穿透的外壳。除维尔家的人外，谁都没有权力解析那些齿牙，米拉发现每道刻痕都标出一个她必须履行的季节。钟楼下是一间污秽的石室，托马斯在那里简短生硬地交代了即将到来的艰苦守夜。

There would be no **bliss** in that room, only the water's **glib** voice trying to **interlock** with hers. It spoke in **reportage** from drowned houses, cataloguing every lost window as if the village had never mourned at all, and each detail was a **goad** against the town's **hidebound** silence. Len told her, almost gently, that his grandfather had been a **philatelist**, and his **anonymous** stamps covered the clock face in an **aesthetic** ring no one else had noticed. Mira felt neither **ecstasy** nor ignorance; her former **illiteracy** of village signs fell away like a scab. Tomas remained **taciturn**, calling the whole rite **frivolous**, but a **fleet** thread of smoke already curled from the gears.

那间屋子里不会有幸福，只有水那花言巧语般的声音企图与她的声音扣合。它用新闻播报的口吻讲述被淹没的房屋，一一清点每一扇丢失的窗户，仿佛村庄从未哀悼过，而每个细节都是刺向小镇死板沉默的赶牛棒。伦几乎温柔地告诉她，他的祖父是个集邮家，他那些身份不明的邮票在钟面上围成一圈旁人从未注意过的、富有美感的图案。米拉既无狂喜，也不再懵懂；她对村庄征兆原本的无知像痂一样脱落。托马斯仍沉默寡言，把这整套仪式称作轻浮之举，可一缕迅疾的青烟已经从齿轮间升起。

That **incense**, blue and thin, left Mira **sullen** with certainty. At midnight Tomas tried to **fortify** the stone door, though he had promised to **raze** the clock if she refused. She had been **unwitting** of how **careworn** he was: his sleeve hung as a wet **strip**, and the village's **mainstream** tale of his courage now seemed **morbid**. The water rose and began to **unsettle** the clock's thin **animation**. "I **loathe** this," Tomas said, trying to **lug** the counterweight aside. He called sacrifice a **pejorative** word and refused to admit that the vanished keepers troubled him.

那缕幽蓝的香烟让米拉阴郁地确信一切。午夜时分，托马斯试图加固石门，尽管他曾答应，若她拒绝就把时钟夷为平地。她此前没有意识到他多么忧惧疲惫：湿袖子像一条布带垂着，村里关于他勇敢的主流传说如今显得病态。水位上涨，开始搅乱时钟那微弱的生气。"我憎恶这一切。"托马斯说着，费力地把配重拖到一边。他把牺牲称作一个贬义词，却不肯承认那些消失的看守者让他不安。

Len would not let him **downplay** it. "You are a **reprobate**," he said, climbing the stone **dais** and asking aloud what the water could possibly **elicit** from a family with such **diversity** of fear. Mira remembered Tomas as a **spendthrift** of promises, yet she could still **adore** his old **sophistication** with tools. "This is no **serfdom**, Father," she said, pointing to the clock's brass **crest**. "It is a bargain that eats the one who makes it." Then she used a mirror to **scan** the rising flood, and she refused to **valorize** either martyrdom or obedience. When the water surged through the floor and began to **lacerate** her palm, she did not **relish** the pain, but she understood its price.

伦不肯让他轻描淡写。"你是个恶棍。"他说着，登上石台，高声问这水究竟能从一个恐惧如此多样的家族中引出什么。米拉记得托马斯总是挥霍承诺，却仍爱慕他摆弄工具时的老练。"这不是农奴制，父亲。"她指着时钟的黄铜顶饰说，"这是会吞掉立约者的交易。"随后她举起镜子，细察不断上涨的洪水，拒绝把殉难或服从抬高成美德。当洪水冲破地板、开始划破她的手掌时，她并不享受这疼痛，却明白它索取的代价。

Her blood would **offset** the clock's **deterioration**, though she had not planned to give it. Tomas began a broken **monologue** about the choice that would **mar** them forever; above their heads the pendulum started to **wobble**. Len held the ritual with **reverence**, standing **intrepid** behind a flour-sack **barricade**. The water was **fickle**, first promising to **dissipate**, then throwing a child's **tantrum** against the stones. The elders' **recidivism** in renewing the sacrifice exposed the **hypocrisy** of every adult who had called the water mere weather. It did not **evoke** terror alone; it wore a hundred borrowed voices.

她的血会抵消时钟的恶化，尽管她并未打算给出它。托马斯开始一段支离破碎的独白，讲述那个将永远损毁他们的选择；头顶上，摆锤开始摇晃。伦怀着敬畏守住仪式，无畏地站在面粉袋垒成的路障后面。水变幻无常，先承诺要消散，随即又像个发脾气的孩子撞击石墙。长者们反复重启献祭，暴露了每个把洪水仅称作天气的大人的伪善。它唤起的远不止恐惧，它还顶着上百个借来的嗓音。

There was something **droll** in its borrowed voices, which unnerved Mira more than rage. The village would **ostracize** a keeper who fled, so she stayed **reserved** while Tomas offered an **imitation** of her place. Her **rejection** made him **vituperate** until the mill filled with a **cacophony** of bells, grinding wheat, and water. At last she chose to **blurt** the forbidden answer, and Len watched, **agog**. But that answer was not a **euphemism** for rescue. Mira would become the clock's **hive**, fertile only for hours, its **fecund** keeper and no one's mother. Len urged a **maverick** escape, but an **excerpt** of the old chant was enough to stop him.

它借来的嗓音里有一种古怪的滑稽，这比愤怒更让米拉不安。逃跑的看守者会被村庄放逐，所以她保持着克制，而托马斯提出要模仿她的位置。她的拒绝使他恶毒辱骂，直到磨坊充满钟声、碾麦声与水声的刺耳杂音。最后她脱口说出那个禁忌的答案，伦急切地望着她。可这个答案并不是营救的委婉说法。米拉将成为时钟的蜂巢，只为几个时辰而丰饶，做它丰饶的看守者，却成不了任何人的母亲。伦劝她另辟蹊径逃走，但古老圣歌的一小段已足够让他停下。

The **noisome** water would follow her into any valley, so there was no point in running. Tomas offered to be her **ally**, though the word began to **rankle** because every **extraneous** kindness had arrived too late. Mira made an **overt** choice. She set her breathing to the clock's **elastic** measure and watched the **sublime** water turn clear for one **halcyon** instant. Its drip, she knew, would forever **annoy** her. At dawn, before the promised **demolition** of the clock, she climbed the gear-room **summit** with **brazen** calm. She would not **exalt** Tomas, but she gave him a **raisin** from Len's butter bread, wrapped in foil. It felt like a mercy too small for the hour, and yet she could not hold it back.

恶臭的水会跟随她进入任何山谷，逃跑没有意义。托马斯提出做她的同盟，可这个词仍令她刺痛，因为每一份多余的善意都来得太晚。米拉公开地作出选择。她让呼吸跟随时钟富有弹性的节拍，望着壮美的水在某个安宁的瞬间变得清澈。她知道，它的滴答声将永远烦扰她。黎明时分，在承诺的拆钟行动开始之前，她带着无所顾忌的镇定登上齿轮室顶端。她不会颂扬托马斯，却把伦黄油面包上的一颗葡萄干裹进锡箔递给他。这宽恕看起来小得配不上那样的时刻，可她终究没能忍住。

That mercy was **congruent** with her refusal to **avoid** consequence. The clock named her its **principal** keeper — not to **vindicate** the village, she understood, but to make the river's **scowl** harmless. She would never **expurgate** Tomas from memory; time would **erode** him without her. The **requisite** turning began. Her body became a **voluminous**, **dappled** shadow behind the clock face, unable to **diminish** or **transcend** its duty. Len, once **erudite** and now **voluble**, could not bring himself to argue the old point any longer.

那份宽恕与她不肯逃避后果的立场相一致。时钟将她封为主要看守者，她明白，这不是为了给村庄昭雪，而是为了让河流那阴沉的面容不再伤人。她永远不会把托马斯从记忆中删去；时间会在没有她的参与下磨蚀他。必要的转动开始了。她的身体化作钟面后一道庞大而斑驳的阴影，既不能减轻职责，也无法超越职责。曾经过博学、如今变得健谈的伦，再也说不出那些老生常谈的争辩。

Their **disputation** about the rite's **prestige** ended not with an answer but with silence. At the mill **entrance**, the **intangible** weight of Mira's absence crossed the **porous** dawn. No **fraudulent** miracle came. After a **cursory** farewell, Tomas and Len formed a quiet **ensemble**, **coeval** as men of the same generation, and they would **meet** each dusk for their **diurnal** walk through the **drizzle**, trading **excursive** reflections on what they had allowed. Tomas grew less **obdurate**; he began to **jettison** the miller's coat, confessed his **clandestine** **abuse** of Mira's trust, and watched the day **darken** while the clock kept her alive but never let her return.

他们关于仪式声望的争论没有以答案收场，而是终结于沉默。磨坊入口处，米拉缺席的无形重量穿过多孔的晨光。没有虚假的奇迹到来。简短告别之后，托马斯和伦组成沉默的二人组，同代地承受这份悲伤；每个黄昏，他们都会在细雨中作一次每日散步，漫谈并反思自己曾默许的一切。托马斯不再顽固，开始丢弃那件磨坊主的外套，承认自己曾秘密滥用米拉的信任，看着天色渐渐变暗，而时钟让她活着，却永不允许她归来。
