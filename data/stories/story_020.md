## Story for Chunk 020 - Paper Lanterns

At dusk, Nessa Vale, the touring company's stage carpenter, found the moon door hanging from a **diaphanous** thread above the orchestra pit. Its painted swan seemed to **waddle** across the canvas, a poor **counterpart** to the graceful creature promised in the program, yet the sight gave her a small **hopefulness**. Someone had **purloin**ed the new safety pin and replaced it with a **deleterious** wire. Tonight's **verse** drama was already **tardy**, and the **nocturnal** theater lived on **minutia** — a knot, a cue, a hinge — so Nessa suspected that a hand had tried to **smuggle** danger into the finale. Her partner, Silas, was **reluctant** to delay the premiere. "We cannot **strike** the set now," he said, while the conductor's temper began to **gush** through the wings in a stream of **limerick**s. Nessa asked for **leeway**, climbed the **protuberant** stair rail, and tested the cracked **girder** with the heel of her hand.

黄昏时，巡演公司的舞台木工内莎·维尔发现月亮门只靠一根半透明的线悬在乐池上方。门上画的天鹅仿佛笨拙地摇摆着走过帆布，与节目单承诺的优雅形象很不相称，可这景象仍给了她一点希望。有人偷走了新的安全销，又换上一根有害的细铁丝。今晚的诗剧已经迟到，而夜间剧场全靠细枝末节活着，一个结、一个提示、一道铰链，所以内莎怀疑有人把危险偷偷运进了终场。她的搭档赛拉斯不愿推迟首演。"我们现在不能撤掉布景，"他说，与此同时，指挥的脾气化作一连串打油诗，从侧幕喷涌而出。内莎请求一点余地，爬上凸出的楼梯扶手，用手掌根试了试那道开裂的横梁。

She called for old Madame Rook, the company's **intercessor** with its creditors. Rook had a **stratagem** for every crisis and a **toady** for every patron, and her **irascibility** could **immure** a room in silence. "This is **ominous**," Nessa said. Rook, **frowsy** in a fur coat, tried to **malign** the absent prop boy with an **oblique** accusation. Nessa would not **shirk** the inspection merely because the evening was a cheap **potboiler** rather than the **vignette** of art Rook advertised. She began to **debunk** Rook's story with an **understated** question: who knew the moon door's **susceptibility** to heat? Silas answered with **conviction** that only he and Nessa had handled the **linen** clouds. Then a sudden **effulgence** from the footlights exposed a smear of wax on the rope.

她叫来了老鲁克夫人，这位总是替剧团向债主斡旋的人。鲁克对每一场危机都有一套计谋，每位赞助人身边都配着一个谄媚者，而她的暴躁足以把一屋子人封进寂静。"这很不祥，"内莎说。穿着毛皮大衣、衣着邋遢的鲁克试图用旁敲侧击的指控诋毁那个缺席的道具男孩。内莎不会因为今晚是一出廉价的通俗剧、而非鲁克吹嘘的那种艺术小品，就逃避检查。她用一个不张扬的问题开始拆穿鲁克的说法：谁知道月亮门受热后容易出问题？赛拉斯坚信地回答，只有他和内莎碰过那些亚麻云幕。接着，脚灯骤然绽放的辉光把绳索上的一道蜡痕照了出来。

The smear was **sordid**, old candle-wax dragged along the splice. Rook reminded them that her **redoubtable** **forebear** had founded the company, whose honor could not **stomach** another scandal. Nessa's own stomach tightened as the **choleric** conductor began to **doze** over the score. The theater was technically **accessible** to anyone in the lobby, but a **lyric** scrap in the wax showed a recent **trend** in the saboteur's thinking: "Let the old moon **persist** until it falls." The line met every **stringent** test Nessa could make. In the prop **larder** she found a **tactile** packet of resin meant to **sustain** the lantern flames and a note about **dissension** among the shareholders. The note's paper was **friable**; she crushed its seal with a brass **pestle** and found a **ponderable** fact, not a rumor: Rook had insured the moon door.

那道蜡痕很肮脏，是旧烛油顺着接合处拖出来的。鲁克提醒他们，她那令人敬畏的祖先创办了剧团，剧团的名誉承受不了又一桩丑闻。内莎自己的胃也紧了起来，因为暴躁的指挥已经伏在总谱上打盹。剧场从技术上说，大厅里的任何人都能进入，但蜡里粘着一小片抒情歌词，显露出破坏者最近的思路："让旧月亮持续到它坠落。"这句话经得起内莎能做的每一项严格检验。在道具储藏室里，她找到一包手感黏稠的树脂，原本用来维持灯笼火焰，以及一张写着股东分裂的纸条。纸很脆，她用黄铜研杵碾碎封蜡，找到了一个可衡量的事实，而非传闻：鲁克给月亮门投了保险。

When Nessa confronted her, Rook tried to **discomfit** her with gossip, then **debase** Silas as a careless laborer. Silas answered with more **diplomatic** restraint than Nessa expected. "Madame favors every **liberalist** donor in town," he said, "but an **impresario** should not have to **agonize** over whether a falling prop is part of the show." Behind the curtain, **pandemonium** spread when Rook ordered the musicians to play. Nessa was too **frugal** with praise to call Silas brave, but she heard the **demotic** crew language turn from complaint to resolve. Rook made a **grandiose** speech about tradition and called any pause **pusillanimous**. Nessa pointed to the **salubrious** open doors and said the audience deserved safety, not false **permanence**. She used a **suture** of wire to secure the painted swan while an **influx** of ticket holders pressed against the lobby. Rook tried to **conceal** the insurance papers in her **anemic** handbag; Nessa, a **stickler** for honest rigging, watched every move.

内莎质问鲁克时，鲁克先想用闲话让她难堪，随后又把赛拉斯贬成粗心的工人。赛拉斯表现出内莎未曾预料的外交克制。"夫人偏爱城里每一位自由派捐助人，"他说，"但一位剧院经理不该为坠落的道具究竟算不算演出的一部分而痛苦不堪。"幕布后面，当鲁克命令乐手演奏时，混乱蔓延开来。内莎对赞美十分吝惜，没有称赛拉斯勇敢，但她听见剧组的通俗用语从抱怨转成了决心。鲁克发表了一场夸张的传统演说，称任何暂停都是怯懦。内莎指着那些空气清新的敞开大门，说观众应得到安全，而非虚假的永恒。她用电线缝合般地固定住画天鹅的门，同时大批持票人挤向大厅。鲁克想把保险文件塞进那只无力的手提包，而对装置诚实近乎苛刻的内莎，看清了每一个动作。

She noticed a symbol from **astrology** on the clasp and began to **fret** over its meaning, but it did not **elude** her long: Rook had made a **tacit** bargain with a banker, and the **muddy** account books showed a growing **schism** between the company and its workers. "That is a **cliche** of management," Silas said, "but the fraud is **incontrovertible**." Nessa needed no **oracle** to know the door would fall. She cut the line, and the empty moon crashed backstage with an **eclat** that silenced the house; the bend of the wire had left a **corrosive** stain on the beam. A young **daredevil** from the chorus laughed in shock, but Nessa's plain **meliorism** held: a ruined effect could be improved, a broken neck could not. Rook made a **furtive** move toward the exit. Nessa blocked her path. "What is the **riddle** of a company you claim to **perpetuate**," she asked, "if you need an **alibi** when its people are hurt?" Rook began to **rail** against them.

她注意到包扣上有一个占星术符号，开始为它的含义烦恼，可答案没有躲开她太久：鲁克和一名银行家达成了默契交易，浑浊的账簿显示剧团与工人之间出现了越来越深的分裂。"那是管理上的一套老掉牙说辞，"赛拉斯说，"但欺诈是无可辩驳的。"内莎不需要神谕也知道月亮门会坠落。她割断线，空月亮在后台轰然坠地，巨响震得全场安静；铁丝弯折处在梁上留下一道腐蚀的痕迹。合唱团里一个爱冒险的年轻人惊得发笑，但内莎朴素的向善信念没有动摇：毁掉的效果可以改善，折断的脖子却不能。鲁克鬼鬼祟祟地朝出口移动，内莎挡住了她的路。"你声称要延续这家剧团，"她问，"可一旦有人受伤就需要不在场证明，这到底是什么谜？"鲁克开始怒骂他们。

But the conductor raised a hand to **allay** the crowd. He did not **chide** Nessa for the ruined finale. Instead he exposed the contract that would **undercut** the crew's wages and had paid for Rook's private **intoxicant**s. Silas became the first **champion** of a new safety committee. The old moon door would **die**, yet its painted swan had no **jaundice** of blame. The conductor, suddenly **gallant**, asked Nessa to compile a **bibliography** of every accident and every ignored repair. No **faction** cheered at first; then the wardrobe keeper brought **lint** and tape, refusing to **rehash** old quarrels. The company voted to **promote** Silas, not as a reward, but because he had remained **solvent** through Rook's unpaid bills. Madame Rook's family **legend** did not survive the night. People could **speculate** about her motives, but the real **boon** was simpler: the next performance would have a trained **conductor** of safety, not merely music.

可指挥抬起手安抚了人群。他没有因终场被毁而责备内莎，反而拿出那份会削减剧组工资、并支付了鲁克私人醉品的合同。赛拉斯成为新安全委员会的第一位拥护者。旧月亮门会死去，可画上的天鹅不该背负任何责难。指挥忽然变得殷勤，请内莎编一份书目，列出每一次事故和每一项被忽视的维修。起初没有派系欢呼；随后服装管理员拿来棉絮和胶带，拒绝重新翻炒旧争吵。剧团投票提拔赛拉斯，并非奖赏，而是因为他在鲁克拖欠工钱时依然有偿付能力。鲁克家族的传奇没能熬过这一夜。人们可以猜测她的动机，但真正的恩惠更简单：下一场演出将有了一位受过训练的安全指挥，而不只是音乐指挥。

Nessa and Silas had one brief **tiff** over who should fetch the emergency **lariat**, then laughed at their **similarity**. Neither mistook their former **impuissance** for innocence. They would **hearken** to the small **ephemera** of work — a loose screw, a torn hem, a cough in the flies — before they could **antagonize** disaster. They would **venerate** no old name merely because it sounded **astounding**, but let a **trenchant** rule **stabilize** the stage: every warning would be recorded **verbatim**. By midnight, the repaired lanterns began to **flourish** beneath the painted **frieze**, and the canceled finale no longer felt like a **charade**. Rook's **perfunctory** apology brought no **serendipity**, only a **raspy** promise to answer for her act.

内莎和赛拉斯为谁该去拿紧急套索拌了几句嘴，随后又为彼此的相似大笑。他们都不再把过去的无能为力当作无辜。他们会先倾听工作中的细小杂物，一颗松螺丝、一条破下摆、吊杆间的一声咳嗽，再让它们招来灾祸。他们不会因为一个旧名字听起来惊人就去崇拜它，而是让一条尖锐的规则稳定舞台：每一次警告都要逐字记录。午夜时分，修好的灯笼在绘有饰带的墙面下重新盛放，被取消的终场也不再像一场骗局。鲁克敷衍的道歉没有带来任何幸运的发现，只有一声沙哑的承诺，说她会为自己的行为负责。

Nessa tightened the **bridle** on a pulley, grateful for her **smattering** of law and for the quiet **luminary** above the empty stage. She would **stem** no artist's desire, **exhaust** no apprentice, and accept no **figurehead** with an **acerbic** voice or inherited **hauteur**. Care could **mitigate** disaster through patient **husbandry**; it could **reassure** without being **effusive**, correct the **incorrigible** without cruelty, and give even the most **pallid** dawn a reason to **inaugurate** another show. Her own **peccadillo**, she decided, had been believing that silence was professional. The new rule required no **pretense**. It required **exemplary** hands.

内莎收紧滑轮上的缰绳，庆幸自己略懂法律，也感激空舞台上方那盏安静的明灯。她不会压制任何艺术家的愿望，不会耗尽任何学徒，也不会接受一个声音尖刻、带着祖传傲慢的挂名领袖。照护能以耐心的经营减轻灾祸；它能不夸张地安慰人，能不残忍地纠正屡教不改者，也能给最苍白的黎明一个理由去开创下一场演出。她下定决心，自己的小过错是曾以为沉默才算专业。新规则不需要伪装，只需要堪称典范的双手。
