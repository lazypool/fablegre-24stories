## Story for Chunk 010 - Breathing Moon

Tomas Reed guided the lander through dust that seemed to hold a **lien** on every joint, a drifting veil they could not shake. Mira Sol, a formerly **dissolute** scavenger who had become a disciplined field engineer, sorted samples in non-**alphabetical** trays, while Inez Vale, a **virtuoso** exobiologist, asked the mission to **patronize** a patient survey rather than a hasty extraction. The engine's **muffler** softened its exhaust as they watched a valley of blue reeds **ebb** and rise with the sunless tide. Tomas could **demonstrate** that the reeds shared nutrients, but no **partisan** theory explained why their roots drew a star-shaped **doodle** around the lander. The **knotty** pattern even included a break shaped like an **apostrophe** in one arm. As a navigation **tyro**, Tomas first called it a **fallacy** of perspective; Inez corrected his **obtuse** angle with a patient sketch. A dominant colony of translucent grazers had begun to **evict** smaller grazers from the basin, a **momentous** migration, and Mira stood **venerating** the quiet precision of it.

托马斯·里德驾驶着陆器穿过一层仿佛对每个接缝都拥有留置权的尘雾，那是他们怎么也甩不掉的游动纱幕。米拉·索尔曾是放荡不羁的拾荒者，如今已成为严谨的现场工程师，她整理着不按字母顺序摆放的样本盒；外星生物学家伊内兹·维尔则是一位技艺精湛的专家，她要求任务组资助耐心的勘测，而非仓促开采。引擎的消声器柔化了排气声，他们看着蓝色芦苇谷随着无日潮汐退落又升起。托马斯能证明芦苇彼此交换养分，却没有偏颇的理论能解释为何根系在着陆器周围画出星形涂鸦。这个棘手的图案甚至在一根枝杈上有一道像撇号的缺口。作为导航新手，托马斯起初把它当成视角谬误；伊内兹耐心地画图，纠正了他钝角般的判断。占优势的一群半透明食草生物已开始把较小的食草生物逐出盆地，这是一次重大的迁徙，米拉敬畏地站在那里，看着它安静的精确。

Their drilling plan went into **abeyance** when Inez raised a red **flag**: the creatures were leaving a mineral **residue** that formed a living **yoke** around the reeds. She learned the organisms' chemical **argot** by tasting air through a filter. The warmth the organisms gave the ground was **unrequited** by the cold regolith, yet their **tenacity** kept the meadow alive. "Do not **construe** the retreat as panic," she said. Her **determination** rested on a **simplicity** Tomas had missed: the colony moved **retrograde** across its own nutrient trail to feed larvae hidden below. Mira, normally **ascetic** about supplies, proposed that they **evacuate** the lander and make an **emendation** to the route. Tomas would not **forsake** the creatures, though the worsening dust began to **beleaguer** their seals. Years in flight had served to **inure** him to alarms, and now he ordered the team to **disperse** and map the roots.

当伊内兹举起红旗时，他们的钻探计划暂时中止了：那些生物留下的矿物残渣正把芦苇连成一道活的轭。她靠过滤器品尝空气，学会了这些生物的化学行话。这些生物给土地带来的温暖得不到寒冷风化层的回应，但它们的韧性让草甸存活下来。"别把后撤解释成恐慌，"她说。她的决心建立在一个托马斯忽略的朴素事实之上：族群沿着自己的养分痕迹逆行，为藏在地下的幼体供食。平时对补给十分克制的米拉建议撤出着陆器，并修订路线。托马斯不愿抛下这些生物，尽管加剧的尘暴不断围困舱体密封。多年飞行已让他习惯各种警报，此刻他命令众人分散开，绘制根系图。

Everyone moved into a shallow **gully** to study the web, and no one would **grovel** before an unknown ecosystem. "We must **bedeck** nothing," Mira said, sealing her helmet, "and **conciliate** it through care, not force." Under their boots, a thin crust began to **dissolve**, revealing the organisms' **provenance**: a warm saline aquifer whose **instability** could turn its **porcelain** ceiling to shards. Mira felt **chagrin** when her earlier core sample proved **dire**: its heat would **accumulate** enough pressure to kill the nursery. Tomas called himself a **simpleton** for accepting the **vacuous** mission **prescription** to extract first and observe later. Spore-feeders were **rife** beneath the **saddle** of rock, and Inez could **anticipate** their response: leave the aquifer untouched and the larvae would drown; vent it and the adults would lose their **autonomy** from the roots.

众人移进一道浅沟研究根网，没有人愿意在未知的生态系统面前卑躬屈膝。"我们不必装饰任何东西，"米拉扣紧头盔说，"要用照料而非强迫去安抚它。"他们脚下，一层薄壳开始溶解，露出这些生物的起源：一片温暖咸水层，它的失衡会让瓷质顶板化为碎片。先前的岩芯样本十分凶险，这让米拉懊恼不已：其中的热量会积聚成足以杀死育幼层的压力。托马斯骂自己是傻子，竟接受了那套空洞的任务规定，先开采、后观察。岩石鞍部下孢子食者极为繁盛；伊内兹预见到它们的反应：不碰含水层，幼体会淹死；泄压，成体族群就会失去相对于根系的自主性。

Nobody bowed, and **obeisance** to the schedule would have been absurd, so Inez proposed an **intuitive** answer: use the lander's **tugboat** thrusters to lift one section of crust. The model was **untenable** until Mira found a **flexible** membrane in the root web. Tomas gave her no formal **commendation**, only a careful **check** of the stresses, because a wrong pulse might **weaken** the cavern, make the grazers **misbehave**, and **taint** first contact with harm. Yet the web's **resilience** answered a low **snarl** of vibration, and the familiar **routine** of extraction now seemed a **glossy**, **labile** lie. Beyond them, a **circular** chamber had opened in a **windy** plume, and inside it crouched an **elephantine** shape.

没有人低头，向日程表献上敬意更是荒谬，于是伊内兹提出一个直觉的答案：用着陆器如拖船般的推进器抬起一片地壳。直到米拉在根网中找到一层柔韧的薄膜，这个模型才算成立。托马斯没有给她正式的嘉奖，只仔细核对了应力，因为一个错误的脉冲可能削弱洞穴，让食草生物行为失控，并给这次初次接触留下伤害的污点。然而，根网的恢复力回应了一声低沉的咆哮，熟悉的开采流程此刻像一则光鲜而易变的谎言。在他们前方，一个圆形空腔在多风的喷流中打开，里面蹲伏着一具庞大的身影。

The brood-mother's **thorny**, **lackluster** mantle protected thousands of larvae, and she remained **sedate**, not hostile; her **cherubic** young made a **measured** clicking, like a distant **tambourine**. The **despotic** impulse to take command felt **feral** in Tomas. A sensor lure could **entice** the adults toward the surface, but their sudden **expansion** would be an **abomination** for the fragile meadow. Inez faced a final **puzzle** and the **obstreperous** tremor of the ceiling. "The **lank** roots are an **estimable** bridge," she said. "The **anterior** chamber can fill. It will not **perturb** the brood." Tomas resisted **avarice** for a dramatic discovery and chose the slower path.

育母多刺而无光泽的外套保护着数千只幼体，她保持沉静，并不敌对；天使般的幼体发出有节拍的咔嗒声，如同遥远的小手鼓。托马斯心中那股想要接管一切的专横冲动显得野性十足。传感器诱饵能把成体引向水面，但它们突然的膨胀会对脆弱草甸造成可憎的破坏。伊内兹面对最后的谜题和顶板喧闹的震颤。"细长的根系是一座值得尊敬的桥，"她说，"前方腔室可以进水，不会扰乱育群。"托马斯压下对轰动发现的贪婪，选择了更缓慢的路径。

He used a slow, **innocuous** thermal **crescendo** instead, and the **credulous** crew watched an **acre** of **serrate** roots lift like an **epaulet**. It was not **imprudent** luck but a calculation made by a **distraught** Tomas through **shale** and mineral ribs that seemed to **emboss** the cavern. They held **neutrality** between rescue and study, chose to **suppress** the drill, and accepted the **immutable** cost: no sample would test the web's **durability**. A green **tint** began to **accrete** on the **enamel** crust as Mira helped **prune** broken filaments.

他改用缓慢而无害的渐进式热流，轻信的队员看着一英亩锯齿状的根系像肩章一样抬了起来。这不是轻率的运气，而是心绪不宁的托马斯穿过页岩与仿佛压印在洞穴壁上的矿物肋所作出的计算。他们在救援与研究之间保持中立，关闭钻头，接受不可改变的代价：没有样本能检验根网的耐久性。米拉修剪断丝时，一层绿色色调开始在珐琅般的地壳上积聚。

Their **success** was no **labyrinth** of claims, no **outlandish** tale to **prate** about. In the cave beginning to **glisten**, they did not **knead** a world into human use; they chose to **pack** their tools and continue the **expedition** on foot. The long **trudge** over **gravel** could **nauseate** them, but Mira shared **tangy** water. Tomas recorded no human **appellation** for the species, only to **assert** its **animus**: it had chosen to live. That was not **speculation**. The rescued web did not **discombobulate** their assumptions; it served to **highlight** Tomas's old **dyslexia** for living patterns.

他们的成功不是一座论断的迷宫，也不是可以拿来吹嘘的离奇故事。在开始闪亮的洞穴里，他们没有把世界揉成供人使用的形状，而是选择收拾工具，徒步继续探险。碎石上的长途跋涉令人反胃，但米拉分了些辛香的水。托马斯没有为这个物种记下人类的命名，只写下一个断言：它有求生的意志。这不是推测。获救的根网没有打乱他们的理解，反而凸显了托马斯对活体图案固有的阅读障碍。

With **alacrity**, they chose to **renounce** extraction. The **grisly** fear that had been a constant **peeve** lost its **calorie** of power, and Mira's **mirth** returned as they left no **salvage** operation, no plan to **augment** the site. Their **parlance** became observation, their promise to **preserve** distance. Behind them, the **moth-eaten** sky seemed to **braid** with the dawn. The dilemma had not been a **disaster** but a **ring** of dependence, a **paradox** the reeds could **loll** within. They would not **circumscribe** its future.

他们以充沛的干劲选择放弃开采。那始终令人恼火的可怕恐惧失去了能量，米拉重展笑颜；他们没有展开打捞作业，也没有扩张此地的计划。三人的言语变成了观察，他们的承诺是保持距离。身后，虫蛀般的天空似乎正与晨曦编织在一起。这个两难不是灾难，而是一圈相互依存，是芦苇可以悠然栖身其间的悖论。他们不会限制它的未来。
