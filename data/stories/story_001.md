## Story for Chunk 001 - Fog Harbor

At four bells, the harbor's **drone** came through the fog: a warning horn, low as a beast beneath water. Nora Venn followed it along the **gnarled** pilings, carrying the paper that proved her uncle's berth was **transferable**. Behind the customs shed, sailors made **pandemonium** over a missing vessel, while the cold seemed to **enfeeble** every sensible answer. Only the night watchman's account sounded **truthful**: the ship had sailed bearing the seal of the Harbor **commonwealth**, then vanished before reaching open sea.

四响钟声时，港口的低沉鸣响穿过浓雾，警号低得仿佛水下潜伏的野兽。诺拉·文恩沿着盘曲的木桩前行，带着证明叔父泊位可以转让的文件。海关棚屋后，水手们为一艘失踪的船闹得不可开交，寒冷似乎削弱了每一个明智的回答。只有夜班守望人的陈述听起来真实可信：那艘船带着港口共同体的印记驶出，却在抵达外海前消失了。

A fresh **gouge** marked the warehouse **entrance**, and Nora found a blue **kerchief** caught in it. A small **floe** knocked softly against the quay although winter had not yet begun. She chose to **initiate** an inquiry before the harbor masters could **squelch** the alarm. The cloth made her hand **quiver**; it belonged to her uncle, who had taught her to **brake** a cart on the steep streets and to regard **abnegation** as no virtue when a family was in danger.

仓库入口处有一道新鲜的凿痕，诺拉发现一块蓝色头巾挂在里面。冬天尚未开始，一小块浮冰却轻轻撞着码头。她决定在港务长们压下警报前展开调查。那块布让她的手发抖；它属于她的叔父，他曾教她在陡峭街道上给马车刹车，也教她在家人身陷险境时，自我牺牲并非美德。

On the door's inside face someone had tried to **inscribe** a message, then stopped at the word "hold." Nora read it as an order to **belay** a line, not a plea to stop. The clerk called her search **unproductive** and demanded a **resumption** of ordinary commerce. She asked whether he would **deign** to open the ledger. A red mark seemed to **underscore** his fear, and he had to **accede** when she invoked the harbor **parliament** and its authority to investigate official **conduct**.

有人试图在门内侧刻下一条消息，却停在了“抓住”一词。诺拉将它理解为系住缆绳的命令，而非要求停下的恳求。书记员称她的搜查毫无成果，并要求恢复正常贸易。她问他是否肯屈尊打开账簿。一道红色标记似乎强调了他的恐惧；当她援引港口议会及其调查官员行为的权力时，他不得不同意。

The ledger showed a **ghastly** discrepancy: a lantern had been used to **singe** the departure record, yet its figures might **outlast** the fire. Nora crossed a covered **breezeway** to the captain's office, where a pearl **brooch** lay beside a chart. The chart marked a facial **deformity** on a sketch of a suspected smuggler, and it sent the clerk into **despair**. "They intend to **legalize** the transfer," he said. "They call it a **marvelous** improvement."

账簿显示出一处骇人的差异：有人用提灯烧焦了离港记录，然而上面的数字或许仍能留存于火灾之后。诺拉穿过有顶的通风走廊，来到船长办公室，一枚珍珠胸针放在海图旁。海图在一名疑似走私者的素描上标记了面部畸形，这使书记员陷入绝望。“他们打算让这次转让合法化，”他说，“他们称之为绝妙的改进。”

His explanations became **evasive** whenever Nora asked about the grammatical **flection** in the altered log. A harbor **dissident** named Iven had objected to the council's **draconian** shipping rule; his nautical **qualification** made him an inconvenient witness. Nora found him at the chapel, where grief gave him a brief **catharsis**. Though **impecunious**, he had bought a scrap of the burned manifest. "The ink can **rive** the lie apart," he said, pointing to its numerical **indices** and a shipment labeled **salmon**.

每当诺拉询问被篡改日志中的语法词形变化时，他的解释便闪烁其词。一名叫伊文的港口异议者曾反对议会严酷的航运条例；他的航海资质使他成了令人棘手的证人。诺拉在小教堂找到他，悲伤让他得到片刻宣泄。尽管囊中羞涩，他还是买到了一片烧毁的货单。“墨迹能把谎言撕开，”他指着其中的数字索引和一批标为鲑鱼的货物说道。

Iven's **patronizing** recital of a dockside **verse** annoyed Nora, but the former **linguist** knew that one altered suffix could conceal a ship. His injured hand left him with an **incapacity** for rigging, so he could not **ignite** the records-room fire. Instead, he explained the tide's **calculus** and showed her a harbor **fresco** whose painted beacon had a new **tremor** of gold. The addition betrayed the council's **complacence** about the fire's **origin**.

伊文居高临下地背诵码头诗句，令诺拉恼火，但这位前语言学家知道，一个被改动的后缀就能藏起一艘船。他受伤的手使他无力操作索具，因此不可能点燃档案室的火。相反，他向她讲解潮汐的计算法，并带她看一幅港口湿壁画，画中的灯塔多了一丝颤动的金色。这个增添之处暴露出议会对火灾起因的自满。

From the tower, Nora could **espy** a **bookish** boy carrying a **tart** to the lighthouse keeper. His lessons in **physics** had taught him that the foghorn's strange **interjection** was an **irrefutable** clue: its echo came from inside the breakwater. She chose to **befriend** him. His mother, an **apiarist**, had seen a violent **gesticulation** on the water and a **frenzied** launch with a green **tint** depart the pier.

从塔上，诺拉瞥见一个好读书的男孩正把一块果馅饼送给灯塔看守人。他学过物理，明白雾号那声奇怪的插入音是无可辩驳的线索：回声来自防波堤内部。她决定和他交朋友。他的母亲是一名养蜂人，曾看见水面上激烈的手势，以及一艘带着绿色色调、疯狂驶离码头的小艇。

The boy's throat was **parched**, yet he spoke **gamely** as Nora rowed him across the harbor. A sudden **row** among gulls exposed a moral **perversion** in the official story: no ship could **flounder** where the water was so deep. A councilor arrived with **fulsome** praise for Nora's **unremitting** diligence, followed by a **rabid** reporter whose flash powder was an obvious **indicator** of surveillance. Nora struck a calm **pose** beneath the lamp's pale **corona** and examined its corroded **anode**.

男孩喉咙干得厉害，但诺拉划船带他横渡港湾时，他仍勇敢地说着话。海鸥间突起的一阵吵闹揭露了官方说法中的道德歪曲：水那么深，船不可能在那里挣扎沉没。一位议员到来，对诺拉不懈的勤勉大加吹捧，随后是一名狂热记者，他的闪光粉显然是监视的信号。诺拉在灯具苍白的光环下摆出镇静的姿态，检查它腐蚀的阳极。

The damaged lamp produced a **clarion** pulse every third minute. At its base was a stamped **gratis** pass and a brass **flip** latch. Under it, Nora found the **verity** the council feared: a child's **titter** had been recorded in the hidden chamber. The boy stayed **imperturbable**, but Nora saw an infected **abscess** on the watchman's hand. He belonged to the private **league** guarding the breakwater, and his **timid** refusal to answer was more useful than a confession.

损坏的灯每隔三分钟发出一次嘹亮的脉冲。它的底座有一张盖章的免费通行证和一个黄铜翻扣。诺拉在下面发现了议会所害怕的真相：暗室里录下了一个孩子的轻笑。男孩依然镇定自若，但诺拉看见守望人手上有一处感染的脓肿。他属于守卫防波堤的私人联盟，而他胆怯地拒绝回答，比坦白更有用。

Nora ordered the ferryman to **reseat** the boy in the cabin, then made a **vigorous** search of the keeper's **arsenal** of tools. The keeper, a **sober** man, conceded that his previous **evasion** had been foolish. A visiting **pollster** had promised him the **pulchritude** of a rebuilt harbor if he would help **effectuate** a midnight transfer. In the chamber hung a **pendulous** bell and stacks of **polemics** against public ownership, tied with a red **token** ribbon.

诺拉命令摆渡人让男孩在船舱重新坐好，接着有力地搜查看守人的工具库。这个一向沉稳的人承认，自己先前的躲闪很愚蠢。一位来访的民意调查员曾许诺，只要他协助促成午夜转让，便可得到重建港口的美景。房间里悬着一口下垂的钟，还有一摞反对公有制的论战文章，用一条红色纪念丝带捆着。

The keeper had gained no **rapport** with the conspirators, who called citizens a **scourge** and hoped to **disappoint** every investigation. They had used the fog to **slough** off a barge; the harbor's **ubiquitous** mist hid it. Nora would not **belittle** the evidence merely because its owner was a reclusive **spinster** who ran the signal station. The woman would not **condone** the crime, but her **urbane** nephew called it an **infelicitous** clerical **typo**; the pin's **monogram** revealed his **tendency** to forge his aunt's name.

看守人与阴谋者并无融洽关系；他们称市民为祸害，并希望让每一次调查都无功而返。他们借雾摆脱了一艘驳船，港口无处不在的浓雾掩盖了它。诺拉不会仅因证据的主人是个经营信号站、离群索居的未婚妇人就轻视证据。那女人不会纵容罪行，但她世故的侄子把它称为一次不恰当的文书笔误；别针上的字母花押暴露出他伪造姑母名字的倾向。

The station's oldest **denizen**, a one-eyed cat, watched Nora **irradiate** a damp wall with a lamp. The light exposed salt **depreciation** on the mural and made the nephew **writhe** when she uncovered a **tessellated** panel. Beneath it, his **ruse** concealed the original **chronicle**. It described him as a **philanderer** who had promised to **persevere** in reform but behaved like a **boor** when challenged by a fishwife and her **rooster**.

信号站最老的住客，一只独眼猫，看着诺拉用灯照亮潮湿的墙壁。光线显出壁画因盐分而损坏的痕迹；当她揭开一块镶嵌图案的面板时，侄子痛苦地扭动起来。在其下方，他的诡计藏着原始编年记录。记录把他描述成一个风流成性的人：他曾承诺坚持改革，却在遭到一位鱼贩妇人和她的公鸡质问时举止粗鲁。

Nora permitted no **levity**. The manifest said the council planned to **remit** the harbor to a company whose dredges would **graze** the reef. A resident **faultfinder**, the old **apothecary**, produced a brass **planisphere** from which the conspirators had copied their routes. He saw the nephew **waddle** into a private **discussion** with a prudish **prude**, then leave her **giddy** with false assurances. The chemist had remained **circumspect** until his own **postmortem** of a drowned sailor found drugging by a known **dipsomaniac**.

诺拉不允许半点轻浮。货单说议会计划把港口交给一家公司，而那家的挖泥船会擦过礁石。一名惯爱挑错的居民，也就是老药剂师，拿出一只黄铜星盘，阴谋者曾从中抄录航线。他看见侄子摇摇摆摆地去和一个拘谨的假正经女人私下交谈，随后用虚假的保证让她晕头转向。化学家一直谨慎行事，直到他亲自验尸一名溺亡水手，发现其遭到一个已知酗酒者下药。

The fog seemed **illimitable**, but Nora refused to **appease** fear with any merely **earthly** comfort. Her only **recourse** was the evidence. The nephew dismissed it as **pretentious**, citing a **paucity** of direct witnesses, but the two routes were **complementary** and together showed a forbidden **penetration** of the sea wall. "This is a question of **ethics**," Nora said. "Your account is **polarized** propaganda meant to **sublimate** greed into civic duty."

雾仿佛无边无际，但诺拉拒绝以任何仅属尘世的安慰来平息恐惧。她唯一的依靠是证据。侄子以直接证人太少为由，斥其为矫饰之谈，但两条路线互为补充，共同显示有人非法穿过海堤。“这是一个伦理问题，”诺拉说，“你的说法是两极化的宣传，意在把贪婪升华为公民义务。”

He tried to **impugn** Iven's character and **repress** the boy's testimony. Nora asked the sentry to **induct** the keeper as a witness. The room's **tension** tightened despite the nephew's **blandishment** of the elderly officials. Their docks would **deteriorate** under his **haphazard** plan; the claimed repairs did not **belie** that fact. A retired **soldier** saw the boy **nestle** against Nora's coat and agreed to **plead** for an immediate search, a decision that would **galvanize** the silent crowd.

他试图诋毁伊文的人品并压制男孩的证词。诺拉请哨兵正式接纳看守人作为证人。尽管侄子对年长官员甜言蜜语，房间里的紧张气氛仍不断加剧。他杂乱无章的计划会令他们的码头日益恶化；所谓维修并未否定这一事实。一名退伍军人看见男孩依偎在诺拉的外套上，便同意请求立即搜查，这一决定将激励沉默的人群。

Nora was too **introspective** to mistake that courage for glory. The **mundane** question was whether boarding the barge was **inadvisable**. Its berth lay on the **opposite** side of the basin, guarded by a **hirsute** man wearing one **earplug** and a ridiculous **tonsorial** cape. Yet the child was a **corporeal** person, not an abstract **indication** in a ledger; the **quotidian** facts made the voyage **perilous**, whatever tales of an **afterlife** the smugglers told.

诺拉太善于自省，不会把那种勇气错当成荣耀。实际的问题是，登上驳船是否不明智。它的泊位在港池对面，由一个毛发浓密、戴着一只耳塞和荒唐理发师斗篷的男人把守。然而孩子是有血有肉的人，不是账簿里的抽象标记；无论走私者讲述何种来世传说，日常的事实都使这趟航程充满危险。

The proper **prim** clerk offered a **kiloliter** of fresh water for the launch. An **employee** brought a **bifurcated** hook, and Iven's **proficiency** with knots overcame the lack of an **alphabetical** inventory. By sheer **pertinacity**, the old **sentinel** admitted that the cargo involved illegal plant **introgression**: imported reeds were being planted to bind the stolen shoreline. Outside, **drizzle** covered a brass **rebus** painted on the wharf.

那位拘谨端正的书记员为小艇提供了一千升淡水。一名雇员带来一只分叉钩，伊文熟练的打结本领弥补了没有按字母排序清单的不足。凭着纯粹的坚持不懈，老哨兵承认货物涉及非法植物基因渗入：进口芦苇正被种下以固定被窃取的海岸线。外面，细雨覆盖着码头上绘制的黄铜字谜图案。

The puzzle showed a crown, a key, and a beggar's bowl: "crown-key-**penury**." Nora recognized the **cant** of debt collectors and felt no **mirth**. The council had filed the charter in **triplicate**, then used a launch to **commandeer** the harbor **archive**. Its secret **fiscal** annex funded ornamental **arboriculture** on the private island. The pattern would **recur** whenever a land transfer looked **accidental**, though it was entirely **credible** that the council expected nobody to read it.

谜图画着王冠、钥匙和乞丐碗：“王冠—钥匙—贫困。”诺拉认出了收债人的行话，丝毫笑不出来。议会把特许状一式三份存档，随后用小艇强行接管港口档案馆。其秘密财政附录资助私人岛屿上的观赏性树木栽培。每当土地转让看似偶然时，这种模式都会重现，尽管议会确实有理由相信没人会去读它。

It was **ironical** that the proof lay in a crate of **crockery**. The recorded **scenario** said it had broken in transit; the harbor master’s gray **frizz** of hair was embedded in its wax seal. His chief **competitor**, a stern advocate of **teetotalism**, had hired an **acolyte** to carry the crate to the **stately** old courthouse. There, the documents could **ground** the case even if one **imponderable** remained: why had the master risked an **embargo**?

讽刺的是，证据竟藏在一箱陶器里。记录中的情节称它在运输途中破损；港务长的一缕灰色卷发嵌在蜡封中。他的主要竞争者是一位严厉的禁酒主义倡导者，雇了一名随从将箱子送往庄严的老法院。在那里，即使仍有一个难以估量的问题，文件也能为案件奠定基础：那位港务长为何要冒险招致禁运？

At dawn, all harbor bells rang in **unison**. Nora's first **interrogative** to the **august** court was whether the master meant to **contaminate** the city's water. He sneered that private rule was no **theocracy**, but his papers were scarcely **cohesive** and their legal language was **tedious**. When he tried to **disengage** from the witness stand, a **furor** rose outside, where the watchman had cast a **dragnet** across the channel.

黎明时，港口所有钟声齐鸣。诺拉向庄严法庭提出的第一个疑问是，港务长是否打算污染全城的用水。他冷笑说私人统治并非神权统治，但他的文件几乎毫无连贯性，法律措辞又冗长乏味。当他试图从证人席脱身时，外面爆发了骚动，守望人已在航道上布下拖网。

A **heedless** deckhand attempted to **ape** a patrol officer's signal, an **indefensible** trick even to an **indifferent** observer. The master used a bitter tonic to **addle** the boy's memory, but the chemist could **gauge** its dose from the bottle and had Iven **lug** it into court. The master was famously **misanthropic**; he claimed the tonic was a harmless **placebo**. The child, now **unfettered**, described being **mired** in a cabin while an **empathetic** cook fed him soup.

一名鲁莽的甲板水手试图模仿巡逻官的信号，即便在冷漠旁观者看来也是无法辩护的把戏。港务长用苦味补剂搅乱男孩的记忆，但化学家能从瓶中估出剂量，并让伊文把它扛进法庭。港务长素以厌恶人类闻名；他声称那补剂只是无害的安慰剂。如今已获自由的孩子描述，自己被困在船舱里时，一位富有同情心的厨师给他喂汤。

By **dint** of the cook's **sequacious** obedience, the captors had left a kettle of **offal** and a list of names. The watchman placed a **cordon** around the pier. A brass automaton in the cabin was an obvious **anachronism**, yet its paired bells had sounded an **antiphony** that alerted a visiting **thaumaturgist**. His **infrequence** of visits made his statement remarkable: he had seen the barge **furnish** its hold with a **buff**-colored cage.

由于厨师盲目顺从，绑架者留下了一锅内脏和一份姓名清单。守望人在码头周围设下警戒线。船舱里的一具黄铜自动人偶显然不合时代，但它的一对铃铛奏出应答式的声响，惊动了一位来访的魔术师。他极少来访，这使他的陈述格外引人注目：他曾看见驳船用一只浅黄褐色的笼子布置货舱。

The cage held no magic, only a **canonical** copy of the deed and the boy's sketch of the men who meant to **victimize** him. Its **vivacious** lines made a **compelling** case that the master had agreed to **underwrite** the escape. Nora had the **nerve** to ask whether a fight of **pugilism** between two sailors had begun the fire. In the **tenebrous** hold, the cook heard the master **rave** that a **crafty** witness would never discover the deed's true **purport**.

笼中没有魔法，只有一份契据的正式副本，以及男孩画下的、意图加害他的男人们的素描。画中活泼的线条有力证明港务长已同意资助逃离。诺拉鼓起勇气询问，两名水手间的拳斗是否引发了火灾。在阴暗的货舱中，厨师听见港务长咆哮，说一个狡猾的证人永远发现不了契据真正的意图。

The master had ordered an artist to **retouch** the seal and cited **penology** to defend indefinite detention. His argument claimed the **predominance** of commerce over law, a **preposterous** assertion from a man wearing a judge's **vestment**. Nora submitted the jewel as a **tribute** to the missing owner and asked the court to **curtail** the master's speeches. His campaign **blurb** had promised clean water, yet the barge's **malodorous** tanks contradicted every **predicate** in its **passive** promotional prose.

港务长命令一名画师修饰印章，并援引刑罚学为无限期拘留辩护。他的论点宣称商业凌驾于法律之上，这种说法出自一个身穿法官袍服的人，实在荒谬。诺拉把珠宝作为献给失主的礼物提交，并请求法庭缩短港务长的发言。他的竞选宣传词曾承诺清洁用水，可驳船恶臭的水箱反驳了其被动宣传文案中的每一项断言。

The court sent harbor **gear** to inspect the tanks. Its **declarative** report identified a corrupt **donor** and a **compliant** inspector. A professor of **ornithology** gave **trenchant** testimony: dead terns proved the discharge. The defense tried to **debunk** her, but the master could only **strut** behind his counsel, performing the **subservience** he expected from every **kindred** official. The sludge's **accretion** had made the inner channel **impassable**.

法庭派出港口设备检查水箱。其明确陈述的报告确认了一名腐败捐助者和一名顺从的检查员。一位鸟类学教授作出犀利证词：死亡的燕鸥证明了排污。辩方试图驳倒她，但港务长只能在律师身后趾高气扬地踱步，表演着他期待每位同类官员表现出的卑躬屈膝。淤泥的堆积已使内航道无法通行。

One **lawmaker** argued that evidence was **scarce**, until the station keeper produced the master's **frowzy** coat and its sealed pockets. It was wrong to **defame** a suspect without proof, Nora said, but the coat supplied the proof: a **yen** note for imported reeds, written in the master’s exact **deportment** of hand, and a receipt for coastal **virtu**. The receipt's **circular** stamp matched the **datum** in the records room. Even the **senile** clerk could recognize it.

一位立法者声称证据稀少，直到信号站看守人拿出港务长那件邋遢的外套及其封住的口袋。诺拉说，没有证据就诽谤嫌疑人是不对的，但外套提供了证据：一张购买进口芦苇的日元票据，笔迹与港务长一贯的书写姿态完全一致，还有一张购买海岸艺术珍品的收据。收据的圆形印章与档案室中的数据相符。即便老迈昏聩的书记员也认得出来。

The master had planned a private **menagerie** to charm investors. He began to **supplicate** the court, claiming that he was a **prohibitionist** against toxic dumping. His **sedentary** accountant produced a jar containing one **animalcule** from the polluted water, taken near an **aboriginal** fishing shore. The creature's **preternatural** glow did not survive close **perusal**; it was dye. That discovery ended the final **bout** of doubt.

港务长原计划建一座私人动物园来取悦投资者。他开始恳求法庭，声称自己反对有毒废料倾倒。他久坐不动的会计拿出一个罐子，里面装着从原住民渔岸附近污染水域取来的一只微小生物。那生物超自然的光芒经不起细看；那只是染料。这一发现终结了最后一轮疑虑。

The boy named the smuggling password, a nonsense **vocable** built from the master's **convoluted** initials. The clerk, thoroughly **discombobulated**, dropped a chart lit by a **moonbeam** and stuck his fingers to spilled **gum**. Beneath the chart was a **fraudulent** invoice for breakfast **cereal**. Nora saw, **finally**, that the master had used a child's **plastic** toy boat to carry a key from pier to barge.

男孩说出了走私口令，那是由港务长复杂姓名首字母拼成的无意义词语。书记员彻底慌乱，掉落了一张被月光照亮的海图，手指还粘上了洒出的胶。海图下有一张早餐谷物的欺诈发票。诺拉终于明白，港务长曾用一只儿童塑料玩具船把钥匙从码头运到驳船。

His **temperament** broke. He had claimed that the reclaimed shore would be **fertile**, an **offbeat** promise from someone prepared to **divest** the town of its docks and make an investor's **utopia**. The court called his plan **grotesque**; even the once **apathetic** **abbot** who ran the sailors' hospice left his **grindstone** and voted to **ostracize** him. The costs were no longer merely **ponderable**. They were **felonious**, and the supposed public mandate was **factitious**.

他的性情终于崩溃。他曾声称收复的海岸会很肥沃；对于一个准备剥夺城镇码头、为投资者打造乌托邦的人而言，这是个反常的许诺。法庭称他的计划荒诞怪异；甚至那位曾经漠不关心、经营水手救济院的修道院院长也放下磨刀石，投票要求把他逐出社会。代价已不再只是可以衡量的。它们属于重罪，而所谓公众授权也是人为伪造的。

Fog began to **billow** past the courthouse windows, making the lamps **sway**. The judge called the clerk's delay **venial**, but ordered the town to **cultivate** the damaged marsh rather than yield it. Nora's old **prepossession** that every rich man was guilty had been a **pestilent** habit; she had needed evidence and **finesse** to **adduce** it. The master, a former **subaltern** in the customs office, had used the fog's **diffusion** to conceal what would soon **transpire**.

浓雾开始翻涌着掠过法院窗外，令灯具摇摆。法官认为书记员的拖延可以宽恕，却命令城镇耕护受损的沼泽，而非将其让出。诺拉过去先入为主地认定每个富人都有罪，是一种有害习惯；她需要证据和巧妙手腕来提出主张。港务长曾是海关办公室的一名下级官员，他利用雾的弥散掩盖即将发生的事。

The cook brought **bouillon** to the recovering boy. She explained that the harbor's oldest families were **consanguine**, and that fear of **rabies** had kept people from approaching the stray dogs near the barge. Nora did not **rebuff** the lesson: public **hygiene** had been another neglected duty. The court ordered workers to **hurl** the contaminated barrels into secure disposal, not into the sea, whose **vitreous** surface had already collected a sailor's broken **cartilage**.

厨师给正在康复的男孩端来肉汤。她解释说，港口最古老的家族彼此有血缘关系，而对狂犬病的恐惧使人们不敢接近驳船附近的流浪狗。诺拉没有拒绝这一教训：公共卫生是另一项被忽略的责任。法庭命令工人把受污染的桶猛力投入安全处置设施，而不是扔进海里；海面如玻璃般，已聚集着一名水手断裂的软骨。

At the quay, the old signal mast seemed to **droop** under its own history. Nora recalled her uncle's **dictum** that secrecy was **counterproductive** in a small town. The station keeper, ordinarily **coy**, identified the master's broad **brogue** in a recorded order to **decamp**. Behind a warped **baseboard** in his office, she found an **exemplar** of the altered charter. Its **theory** of lawful transfer dissolved before the court, leaving Nora in brief **awe** at how close the harbor had come to ruin.

在码头边，老信号桅杆似乎在自身历史的重压下低垂。诺拉想起叔父的格言：在小镇里，保密适得其反。平日羞怯的信号站看守人从一则录下的逃离命令中辨出了港务长浓重的地方口音。在他办公室一块翘曲的踢脚板后，她发现了一份被改动特许状的范本。它关于合法转让的理论在法庭前瓦解，让诺拉短暂地惊叹港口离毁灭竟如此之近。

The rescued boy would not return to the smugglers' **camp**. An **expeditious** magistrate began to **eulogize** Nora, but she stopped him; the boy's headaches were not **psychosomatic**, and no praise could **propitiate** the families awaiting news. Iven made an unplanned **spoonerism**, calling the barge a "fool's harbor," and asked what the charter could **mean** after so much **premature** confidence. Its promise had been **nugatory** from the start.

获救的男孩不会回到走私者的营地。一位办事迅速的治安法官开始赞颂诺拉，但她制止了他；男孩的头痛不是心理因素造成的，任何赞美也无法安抚等待消息的家属。伊文无意间说了个首音互换的口误，把驳船叫作“傻子的港湾”，又问在如此过早的自信之后，这份特许状还能意味着什么。它的承诺从一开始就毫无效力。

The court chose to **demarcate** the public shore with bright markers. Nora, still **sensitive** to the difference between legal story and **realism**, refused to label the master an **infidel** merely for his politics. Instead, the new harbor **prospectus** described an **inexorable** restoration funded by a modest **modicum** from every berth, with the mayor as its **titular** steward. Bells answered from the fog, their **resonance** reaching the hills where ruined sailors' cottages would be allowed to **rusticate** no longer.

法庭选择用明亮标记划定公共海岸。诺拉仍敏感于法律叙事与现实主义之间的差别，拒绝仅因港务长的政治立场就给他贴上异教徒标签。新的港口说明书转而描述一项不可阻挡的修复工程，由每个泊位缴纳的一小笔款项资助，市长担任名义上的管理人。钟声从雾中回应，其回响传到山丘；那些破败水手小屋将不再被允许沦为乡间废屋。

With **ample** proof preserved, the assembly accepted Nora's **ratiocination** and opened the records room for all to **browse**. The keeper's niece, a young **debutante** who had once expected truth to **disappear** behind manners, said resistance had not been **futile**. The harbor's last horn sounded **mournful**, then the tide began to **ripen** the marsh for proper **husbandry**. A tiny **hexapod** climbed a clean piling, and Nora considered it **unconscionable** that the town had nearly looked the other **way**.

在充足证据得以保存后，议会接受了诺拉的逻辑推论，并向所有人开放档案室供其浏览。看守人的侄女是一位年轻初入社交界的女子，曾以为真相会在礼数背后消失；她说抵抗并非徒劳。港口最后一声号角哀伤地响起，随后潮水开始使沼泽成熟，以便妥善经营。一只微小的六足虫爬上一根干净的木桩，诺拉认为这个城镇险些视而不见，实在不可原谅。

The master, whose **misogamy** had colored every insult, was convicted without allowing his lawyer to serve as a **proxy** for facts. Nora could not **predict** whether the city would **dispossess** every conspirator, but it barred their company from using a reed **crossbreed** on public soil. Her uncle's vessel was found tied beneath the **forecastle** of the barge, a **herculean** recovery task made necessary by corruption **rife** through each **stratify** of harbor authority.

港务长因厌恶婚姻而给每句侮辱都染上色彩；他被定罪，法庭不允许其律师代替事实说话。诺拉无法预测城市是否会剥夺每一名阴谋者的财产，但城市禁止他们的公司在公共土地上使用芦苇杂交种。她叔父的船被发现系在驳船船头楼下方；港口权力的每一层级都充斥腐败，因此这成了一项艰巨的打捞任务。

There remained **contention** over the council's old **gerontocracy**, which had let the master **squat** in office after the unlawful **cession**. Nora's **deft** handling of the evidence led the town to restore the signal station as a **shrine**, its walls deliberately **unadorned** except for a brass **mesh** protecting the recovered ledger. The master remained **impenitent** and **magniloquent**, but the final judgment was **apposite**: every **dissentient** could speak at the public **school**, where a **liberal** record of the case showed **nil** excuse for the harbor's **dilapidation**. At sunset, the repaired beacon cast its light beyond the quay like a **catapult**, and the court's new **edict** made the foggy harbor answerable to those who lived by it.

围绕议会旧有老人统治的争议仍未平息；在那次非法割让之后，它让港务长赖在职位上。诺拉娴熟地处理证据，使城镇将信号站修复为一处纪念地，墙壁刻意不作装饰，只有保护找回账簿的黄铜网罩。港务长仍不知悔改且言辞浮夸，但最终判决恰如其分：每位持异议者都可在公立学校发言，那里一份宽容公开的案卷表明，港口的破败毫无借口。日落时，修复的灯塔像投石器般把光投向码头之外，而法庭的新法令使这座雾港必须向依赖它生活的人负责。
