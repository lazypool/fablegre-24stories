## Story for Chunk 019 - Circuit Court

When the circuit court rolled into Bellwether on a rain-stained coach, its clerk found a **derogatory** notice nailed to the door. Its intended **recipient** was Judge Elian Vale, who had once sat in a **palatial** capital chamber and now traveled the hill counties to **arrange** justice where no permanent bench could be kept. The notice offered a **prize** to anyone who could **adumbrate** the judge's disgrace.

巡回法庭乘着雨痕斑斑的马车抵达贝尔韦瑟时，书记员发现门上钉着一张诋毁性的告示。它原本要给的是埃利安·维尔法官；他曾在华丽的首都议事厅任职，如今巡行山区各县，在无法设立常设法庭之处安排司法。告示悬赏给任何能勾勒法官耻辱的人。

Bellwether was too **parochial** to ignore such a **contretemps**. In the **fitful** light, the mayor would not **vouchsafe** an explanation; he called the inquiry **tiresome** and blamed a wandering **barterer** who wished to **depopulate** the town of custom. Yet Dalia Saye, a **frowsy** miller's widow, appeared in strict **compliance** with the summons, carrying a damp **quarto** beneath the **yoke** of her shawl. Its **caption** made the mayor **exasperate** himself: Saye v. Bellwether Land Company.

贝尔韦瑟太过狭隘，无法忽略这场尴尬冲突。在忽明忽暗的光线中，市长不肯恩赐解释；他称调查烦人，归咎于一名想让小镇失去顾客的流动易货商。然而，衣着邋遢的磨坊主遗孀达莉娅·赛伊严格遵从传唤，披肩的束缚下夹着一本潮湿的四开本。其案由让市长自我恼火：赛伊诉贝尔韦瑟土地公司。

Clerk Nera Quill tried to **decode** the faded deed. It concerned a river **trade**, and two **concurrent** claims: Dalia said her late husband had held the water-right; Alderman Croft said an old **infirmity** of title voided it. Vale heard in Croft's speech the **cupidity** of a man eager to **engage** every technical **maneuver**. The contested spring was a **perennial** source, and the page's unusual **perspicuity** made its altered line stand out as plainly as a red **balloon** above a fair.

书记员内拉·奎尔试图解读褪色的契据。它涉及一项河流交易和两项同时存在的主张：达莉娅说亡夫拥有水权；市议员克罗夫特说所有权的旧瑕疵使其无效。维尔从克罗夫特的话里听出了一个急于动用每种技术手段者的贪婪。争议泉水常年不竭，而页面异常清晰，使被改的一行像集市上方的红气球般醒目。

Croft began to **pander** to the crowd. A lantern's **luminescence** fell across the steep **declivity** beyond the hall, where Dalia had initially seen wagons at work. “The **first** clause is only **metaphorical**,” said Celia Venn, the town's celebrated **belle** and Croft's niece. Croft insisted his ownership was **absolute**. A boy in a blue **bandanna** then reported that a strange **virus** had struck the **canyon** camp, silencing the laborers who might testify.

克罗夫特开始迎合人群。灯笼的亮光落在大厅外的陡坡上，达莉娅最初曾在那里看见马车作业。“第一条条款只是比喻性的，”镇上著名美女、克罗夫特侄女塞莉娅·文恩说。克罗夫特坚持其所有权绝对无误。随后，一个戴蓝头巾的男孩报告，一种奇怪病毒袭击了峡谷营地，使本可作证的工人噤声。

The boy sang a scrap of **ballad** learned from his father. Nera began to **appreciate** its significance, while Dalia watched in **dismay** as Croft's hired **windbag** claimed that company records must **preponderate** above a widow's memory. “An **egotist** speaks so,” Vale said, and that rebuke seemed to **impel** the man toward the rear **mews**, where fresh printed notices began to **proliferate**.

男孩唱了一小段从父亲那里学来的民谣。内拉开始领会其意义；达莉娅则惊愕地看着克罗夫特雇来的夸夸其谈者声称，公司记录必须比遗孀的记忆更有分量。“自我中心的人才会这样说，”维尔说道；那句斥责仿佛驱使那人走向后方马厩，那里新印的告示开始大量出现。

Vale ordered the bailiff to **quell** the disorder. Someone had threatened to **raze** Dalia's mill. In the street, Nera found a printer's **rag** made to **glisten** by rain, its ink still **deliquescent**. A line of **sarcasm** sought to **traduce** Dalia as a thief. The type could not **preexist** in the town's ordinary supplies; its worn letters showed the same **attrition** as the pressmarks in the altered deed.

维尔命令法警平息混乱。有人威胁要夷平达莉娅的磨坊。街上，内拉找到一块被雨打得发亮的印刷工抹布，墨水仍在潮解。一句讽刺企图诋毁达莉娅是贼。该字体不可能预存在镇上的普通库存中；磨损的字母与改过的契据上压印痕迹有同样的损耗。

The circuit coach followed a **serpentine** lane to Croft's warehouse, where a guarded **hoard** of documents lay behind barrels. The gatekeeper answered with **billingsgate**, but Vale named the court's **repository** authority and refused a **perfunctory** search. Among the ledgers, Nera noted a suspicious **homogeneity** of paper. Wind had begun to **becalm** the valley, leaving every sound **innocuous** and distinct.

巡回马车沿着蜿蜒小路来到克罗夫特的仓库，成堆受守卫保护的文件放在木桶后。看门人以污言秽语回答，但维尔说明法庭的保管权限，拒绝敷衍搜查。内拉在账册中注意到纸张可疑的同质性。风开始使山谷平静，让每种声音都无害而清晰。

Her father had taught county **pedagogy**, and Nera knew enough figures to question the company's **actuary**. The ledger used a **plural** signature where the original contract named one grantee. Vale made no **animadversion** until an **artisan** brought a thread of blue **lint** from the warehouse **warren**. It matched the blue cloth. The boy, found collapsed in a near **coma**, held a **brittle** receipt in his **tempered** fist.

她的父亲教授县级教育学，内拉懂得足够多的数字，能质问公司的精算师。账册在原合同只写一名受让人的地方用了复数签名。直到一名工匠从仓库迷宫般的角落带来一根蓝色棉绒，维尔才作出批评。它与蓝布相符。男孩被发现时近乎昏迷，坚韧的拳头里握着一张脆弱收据。

The receipt had a **consonance** of dates with Dalia's volume, but the case reached a **stalemate** when Croft produced a scarred **pugilist** who swore Dalia had signed a **submission** to arbitration. Dalia, ordinarily **courageous**, could barely cross the crowded **thoroughfare**. A local **gourmet**, leaving the inn, recalled an abrupt **exodus** from Croft's camp on the night in question.

收据日期与达莉娅书册相协调，但克罗夫特带来一名满身伤疤的拳击手，发誓达莉娅签过提交仲裁的文件，案件便陷入僵局。一向勇敢的达莉娅几乎无法穿过拥挤的通道。一名本地美食家离开客栈时，回忆起事发当夜克罗夫特营地突然的大批离去。

At the borrowed **lectern**, Nera took a higher **vantage** and showed the court a **lifelike** wax seal. Croft asked her to **waive** formal proof, arguing that its **univalence** made comparison useless. Doctor Fen replied after an **auscultation** of the sick boy that the coachman’s **corps** had been fed no plague-ridden meal. Croft answered with a **harangue** measured like bad **decameter**, until Vale warned that testimony and rhetoric might **collide**.

内拉站上借来的讲台，占据较高视角，向法庭展示一个逼真的蜡封。克罗夫特要求她放弃正式证明，称其单一性使比较无用。芬医生给病童听诊后回答，车夫团体没有吃过染瘟疫的饭。克罗夫特用一段像糟糕十音步诗般的长篇大论回应，直到维尔警告证词与修辞可能冲突。

The doctor smelled **frankincense** in the boy's cloak and was **confident** it had masked an irritant. Nera held the seal to a **reflectible** pane and saw a **proletarian** emblem beneath its **restrained** surface: the mark of the old mill guild. It would **haunt** Croft. Behind the courthouse **hedge**, someone had left a **heathenish** charm to make witnesses fear to **tread** near the spring.

医生在男孩斗篷上闻到乳香，并确信它掩盖了刺激物。内拉把封印对着可反射的玻璃板，看到其克制的表层下有一个无产者徽记：旧磨坊行会的标志。这会令克罗夫特摆脱不掉。法院篱笆后，有人留下异教护符，让证人害怕靠近泉水。

Vale asked Dalia for quiet **introspection**, then called an **ecumenical** council of elders. Croft, a recent **parvenu**, sneered at a river **phylum** diagram painted on a bread **crust** by a child. The child’s **symphonious** tune showed that the company had begun to **machinate** long before Dalia’s husband died. Croft tried to **dramatize** a fainting spell, but Nera used a ruler to **trisect** the disputed map.

维尔请达莉娅安静内省，随后召集各教派长老会议。新近暴发的克罗夫特嘲笑一名孩子画在面包皮上的河流门类图。孩子和谐的曲调表明，公司早在达莉娅丈夫去世前就开始策划。克罗夫特试图戏剧化地假装昏倒，但内拉用尺把争议地图三等分。

Each third exposed an **exponent** of the same scale. The ink’s **consistency** differed from the original, and a spilled bottle of **liquor** had blurred only the new boundary. “No claim stretches to **infinity**,” Vale said. He summoned the company **nominee** and made him **entreat** the court for delay. The man admitted that Croft sought the **usufruct** of every channel, leaving Dalia's field **drab** and dry.

每一部分都露出同一比例尺的指数。墨水的一致性不同于原件，而一瓶洒出的酒只模糊了新的边界。“没有主张能延伸到无穷，”维尔说。他传唤公司的被提名人，迫使其向法庭乞求延期。那人承认，克罗夫特谋求每条水道的收益使用权，使达莉娅的田地暗淡干涸。

Vale issued a search **warrant**. A neighboring **fable** told of a river **divinity** who punished pledge-breakers, but the judge preferred proof. At a **dappled** ford, a **timorous** ferryman warned them not to **underrate** Croft. Dalia began to **agonize** when the old **seignior** who leased the ferry denied seeing the wagons. Nera asked to **extract** a splinter from his boot; its red clay was **unwitting** evidence.

维尔签发了搜查令。邻近的传说讲述一位惩罚失信者的河神，但法官更偏好证据。在斑驳的浅滩，一名胆怯的摆渡人警告他们不要低估克罗夫特。租下渡口的老领主否认见过马车时，达莉娅开始焦虑。内拉要求从他靴子中取出一根木刺；其上的红土成了无意的证据。

She used a surveyor's rod to **calibrate** the distance to the **confluent** streams. The wagon tracks proved the company had sent forced **labor** there to **bloat** its reservoir. The ferryman's **ingratitude** toward Dalia was explained by a debt to Croft. A retired **pedagogue** identified three **dissimilar** inks, while a **trio** of workers led the court to an **agronomist** with a **plump** notebook.

她用测量杆校准到汇流溪水的距离。车辙证明公司派遣强迫劳工到那里膨胀水库。摆渡人对达莉娅的忘恩负义可由欠克罗夫特的债解释。一名退休教育家辨认出三种不同的墨水，三名工人则把法庭带到一位带着厚笔记本的农学家那里。

The soil expert had watched company men **forage** for reeds, then use flour **paste** to **banish** a genuine page from the book. His account rebutted the printed **libel** with unexpected **bravura**. On the recovered leaf, a trace of **detergent** showed that the maker had washed a seal while working **ambidextrous** presses. That **aberration** allowed Nera to **retain** the impression for comparison.

土壤专家曾看见公司的人搜寻芦苇，再用面粉糨糊把真页面从册中移除。他的叙述以出人意料的勇气驳斥了印刷诽谤。找回的纸页上，一点洗涤剂痕迹表明制作者在操作双手并用的印刷机时洗过封印。这个异常让内拉得以保留印痕作比较。

By dusk, floodwater threatened the records with **submersion**. Nera set a **snare** for the document thief near the warehouse's upper **prominence**, above the river's **nadir**. Croft's clerk came to **amortize** a debt in the town **morgue**, where bodies from the camp had been stored. He was not **fanatical**; under questioning, he surrendered an **armful** of letters and described the reservoir’s dangerous **amplitude**.

到黄昏，洪水威胁淹没档案。内拉在仓库上方突出处、河流最低点之上，为偷文件者设下圈套。克罗夫特的文书来到镇停尸房摊销一笔债，营地的尸体曾被存放在那里。他并不狂热；在盘问下，他交出满怀书信，并描述水库危险的规模。

It would drown eighty **acreage** of otherwise **decent**, **fecund** farms. One letter quoted a **libretto** drafted by Croft's **obstructionist** counsel, promising **clemency** to workers who kept silent. A county **genealogist** found the promised route in Croft's **itinerary**. Its margins directed a driver to **lubricate** an axle at a shrine said to be **supernatural**.

它会淹没八十英亩本来体面肥沃的农田。一封信引用克罗夫特阻挠派律师起草的歌剧脚本，承诺宽恕保持沉默的工人。一名县谱系学家在克罗夫特的行程表中找到承诺路线。页边指示司机在一座据说超自然的神龛给车轴上油。

Croft tried to **ascribe** every discrepancy to harmless **fraud** by underlings. Vale saw through the **seedy** defense and watched it **discomfit** him. The company planned a **gargantuan** dam, using **glutinous** clay from the ravine, then calling the destruction **detoxification**. Dalia's **sentimental** attachment to the mill did not make her wrong. She would not **sidestep** the injury merely because the crops were **vendible** and the old term was **over**.

克罗夫特试图把每个不一致都归咎于下属无害的欺诈。维尔看穿这套下流辩护，也看着它使他窘迫。公司计划建造巨型水坝，用峡谷里的黏土，然后把破坏称为去毒。达莉娅对磨坊的感情依恋并不代表她错了。她不会仅因作物可出售、旧期限已结束便回避伤害。

A threatened **riot** gathered as the moon reached its **perigee**. The guard feared the evidence would **perish** before it could **nucleate** into a coherent case. Vale required an **oath** from every witness, including a **singular** river **savant** who denounced Croft's **cowardice**. During the judge's modest **investiture** years earlier, the scholar had kept the county **coffer** and knew each levy.

月亮到达近地点时，一场受威胁的骚乱聚集起来。守卫担心证据在凝聚成连贯案件前会毁灭。维尔要求每位证人宣誓，包括一名谴责克罗夫特怯懦的非凡河流学者。多年前法官简朴的就职期间，这位学者曾保管县库，了解每项征税。

He could only **deplore** the clerk's initial **bungle** in copying a number. Yet that error revealed the central **provocation**: Croft's **solicitor** had ordered a **cloudburst** of water released from the test gate. Its **concentric** wash marks proved the company intended Dalia’s **subjection**, hoping to **bewitch** the town with disaster and blame the widow.

他只能哀叹文书最初抄错数字的失误。然而这个错误揭示了核心挑衅：克罗夫特的律师命令从试验闸门放出暴雨般的水流。其同心冲刷痕迹证明公司意图使达莉娅屈从，希望以灾难蛊惑小镇并嫁祸遗孀。

The court made its **plodding** **transition** to the dam at dawn. There Vale accepted a formal **presentment** from the grand jury, though a supervisor’s **whine** accused the court of rejecting local **munificence**. Croft began to **pontificate**, but an anxious **murmur** rose when Nera read the workers’ **proscription**: no one could speak to Dalia during the **vernal** survey.

法庭在黎明时缓慢转移到水坝。在那里，维尔接受大陪审团的正式呈递，尽管一名主管抱怨法庭拒绝当地的慷慨。克罗夫特开始高谈阔论，但内拉读出工人的禁令时，焦虑低语升起：在春季勘测期间，任何人不得同达莉娅交谈。

That was an **unscrupulous** order. Under a **microscopic** lens, an **epicure** from the inn identified grain adhesive in the seal, not wax. Croft called the finding **jejune**, and his contempt deepened the workers' **alienation**. His absurd claim of **knighthood** gave Vale a sharp sense of **irony**. The alleged privilege was **insubstantial**, purchased from a traveling **speculator** who described Croft as merely **manlike** in his vanity.

那是一道毫无廉耻的命令。在显微镜下，来自客栈的美食家认出封印中的是谷物胶而非蜡。克罗夫特称这一发现浅薄，他的轻蔑加深了工人的疏离。他荒谬的骑士身份声称令维尔强烈感到讽刺。所谓特权毫无实质，是从一名旅行投机客那里买来的；那人形容克罗夫特的虚荣不过像个普通男人。

Vale ordered a **safeguard** around the spring and told the jury that **prejudice** must not govern them. Croft raised a **cavil** about Dalia's supposed **adoration** of the river **omnipotence** in the old tale. Nera called it a familiar **trope**. Against all **odds**, she could **replicate** the seal's hidden mark during a sudden **downpour**.

维尔命人围绕泉水设置保护措施，并告诉陪审团不得受偏见支配。克罗夫特对旧故事中达莉娅据称崇拜河流全能的说法提出吹毛求疵的异议。内拉称那是熟悉的套话。尽管困难重重，她仍能在突降大雨中复制封印的隐藏标记。

The stamp was **diminutive**, but it exposed a **venal** clerk. Its faint **opalescence** appeared **contradictory** only until water warmed the gum. Croft’s face became **dolorous**. The original deed contained a **concordat** between mill and town, witnessed by a **neophyte** surveyor beneath **luxuriant** willow branches. It allowed no **intolerance** of either user’s share.

印记很小，却揭露了一名贪腐文书。它微弱的乳白光泽起初似乎矛盾，直到水温暖胶料。克罗夫特的脸变得悲痛。原契据含有磨坊与镇之间的协约，由一名新手测量员在繁茂柳枝下见证。它不允许对任何使用者份额的不容忍。

The company had provided workers only **inedible** rations and treated their protests as **insensate** noise. A **stellar** witness, the camp nurse, described **gangrene** in an injured boy's foot and Croft's **haughtiness** toward requests for medicine; **notwithstanding** fear, she took a long **stride** to the witness rail. A **gaseous** lamp began to **wither** in the storm, and even the shrine's **deity** seemed to **consider** the testimony.

公司只给工人提供不可食用的口粮，并把他们的抗议当作无知噪声。一位出色的证人，营地护士，描述受伤男孩脚上的坏疽和克罗夫特面对药品请求时的傲慢；尽管害怕，她仍大步走向证人席。气灯在风暴中开始衰弱，连神龛的神祇似乎也在考虑证词。

Nera offered the nurse a **raisin** from her pocket, an act of ordinary **sensibility**. The judge’s **collapsible** camp table served as a desk for the **outcast** workers. He could not yet **absolve** the clerk who had made the forged **infusion** into the record, but his reasoning was **logical**: a missing mark, like an **ellipsis**, invited inquiry rather than certainty.

内拉从口袋里给护士一颗葡萄干，这是平常的体贴。法官的折叠营地桌充当被排斥工人的书桌。他尚不能宽恕把伪造内容注入记录的文书，但他的推理合乎逻辑：一个缺失标记，如同省略号，邀请调查而非确定。

He sent the bailiff to **prowl** the rear trail while remaining **impassive** through the **drudgery** of inventories. Nera’s **encyclopedic** recollection caught a **convex** buckle stamped with the dam's **magnitude**. Its owner pleaded **propriety**, claiming he had attended a **choral** supper. But his **indolence** had left mud from the **bulrush** bank on the buckle.

他派法警巡查后方小径，自己在清点的苦差中保持冷静。内拉百科全书般的记忆发现一枚凸扣，上面压着水坝规模的标记。它的主人以得体为由辩称自己参加了合唱晚餐。但他的懒散在扣上留下了香蒲岸边的泥。

No **mobocracy** would decide the case, Vale said, even though a rude **colloquialism** passed through the crowd. The **monotonous** rain made the dam’s **grandeur** seem **barbarous**. A lantern **luminary** and a brass **sextant** helped Nera demonstrate that the altered survey was **monochromatic**, copied in a single night. Croft could not **abide** that conclusion or **habituate** himself to its consequence.

维尔说，不会由暴民统治决定案件，尽管粗俗俗语在人群中传开。单调的雨让水坝的宏伟显得野蛮。一盏灯具和一架黄铜六分仪帮助内拉证明，改过的勘测图是单色的，在一夜间抄成。克罗夫特无法忍受那个结论，也无法习惯其后果。

At the channel, fresh **sediment** led them to an **impresario** who had been hired to **entice** laborers with music and **garnish** their wages with false bonuses. His testimony showed the **detriment** of Croft’s scheme. The court found forged **padding** in every pay roll and ordered the immediate **discharge** of the gate crew, **whereupon** a near **mishap** became a rescue: workers stopped the release in time.

在水道处，新鲜沉积物把他们引向一名被雇来用音乐引诱劳工、以虚假奖金装点工资的演出经理。他的证词展现了克罗夫特阴谋的损害。法庭在每份工资单中发现伪造的填充款，命令立即解雇闸门班组；于是，一场险些发生的事故变成救援：工人及时停止放水。

Vale moved to **establish** a temporary water trust. A midwife testified that the diversion had nearly interrupted a difficult **parturition**, leaving a mother in **psychosis**. Her husband, once a boxing **champ**, stood at the reservoir’s **brink** beside the **grove** and watched deputies **frisk** Croft’s agent. The search exposed the **illiberality** of a hidden **prop** used to stage the supposed accident.

维尔着手建立临时水资源信托。一名助产士作证，改道险些打断一次困难分娩，令母亲陷入精神病状态。她的丈夫曾是拳击冠军，站在水库边缘的树林旁，看着副警长搜查克罗夫特的代理人。搜查揭露一个隐藏道具的狭隘用意，它被用于摆演所谓事故。

When the water began to **recede**, farm **activism** found a disciplined voice. The judge promised to **circumnavigate** no legal requirement. The company’s **stock** had fallen into **flux**; Croft blamed a woman whose **maidenhood** had been spent at the mill. Yet the county **hierarchy** had no place for his **prognathous** sneer. From a broader **perspective**, Nera said, the dam's false readings spun like a **gyroscope**.

当水开始退去，农场行动主义找到了有纪律的声音。法官承诺不会绕过任何法律要求。公司的股票陷入波动；克罗夫特责怪一名少女时代在磨坊度过的女子。然而县级等级体系容不下他下巴突出的讥笑。内拉说，从更广的视角看，水坝的虚假读数像陀螺般旋转。

There were **innumerable** small lies, but at this **juncture** one mattered most: Croft's **lackey** had carried a false deed from the print shop. The court named it **dishonor**, not cleverness. Croft’s **relentless** resistance left him **enervated**, and his only **optimum** strategy would have been candor. Instead he had tried to **macadamize** the access road with stolen stone and displayed pure **wantonness** toward the farms.

小谎言不计其数，但在这一关头有一件最重要：克罗夫特的走卒从印刷店带来了假契据。法庭称其为不名誉，而非聪明。克罗夫特无休止的抵抗使他精疲力竭，唯一最优策略本应是坦白。相反，他企图用偷来的石头铺设碎石路，对农场表现出纯粹的肆意妄为。

The **prospective** reservoir investors had used an **intermediary** to **furbish** the records. Nera found a **prehensile** metal clip in a sealed **packet**, bearing the crest of Croft's **entourage**. That plain object carried more **vitality** than the company’s polished **veneer** of respectability. Its clasp held an **amorphous** smear of seal gum and caught on the **crook** of the agent's walking stick.

潜在的水库投资者通过中间人粉饰记录。内拉在密封包裹中发现一个可抓握的金属夹，上有克罗夫特随从团的纹章。那个朴素物品比公司抛光的体面外表更有生命力。其扣环留有一团无定形封胶，挂在代理人手杖弯钩上。

An **indiscreet** servant admitted that, on a **bleak** evening, Croft had worn an **auburn** coat while drafting a **blueprint** for the flood. The plan was **flagrant** evidence of bad faith. Vale declared that **equity** must **precede** profit. He ordered a clerk to **compress** the wet papers and gave Nera a quiet **nudge** to read the document's **preface** aloud.

一名不谨慎的仆人承认，在一个凄冷夜晚，克罗夫特穿着赤褐色外套起草洪水蓝图。该计划是恶意的明显证据。维尔宣布，公平必须先于利润。他命一名文书压缩湿纸，并轻轻示意内拉朗读文件前言。

It described Croft's **compulsive** urge to possess the spring. Though the ink was **hazy**, Nera recognized a scratched **hieroglyph** used to **distract** auditors. The company accountant, a **guileless** young man, explained that Croft intended to **abrogate** the compact after a **momentary** diversion. The judge asked whether the scheme was **psychopathic**; the accountant answered that it had begun in fevered **optimism** and ended in a desperate **wrangle**.

它描述克罗夫特强迫性地占有泉水的冲动。尽管墨迹模糊，内拉认出一个刻痕象形符号，用于分散审计员注意。公司会计是一名诚实的年轻人，他解释克罗夫特计划在短暂改道后废除协定。法官问此阴谋是否精神病态；会计回答，它始于狂热乐观，终于绝望争吵。

The deed called Dalia’s claim **despicable**, but the hidden survey showed a deep **furrow** cut from her mill to the dam. Croft’s physician, a noted **hypochondriac**, had invented the camp illness. A former **privateer** who owned the riverboat supplied a **zesty** detail: sacks of red clay had been moved at night for **redistribution** along the banks. That work was ordered to **suspend** the true water marks.

契据称达莉娅的主张卑劣，但隐藏勘测显示从她磨坊到水坝挖出一道深沟。克罗夫特的医生是著名疑病症患者，他编造了营地疾病。一名拥有河船的前私掠者提供生动细节：红土袋在夜间被搬动，以沿岸重新分布。那项工作受命暂停真实水位标记。

Croft grew **bilious** when the judge called the danger **dire**. “Your **gripe** is merely **amorous** loyalty to a mill,” he told Dalia. The **affected** phrase could not **uncouch** the truth. Even the **disaffected** workers saw the proof as **indivertible**. Vale said the court could **adapt** its remedy without making it **inoffensive** to wrongdoing.

法官称危险严峻时，克罗夫特变得暴躁。“你的抱怨不过是对磨坊多情的忠诚，”他对达莉娅说。这句矫揉造作的话无法揭露真相。即便心怀不满的工人也认为证据不可偏移。维尔说，法庭可以调整补救措施，而不必让它对不法行为显得无害。

He scheduled a **biennial** audit, then faced the **throng** outside after the verdict. There was no **fete**. Deputies found Croft trying to **lurk** beneath the bridge, relying on his old **charisma** while the river began to **congeal** with silt. In the **wake** of his arrest, the company’s power seemed to **atrophy**. A nervous **bowler** from the capital called the prosecution **trifling** until he saw the **atrocious** camp ledger.

他安排两年一次审计，判决后面对外面的人群。没有庆典。副警长发现克罗夫特试图潜伏桥下，依靠旧日魅力，而河水开始因淤泥凝结。在他被捕之后，公司的权力似乎萎缩。一名来自首都、神经紧张的保龄球手称起诉微不足道，直到看见可怕的营地账册。

The court followed the **estuary** road to identify the dead workers. Dalia gave each family a small **keepsake**, though compensation could never make grief less **galore**. The **felon** clerk confessed that the forged seal had required weeks of **incubation** in a warm pressroom. Deputies would **traverse** the county with warrants; the judge's stance remained **defensive** only of the innocent.

法庭沿河口道路去辨认死亡工人。达莉娅给每个家庭一件小纪念品，尽管赔偿永远无法减少丰沛的悲伤。重罪文书承认，伪造封印需要在温暖印刷间中酝酿数周。副警长会带着搜查令穿行全县；法官的防御立场只保护无辜者。

The court had to **amputate** part of the dam from its unfinished wall. Nera could **parse** every payment now, and the proof showed Croft meant to **wreak** ruin, not merely **misapprehend** a boundary. His claimed **betrothal** to Celia had been a **despotic** bargain for her uncle’s shares. Her **covert** letter helped the prosecutor **enlist** the last resistant juror.

法庭不得不从未完工的坝墙切除一部分水坝。现在内拉能解析每笔付款，证据显示克罗夫特意在造成毁灭，而非仅误解边界。他声称与塞莉娅订婚，实则是为她叔父股份达成的专横交易。她的秘密信件帮助检察官争取到最后一名抵触陪审员。

At sentencing, rain gave the restored stream a clean **luster**. Dalia spoke with **trepidation**, yet her voice was **supple** enough to **trigger** silence. She thanked Nera for following the **intricacy** of a case others wanted buried in an **obituary**. Croft offered a **grudging** confession and asked that no **posthumous** stain touch his name.

宣判时，雨让恢复的溪流泛出干净光泽。达莉娅带着颤栗发言，但她的声音足够柔韧，能引发沉默。她感谢内拉追随了一个他人想埋进讣告的复杂案件。克罗夫特不情愿地认罪，并请求死后不要有污名沾上他的名字。

Vale found his remorse **scant**. The law could not **hide** what he had done, nor could wealth make consequence **resistless** in his favor. The court restored Dalia's water-right, ordered repair of the channel, and instituted wages and care for the workers. A **belated** **altruist** from the capital funded the initial repairs. As the traveling bench departed, Bellwether's renewed river shone with a plain, durable **esthetic**.

维尔认为他的悔意微薄。法律无法隐藏他的所作所为，财富也不能让后果无可抗拒地偏向他。法庭恢复达莉娅的水权，命令修复水道，并为工人设立工资和照护。一名迟来的首都利他者资助了初步修复。巡回法庭离去时，贝尔韦瑟重获生机的河流闪耀着朴素而持久的美感。
