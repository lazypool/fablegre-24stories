## Story for Chunk 015 - Lighthouse Debt

Anwen Bell, the island's **oculist**, spent her mornings testing fishermen's eyes and her afternoons tending Bellrock Light. The council had received a perfect **facsimile** of the bank's notice, though its delivery had been **clandestine**. Her father had needed **analgesia** after the fall that ended his watch, and now the town's **diversity** of families faced the same cold **compulsion**: pay the arrears or lose the beacon. Anwen read every line aloud, even its absurd **syllabication**, while an hour seemed to **elapse** between each gust. She had come to **abhor** the investor's **narcissism**. His offer was **lopsided**: surrender the tower, and he would erase a **natal** debt supposedly inherited at her birth.

岛上的眼科医生安温·贝尔恩，上午为渔民检查视力，下午照料贝尔罗克灯塔。议会收到了一份与银行通知完全相同的副本，尽管它是秘密送达的。父亲在结束守灯生涯的那场坠落后需要止痛，如今镇上各式各样的家庭都面临同样冷酷的强迫：偿清欠款，否则失去灯塔。安温逐行朗读，连荒谬的分音也不放过，每阵风之间仿佛过了一小时。她渐渐厌恶投资人的自恋。他的提议极不公平：交出高塔，他便抹去据称在她出生时继承的先天债务。

His agent, a **buxom** woman in a green coat, called the debt **imaginary** with a smile that made Anwen think of a **semicivilized** court. Her voice was **labored**, but her appeal to **religion** was smooth: Bellrock was a **hereditary** burden, she said, and Anwen should be grateful. The agent's **supercilious** assistant, an **executive** from the mainland, watched Anwen **clamber** to the lantern room. Anwen could not **conceive** why a debt collector cared about a light until anger near **apoplexy** made her **analyze** the figures. A **coincidence** troubled her: every payment had been timed at the moon's **equipoise**, yet the ledger described an **abnormal** service fee.

她的代理人是一名穿绿外套、身材丰满的女人，她笑着说债务是虚构的，让安温想起半开化的法庭。她说话吃力，但诉诸宗教时却很流畅：她说贝尔罗克是世袭负担，安温应当感激。代理人傲慢的助手，一名来自大陆的高管，看着安温爬上灯室。安温无法理解收债人为何关心一盏灯，直到近乎中风的怒气使她分析数字。一个巧合令她不安：每笔付款都安排在月亮平衡之时，账册却记有异常服务费。

The claim might have been a **hallucination**, except Father kept a bottle of **paregoric** beside the account book and had marked one page: "The reef is **treacherous**; keep the light **mandatory**." Anwen found the agent's **natty** signature beneath it. She had begun to **dote** on her father's stubborn notes, pinned beside an old **easel** holding charts of an earlier **invasion** by private salvagers. The new offer meant to **seduce** her with money, but it felt like an **audition** for betrayal. Her father's death had been **traumatic**; the agent's **captious** corrections to every question led Anwen to issue a public **proclamation** from the quay.

这项主张或许像幻觉，若非父亲在账本旁留着一瓶止咳鸦片酊，并在一页上标记：“礁石危险；必须保持灯光。”安温在下面发现代理人利落的签名。她已开始珍爱父亲固执的笔记，它们钉在一只旧画架旁，画架上有私人打捞者早年入侵的图表。新提议意在用金钱诱惑她，却像一场背叛的试演。父亲的死很创伤；代理人对每个问题吹毛求疵的纠正，使安温在码头发布公开声明。

Beyond the keeper's garden, the ruined **apiary** hummed. The bank claimed **intestacy**, saying Father had died without heirs. Anwen saw a boot **scuff** the path toward the shed, where an **internecine** quarrel between the lender's men had left a torn invoice. One man called another a poor **husband** of funds. In a tin she found a **potpourri** of receipts, enough to **accept** as evidence if she could **meet** the storm boat before it left. She asked the dockmaster to **circulate** copies among the village. A **vagabond** sailor said he had seen the agent at the **cemetery**, speaking with Anwen's **divorced** uncle.

守灯人的花园外，毁坏的蜂场仍嗡嗡作响。银行声称无遗嘱继承，说父亲去世时没有继承人。安温看见一只靴子蹭过通往棚屋的小路，放贷人手下的一场内讧在那里留下撕裂的发票。一人说另一人不善管理资金。她在铁盒中找到一杂堆收据，若能赶在风暴船离开前登船，便足以作为证据。她请码头长向全村传阅副本。一名流浪水手说，他曾在墓地看见代理人与安温离异的叔叔交谈。

Anwen tried to **simplify** the proof. The tower's **numinous** lens had survived a century because of its **resilience**; its brass **scabbard** and six-sided **hexagon** housing bore a maker's stamp. A truck's **bumper** had struck the supply gate during an **intensive** night visit. Mrs. Solly showed genuine **solicitude**, pressing tea on Anwen to **stimulate** her thoughts. "Closing it would be **irreparable**," she said. In the keepers' **lounge**, Anwen rejected the **inanity** of the bank's story and inventoried Father’s **paraphernalia** under the **stygian** stair.

安温试图简化证据。灯塔神圣般的透镜因韧性历经百年；它的黄铜护套和六边形外壳带着制造者印记。一辆卡车的保险杠曾在一次密集的夜访中撞到补给门。索利太太真诚关怀，硬塞给安温一杯茶以激发思路。“关闭它会造成无法弥补的后果。”她说。在守灯人的休息室，安温拒绝银行荒唐的说法，在冥暗楼梯下清点父亲的用具。

There, a child's **exuberance** in a faded photograph clashed with the **unmitigated** salt smell. A grain of sand was an **irritant** in the binding. Father had rejected **primogeniture** as the basis for ownership; the lender used it to **obstruct** her claim. When she rubbed the paper, an **effervescent** wash exposed a second ink. The room's **ambience** seemed to **ruffle** as lamplight began to **refract** through the wet page. The original agreement required local **conformity** with a maritime trust, not payment to a banker. Its language invoked the **gravity** of a beacon, forbade anyone to **deforest** the cliff, and warned the keeper never to **loll** while wreckers came to **loot** it.

那里，一张褪色照片中孩子的活力与浓烈的咸味相冲突。装订中一粒沙子成了刺激物。父亲拒绝以长子继承权作为所有权基础；放贷人却用它阻碍她的主张。她摩擦纸面时，一层起泡般的洗液显出第二种墨迹。灯光穿过湿页开始折射，房间的氛围仿佛被拂动。原始协议要求地方遵从海事信托，而非向银行家付款。其文字强调灯塔的重要，禁止任何人砍伐悬崖，并警告守灯人绝不可在劫掠者来抢夺时闲散。

That public trust was Bellrock's **hallmark**, meant to **unify** the coast. It was **apropos** of a marginal compass sketch that Anwen noticed a **medial** column of figures the bank had tried to **elude**. A nineteenth-century **lawgiver** had granted the light to every **outgoing** generation, excluding private **taxidermy**, commercial **vulgarity**, and any **pedestrian** toll. Father had hidden the original seal in a jar of **condiment**, behind a **carafe**. Even a less **discerning** keeper would know the seal was **unique**. Anwen ran to the pier as the **tide** rose.

这项公共信托是贝尔罗克的标志，意在团结海岸。安温从边缘的一幅罗盘草图想到此事，注意到银行试图回避的一列中间数字。十九世纪的立法者把灯塔授予每一代离去又到来的居民，排除了私人标本制作、商业庸俗行为和任何行人收费。父亲把原始印章藏在调味品罐里，放在玻璃水瓶后。即使不那么敏锐的守灯人也会知道那枚印章独一无二。潮水上涨时，安温跑向码头。

The agent tried to **moralize** about duty while her men **mash** tar into the road. She promised Anwen a city **luxury**, but her **saturnine** driver recited a **litany** of threats. They would **importune** every creditor; a **horde** of inspectors would arrive by dawn. Anwen answered with an **antistrophe** of her own: the light belonged to those it saved. The driver whispered of a planned **mutiny** aboard the relief cutter and a **synthetic** emergency signal meant to justify seizure. The threat deserved **condign** punishment, and the weather made action **urgent**. The plot had seemed **inapprehensible** until the evidence began to **burgeon**.

代理人一边谈论责任道德，一边让手下把焦油捣进路面。她许诺给安温城市奢华生活，但她阴沉的司机念出一长串威胁。他们会纠缠每位债主；一大群检查员将在黎明前抵达。安温以自己的回环句回应：灯塔属于被它拯救的人。司机低声说，救援快艇上策划了叛变，还准备了合成的紧急信号，以证明没收合理。威胁应受应有惩罚，天气使行动刻不容缓。阴谋起初难以理解，直到证据不断涌现。

At low water, a stranded **crustacean** clicked beside a crate. Anwen would not **relinquish** the crate to the agent's **outfit**. With **perseverance**, she pried it open and found a dead radio operator's statement addressed to his **confessor**. A company **censor** had ordered him to **withhold** reports of false lamps. The smugglers planned to **remodel** Bellrock's **venerable** optics into a signal for their ships. Anwen made a **grimace** at the corroded **metal**, the **filth**, and a packet of **sudorific** powder used to make a feverish guard look ill.

低潮时，一只搁浅的甲壳动物在板条箱旁咔嗒作响。安温不肯把箱子交给代理人的团伙。她坚持撬开箱子，发现一名死去无线电报务员写给忏悔神父的陈述。公司审查员曾命令他隐瞒假灯报告。走私者计划把贝尔罗克古老的光学设备改成他们船只的信号。安温对腐蚀的金属、污秽以及一包用来让发烧守卫显得病重的发汗粉皱起脸。

The crate's **bevel** hid a compartment. A **snicker** came from outside, and a **hapless** clerk stumbled in, chased by rain. He was a **philologist** hired to draft the bank's **emendation** to the old charter. His sister's **nuptial** ring had been taken as leverage. They chose to **amble** through the papers, searching for the **instance** when the trust had been altered. When they found it, the clerk was **euphoric**: the forged clause carried a legislative **veto** mark from a year before the law existed.

箱子的斜面藏着一个隔层。外面传来窃笑，一个倒霉的文员被雨追着跌撞进来。他是一名语文学家，受雇起草银行对旧章程的修订。他姐姐的婚戒被拿走作为胁迫。他们慢慢翻阅文件，寻找信托被改动的那一处。找到后，文员欣喜若狂：伪造条款带着一枚立法否决标记，而它来自法律出现前一年。

The relief **crew** arrived after dark, and the agent offered her final **bargain**. Anwen could take money and make a quiet **hegira** from Bellrock, she said, or remain **seclude** in the tower while the storm did its **laborious** work. The villagers' **disfavor** of the banker filled the pier. A sea **miasma** made Father’s old mate, now **infirm**, cough over his **reportage** notes. The agent gave a **grandiloquent** speech about progress, but the mate named her family **vendetta** against the coast. Anwen, too tense to **famish**, pulled on one **moccasin** after another and called the offer an **impropriety**.

救援人员天黑后抵达，代理人提出最后交易。她说安温可以拿钱悄悄离开贝尔罗克，或者留在塔中与世隔绝，任由风暴辛苦地完成工作。村民对银行家的不满充满码头。海上的瘴气使父亲如今体弱的老伙伴对着报道笔记咳嗽。代理人发表一通夸张的进步演说，老伙伴却点出她家族对海岸的报复。安温紧张得无暇饥饿，穿上一只又一只软皮鞋，称那提议不妥。

The agent became **bellicose**. Anwen's partner, an **exemplary** mechanic and former gambling **addict**, refused to **downplay** the danger. "Be **stouthearted**," he told her, as a lightning strike caused a **suspension** of power. The lantern's last **effulgence** glinted over the harbor like **homage** to every keeper. A school **truant** ran up with the clerk's notes; Anwen told him to **wean** himself from fear. The forged accounts were **ambiguous** only until compared with the old **idyll** of payments. Their **inertia** had let the fraud grow. The agent tried to **obfuscate**, but the town's **devoted** harbor master recognized the charter's official **appurtenance**.

代理人变得好战。安温的伴侣是一名模范机械师，也曾沉迷赌博，他拒绝淡化危险。“要勇敢。”雷击造成停电时，他这样告诉她。灯塔最后的光辉在港湾上闪耀，仿佛向每位守灯人致敬。一名逃学孩子带着文员笔记跑来；安温告诉他要戒除恐惧。伪造账目只有在与旧日理想化付款记录对照前才显得模糊。他们的惰性让欺诈滋长。代理人试图混淆视听，但镇上忠诚的港务长认出章程的正式附属印记。

In the kitchen, crates of **condiments** supplied a useful hiding place. The banker had called public ownership a **misnomer**, hoping to **nonplus** the council with legal **mnemonics**. Yet Anwen's **obsession** with her father's routines now mattered. Her conclusion came **secondly**: the ledger's dates showed the agent's **avidity** for a wreck. Outside, a **welter** of white water struck the rocks. Two **divergent** ink samples survived an **ablution** in rain. Anwen heard the agent **berate** her team, whose **insomnia** and **detachment** had frayed their nerve.

厨房里，一箱箱调味品提供了实用藏身处。银行家称公共所有权是用词不当，希望用法律记忆术让议会不知所措。然而安温对父亲日常惯例的执着此时变得重要。她随后得出结论：账册日期显示代理人渴望发生事故。外面一片翻涌白浪拍击岩石。两种不同墨样经雨水冲洗仍留存。安温听见代理人斥责团队，失眠和疏离已使他们心力交瘁。

The dockmaster gave no **oration**; he gave Anwen **carte** blanche to board the cutter. In its **forepeak**, she found the false lamp battery pointed **forward**. To **err** now would drown the fishing fleet. A carved **seraph** on the radio case reminded her of Father's warning against **procrastination**. The clerk uncovered a **lagniappe**: a signed memo showing the bank had begun to **accumulate** forged charges from the first wreck. Rain-driven **detritus** swept over the deck. Anwen said, "I **detest** coercion," and the mechanic named the lender's **faction** before the gathered crews.

码头长没有发表演说；他全权允许安温登上快艇。在艇首舱，她发现假灯电池朝前安装。此刻犯错会淹没捕鱼船队。无线电盒上一尊雕刻天使提醒她父亲反对拖延的警告。文员又发现额外线索：一份签署备忘录表明，银行从第一次沉船起就开始累积伪造费用。雨驱动的碎屑扫过甲板。安温说：“我厌恶胁迫。”机械师则在聚集的船员面前点出放贷人的派系。

The banker answered in a **pedantic** tone that the old **edifice** could not belong to everyone. The fishers answered with **solidarity**. Their plan was **economical**: restore the generator, broadcast the proof, and call the coast guard. The agent's **assumption** that no one had read the charter was an **extensive** error. The town librarian, a contract **maven**, recognized the paper's **velvety** watermark. She became Anwen's **ally**, taking a **dart** of wire to reconnect the radio while Anwen solved the remaining **puzzle**.

银行家以迂腐口吻说，老建筑不可能属于所有人。渔民以团结回应。他们的计划很经济：修复发电机、广播证据并呼叫海岸警卫队。代理人以为无人读过章程，这是一项广泛而严重的错误。镇图书管理员是合同行家，认出纸张柔滑的水印。她成了安温的盟友，拿着一根导线飞快接回无线电，而安温解决剩余谜题。

It was **politic** to keep the evidence public. The bank's corporate **status** depended on hiding the tower's **degradation**. The agent had used fake **divination** reports to claim the lens would **dilate** cracks in bad weather, relying on the council's **naivety**. Her plan was not **avantgarde** innovation but a **virtual** foreclosure, with the tower as **collateral** for invented debt. The tower stood **monolithic** against the gale; ropes **entwine** around the rail where smugglers meant to **cavort** after the wreck. A **pathogenic** mold in the office had helped **deject** Father during his final weeks.

让证据保持公开是明智的。银行的公司地位依赖于掩盖灯塔的破败。代理人利用虚假的预测报告，称透镜会在坏天气中扩大裂缝，倚仗议会的天真。她的计划不是前卫创新，而是虚拟止赎，以灯塔作为虚构债务的抵押。灯塔如整体巨石般对抗狂风；走私者打算在事故后狂欢的护栏上，绳索缠绕。办公室里的致病霉菌曾让父亲在最后几周消沉。

The radio gave a **sibilant** cough. Its charge was **negligible**, and the mechanic's **tatty** gloves slipped on the terminals. The agent began to **declaim** that an **invasive** federal **battalion** would occupy Bellrock. Her **enmity** toward the town exposed an **antilogy**: she claimed safety while steering boats toward danger. A **mite** of copper from the antenna fixed the circuit. The librarian, an **academic** by habit, found the agent's **malice** in the altered serial numbers. Anwen held the charter **upright** to the lamp; the paper's **workmanship** made the alteration visible without **melodrama**.

无线电发出嘶嘶的咳声。电量微不足道，机械师破旧的手套在接线端上打滑。代理人开始高声宣称，入侵性的联邦部队将占领贝尔罗克。她对镇子的敌意暴露了矛盾：她声称为了安全，却把船引向危险。天线上一点铜屑修好了电路。习惯于学术工作的图书管理员在被改动的序列号中发现代理人的恶意。安温把章程竖在灯前；纸张的工艺让篡改变得明显，无需夸张。

The agent was **refractory**, but a small bronze **faun** on the seal pointed to a hidden gauge. Anwen measured an **inch** of fresh scratch marks. The coming **anticyclone** had delayed the storm long enough for a **systematic** theft. She felt **melancholy** for Father, who had trusted the **papacy** of banks less than the sea. Her shiver was **involuntary**, not an **escapade**. In the tower's **reclusory** archives, a **capacious** drawer held the **normative** maintenance log and proof of the agent's **treachery**.

代理人顽固不化，但印章上的一只小铜牧神指向隐藏刻度表。安温量出一英寸新的抓痕。即将到来的反气旋让风暴延后足够久，以进行系统盗窃。她为父亲感到忧伤，他对银行教皇般权威的信任远少于大海。她的颤抖不由自主，并非一时冒险。在塔中隐居式档案室，一个宽大抽屉放着标准维护日志和代理人背叛的证据。

The log lay beneath clear **acetate**, next to a cold **tortilla** left by the night watch. It recorded how the false lamp would make ships **excrete** ballast near the reef. An **epitaph** for a drowned keeper had been used to **pillory** Father as careless. Anwen found a vial of **antitoxin** intended for poisoned dogs and felt an **inexpressible** certainty: the storm was timed. During a rescue **rehearsal**, the lender's men would **cull** witnesses. The clerk prepared a **collation** of documents showing that **anthropocentrism** had no place in a law written for sea and shore.

日志放在透明醋酸片下，旁边有夜班人留下的一张冷玉米饼。它记录假灯将如何让船只在礁石旁排出压舱物。一段为溺水守灯人写的墓志铭被用来把父亲钉成粗心之人。安温发现一瓶给中毒狗用的抗毒素，感到一种无法言说的确定：风暴经过精确安排。在一次救援演练中，放贷人的手下将清除证人。文员整理文件，显示为海洋与海岸写成的法律中没有人类中心主义的位置。

The charter promised **redemption** through service, not profit. Beneath the lens **pedestal**, Anwen found the missing page and its named **occupant**: the Bellrock community, in every **season**. A child's red **legging** had caught on the drawer. The agent's faith in money was **idolatrious**; she seemed to **adore** its **lurid** power. But the page alleged attempted **matricide** against the town's founding trust. Its official stamp was **ovoid**, its clerk merely **fallible**, yet a **surreptitious** notation ordered the coast guard to **elevate** any claim of sabotage.

章程承诺通过服务而非利润获得救赎。安温在透镜底座下找到失页及其指定归属者：每个季节里的贝尔罗克社区。一只孩子的红色裤腿挂在抽屉上。代理人对金钱的信仰近乎偶像崇拜；她似乎迷恋它刺眼的权力。但该页指控有人企图杀害镇子创设信托。它的官方印记呈卵形，文员也只是会犯错，然而一条秘密批注命令海岸警卫队重视任何破坏指控。

The agent called the evidence **inconclusive**. Then an **abrupt** flash revealed the **luminous** harbor signal blinking from the cutter, not the lighthouse.
