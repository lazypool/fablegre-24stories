## Story for Chunk 023 - Borrowed Garden

At dawn, Nara Pell, a curator of **horticulture** at the old Marrow Garden, found a **shopworn** suitcase beneath a **frond** of the palm house. Its **varnish** was cracked, its brass corners green, and its contents were an **apt** answer to the director's polished announcement: a **pristine** packet of seeds, a **concise** field note, and a photograph of a minaret above a river that villagers once crossed by **ford**. Nara was **jaded** by ceremonies, but the image made her stop. The director, a **loquacious** man of **worldly** manners, had declared the collection a "**paradise** of native genius" and planned to **designate** the seeds for a donor's private conservatory.

黎明时，老马罗花园的园艺馆长娜拉·佩尔在棕榈温室一片叶子下发现了一只陈旧的手提箱。箱面清漆开裂，黄铜边角泛绿，里面的东西恰好回答了馆长那场光鲜的公告：一包洁净如新的种子，一页简洁的田野笔记，还有一张照片，照片里一座光塔矗立在河边，村民们曾从浅滩涉水过河。娜拉早已厌倦各种仪式，但那张照片让她停下了脚步。馆长是个话很多、举止世故的人，他把这批收藏宣布为"本土天才的乐园"，并计划把种子指定给一位捐赠人的私人温室。

He was **oblivious** to the label in the old script, which said the seeds had been gathered from a garden belonging to Salim Daryan's grandmother. Salim, the garden's quiet foster gardener, stood beside Nara with an **artlessness** that concealed a headlong anger. "The **gist** is simple," he said. "They were taken, not given." The director had a **warrant** from the trustees to **repatriate** Salim if he challenged the transfer. He called the order a **commonsensical** compromise, though its real purpose was to **defraud** Salim of any claim and **deforest** the old garden's memory before anyone could **fathom** it. "**please**," he said, "do not make this intrigue into a public quarrel."

他没有意识到旧文字标签上的内容，标签写着这些种子采自萨利姆·达里安祖母的花园。萨利姆是这座花园安静的养子园丁，他的天真坦率掩盖着一股鲁莽的怒火，站在娜拉身边。"要点很简单，"他说，"它们不是被赠送的，是被拿走的。"董事会给馆长发了一份授权，如果萨利姆反对转移，就把他遣返回国。他把这项命令称作符合常识的妥协，可它真正的目的，是用欺骗剥夺萨利姆提出主张的权利，在任何人来得及弄清真相前，把老花园的记忆从土地上砍掉。"拜托，"他说，"请不要把这场阴谋变成公开争吵。"

His assistant, a **subordinate** with a **timorous** smile, tried to **mask** the **fraudulence** of the plan with a shopworn appeal to science. Nara had a disinterested duty to the collection, but not to its subterfuge. She opened the packet and saw a **parasite** curled among the seeds, a living **mimicry** of a dried root. The director wanted to **overemphasize** its rarity, a **phenomenal** specimen that would please the donor. Nara's objection was blunt: "The garden is not a **cache** for your reputation."

他的助手是个服从上级、笑容胆怯的下属，试图用陈旧的科学诉求掩饰计划中的欺诈。娜拉对这批收藏负有无私的职责，却不负责替它的借口效劳。她打开种子包，看见一只寄生虫盘绕在种子之间，活像一截干枯根茎的模仿物。馆长想过分强调它的稀有性，说这是一件惊人的标本，足以讨捐赠人的欢心。娜拉的回答直截了当："这座花园不是你用来藏匿名声的仓库。"

At noon, a martial brass band arrived for the donor's **debut** tour, and the director began to **brandish** a silver trowel as if he were a **gladiator** of progress. The band played an **impromptu** march beneath the glass roof; its **boisterous** brass made the palm leaves **oscillate**. A rumor began to **bruit** through the guests that Salim was a **xenophobe**, a **renegade** who hated the country that had given him work. The rumor was a subterfuge designed to **disparage** him and **hinder** his appeal. Salim's face went pale, but he did not **flounder**.

中午时，一支尚武气息十足的铜管乐队为捐赠人的首次参观而来，馆长挥舞着一把银色小铲，仿佛自己是进步的角斗士。乐队在玻璃屋顶下即兴演奏进行曲，喧闹的铜管让棕榈叶来回摆动。宾客中开始传播一则谣言，说萨利姆是个憎恨外国人的人，是一个背叛者，憎恨那个给他工作机会的国家。这则谣言是一种托辞，目的是贬低他，阻碍他的申诉。萨利姆脸色苍白，却没有在困境中手足无措。

He said the director had used mimicry before, copying his grandmother's planting diagrams and passing them off as an **exemplar** of European ingenuity. Nara asked him to **substantiate** this. He led her to a **corrugated** shed, where an **unruly** vine began to **entangle** itself around a **ferrous** box. Inside lay letters, a **mattress** of old receipts, and a photograph whose **variegation** of ink showed three hands had written the same account. The letters could **convey** the order in which the seeds were stolen, but the final page had been torn **asunder**.

他说馆长以前也用过模仿的手法，抄袭他祖母的种植图，把它们冒充成欧洲才智的典范。娜拉要他证实此事。他把她带到一间波纹铁皮棚，一株纠缠的藤蔓缠绕着一个含铁箱子。里面有信件、一叠旧收据和一张照片，墨迹的斑驳显示同一份记录由三个人写过。信件能够传达种子被盗的次序，可最后一页已经被撕成两半。

The director arrived **headlong**, **irate** and **impudent**, with the donor's **sycophant** behind him. He tried to **detain** the box and **obliterate** the letters, but Nara stepped between them. "Your **stature** does not warrant this," she said. "Nor does your **largesse**." He laughed and brandished the trowel again. Salim made a quick evasive movement, seized a **martial** cane from the band leader, and **disarm**ed him without striking. The movement was almost **humble**, and it made the director's **euphoria** **deflate**.

馆长怒气冲冲、鲁莽无礼地冲了过来，捐赠人的谄媚者跟在他身后。他试图扣押箱子并抹去信件，可娜拉挡在两人之间。"你的身份并不能证明你有这种权力，"她说，"你的慷慨赠与也不能。"他笑了，又挥起小铲。萨利姆迅速一闪，从乐队领队手里夺过一根军用手杖，在没有击打的情况下解除了他的武装。这个动作近乎谦逊，也让馆长的兴奋感泄了气。

Nara saw the whole intrigue **diverge** from its intended path. She could **denounce** the director, but a public scene would let him call the evidence stolen. Instead, she asked the donor to **assent** to an impromptu hearing in the palm house. The donor, a **myopic** man who could not see beyond his own collection, refused until the parasite began to **dilate** and exude a **noxious** smell. Then he yielded. Nara read the letters with **celerity**, and the director's **slipshod** records could not **gainsay** them. The hearing did not produce a clean victory. The director admitted a **shortcoming**, blamed an **adventitious** assistant, and tried to excuse the theft as a mild **lapse**. Nara would not let him **put** his own **stricture** on the truth.

娜拉看见整场阴谋正在偏离原来的方向。她可以谴责馆长，但公开场面会让他反过来说证据是偷来的。于是她请捐赠人在棕榈温室里同意举行一场即兴听证。捐赠人目光短浅，看不见自己的收藏之外的世界，起初拒绝了，直到那只寄生虫开始膨胀，渗出有害的气味，他才让步。娜拉迅速读完信件，馆长潦草的记录再也无法反驳这些证据。听证会并没有带来一个干净利落的胜利。馆长承认存在缺点，随后把责任推给一名偶然卷入的助手，还试图把盗窃宽恕成一次轻微的过失。娜拉不允许他把自己的限制强加给真相。

She proposed that the seeds be returned, that the garden be **foster**ed by a trust, and that Salim be free to stay or leave without being **fetter**ed by the warrant. The donor, suddenly **wary**, offered a **profusion** of money, but Salim refused it. "I want the soil, not your largesse," he said. He used a knife to **etch** the family name into the box, then asked Nara to **adapt** the old beds for local growers. The director's face began to **tarnish** beneath the glass. His posture collapsed; his **acrimony** became a **ribald** mutter about foreigners and weeds. Nara told him that such **barbarous** talk could not be excused. He threatened to **constrain** the trust with legal rules, but the donor's **disinterested** lawyer answered that the **precedent** was clear. The garden's **homogeneity** would end; its variegation would be its new exemplar.

她提议归还种子，让一个信托来培育这座花园，并让萨利姆可以自由留下或离开，不再被授权令束缚。捐赠人突然警觉起来，提出一大笔钱，但萨利姆拒绝了。"我想要土地，不要你的慷慨赠与，"他说。他用刀把家族姓氏蚀刻在箱子上，然后请娜拉把旧花圃改造给本地种植者使用。玻璃屋顶下，馆长的脸开始失去光泽。他的姿态垮掉了，尖刻化作关于外国人和杂草的粗俗咕哝。娜拉告诉他，这种野蛮的话不能被原谅。他威胁要用法律条款限制信托，可捐赠人的无私律师回答说，先例已经很清楚。花园的同质性将会终结，多样性会成为它新的典范。

The **intrigue** had become a **subterfuge** exposed by its own evidence. The registrar's **pedant** of a clerk read the revised terms in an **offhand** voice, but Nara heard the gist beneath the legal clause: the garden must **adhere** to its own history. A **disjointed** inventory showed the director had tried to **usurp** seed beds, **impair** local growers, and **stunt** the collection's **endemic** varieties. His **sloppy** accounts charged visitors for private **cuisine** and **dessert** tastings.

这场阴谋已经变成一个因自身证据而暴露的托辞。注册员手下那个书呆子般的职员用随口的语气宣读修订后的条款，可娜拉听见了法律条款下的要点：花园必须坚持自己的历史。一份脱节的清单显示，馆长曾试图篡夺种植床，削弱本地种植者，并阻碍这些地方特有品种的生长。他潦草的账目向游客收取私人烹饪课程和餐后甜点品尝费。

"We will **inter** the contract," Nara said, "and **requite** the people who kept these plants alive." The lawyer found a **tenuous** loophole but could not **thwart** the settlement. Salim's **ingrained** suspicion loosened; he gave a small **chuckle** and called the director's **invective** a **parable** of fear. No one could **condone** the attempted **revenge** against him, but no one needed a stunt of forgiveness. The director would receive his **merited** dismissal, and the garden would **improve** through patient work, not a **rapacious** owner's largesse.

"我们要把这份合同埋葬，"娜拉说，"也要报答那些让这些植物活下来的人。"律师找到一条脆弱的漏洞，却无法阻挠和解。萨利姆根深蒂固的怀疑开始松动，他轻轻笑了一声，说馆长的辱骂是一则关于恐惧的寓言。没有人能够宽恕针对他的报复企图，但也没有必要表演一个宽恕的特技。馆长将得到应得的解职，花园将依靠耐心的劳动改善，而不是依靠贪婪的所有者施舍。

The lawyer's **exacting** final clause was meant to **nip** every future claim, but Nara found one **precipice** in its wording: no one could **belie** the village's ownership. An **obedient** signature would not suffice. Salim asked whether a **beneficent** trust could **corroborate** the planting records, and whether the garden's **latitude** would **saturate** the soil with local varieties. The lawyer assented.

律师最后那条苛刻的条款原本想剪断未来所有的主张，可娜拉在措辞中发现了一处悬崖般的危险：任何人都不能掩饰村民对土地的所有权。一个服从式的签名还不够。萨利姆问，一个仁慈的信托是否可以证实种植记录，花园的纬度范围是否足以让土壤充分吸收本地品种。律师表示同意。

By dusk, workers carried the seeds across the **sand** path to the river plot. A **siren** from the nearby road briefly made the children **plunge** behind a **minaret** wall, but Salim called them back. The old roots would not **ossify** into a museum story. He would **yield** cuttings, cultivate them, and teach the village children their names. Nara watched him **stride** through the beds with **sincere** care that made the director's **counterfeit** benevolence look small. The parasite had died, but its consequences remained in the soil, a warning that foreign beauty could carry noxious guests. Salim's first planting was **heartrending** and **relished** at once: he buried the damaged packet, covered it with clean earth, and placed a **pine** marker above it. No one could gainsay the loss, but no one would obliterate it either. The garden was no paradise. It was a place that had begun to tell the truth.

黄昏时，工人们沿着沙地小路把种子搬到河边的地块。一声来自附近道路的警报器短暂响起，孩子们躲到光塔墙后，但萨利姆把他们叫了回来。那些古老的根不会僵化成博物馆里的故事。他会分发插枝，培育它们，并教村里的孩子记住它们的名字。娜拉看着他迈着大步穿过花圃，那份真挚的爱护让馆长假冒的慈善显得如此微小。寄生虫已经死去，但它的后遗症仍留在土里，提醒人们外来的美丽可能携带有害的客人。萨利姆第一次播种既令人心碎，又充满热烈的享受：他把受损的种子包埋进土里，用干净的泥土覆盖，并在上面立了一根松木标记。没有人能反驳这份损失，但也没有人会将它抹去。这座花园不是天堂，而是一个开始讲述真相的地方。
