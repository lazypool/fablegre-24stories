## Story for Chunk 009 - River Election

At dawn, the old packet *Mercy Belle* pushed away from Port Garland, its iron **bulwark** catching light as a storm began to **precipitate** rain. Dalia Saye fastened the cabin **latch** and checked the campaign's pledge of **temperance**. The river was deceptively **quiet**. Her candidate, schoolmaster Iven Reed, had proposed a **compromise** on the ferry tax; his rival, Alderman Voss, called it **overreach** and promised its **repeal**.

黎明时分，老旧的“仁慈美人号”客轮离开加兰港，暴风雨开始催降雨水，它的铁制舷墙映着晨光。达莉娅·赛耶扣好船舱门闩，并核对竞选团队关于禁酒的承诺。河面看似平静。她支持的候选人、校长艾文·里德提出就渡船税作出折中；对手沃斯议员称此举越权，并承诺将其废除。

Froth made a **frothy** border along the wake while Voss's aide delivered a **mincing** speech. Dalia found him **impersuadable**, dressed in a **panoply** of ribbons. Beyond his shoulder, a pale **wraith** seemed to cross the lower deck, an **anomaly** that brought **perspiration** to the captain's brow. “An **autocrat** would call this voyage a show,” Reed said, accepting an **eatable** pear from a deckhand. “Let the voters **enrapture** themselves with plain facts; let us **interact** with them because it is a **necessity**.”

泡沫沿着尾流形成一道泡沫般的边缘，沃斯的助手则发表了矫揉造作的演说。达莉娅觉得他冥顽不灵，身上披挂着一整套缎带。越过他的肩头，一个苍白的幽灵似乎穿过下层甲板；这一反常景象令船长额头冒汗。“专制者会把这趟航行称作一场表演，”里德说着，接过水手递来的可食用梨子。“让选民被朴素的事实吸引；我们应当与他们交流，因为这是必要之事。”

At the first landing, a **bohemian** fiddler played for the farm **confederacy**. A known **brigand** leaned against a piling, studying a **cognate** word on a **phonetic** campaign poster. Dalia carried Reed's **portfolio** ashore, where the clerk would **denominate** each contribution and add it to a public **network** of ledgers. Two county committees would **conjoin** for the vote, though Dalia suspected an **ulterior** design. A boy in a brass **helmet** pointed toward the hill **ascent**, where the next meeting waited.

在第一处停靠点，一名波希米亚风格的小提琴手为农场联盟演奏。一名出了名的强盗倚着木桩，研究一张按语音拼写的竞选海报上的同源词。达莉娅把里德的文件夹带上岸，书记员将在那里给每笔捐款定名，并把它加入公开账册网络。两个县委员会将为投票联合起来，但达莉娅怀疑另有图谋。一个戴黄铜头盔的男孩指向山坡上的上行路，下一场集会正在那里等候。

Reed's **tentative** greeting drew a **meek** **chuckle** from the crowd. The noon sun could **parch** a mouth over a **picayune** dispute, but a woman with a **viol** asked whether Voss, as the **defendant** in a river suit, had made an **allusion** to a secret **fetich** in his speech. Dalia noticed an **interpolation** in the printed platform: a clause said to be **precedential** that would **palliate** no farmer's loss. It opened a **rift** between Reed's supporters and the town physician, a **cardiologist** who had declared Voss **infallible** after one brief, **fugacious** encounter.

里德试探性的问候引来人群一阵温和的轻笑。正午的太阳足以让人为一场琐细争执而口干舌燥，但一名携带古提琴的女子问道：身为河流诉讼被告的沃斯，是否曾在演说中影射某个秘密迷信物。达莉娅注意到印刷政纲中插入了一段文字：其中一条据称具有先例效力的条款，无法减轻任何农民的损失。这在里德的支持者与镇上心脏科医生之间造成裂痕；那位医生只在一次短暂的会面后便宣称沃斯绝不会错。

“That is **imprudence**,” Dalia said as the boat entered a green **vale**. “We need not call anyone **dissolute** or **demoniacal**; we must not **tolerate** intimidation.” The physician admitted that Voss had offered treatment for his brother's **alcoholism**, paid the boat's **kilowatt** bill, and promised him a new **saddle**. It was a **bribe**, plain enough to **startle** even the physician. Dalia began to **scan** receipts while the cook tended the **grill**. Voss's **opportunism** had reached every **creek** town.

船驶入一片翠绿山谷时，达莉娅说：“那是鲁莽之举。我们不必把任何人称作放荡或恶魔般的人；绝不能容忍恐吓。”医生承认，沃斯曾提出治疗他兄弟的酗酒症，替船付了千瓦电费，还答应送他一副新马鞍。这显然是一笔贿赂，连医生自己都为之震惊。厨师照看烤架时，达莉娅开始查阅收据。沃斯的投机主义已蔓延到每个溪边小镇。

The captain agreed to **instate** a rule: no campaigner could **disarm** a guard or board alone. Dalia felt **ambivalence** about accusing a rival during an **incident** still half-seen. Then, beneath a cargo tarpaulin, she found a painted **sarcophagus** prop from Voss's touring pageant, and inside it a sealed envelope. It was **indubitably** addressed to the **municipal** registrar and demanded a false **surcharge** on ballots. An **appellate** judge's name had been forged beneath a blot of river **spume**.

船长同意确立一项规则：任何竞选人员都不得解除卫兵武装，或独自登船。对在一桩尚未完全看清的事件中指控对手，达莉娅心存矛盾。随后，她在货物油布下发现沃斯巡回演出用的一具彩绘石棺道具，里面有一个密封信封。它毫无疑问是写给市政登记员的，要求对选票虚增附加费。一名上诉法院法官的名字被伪造在一片河水泡沫污渍下方。

Reed asked citizens to **participate** in a deckside hearing. Voss arrived **belligerent**, seeking to **divert** attention with a **parody** of Reed's speeches. Dalia produced the **document** but did not **effuse** outrage. She offered each witness a **succulent** peach and listened. A **stevedore** recalled Voss invoking the **precept** that a candidate may **shed** voters like a **subtrahend** from a sum. His testimony made the alderman appear **profligate**; the crowd's mood swung like a **pendulum**.

里德请市民参与甲板边的听证会。沃斯气势汹汹地赶到，试图以模仿里德演说的滑稽表演转移注意。达莉娅出示文件，却没有肆意表达愤怒。她给每位证人一颗多汁的桃子，认真倾听。一名码头装卸工回忆，沃斯援引的准则是：候选人可以像从总数中减去减数一样抛弃选民。他的证词让这位议员显得挥霍无度；人群情绪像钟摆般摇摆。

Voss tried to **renege** on his promise to answer. “A **shyster** made that paper,” he said. In the humid cabin, the ink began to **deliquesce**, yet the clerk could still **appoint** its origin: an **extemporaneous** note had copied an **indistinct** seal from Voss's own desk. Dalia had the **pluck** to name the discrepancy. A sudden hull **bump** sent a **fawn** of a volunteer into the rail, but her quick help gave him renewed **rejuvenescence**.

沃斯试图违背答应作答的承诺。“那份纸是一名讼棍做的，”他说。潮湿的船舱里，墨水开始潮解，但书记员仍能指认其来源：一张即兴写就的便条仿制了沃斯自己桌上一个模糊的印章。达莉娅有勇气指出其中的差异。船体突然一撞，一名小鹿般的志愿者撞向栏杆；她迅速相助，让他重新恢复了活力。

By evening the water was **equable**. Dalia would not **avulse** a conclusion from a **vacuous** rumor; she needed to **derive** proof from Voss's **antecedents**. In an old register she found an **archaism** beside a map of a **capillary** channel: the notation named the medical charity that had treated the physician's **anemia**. His shame did not **abash** her. The ledger instead revealed a **grisly** pattern: Voss charged each **indigent** patient a hidden **guinea** fee.

到傍晚，水面变得平稳。达莉娅不会从空洞的传闻中强行拔出结论；她需要从沃斯的过往行径中推导证据。在一本旧登记册里，她在一条毛细水道地图旁发现一个古语：该注记写着曾治疗医生贫血的医疗慈善机构。医生的羞愧没有让她局促。账册反而揭示出一个可怖的模式：沃斯向每名贫困病人收取一笔隐蔽的几尼费用。

The campaign had become **episodic**, broken by landings and storms, yet the evidence ended the physician's **immobility**. He described a **pestilential** storehouse where Voss kept citizens **blinkered** with **piddling** favors. Dalia made him **stipulate** every fact without theatrical **hypnotism** or a **jumble** of guesses. Voss's **notoriety** made **conformance** difficult for fearful witnesses. Reed refused to **lambast** them, even when a crate seemed to **plummet** from the upper deck.

竞选因停靠与暴风雨而变得断断续续，但证据结束了医生的僵持。他描述了一座瘟疫般恶臭的仓库，沃斯用微不足道的恩惠蒙蔽市民。达莉娅让他明确陈述每一项事实，不容许戏剧性的催眠或混乱的猜测。沃斯的恶名使害怕的证人难以遵从程序。即使一个箱子似乎要从上层甲板坠落，里德也拒绝痛斥他们。

A **sociable** mechanic caught the rope. He reported the **fomentation** of a private **amour** between Voss's treasurer and the registrar, then apologized after he had begun to **misplace** a key during bad **weather**. Dalia's **hackles** rose. A masked man had threatened to **slay** the mechanic unless he stayed silent. Reed, a lifelong **freethinker**, inspected the broken **enamel** on the mask and found an **incontrovertible** crest used by Voss's river **junta**.

一名和善的机械工接住了绳子。他报告说，沃斯的财务主管与登记员之间有人煽动出私情；随后又因自己在恶劣天气中开始弄丢一把钥匙而道歉。达莉娅警觉起来。一个蒙面人曾威胁要杀死这名机械工，除非他保持沉默。终身主张自由思想的里德检查面具上破损的珐琅，发现了沃斯河上小集团所用、无可争辩的徽记。

The captive mechanic lay **supine** in the infirmary beneath a poster of **britannia**, while Voss, ever **suave**, claimed the matter belonged to **psychiatry**. An **odoriferous** smoke drifted from the engine room, a possible **repercussion** of sabotage. Dalia led the passengers past a **resplendent** figurehead carved as a **simian** saint. At the riverside **seminary**, Sister Ada admitted her **hesitance**: Voss had sent a **ukase** ordering her pupils to praise him. Her **hesitancy** ended when Dalia offered **verification** through the original delivery book.

被扣押的机械工仰面躺在医务室里，身下是一张不列颠女神海报；一贯圆滑的沃斯则声称此事属于精神病学范畴。一阵有气味的烟从机舱飘来，可能是破坏行为的后果。达莉娅带着乘客经过一尊雕成猿猴圣徒的华丽船首像。在河边神学院，艾达修女承认自己犹豫不决：沃斯送来一份敕令，命令她的学生赞美他。当达莉娅提出可通过原始送货簿核验时，她不再迟疑。

Ada chose to **confide** that the messenger was Voss's **sycophant**, who sought a **readjustment** of the school's grant. The local **prelacy** had received a **peremptory** demand as well, delivered with a shell **conch** trumpet. To **forestall** fraud, Dalia compared the **flimsy** orders with copies Reed would **emend** before dawn. The work was **phenomenal** in its patience, not a promise of **beatitude** or **adulation**. Reed said public office was not **kingship**; it required **vim** and an honest **tableau** of accounts.

艾达决定吐露，信使是沃斯的谄媚者，想要重新调整学校的拨款。当地教会高层也收到了一项强硬要求，传令者还吹着贝壳海螺号。为防范欺诈，达莉娅将这些单薄的命令与里德会在黎明前修订的副本逐一比较。这项工作耐心非凡，不是为了许诺福佑或博取赞颂。里德说，公职不是王权；它需要活力和一幅诚实的账目图景。

They chose to **shun** spectacle. A frightened **suppliant** waited by the rail, and the captain felt **suspense** as an old **crone** from Willow Bend named the men behind the **machinations**. Their **ultimate** object was a glossy **brochure** claiming that a new river **scooter** would save the county. Its **animated** drawings hid an **officious** plan to **smother** the election office with petitions. Each forged signature came from a single ink **molecule** in a cheap **reproduction** stamp.

他们决定避开哗众取宠的场面。一名受惊的哀求者在栏杆边等待；来自柳树湾的老妪点出幕后操纵者时，船长感到悬念陡增。他们最终的目标是一册光鲜的宣传册，声称一辆新式河上滑行车能拯救全县。其动画图画掩盖了一项好管闲事的计划：用请愿书淹没选举办公室。每个伪造签名都来自廉价复制印章中的单个墨水分子。

Fresh evidence seemed to **invigorate** the passengers, though Dalia felt **compunction** for the clerk who had been **remiss**. The petition sheets were too **homogeneous** to be genuine. His **inexperience** and **insouciance** had made him a **hostage** to Voss's **idolatrous** ambition. “A fair vote is more **sublime** than any man's **idiosyncrasy**,” Reed said, asking Dalia to **compile** statements and **hasten** them to the sheriff, before Voss could threaten **deportation** of immigrant dockworkers.

新证据似乎振奋了乘客，尽管达莉娅对那名失职书记员感到内疚。请愿书的样式过于同质，绝非真实。他的缺乏经验和漫不经心使他成了沃斯偶像化野心的人质。“公平的投票比任何人的怪癖都更崇高，”里德说，请达莉娅汇集陈述并赶紧送交警长，以免沃斯威胁驱逐移民码头工人。

The sheriff would **assert** that such pressure was **adverse** to every voter, however much Voss dismissed their fears as **banality**. The alderman had tried to **amass** sealed ballots through **impertinence**, and his guards had threatened to **pummel** a witness behind the engine's steel **carapace**. Yet Voss was **unforthcoming** when asked to explain the **variegate** inks. He offered the guards a **furlough** if they stayed **willful** in their silence, then spread **innuendo** that Reed was **indolent** and merely **mobile** enough to escape blame.

警长将断言，这种压力不利于每一位选民，不管沃斯如何把他们的恐惧斥为平庸。他曾以无礼手段聚集密封选票，卫兵还威胁要在发动机的钢制甲壳后痛打证人。然而被要求解释多色墨水时，沃斯却不肯坦白。他承诺只要卫兵执意沉默，就给他们休假，随后又散布影射，说里德懒惰，只是机动得足以逃避责任。

Dalia recognized the charge as the habit of a petty **kingling**. Voss would **contravene** any rule to **reorganize** the count around himself. The voyage became **memorable** when a shrewd deckhand asked him to **justify** the marks on the ballots. His **canny** question punctured the **pollyannaish** story Voss had sold. Under oath, the treasurer chose to **forswear** him and describe the proposed **reform** as a **separatist** scheme to exclude the marsh settlements.

达莉娅认出这项指控不过是小暴君的惯常伎俩。沃斯会违背任何规则，把计票重新组织得只围绕自己。当一名精明的水手要求他说明选票上标记的正当性时，这段航程变得令人难忘。这个机敏的问题戳穿了沃斯兜售的盲目乐观故事。财务主管在宣誓后选择与他决裂，称拟议的改革是一项排斥沼泽聚居地的分离主义方案。

At the next stop, an **amphibian** splashed from the reeds as Dalia said she had come to **loathe** intimidation. No **jubilation** followed; even the visiting **dignitary** listened soberly while a **courier** carried affidavits from the **agrarian** league. Several voters had been told to **abstain** at the river **outpost**. Voss meant to **undermine** turnout, and his hired **pyromaniac** had left a **gleam** of oil on the ballot hall. The clue could **augur** an attempt to burn the **filing** cabinet.

在下一站，一只两栖动物从芦苇中扑通入水；达莉娅说自己已开始憎恶恐吓。没有欢庆，连来访的要人也严肃聆听，一名信使正送来农业联盟的宣誓书。数名选民被告知要在河边前哨弃权。沃斯意在破坏投票率，他雇佣的纵火犯在选票大厅留下了一点油光。这条线索预示有人企图烧毁档案柜。

A **homespun** deputy, acting as **inspector**, assembled a **contingent** of volunteers. Dalia looked **searchingly** at Voss when he began to **taunt** the crowd with a **bawdy**, **nebulous** allegation. A **precocious** student called it **heresy** against the town's civic faith, but Reed asked the deputy to **deputize** no child. Such **indecorous** theater concealed an **oddity**: an **accordion** case held a key to the supposedly **indivisible** ballot box.

一名朴实的副警长以监察员身份召集了一队志愿者。当沃斯开始以一项粗俗而含糊的指控嘲弄人群时，达莉娅审视着他。一名早慧的学生称其为对全镇公民信念的异端，但里德要求副警长不要委任任何儿童。这种不得体的表演掩盖了一个怪事：手风琴盒里放着那只据称不可分割的票箱钥匙。

The case belonged to **rosa**, the registrar's assistant. Her sworn statement led to Voss's **acquittal** on a minor charge but made his control of the office **incoercible** no longer. The **sycophantic** aide had used a **windy** speech to distract a **nihilist** hired for noise. Under proper **stringency**, the deputy could **incarcerate** the armed guard whose **holster** held a receipt. He had been told to **aggress** against anyone carrying a **tambourine**, while **lying** about a secret signal from a nearby **constellation** signboard.

这个盒子属于登记员助理罗莎。她的宣誓陈述使沃斯在一项轻罪上获判无罪，但也令他对办公室的控制不再不可抗拒。那名谄媚的助手用一篇空洞演说分散注意，干扰一名被雇来制造噪声的虚无主义者。在恰当严厉的条件下，副警长可以监禁那名枪套里藏着收据的武装卫兵。他受命攻击任何携带铃鼓的人，同时谎称附近星座招牌传来秘密信号。

That absurd plan endangered the **livelihood** of musicians and vendors, but it exposed the actual **graft**. No campaign was **invulnerable** to daylight. An **enthusiastic**, if **froward**, baker identified the **lacteal** smear on the receipt as butter from Voss's private supper. The treasurer's **libido** for luxury had helped him **winnow** suppliers toward friends with a crooked **bent**. Dalia called the practice **opprobrious**; an audit was already **afoot**.

这个荒谬计划危及音乐家和摊贩的生计，却暴露了真正的贪污。没有任何竞选经得起阳光照耀。一名热情却乖戾的面包师认出收据上的乳白色污渍是沃斯私人晚餐留下的黄油。财务主管对奢华的欲望使他筛选供应商，偏向那些心术不正的朋友。达莉娅称这种做法可耻；审计已经开始。

Voss claimed his critics were driven by **atheism**, yet a ferryman at the **crag** produced the **purported** charity invoices. Their cheerful **satire** showed a **canine** mascot that could not **withstand** a close reading. There was much **ado**, but the **taciturn** ferryman gave the clearest testimony, while a **voluble** driver identified the delivery van's **chassis**. Its serial number proved that someone had sought to **falsify** the invoices; even Voss's **lackluster** defense could not explain the missing **artifacts** from the county museum.

沃斯声称批评者受无神论驱使，但悬崖边的一名摆渡人拿出了所谓的慈善发票。发票上欢快的讽刺画有一个犬类吉祥物，经不起仔细审读。现场喧闹不已，但沉默寡言的摆渡人提供了最清楚的证词，一名健谈的司机则认出送货车的底盘。其序列号证明有人试图伪造发票；即使沃斯苍白无力的辩护，也无法解释县博物馆失踪的文物。

The museum **bursar** testified that Voss had tried to **sully** Reed's name by hanging a receipt **askew** in the archive. The deception was **detrimental**, said the river **sergeant**, because it kept the actual records **involved** in needless dispute. On the sound **premise** that every ballot deserved protection, the **ungainly** packet's crew created an **effectual** guard. They would **evert** the cargo net, **curb** entry to the box, and ignore Voss's slur about **polygamy**.
