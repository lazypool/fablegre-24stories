## Story for Chunk 016 - Desert Archive

Leila Sada came to Qasr Nahl because the village's old water books had an **affliction**: their ink was fading. She would **bide** at the survey camp until a sandstorm might **hap**, then enter the sealed rooms beneath the escarpment. Her first **principle** was to copy nothing **readily**. Even a **spiny** thorn, a **diversified** seed store, and the altered **sensorium** of a tired digger could change an interpretation. Karim, the camp's **zany** mechanic, warned that the developers meant to **reduce** the hill, **acidify** its soil with runoff, and call the old people **debased** squatters. A hospital **transfusion** had saved Karim's mother; he regarded saving the archive with equal **mesmerism**.

莱拉·萨达来到纳赫勒堡，是因为村里的古老水簿出了问题：墨迹正在褪色。她会留在勘测营地，等一场沙暴来临，再进入陡坡下封闭的房间。她的首要原则是不轻易抄录任何内容。即使一根多刺的荆棘、储存多样种子的仓库，或疲惫挖掘者改变的感知，都可能改变解释。营地古怪的机械师卡里姆警告，开发商打算削平山丘，用径流酸化土壤，并把老人称作卑贱的占地者。医院输血救过卡里姆母亲；他对拯救档案也怀有同样入迷的看重。

Leila Sada was **hesitant** to **wage** war from a **rickety** office, yet the ownership papers formed a **conundrum**. The proposed mine promised public **utility**, rail **propulsion**, and a temporary **gig** for villagers. Its director called the project a civic **crusade** and tried to **repel** objections. After the recent **recrudescence** of dust fever, he would **vociferate** at meetings, then **recline** behind hired guards. His maps erased the community's **tenancy** and described Qasr Nahl as **insular**, though its keepers had helped **propagate** crops across the desert. An old **lyric** named the nearby **butte** the place where a governor made a **surrender**. Leila Sada believed the line might **indict** the mine's title through an **adventitious** clue: a forgotten canal **inlet**.

莱拉·萨达犹豫是否要从摇摇欲坠的办公室发动斗争，但所有权文件构成难题。拟议矿场承诺公共效用、铁路动力和给村民的临时工作。其主任称项目为公民十字军行动，试图击退反对意见。近期尘热病复发后，他会在会议上高声叫嚷，随后躺在雇佣警卫后方。他的地图抹去社区租佃权，把纳赫勒堡描述为孤立之地，尽管其守护者曾帮助在沙漠传播作物。一首旧歌词称附近孤峰为总督投降之处。莱拉·萨达相信这行字或可凭一条偶然线索控告矿场产权：一处被遗忘的运河入口。

Leila, the local **advocacy** organizer, brought a stone **polyhedron** used as a seal. Someone had tried to **bedizen** it with tourist paint, but its careful **modulation** of marks matched a decree ordering a **moratorium**. The keeper, a **recluse** called Aunt Sada, said the chambers had once stored **propellant** for signal fires and records for **doughty** farmers. "Do not let the digging **cease**," she said, pointing to an eagle's **eyry** above the cliff. "Let it **infuse** the council with patience." Leila Sada tried not to show how the director's smugness could **irk** her. He was a celebrated **polygamist** who seemed to **aspire** to every local honor.

本地倡议组织者莱拉带来一块用作印章的石质多面体。有人试图用游客颜料把它装饰得花哨，但其精细变化的刻痕与一道要求暂停的法令相符。守护人萨达阿姨是位隐居者，她说这些房间曾储存信号火的燃料，也保存勇敢农民的记录。“别让挖掘停止，”她指着悬崖上的鹰巢说，“让它把耐心注入议会。”莱拉·萨达尽量不显露主任的自鸣得意多么使她恼火。他是著名的一夫多妻者，似乎觊觎每项本地荣誉。

The next **outbreak** of wind left the trench bare. Sun **exposure**, a little **anesthetic** from the clinic, and her old **wanderlust** made Leila Sada **doubly** reckless. A child began to **bawl** when a wall shifted. The village **ecologist**, an **uxorious** man named Nuri, found an **emblem** carved near the breach: two hands holding a reed. At a public **disputation**, the water tasted **brackish** and the mine lawyer sounded **priggish**. Still, Leila felt **elate** when Leila Sada did not **stumble** over the inscription. A thin **purl** of water ran to a sheltered **berth** in the rock. The director dismissed it as a **spoof** devised by an **underman** in the archives.

下一阵狂风把沟槽吹得裸露。日晒、诊所的一点麻醉剂和她旧日的漫游欲，使莱拉·萨达更加鲁莽。墙体移动时，一个孩子开始大哭。村里的生态学家努里是个爱妻之人，他在裂口旁发现一枚雕刻徽记：两只手握着芦苇。在公开辩论中，水尝起来发咸，矿方律师听来故作正经。即便如此，当莱拉·萨达没有在铭文上犯难时，莱拉感到振奋。一缕细水流到岩石中受庇护的凹处。主任把它斥为档案中卑微职员编造的恶作剧。

That night a desert **hurricane** stripped the last **foliage** from the wadi. It made the choice between emergency works and archival care seem **invidious**. In the exposed masonry, a painted **torso** appeared. During the **interim** after the storm, a mining scout claimed the find as a **windfall**. Leila Sada saw **myriad** names on tablets and began to **cherish** their uneven handwriting. Even the village **disbeliever** admitted that "canal" was **synonymous** with survival here. A **venous** red line on one map showed the flow. A traveling **dramatist** said the tablets seemed to **prophesy** a drought, but Leila Sada found their **manifold** accounts more useful than any **materialism** of ore. Her brief **bliss** ended when the director made a **petty** film with a visiting **cineast**, claiming the ruins were empty.

那夜，一场沙漠飓风从干河床剥走最后的叶片。它使应急工程与档案保护之间的选择显得两难。裸露的砖石中出现一具彩绘躯干。风暴后的间歇，一名矿业勘察员把发现宣称为意外之财。莱拉·萨达在泥板上看到无数名字，开始珍爱其不均匀笔迹。连村中怀疑者也承认，“运河”在这里等同于生存。一张地图上血管般红线显示水流。一名旅行剧作家说泥板似乎预言干旱，但莱拉·萨达觉得它们多方面的账目比任何矿石唯物观都实用。她短暂的喜悦在主任和来访电影人拍摄一部小气影片、声称遗址空无一物时结束。

Leila Sada chose to **dwell** in the heat until the work was **endurable**. A cool **glacier** of salt lay inside a jar marked with a tax **denomination**. The earliest account forced the council to **concede** that blasting would **doom** the spring. Beyond a narrow **chasm**, a brass **trumpet** lay beside the missing register. The director entered with astonishing **effrontery**, his voice already **hoarse**, and offered to **donate** a museum wing. Leila Sada refused; the evidence had gained **momentum**, and a private display would be **improper**. Her **novice** assistant had seen guards **conspire** to remove the box. He had watched one **don** a porter’s coat, then **enumerate** the tablets before trying to **ferret** out the seal.

莱拉·萨达选择留在热浪中，直到工作可以忍受。一个标有税额的罐中放着一块凉冷的盐晶。最早的记录迫使议会承认爆破会毁掉泉水。越过狭窄裂谷，一把黄铜喇叭躺在失踪登记簿旁。主任以惊人的厚脸皮进来，声音已经嘶哑，提出捐建一座博物馆翼楼。莱拉·萨达拒绝；证据已有势头，私人展示并不妥当。她的新手助手看见守卫共谋移走盒子。他见一人穿上搬运工外套，再清点泥板，随后试图搜出印章。

The assistant became **lachrymose**, but Leila, a lifelong **pacifist**, replied with an **oracular** calm. Her **commentary** made the crowd **persist** through the night. It named the old regime's **misogyny**, which had concealed women custodians behind an **underling**'s signature, and rejected the **paralysis** of polite delay. The council's most **principled** member said their extractive **mentality** had blinded their **ocular** judgment. The director's performance was too **stagy** for the visiting **legislator**, who studied a wheel drawn as an **epicycloid**. When a guard tried to **contort** the wheel into a company insignia, Leila Sada explained its **newtonian** water measure. A local **pamphleteer** copied her **precise** figures, though the director continued to **pretend** that the canal gates would **actuate** themselves.

助手泪流满面，但终身和平主义者莱拉以神谕般平静回应。她的评论使人群坚持过夜。她点出旧政权的厌女，那里女性保管人被隐藏在下属的签名后，也拒绝礼貌拖延造成的瘫痪。议会中最有原则的成员说，他们的开采心态蒙蔽了视觉判断。主任的表演对来访立法者而言过于舞台化，后者研究一个画成渐开线的轮子。警卫试图将轮子扭成公司徽记时，莱拉·萨达解释其牛顿式测水方法。本地小册子作者抄下她精确的数字，尽管主任继续假装运河闸门会自行启动。

At dawn they uncovered an **alluvion** beneath torn **apparel** and a letter from a former **tempter** of village leaders. The **sawdust** from a false floor had preserved its **impersonal** hand. By the elders' **insistence**, they held a **plebiscite** in the shade, where Sada mended a **crochet** border. A former company guide, branded a **turncoat**, led Leila Sada to a **subterranean** channel. Its **eddy** fed a small **vortex** beside words of **hortatory** warning: a **vicious** ruler had ordered the water hidden. The **lapidary** script told keepers to **mingle** accounts from every household in a bronze **salver**, so no lord could own one history. Leila Sada's late **lucubration** convinced even a skeptical **philistine** that the **hoary** records deserved more than an **encore** of official neglect.

黎明时，他们在破衣下发现冲积层和一封来自曾诱惑村领导者之人的信。假地板留下的锯末保存了其非个人化笔迹。应长者坚持，他们在树荫下举行公民投票，萨达在那里修补钩织花边。一名被称为叛徒的前公司向导带莱拉·萨达到地下水道。其回流喂养着小漩涡，旁边有劝诫文字：一名残酷统治者曾下令藏起水源。石刻文字告知守护人，要把每家账户混放在青铜托盘中，免得某个领主独占一段历史。莱拉·萨达深夜的苦思说服了连怀疑又庸俗的人：这些古老记录值得获得比官方再次忽视更好的对待。

The director's **conniving** appeal proved **bootless**. His **pontifical** lawyer invoked an **achillean** flaw in the charter, saying a **gynecocracy** of custodians could not hold land. Then a masked **saboteur** cut the trench ropes. The fall caused **considerable** damage, but Leila gave **assertive** orders. A carved **monition** warned that **cajolery** had once divided the village; the state **assessor**, no longer **disheartened**, recognized the seal. Under the **dun** light, the director remained **cavalier** about a worker's **abrasion** and blamed strangers with **xenophobia**. Nuri, **adroit** with fragile pages, lifted a bound **folio**. Its **sequent** columns recorded a royal **manumission** of canal laborers, a **rare** legal protection against the company's **deviance**.

主任的串通申诉毫无用处。他自命不凡的律师援引章程中的阿喀琉斯弱点，说由女性统治的保管者不能持有土地。随后一名蒙面破坏者割断沟槽绳索。坠落造成相当大损害，但莱拉下达果断命令。一则雕刻警示说，花言巧语曾分裂村庄；州评估员不再灰心，认出了印章。在褐灰光线下，主任对一名工人的擦伤仍满不在乎，并带着排外心理责怪陌生人。努里擅长处理脆弱书页，提起一本装订对开的册子。其连续栏目记录国王解放运河劳工的法令，这是对公司越轨罕见的法律保护。

The register's **superlative** preservation did not invite anyone to **disport** in the chamber. A careful **caret** in the margin revealed an **ingenious** cross-reference. Sada, usually **aloof**, showed them a dry **moat** around the archive and a sealed **deposit** beneath it. A sleeping guard gave a **stertorous** warning sound that seemed to **forebode** disaster. They had to **rebuild** a collapsed brace without touching the **sebaceous** residue on a fingerprint. The **incidence** of that print matched the director's clerk. Beside it lay an **antler** handle, proof that he had tried to **purvey** artifacts through a **rakish** dealer. His **imprecation** when arrested was no **elusion** of the fact.

登记簿保存得极好，却不意味着任何人可在墓室中嬉戏。边缘一个细心的插入符揭示了巧妙的交叉引用。平时冷淡的萨达给他们看档案周围的干涸壕沟和下方封存储藏处。一名熟睡警卫发出沉重鼾声，像在预示灾难。他们必须重建倒塌支撑，又不能碰到指纹上的皮脂残留。该指纹的出现与主任文员相符。旁边有一只鹿角把手，证明他曾试图通过一个放荡商人供应文物。他被捕时的诅咒无法回避事实。

At the **introductory** hearing, the mine counsel was **aggressive**; Leila Sada had to **repudiate** his invented dates. She used the **precession** of a star mark and the **statics** of a sluice to **gird** her conclusion against a **delusory** claim. A piece of **needlework** found in the box helped **endear** the archive to the elders: it repeated a line from their **breviary**. The regional **chancellor** called the missing deed a **fugitive** document, but the archive's recording **facility** had kept an image in a **fathomless** well. Leila Sada offered a **recapitulation**; no witness could **gainsay** the **interplay** of water allotments, kinship, and repair duties. The company was in a legal **strait** and had to **capitulate**.

在初步听证会上，矿方律师咄咄逼人；莱拉·萨达必须驳斥他编造的日期。她利用星标的岁差和水闸的静力学，加强结论以对抗虚妄主张。盒中发现的一件针线活使长者更珍爱档案：它重复了祈祷书中的一句话。地区大法官称失踪契约是逃逸文件，但档案记录设施在一个深不可测的井中保留了影像。莱拉·萨达作出概述；没有证人能否认配水、亲属关系和维修职责之间的相互作用。公司陷入法律困境，只能投降。

The seal's reed **motif** became an **altruistic** promise from each **burgher** to share the spring. On a survey **scale**, the protected ground extended beyond the mine lease. A celebratory **shriek** gave way to quiet talk of **bereavement**, for the records named families lost in earlier droughts. Their **saga** was not an **atheistic** lesson against faith, but a practical memory. The director's **suggestible** followers watched a **demonstrative** elder trace the old **landscape** and ceased to **grumble**. Only one **deranged** pamphlet insisted that the archive was a **pervasive** fraud, although the buried **irrigant** channels could be tested. Sada refused to turn the evidence into a **sermon** or a patriotic **jingo** display. She pointed instead to the **igneous** ridge and its **prolix** record of floods.

印章上的芦苇图案成为每位市民无私分享泉水的承诺。按照测绘比例，受保护土地延伸到矿租范围之外。庆祝的尖叫让位于对丧亲的低声谈论，因为记录写有早年干旱中失去的家庭。他们的故事不是反信仰的无神论教训，而是实用记忆。主任易受影响的追随者看着一位示范性的长者勾画旧日景观，不再抱怨。只有一份疯狂小册子坚持说档案是无处不在的骗局，尽管埋藏灌溉水道可被检验。萨达拒绝把证据变成说教或爱国沙文主义表演。她转而指向火成岩脊及其冗长的洪水记录。

A child found a copper **doublet** of the seal, helping the team **succeed** without an **apology** to the company. The clinic could **recure** the injured worker, and the village held a modest **raffle** for supplies. Above them, the night **galaxy** shone over hardy **flora**. The judge ruled that no contract could **nullify** the archive's proof of communal **hydrodynamics**. A **seasonal** committee would maintain the channel, while the company must **expiate** its damage. The documents had legal **salience** as well as human value; water was a shared **resource**, not a prize. When Karim began to **chide** himself for nearly leaving, Leila Sada told him to **spruce** up the shelter and **perambulate** no farther than the marked path.

一个孩子找到印章的铜制副件，帮助团队成功，无需向公司道歉。诊所能重新治愈受伤工人，村庄举行小型抽奖筹集物资。夜间银河照耀着耐寒植物。法官裁定，任何合同都不能使档案关于共同水力系统的证据无效。一个季节性委员会将维护水道，公司必须赎清造成的损害。文件既具法律显著性，也具人性价值；水是共享资源，不是奖品。卡里姆开始责怪自己险些离去时，莱拉·萨达要他修整避难处，并且不要在标记路径外徘徊。

The reopened archive was **sensuous** only in its cool air and cedar smell. Leila Sada would **abridge** no testimony for a **jaundiced** reporter. An **emaciated** elder chose to **personify** the former custodians, but reminded listeners that their labor was not **immaterial**. The system was **comprehensible**: every household could **breed** dates, copy the roster **verbatim**, and let new readers **leaven** an old **sonnet** with fresh witness. Progress came **inchmeal**, through a **figurative** bridge between past and present. That **rapprochement** did not **discolor** the crime. It could **juxtapose** the mine's threat with the caretaker's resolve without trying to **maim** anyone's reputation. Even an **ornery** inspector accepted the **decagon** seal as authentic.

重新开放的档案馆只有凉爽空气与雪松气味令人感官愉悦。莱拉·萨达不会为一名偏见记者删节任何证词。一位消瘦长者选择让前任保管人具象化，却提醒听者，他们的劳动并非无关紧要。这个系统可以理解：每个家庭都能种植椰枣，逐字抄录名册，并让新读者以新的见证使一首旧十四行诗发酵。进步一点一点到来，经由过去与现在之间的比喻桥梁。这种和解没有粉饰罪行。它能并置矿场威胁和照料者决心，而不试图损害任何人名誉。连一个倔强检查员也接受十边形印章为真品。

The company's **extremist** shareholder demanded a dramatic **finale**, but the hearing merely added a **stipple** of facts to the public record. The court found the lease **licit** only outside the protected boundary. With **foresight**, the families would **cohabit** with researchers under rules approved by the **legislature**. The director left **dour** and unable to **reclaim** the spring through a **vicarious** agent. A boy's **gibe** made the crowd laugh, though Leila cautioned them not to **overdo** triumph. The **hubbub** disturbed the clinic's **pediatrician**, who had been studying the children for dust illness. She found the company had tried to **whitewash** a report by tying it to a sham **matrimony** settlement, an arrangement that could **entail** fraudulent **probate** claims.

公司的极端股东要求戏剧性结局，但听证只是在公共记录中添上一点事实。法院认定租约只在保护边界外合法。凭着远见，家庭将在立法机关批准的规则下与研究人员共处。主任阴沉离去，无法通过代理人夺回泉水。一个男孩的嘲讽使人群发笑，莱拉却提醒他们不要过度庆祝。喧闹打扰了诊所儿科医生，她一直研究孩子的尘病。她发现公司曾试图把报告与一场虚假婚姻和解绑定，以粉饰报告，而该安排可能导致欺诈性遗产认证主张。

That discovery made dismissal **impermissible**. A **conscientious** clerk noted that the chamber had become **humid** after an unexpected **migratory** storm. Karim used his **skill** to **preengage** the pump before water reached the shelves. Each resident's **disposition** of rights was copied beneath the carved **arcade**. The director tried to **retract** his statement, calling the archive a tale for a desert **gnome**, but the ink's **recessive** layers refuted him. Leila Sada avoided **metaphysics** and showed the deed: Sada's ancestor was the **pledgeor**, and the company had sought to **betray** a trust by which the community could **subsist**. The judge ordered a **strike** against the drilling permit.

这一发现使撤销案件不被允许。一名尽责文员注意到，意外迁徙性风暴后墓室变得潮湿。卡里姆运用技能，在水到书架前预先接合水泵。每位居民的权利处置都被抄在雕刻拱廊下。主任试图收回说法，称档案是给沙漠侏儒听的故事，但墨迹的隐退层次驳斥了他。莱拉·萨达避开形而上学，展示契约：萨达的祖先是出质人，公司企图背叛一项让社区得以存续的信托。法官下令撤销钻探许可。

In a **vintage** ledger, the first keeper described how to **surmount** a **ramshackle** dam: shared labor, **whereas** private hoarding brought only **clangor** and **ludicrous** blame. The villagers used a rope **harness** to lower fresh supports.
