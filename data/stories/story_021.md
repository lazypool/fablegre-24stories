## Story for Chunk 021 - Arctic Relay

The first **excerpt** from Station Nereid arrived at 03:10, a burst of static asking command to **revoke** an evacuation order. Its final coordinates were **irretrievable** beneath aurora interference, though a **supplementary** pulse repeated one clear imperative: **evacuate** before dawn.

尼瑞德站的第一段摘录于03:10抵达，是一阵杂音，要求指挥部撤销撤离命令。它的最终坐标在极光干扰下已无法找回，不过一道补充脉冲重复了一条明确指令：黎明前撤离。

Lieutenant Orla Venn studied the **diatomic** oxygen readout. Supplies had been issued with **miserly** care, yet the storm made ordinary **circumstances** useless. Her **contribution** would be to take the old rescue cutter north, beyond every **primitive** chart, **and** discover who had divided the watch into **factious** camps.

奥拉·文恩中尉审视着双原子氧读数。补给发放得极其吝啬，可风暴令寻常情形下的办法都失去了作用。她能做的是驾着旧救援艇北上，越过每一张原始海图，并找出是谁把值守人员分裂成派系林立的阵营。

Station chief Orlov, nominal **successor** to its dead founder, called her proposal **outrageous**. Still, his tiny **flotilla** had no better pilot than Venn's **unfledged** radio officer, Eli, whose **beau** back home sent him music in coded bursts. Before departure, he watched seal pups **gambol** beside an **illustrious** ice shelf.

名义上继承了已故创始人的站长奥尔洛夫称她的提议荒唐透顶。然而，他的小舰队没有比文恩那位初出茅庐的报务员伊莱更好的驾驶员。伊莱家乡的恋人会用编码脉冲给他送来音乐。出发前，他看着海豹幼崽在一片著名冰架旁嬉戏。

The cutter took a hard **plunge** through newly opened water. Venn meant to **atone** for leaving Nereid during its previous blackout. Inside her cabin she found a brass **pentagram**, the same mark worn by the director's **counterpart** in the intelligence bureau. It looked less like a charm than an **obsequy** for somebody not yet dead.

救援艇猛然扎进新裂开的水道。文恩想为上一次停电时离开尼瑞德赎罪。在舱房里，她发现了一枚黄铜五芒星，与情报局中那位主任对应人物佩戴的标记相同。它不像护符，倒像是为尚未死去的人举行的悼礼。

Her decoder was **invaluable** when the signal returned, **resurgent** within the **vacuum** of a dead channel. It came from a **serried** line of relay masts. Maintenance records were **lax** enough to invite a **misdemeanor**, but no small theft could explain why the transmitter had failed.

信号在死寂频道的真空中重新出现时，她的译码器价值连城。信号来自一排密集的中继塔。维护记录松懈到足以诱发轻罪，但再小的盗窃也无法解释发射机为何失效。

At noon, cook Daro served **millet** beneath a portrait of his **cherubic** daughter. The meal tasted **wretched**; the ship's **orientation** system was drifting. Venn suspected somebody meant to **bilk** the crew of time, but the engineer treated her alarm with theatrical **eclat** & a **sectarian** slur.

中午，厨师达罗在他那天使般女儿的肖像下端上小米。饭菜难吃透顶，船的定向系统正在漂移。文恩怀疑有人想骗走船员的时间，可工程师却以戏剧化的炫耀和宗派性的辱骂回应她的警报。

She ordered him to **solder** a loose antenna joint while Eli checked the aluminum-lined cable for a **foil** switch. Meltwater left every panel **damp**. The damage showed a slow **degeneracy**, not storm shock, & in the galley an untouched crate of **endive** had been crushed beneath a deliberately **equitable** stack of ration tins.

她命令他焊接松动的天线接头，同时伊莱检查铝衬电缆中是否有箔片开关。融水令每块面板都潮湿。损坏显示出缓慢的退化，并非风暴冲击；而在厨房，一箱未动过的菊苣被故意码放得均等的口粮罐压碎了。

The engineer, **obese** with layers of polar gear, became a visible **menace** when Venn accused him. He smiled in an **artful** way & promised to **outmaneuver** ice, weather, suspicion. From his berth floated a recorded **cantata**, absurdly polished for a vessel whose radio had fallen silent.

穿着层层极地装备而显得臃肿的工程师，在文恩指控他时成了显而易见的威胁。他狡黠地微笑，承诺会智胜冰层、天气和怀疑。从他的铺位飘出录制的康塔塔，放在一艘无线电已沉默的船上，精致得荒谬。

Venn checked his service **rank**: an **anemic** record, endorsed years ago by an **archdeacon** who had served as a cover officer. Nereid was listed as **habitable** year-round by ministry decree. His **excellency** Orlov had signed it, though Venn could **glean** no proof that anyone had inspected the place.

文恩核查了他的服役军衔：履历单薄，由一位曾担任掩护官员的副主教多年前背书。部令将尼瑞德列为全年宜居之地。阁下奥尔洛夫签过字，但文恩找不到任何人曾检查该地的证据。

A **glaring** scrape on the aft **stanchion** revealed fresh tool marks. The engineer said he had tried to **reestablish** a ground wire. Venn saw instead a map altered to **gerrymander** search sectors around a black inlet. Eli called the explanation **clown** work, then apologized for the **minutia** of his complaint.

船尾支柱上一道刺目的擦痕露出新鲜工具印。工程师称自己试图重新接上地线。文恩却看见一张被改动过的地图，以不公正划分方式把搜索区域绕开黑色海湾。伊莱称这解释是小丑把戏，随后又为自己抱怨的琐细道歉。

The boy's **ingenuous** apology sounded oddly **disingenuous** after Venn noticed his hands shaking. Their cutter's **outrigger** struck brash ice, enough to **exhilarate** the deckhands. Venn stayed **unbiased** until a second **occurrence**: Eli's private receiver had copied the same distress pulse before the public set did.

文恩注意到男孩双手发抖后，他那天真的道歉听来颇不真诚。救援艇的舷外浮架撞上碎冰，足以让甲板水手兴奋。文恩一直保持公正，直到第二件事发生：伊莱的私人接收机比公用设备更早复制了同一求救脉冲。

She told him to **bate** his fear & show the message. He produced an **emollient** lie about a family broadcast. Venn felt **contempt**, because there was **nowhere** a civilian signal could have crossed that latitude. The **prevalence** of false timestamps forced her to **annotate** every transmission.

她叫他减轻恐惧并交出信息。他编出一个安抚性的谎言，说那是家人的广播。文恩感到轻蔑，因为民用信号不可能穿过那条纬度。虚假时间戳的普遍存在迫使她为每次传输作注。

One phrase named a **blasphemy** hidden under the ice. Its grammar was **severe**, stripped of the usual excess of a **spendthrift** cipher clerk. Somebody had tried to **expurgate** the place-name. An **incipient** crack opened between officers, but Venn demanded **consensus** before turning back.

有一句话点出冰下藏着的亵渎之物。它的语法严峻，去除了挥霍型密码文员惯有的冗余。有人试图删去地名。军官间出现初起裂痕，但文恩要求取得共识后才返航。

Eli offered to **obvert** the encrypted phrase through a mirror table, then asked Venn to **grant** him an hour. Daro, acting as a **gadfly**, questioned every **preparatory** order. He believed the relay was a private **monopoly**, its **spectrum** leased to a northern **evangelist** with **reckless** ambitions.

伊莱提出通过镜像表倒置这句加密短语，随后请求文恩给他一小时。达罗像牛虻一样质疑每一项准备命令。他认为中继站是私有垄断，其频谱租给了一位有鲁莽野心的北方福音传教士。

His theory was a **melange** of facts & **tangential** gossip, yet it named the **verdant** southern estate from which the engineer had come. The sea turned **frenetic** beneath crosswinds. Venn wondered whether a political **proselyte**, rather than a spy, had poisoned the relay, driven by **lechery** for power.

他的理论混杂了事实与离题闲谈，却提到工程师来自的那座青翠南方庄园。横风下大海变得狂乱。文恩怀疑，毒害中继站的或许不是间谍，而是一名为权力所欲驱使的政治鼓吹者。

They **encamp** on a floe when visibility dies. In the snow shelter, Eli found a **pastiche** of old navigation orders, including a note that called Orlov an **odious** master. The paper was too **responsive** to heat, revealing names of station **personnel** when breathed upon.

能见度消失时，他们在浮冰上扎营。伊莱在雪屋中找到一份旧航行命令的仿作，其中一张字条称奥尔洛夫是可憎的主人。纸张对热反应过强，呵气时会显现站内人员的名字。

The list made rescue **practicable**. A **plebian** mechanic at Nereid had left a message inside a **molding** strip: "**boost** the backup cell." Its wording was **disjointed**, almost that of a **dolt**, but a red stain on the **porphyry** paper made Venn **sympathize** with the writer's terror.

这份名单令救援变得可行。尼瑞德一名平民机械师在一条模塑条内留下信息：“增强备用电池。”措辞支离破碎，近乎笨蛋所写，但斑岩纸上的红色污迹令文恩同情写信者的恐惧。

The next sheet contained an **allegory** of a lamp that refused to **solidify** its light. Eli traced a **transverse** line through an **aromatic** oil stain into the rock **stratum** beneath the station. It suggested a buried cable whose **influence** reached farther than the weather service.

下一页含有一盏灯拒绝凝固其光的寓言。伊莱沿着芳香油渍穿过一条横向线，追至站点下方的岩层。这暗示一根埋藏电缆的影响力远及气象部门之外。

Cutting it would do a **disservice** to the coast, yet leaving it live was plainly **deleterious**. Venn would not **niggle** over authority. As an intelligence **analyst**, she had arrived with **apprehension** of a **causal** link between the relay failure & a **midsummer** disappearance.

切断它会损害海岸，任其通电则显然有害。文恩不愿在权限上斤斤计较。作为情报分析员，她带着对中继故障与盛夏失踪案存在因果联系的忧虑而来。

The missing woman's **biography** identified her as a **smarmy** contractor hired to **undergird** Nereid's towers. Orlov's claim that she defected was **fallacious**. A **querulous** technician had filed a **sophomoric** complaint, then vanished. Venn's **levelheaded** reading found a **canker**: **copious** funds had been diverted through emergency **rhetoric**.

失踪女子的履历称她是一名受雇加固尼瑞德塔楼的油滑承包商。奥尔洛夫所说她叛逃的说法是谬误。一名爱抱怨的技术员提交过幼稚投诉，随后失踪。文恩冷静研读后发现了毒瘤：大量资金借紧急措辞被转移。

The paper trail described **misrule** at the relay. A **rotary** cipher wheel had been removed, its filings swept into a **sift** tray. Venn felt no **qualms** breaking Orlov's seal. Beneath it lay a photograph of a **quintet** standing at the station, their faces turned toward the white **breadth**.

书面线索描述了中继站的失政。一只旋转密码轮被移走，锉屑扫进筛盘。文恩毫不犹豫地拆开奥尔洛夫的封印。下面是一张五人站在站点的照片，脸朝向一望无际的白色。

One figure held a **trifle** of black metal, not native to that **arboreal** world of memory. Eli used its magnetic clasp to **magnetize** a compass needle. It pointed **headlong** toward the inlet. **prudence** urged delay, but the **celestial** green overhead was fading, their only shield from enemy listening.

其中一人拿着一小块黑色金属，不属于那片树木繁茂的记忆世界。伊莱用它的磁性扣将指南针针磁化。针直指海湾。谨慎促使他们延迟，但头顶天际的绿光正在消退，那是抵御敌方监听的唯一屏障。

Daro proved **stingy** with fuel, while Eli played **accompanist** to the engine's rhythm on a tin cup. A ministry **functionary** might have found the scene **jocose**; Venn found it **irksome**. An **anonymous** vessel had appeared on radar, following at **fundamental** range with unnerving **indifference**.

达罗对燃料十分吝啬，伊莱则用锡杯为引擎节奏伴奏。部里的一名官员或许会觉得此景滑稽；文恩却觉得烦人。一艘身份不明的船出现在雷达上，以基本距离冷漠得令人不安地尾随。

They reached the inlet in a **thorough** whiteout. The station was **puny**, its power supply **exiguous**, its security **peccable**. A **gallant** flag snapped over the **blue** roof. Venn recognized the maintenance crew's **habitude**: they marked each **nocturnal** inspection with chalk, though the marks became **obsolete** each thaw.

他们在彻底的白茫中抵达海湾。站点弱小，电力供应极少，安保容易出错。一面英勇的旗帜在蓝色屋顶上猎猎作响。文恩认出维护组的习惯：他们用粉笔标记每次夜间检查，尽管每次解冻后标记都会过时。

Her **extrapolation** proved right. Someone had used the chalk code to **inveigle** the missing contractor into the antenna shed. Inside, the **absence** of footprints gave the room a **purgatory** stillness. A bloodstained parka suggested **martyrdom**, yet an **arch** vent stood open despite a **discrepant** weather log.

她的推断被证实正确。有人用粉笔代码诱骗失踪承包商进入天线棚。里面没有脚印，令房间有一种炼狱般的死寂。带血的派克大衣暗示殉难，然而拱形通风口却在矛盾的天气日志下敞开着。

Venn ordered Eli to **encipher** a warning. His **aversion** to the task made her **bethink** the old bureau **inquisitor** who had trained him. A small **excitation** blinked beneath a shelf, beside a **cruet** of frozen solvent. She tried to **foreclose** panic when Daro muttered that only a **feebleminded** saboteur would leave such evidence.

文恩命令伊莱把警告加密。他对这项任务的厌恶让她想起训练过他的老局审讯官。架子下方、盛着冷冻溶剂的小瓶旁，一个微弱激发信号闪烁。达罗嘟囔说只有弱智破坏者才会留下这种证据时，她试图防止恐慌。

The device required **restraint**. Venn answered his **acerbity** by pointing out its **symmetrical** wiring, built to trigger an **elegy** of sparks. Her **ego** wanted a triumph; the mechanism was **incoherent** enough to **oust** reason. Its **ideology** was simple: **infatuate** the rescue party with a false clue, then make them its lesson.

这装置需要克制。文恩回应他的尖刻，指出其对称布线被造来引发一曲火花挽歌。她的自我想赢得胜利；该机制混乱得足以驱逐理性。它的理念很简单：让救援队迷恋一条假线索，再把他们变成自己的教训。

They escaped the blast by seconds. A **didactic** recording began after the **spat** of fire, an **edifying** voice grown **overwrought** with rage. It called Venn **overweight** with guilt, then described a **freshet** under the ice as a **mirage**. The speaker had held **tenure** at the agency, a **dubious** fact absent from public files.

他们在爆炸前数秒逃脱。火焰爆裂后，一段说教式录音开始播放，那个训诲口吻的声音被愤怒逼得过度激动。它说文恩被罪疚压得过重，又把冰下洪水说成海市蜃楼。说话者曾在机构获任期，这一可疑事实不见于公开档案。

"**ichthyosaurs**," Eli said, reading a codeword that made no sense. Daro began to **gibber** until a green **sprout** pushed through a floor seam. Venn sent him below as a **spelunker** would go, tied to a line. From the shaft came a **whinny** of wind, then proof of **pillage**: the relay's spare **telescope** had been removed.

“鱼龙，”伊莱读出一个毫无意义的代码词。达罗开始胡言乱语，直到一株绿芽从地板缝中钻出。文恩像派洞穴探险者那样把他系在绳上送下去。竖井传来风的嘶鸣，接着是遭掠夺的证据：中继站备用望远镜被拆走了。

The tunnel held a ledger of **preferment** payments, reaching a **cosmopolitan** broker in Murmansk. Venn remained **unflappable** while Eli delivered a shaken **monologue**. He had seen the broker's **dainty** signature in a **piscatorial** magazine. Venn asked him to **cite** the issue, then gave Daro **balm** for a frost-burn.

隧道里有一本晋升酬金账簿，款项流向摩尔曼斯克一名见多识广的经纪人。伊莱颤抖着独白时，文恩仍镇定自若。他曾在一本渔业杂志中见过经纪人娟秀的签名。文恩让他引述期号，又给达罗冻伤膏。

The ledger could **promulgate** scandal, but first it might **inflame** a coup. Venn chose to **delegate** its custody to Eli. From its margins she could **infer** the saboteur's **conveyance**: a snow tractor concealed past a **delimit** marker. Its **bearing** was marked by an **ewer** painted on a fuel drum.

账簿能公布丑闻，但也可能先煽动政变。文恩决定把保管职责委派给伊莱。她从页边可推断出破坏者的交通工具：一辆藏在界限标志之后的雪地拖拉机。其方位由油桶上画的水罐标示。

The cabin beyond was **intolerable** with diesel fumes. Still, its equipment was **superb**: a **syllabic** code printer, a **refuse** bin full of burned passports, & an **emigrant** coat. Venn felt **bereft** of certainty. The **conservative** answer was that Orlov had arranged it, but **foreknowledge** of their arrival argued for a larger hand.

前方舱室的柴油烟气令人难以忍受。不过设备极好：一台音节代码打印机、装满烧毁护照的垃圾桶和一件移民外套。文恩失去了确定感。保守的答案是奥尔洛夫安排了一切，但预先知道他们会来说明背后有更大的势力。

A **limousine** was impossible here, yet its tire pattern appeared in a photograph beneath the desk. The caption seemed to **adulate** a minister. Venn let the thought **perturb** her only briefly. It took **temerity** to stage an **exotic** deception in ice country, & the note addressed its recipient as "**laddie** of the **lune**."

豪华轿车不可能出现在这里，它的胎纹却出现在桌下照片中。说明文字似乎在谄媚一位部长。文恩只让这个念头短暂扰乱自己。在冰原上布置如此奇异的骗局需要胆大妄为，纸条则称收件人为“月亮的小伙子”。

The phrase meant **extradite** the witness, then **jettison** the proof. Venn had to remain **unfaltering**. She found a **seine** net around the tractor's radiator, an **imposture** meant to resemble field repair. Fuel was **volatile**. They **ought** to leave, Daro said, but Eli wanted to **romp** through the files before the enemy returned.

这句话意为引渡证人，再抛弃证据。文恩必须坚定不移。她发现拖拉机散热器上套着一张围网，是伪装成野外维修的骗局。燃料很易挥发。达罗说他们应当离开，但伊莱想在敌人回来前翻遍文件。

Venn refused. The conspiracy did not **federate** loyal stations; it made them **contemptible** islands. A warm, **ingratiating** message arrived, **persuadable** only to someone who ignored its one-**centimeter** timing error. The sender tried to **flaunt** knowledge of Venn's childhood, a **prickle** beneath her hood.

文恩拒绝了。阴谋没有使忠诚站点结成联盟，反而把它们变成可鄙的孤岛。一条热情讨好的信息到来，只有忽略其一厘米计时误差的人才会被说服。发信人试图炫耀对文恩童年的了解，令她兜帽下泛起刺痛。

She answered with **reverent** formality, asking the sender to **deem** the rescue delayed by **subsistence** trouble. The reply called her a **witling**. Its **signification** was clear: the enemy trusted **insularity** to hide him. Venn promised to **perjure** herself on the open channel, pretending the crew had been lost, then **outdo** his patience.

她以恭敬的正式口吻答复，请发信人认定救援因生计困难而延误。回信称她是小聪明的人。含义明确：敌人相信孤立状态能藏住自己。文恩答应在开放频道作伪证，假称船员已失踪，再耗尽他的耐心。

At an **arbitrary** hour, a man came to the shed. He tried to **encroach** through a side door, **forehead** pressed to the glass. Venn saw Orlov, **dauntless** in a borrowed parka. He claimed he had come to **sustain** them & called the evidence **superfluous**, each **relic** from an old **rudiment** of war.

在一个随意的时刻，一名男子来到棚屋。他试图从侧门侵入，额头贴在玻璃上。文恩看见借穿派克大衣、无所畏惧的奥尔洛夫。他声称自己来支持他们，称证据多余，每件遗物都来自一场旧战争的残余。
