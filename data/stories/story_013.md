## Story for Chunk 013 - Night Express

By midnight the **cumulative** delays had made every passenger **offish**. Rain fell as cold **precipitation** over the platform, while a **nonchalant** porter led Nera Quinn past a **squalid** luggage cart. In her valise lay a **spherometer**, an absurd instrument for a railway journey, but it was the agreed signal: the brass case bore an **incantation** of numbers known only to the missing clerk. The station lamps were **dwindling** behind the train when Nera saw his gray **garment** caught beneath a bench.

到了午夜，累积的延误已使每位乘客都显得冷淡疏离。冰冷的雨水落在站台上，一名漫不经心的搬运工领着内拉·奎因经过一辆肮脏的行李车。她的手提箱里放着一具球面测量仪，这种东西带上火车实在荒谬，却是约定的信号：黄铜盒上刻着一串只有失踪文员才懂的数字咒文。列车驶离时，站灯在身后渐暗，内拉看见他灰色的衣服被夹在长凳底下。

The conductor dismissed her question with a **diatribe** about late travelers. Nera, **rapt** by the clue, noticed a **vegetal** stain on the cuff and an **incorporeal** smell of old smoke. Someone had used an **underhand** hand to pass a folded note containing one **drachma**, a **notable** foreign coin, held fast by tiny **pinchers**. The note said the clerk had been taken aboard during a depot **broil**, and that the rail company's forged land deed must not reach the capital.

列车长用一通斥责迟到旅客的长篇谩骂打发了她的问题。内拉全神贯注于线索，注意到袖口有植物污渍，还有一股无形的陈烟气味。有人暗中递来一张折好的纸条，里面夹着一枚用小钳子固定的德拉克马银币，一种颇有名气的外国钱币。纸条说文员是在车库的争斗中被带上车的，铁路公司伪造的土地契约绝不能送到首都。

In the first coach, a painted **digraph** marked the compartment door. Its occupant was a **gaunt** surveyor whose **corrosive** temper broke when Nera touched the handle. He raised a coal **mallet** and said the deed concerned a mountain **granary** whose lease had been **enduring** proof of village ownership. Nera had learned not to **idolize** frightened men. She watched moonlight **shimmer** on his boots and waited for him to **prevaricate**.

第一节车厢里，一组彩绘字母标在包厢门上。里面住着一名瘦削的测量员，内拉碰到门把手时，他那刻薄暴躁的脾气立刻爆发。他举起一把煤锤，说那份契约关乎山中的粮仓，其租约一直是村民所有权的长久凭证。内拉早已学会不去崇拜害怕的人。她看着月光在他的靴子上闪动，等待他支吾其词。

He did, reciting nervous **poesy** when she asked why his boots bore the same mud as the cuff. Nera would not **quibble**; her **prescience** came from observing, not magic. When she named the isolated branch line, he began to **blanch**. He tried to **flout** her authority, calling her a mere station **urchin** with a **coltish** imagination. Instead, she produced the **shred** of paper found in the baggage room, upon which the clerk had written a **limerick** about a false milepost.

果然如此。当她问为何他的靴子沾着和袖口相同的泥土时，他紧张地背起诗句。内拉不愿争论；她的预见来自观察，而非魔法。她说出那条偏僻支线的名字时，他脸色发白。他试图藐视她的权威，说她不过是个车站小孩，有着小马般莽撞的想象力。内拉却拿出行李房找到的一张碎纸，文员在上面写了一首关于假里程碑的打油诗。

"You cannot **divine** a crime from rhyme," the surveyor said. A **scuffle** in the corridor interrupted his **discursive** defense. The train's guard, a **nettlesome** man called Finch, arrived with his anger still **unassuaged**. He had found the clerk's sketch: a triangle, with the tunnel as its **hypotenuse**, and a stick figure in the pose of a **mime**. Finch accused Nera of **mendacity**, but the clue was perfectly **apprehensible** to someone who knew the line.

“你不可能从韵文里占出罪行，”测量员说。走廊里的一场扭打打断了他冗长的辩解。列车警卫芬奇赶来，怒气仍未消散，是个令人厌烦的人。他找到了文员的草图：一个三角形，隧道是它的斜边，还有一个摆出默剧演员姿势的火柴人。芬奇指责内拉撒谎，但熟悉线路的人完全能读懂这个线索。

At the next table sat a traveling **synod** of churchmen. Their papers began to **flutter** in the **diurnal** timetable breeze. Nera had grown **accustom** to their patronage at the old **manor**, yet she accepted no **undue** praise or **panegyric** from them. The eldest remained **stoic** as Nera showed him the **indelible** stamp on the torn paper. It named a **pallid**, **secular** foundation that had purchased the route.

邻桌坐着一群外出的教会人士。他们的纸张在每日时刻表带来的风中飘动。内拉早已习惯他们在老庄园里的资助，却不接受不当的赞美或颂词。她向最年长者展示碎纸上无法抹去的印记时，他依然镇定。印记写着一个苍白而世俗的基金会，它买下了这条线路。

The stamp was **suggestive**, not conclusive, but the timing was **exceptional**. A loose **filly** had blocked the northern signal only an hour earlier. Through a **sidelong** glance, Nera saw Finch conceal a ledger beneath a blanket. He claimed it recorded railway **philanthropy**, though its first entry was a **forerunner** shipment of **edible** stores marked with a printer's **lithotype** seal. Every **participant** in the shipment had been told to **indoctrinate** the villagers into accepting the sale.

这个印记只能提供暗示，并非定论，但时机异常。仅一小时前，一匹走失的小母马还挡住了北边的信号。内拉从侧目中看见芬奇把一本账册藏到毯子下。他称那是铁路慈善记录，然而第一笔却是一批带有印刷石版封印的先行食品货物。每个参与运送的人都被吩咐向村民灌输接受出售的观念。

The dining-car cook, a **termagant** in a floury apron, said the ledger showed plain **venality**. Her knowledge of **botany** told her that the green stain was crushed yew, gathered beside the tunnel. A **rookie** fireman had carried it in. The boy was **mercurial**, alternating between bravado and tears, until the train's stern **matriarch** passenger handed him a chipped **porcelain** cup. He said an **epizootic** among livestock had emptied the valley, leaving the railway a small **microcosm** of its frightened people.

餐车厨师穿着沾满面粉的围裙，脾气火爆，她说账册清楚显示了贪腐。凭着植物学知识，她认出绿色污渍是隧道旁采集的压碎紫杉。一名新手司炉把它带了进来。男孩情绪多变，一会儿逞强，一会儿落泪，直到车上一位严厉的女长者递给他一只缺口瓷杯。他说牲畜间的流行病已掏空山谷，铁路成了那群惊恐人们的小小缩影。

"I can **acquit** the witness of theft," he said, "but not the men who took him." Nera asked to **assay** the metal dust on his sleeve; it matched the mine. She refused to **prolong** his fear with false comfort. His account was **sincere**, despite the **exiguity** of what he knew: the clerk had seen something **unutterable**, something nearly **unspeakable** in its **fraudulence**. It had made the cab crew **roil** with panic.

“我能证明那名证人没有偷窃，”他说，“但不能证明带走他的人无罪。”内拉要求化验他袖子上的金属粉尘；它与矿场相符。她拒绝用虚假的安慰延长他的恐惧。尽管所知甚少，他的讲述很真诚：文员看见了某种难以言说的东西，其欺诈性几乎令人无法开口。这使驾驶室人员陷入恐慌。

The company's **doctrine** of private efficiency had become pure **extravagance**. Its promised worker **complement** and widows' **pension** were a lie, yet executives laughed with **uproarious** confidence in an **austere** rear saloon. Nera felt **ire**, not surprise. The deed authorized a **divestiture** of the valley at the turn of the **neap** tide, when river barges could **tarry** unseen. Each agent had a fixed **quota** of signatures to secure.

公司所谓私人效率的信条已变成纯粹的奢靡。承诺的完整工人编制与寡妇养老金都是谎言，可高管们仍在朴素的后车厢里放声大笑，自信满满。内拉感到愤怒，却不惊讶。契约授权在小潮转向时剥离山谷，那时河驳船可以悄然停留。每名代理人都有必须取得的固定签名额度。

She tried to **soothe** the fireman, but a muffled **carnage** of breaking crockery came from the baggage van. The deed was legally **alienable**, Finch insisted, since no **consanguinity** linked the villagers to the original grantors. His voice had the dulling force of a **depressant**. Nera was **partial** to documents over speeches, and she recognized the brittle **papyrus** fibers in the forged deed. Then a sharp **detonation** shook the train; someone had fired a signal cap.

她试图安抚司炉，却听见行李车传来瓷器破碎的沉闷声响。芬奇坚持说，这份契约在法律上可以转让，因为村民与最初授予人之间没有血缘关系。他的声音有镇静剂般令人麻木的力量。内拉更相信文件而非演说，她认出伪造契约上脆弱的纸莎草纤维。随后一声尖锐爆响震动列车；有人点燃了信号火帽。

A fresh **shaving** of brass lay by the brake wheel. Nera's own **foible** was noticing small mechanisms, and this one revealed a tampered release. The clerk's **mandate** had been to deliver the authentic deed to a **parisian** archivist. To steal it was **ignoble**; to kill for it worse. Nera began to **brood** over every overlooked detail, then chose to **garner** evidence instead. The forged seal would **characterize** the culprit better than any accusation.

制动轮旁有一片新削下的黄铜屑。内拉的习惯是留心小型机械，而它揭示了被动过手脚的释放装置。文员受命将真正的契约交给一位巴黎档案员。偷走它已属卑劣，为它杀人更为恶劣。内拉起初反复思索每个被忽略的细节，随后决定收集证据。伪造的印章比任何指控都更能刻画凶手。

At the rear window, Finch stood in **gape** at the black valley. A locomotive **growl** seemed to **exude** from the **empyreal** darkness above the ravine. Nera ordered the crew to **rearrange** the passengers forward. The prospect of a runaway wagon began to **appall** them, but the conductor could **constrain** the panic. Finch called the precaution **frivolous**, a fashionable safety **fad**, though the tunnel mouth flashed with a strange **radiance**.

芬奇在后窗前张口凝望漆黑的山谷。机车的低吼仿佛从峡谷上方天际般的黑暗中渗出。内拉命令乘务员把乘客重新安排到前方。失控车厢的可能性令众人惊恐，但列车长能够控制恐慌。芬奇把这项预防叫作轻浮、赶时髦的安全风潮，尽管隧道口闪过奇异的光亮。

Nera told him to **dispose** of the false ledger. His **repugnance** exposed his **egoism**. In the mail van she found a **bale** of maps and, beneath it, the clerk's **conviction** that the true deed survived. He had left a **proffer** of proof for anyone willing to look. A child's toy car had been used to **coddle** suspicion away from the cargo; its hollow held a key. Nera understood the **sanctity** of the promise she had made the clerk and took only water for **sustenance**.

内拉要他处理掉假账册。他的抗拒暴露了自私。在邮政车厢里，她找到一捆地图，下面是文员坚信真正契约仍存的证据。他为愿意查找的人留下了一份证明。一辆儿童玩具车被用来把怀疑从货物上引开，空腔里藏着一把钥匙。内拉明白自己对文员所作承诺的神圣性，只喝了水维持体力。

The key opened a lockbox containing a provincial **gazette**, a marriage **troth** record, and an **earthenware** token from the valley. Together they would **underlie** the villagers' claim. The box also held train **mechanics** sketches, marked by a greasy **hack**. Nera was **chary** of the obvious interpretation, yet the marks showed where heat would **vitrify** the brake pipe. Their **pervasion** through the drawing was as exact as an **intracellular** stain.

钥匙打开的锁盒里有一份省报、婚约记录和一枚山谷出土的陶质信物。它们合在一起将支撑村民的主张。盒中还有火车机械草图，上面留着油腻的粗劣刻痕。内拉对显而易见的解释保持谨慎，但痕迹显示高温会在哪处使制动管玻璃化。它们遍布图纸的方式精确得像细胞内的染色。

The scheme's **legitimacy** depended on the crash. Nera did not need to **emphasize** its horror. Finch's **predilection** for shortcuts had made the sabotage **intractable** to ordinary repair. He tried to **manipulate** the conductor, who answered, "**damn** your orders." The plan was plainly **iniquitous**. Finch became **choleric**, then offered a **soupcon** of truth: the company had paid him after the genuine deed was **tainted** with a forged notation.

这项阴谋的合法外观依赖于撞车。内拉无需强调其可怕。芬奇偏爱走捷径，使破坏难以用普通手段修复。他试图操纵列车长，对方回答：“见鬼去吧，你的命令。”计划显然邪恶。芬奇勃然大怒，随后透露了一点真相：真正契约被伪造批注污染后，公司就付钱给了他。

He had memorized the story by **rote**, he said, because a **skinflint** director held every debt over him. Nera struck the emergency **gavel** in its case and announced an **eventual** stop. Finch watched her hands **hover** above the brake valve, looking for an **appropriate** moment to interfere. His earlier attempt to **mismanage** the controls could not **discombobulate** her. The pressure gauge's **denominator** scale showed a tiny **glitch**, but it was enough.

他说自己靠死记硬背记住那套说辞，因为一位吝啬的董事拿所有债务压着他。内拉敲响盒中的紧急锤，宣布最终将停车。芬奇盯着她悬在制动阀上方的手，寻找合适时机干预。他先前试图错误操纵控制装置，却无法使她慌乱。压力表分母刻度上显示出一个微小故障，但已经足够。

She chose an **eclectic** remedy: **mediate** the brake force from three cars, then tie a red **chevron** cloth to the lamp. The conductor began to **convince** the passengers to stay low. From his pocket he produced a small **missal**, where the clerk had hidden a coded **plait** of thread. Nera's **lithe** fingers could **extricate** it without tearing the page. It led to a guard's **badge** sewn inside Finch's wool **mantle**.

她选择了一种综合办法：从三节车厢调节制动力，再把红色人字纹布系在灯上。列车长开始劝说乘客伏低身体。他从口袋掏出一本小祈祷书，文员在其中藏了一股编码的线绳。内拉灵巧的手指能将它解出而不撕破书页。线索指向缝在芬奇羊毛披肩内的一枚警卫徽章。

The insignia was intended to **alter** the duty record and place Finch aboard during the theft. Outside, **coniferous** trees crowded the line. Nera told the young fireman he could **earn** his courage by carrying a warning **spur** to the engine. He made an **outstretch** of his arm and ran. Finch, now the chief **suspect**, leaped after him, but a coal-heaver with **amphibious** boots blocked the passage. The resulting **obstruction** saved the boy.

这枚徽章意在篡改值勤记录，证明芬奇在盗窃时就在车上。车外，针叶树拥挤在铁路线旁。内拉告诉年轻司炉，他可以把警示信号送到机车来赢得勇气。他伸开手臂跑了出去。如今成为头号嫌疑人的芬奇跳起来追他，却被一名穿防水靴的铲煤工挡住通道。这场阻挡救了男孩。

The heaver braced himself against a steel **pillar**, trembling but **tremulous** only in appearance. A loose, **motile** coupling swayed above an **excess** of steam. Nera judged him **estimable**. He revealed that the company's **regent** had ordered a secret **merger** with a mining concern whose **submarine** mortgages were worthless. A **slapdash** wreck would erase the evidence and blame the valley's poor **drainage**.

铲煤工靠住钢柱，虽然发抖，却只是表面颤栗。一只松动且活动的联结器在过量蒸汽上方摇晃。内拉认为他值得敬重。他透露，公司掌权者已下令与一家矿业企业秘密合并，其水下抵押物毫无价值。一场草率制造的事故能消灭证据，并把责任归咎于山谷排水不良。

Nera could **draw** no comfort from that. The directors had falsely blamed local **obesity** for laborers' illness, a **reproach** designed to deny their **rightful** claims. Their language was **repulsive**. In the food stores she found a dead **decapod** preserved in a jar, a prank beside a book on **cosmology**. Then another **blast** sounded. A **portly** man in a fur collar seemed to **evanesce** through the next door.

内拉无法从中得到安慰。董事们谎称工人的病是当地肥胖所致，以此责难并否认他们应有的诉求。这种说法令人反感。在食品储藏处，她发现一个罐中保存着死去的十足目甲壳动物，旁边是一本宇宙学书，像个恶作剧。接着又响起一声爆炸。一名戴毛皮领的肥胖男子仿佛从隔门消失了。

It was Director Hale, already in **disrepute** for **multiform** fraud. His crops had failed to **fructify**, he explained, despite his public lectures on **frugality**. In **lieu** of honest payment, he had sold the line. Nera saw with **lucid** certainty that the sabotage **draft** had come from him. He had mapped a **semicircle** around the tunnel, intended to make the derailment look **seditious**, as if villagers had begun the **genesis** of a revolt.

那是黑尔董事，他早因多种欺诈而声名狼藉。他解释说，尽管公开宣讲节俭，他的庄稼却没有结果。作为诚实付款的替代，他卖掉了这条线路。内拉清楚地确定，破坏计划出自他手。他在隧道周围画出半圆，意图让脱轨看似煽动性行为，好像村民发动了一场叛乱。

The emergency stop lay beyond a low **knoll**. Hale planned to **desecrate** the evidence by fire. The lamp-lit clock showed **dimly** that dawn was near. Nera needed to **stymie** him before the train entered the gorge. Finch tried to **sneak** toward the coupling with a **preemptive** wrench. Hale's **agenda** required the clerk to vanish too. The captive had a swollen **sprain** at his wrist, but he was alive behind a cargo **buffer**.

紧急停车点在一座低丘之后。黑尔计划用火毁坏证据。灯照着的时钟模糊显示黎明将近。内拉必须在列车进入峡谷前阻止他。芬奇拿着预先准备的扳手，试图偷摸走向联结器。黑尔的计划也要求文员消失。俘虏手腕扭伤肿起，却还活着，藏在货物缓冲器后。

The clerk said the valley's **ecosystem** would be destroyed by the mine. Hale called him a **bastard** and promised to **reinstate** the brakes only after he signed. Nera let Hale **ramble** until his **propinquity** to the brake cabinet betrayed him. When he moved to **trample** the clerk's satchel, she used the signal cord to **elicit** a shriek from the whistle. The sound crossed an **arborescent** hillside and brought the coal-heaver back with a tin of **liniment**.

文员说矿场将毁掉山谷生态系统。黑尔骂他是私生子，并承诺只有签字后才恢复制动。内拉任由黑尔胡扯，直到他靠近制动柜的举动暴露自己。当他要踩踏文员的书包时，她拉动信号绳，让汽笛发出尖啸。声音越过树木繁茂的山坡，把带着一罐药膏的铲煤工叫了回来。

The whistle's **plangent** note carried her warning to the engine. With her **utmost** strength, Nera climbed the little **pulpit** beside the mail rack and worked the **unwieldy** manual brake. The task seemed **insurmountable**, until the conductor chose to **humanize** the frightened passengers by giving each one a clear job. Even Finch's former **opponent**, the cook, showed **irrepressible** resolve. She named the company's **corruption** before everyone.

汽笛凄切的声音把警告传到机车。内拉用尽力气爬上邮架旁的小高台，操作笨重的手动制动器。任务似乎无法完成，直到列车长给每位惊恐的乘客一项明确工作，使他们重新感到自己被当作人对待。连芬奇以前的对手，那位厨师，也展现出不可遏制的决心。她当众揭露公司的腐败。

A **seasoned** miner seized Finch. The guard was **pugnacious**, but a **congenital** limp slowed him. Hale, a stage **magician** in his spare time, made an **acute** distraction with an **extempore** shower of coins. Nera did not pause to **scavenge** them. The brake system needed a dangerous **fusion** of manual and air pressure, and she made the clerk help under **duress**. This was no **championship** contest; it was survival.

一名经验丰富的矿工抓住芬奇。警卫很好斗，但天生的跛行拖慢了他。黑尔闲时是舞台魔术师，临时撒出一把硬币，制造了尖锐的干扰。内拉没有停下来捡钱。制动系统需要危险地结合手动与气压，她强令文员协助。这不是争夺冠军的比赛，而是求生。

The engine **foreman** understood when the whistle changed pitch. He sent a **bromide** message over the telegraph, then refused to **misattribute** the failure to the villagers. Water began to **impinge** on the rails from a broken tank. Nera and the crew had to **collaborate** across the jolting cars. They would **confront** Hale at the engine, but first the cook used a cloth and **salve** to bind the clerk's wrist.

汽笛变调时，机车领班明白了。他通过电报发出一则平淡的信息，随后拒绝把故障错误归到村民头上。破损水箱的水开始冲击铁轨。内拉和乘务员必须在颠簸的车厢间协作。他们将到机车处面对黑尔，但厨师先用布和药膏包扎了文员的手腕。

Their **diverse** skills made the choice less **temerarious** and more **actionable**. The hand brake was **hamstrung** by a cut chain; each turn made Nera's arms **throb**. The engine chief spoke in a **paternal** tone that did not suit the **humdrum** emergency procedure. Beyond the last bridge, no **urban** patrol could help. Only the old stone **ford** and its floodwater would **permeate** the valley if the train left the rails.

他们多样的技能让选择不再鲁莽，而更能实施。手制动被一根断链废掉，每转一次，内拉的手臂便抽痛。机车长以父亲般的口吻说话，却不适合这项平常的紧急程序。过了最后一座桥，城里的巡逻队再无法帮助他们。若列车出轨，只有那座旧石渡口和它的洪水会漫过山谷。

Hale offered to **withdraw** the deed charge if Nera released him. He performed like an **orator**, asking her to **restrain** the crowd and accept the **mutability** of property. Nera rejected that **concession**. The passengers were no **rabble**; their fear and courage formed a **counterpoint** to the wheels. The clerk, still **enamored** of legal precision, pointed out a **distortion** in Hale's signature. It was **weird** but decisive, like a **kaleidoscope** pattern made by a broken seal.

黑尔提出，只要内拉放了他，他就撤销契约指控。他像演说家一样表演，要求她约束人群，并接受财产的可变性。内拉拒绝让步。乘客不是乌合之众；他们的恐惧和勇气与车轮声构成对位。仍热衷法律精确性的文员指出黑尔签名中的变形。那很怪异却很关键，像破损印章留下的万花筒图案。

No newspaper **pundit** could call the evidence less than **paramount**. The key witness was an **albino** switchman hidden in the sleeping car's laundry closet, not a **parasite** on the railway as Hale had claimed. Nera began to **acquaint** him with the facts while the cook produced the recovered **contraband** papers. The testimony was **proportionate** to the crime: a planned wreck, theft, and coercion. The real **drama** had a **transparent** motive, Hale's hunger for **preeminence**.

没有哪位报纸专家能说这些证据不至关重要。关键证人是一名藏在卧铺车洗衣间的白化病扳道工，并非黑尔所称的铁路寄生者。厨师拿出找回的违禁文件时，内拉开始向他说明事实。证词与罪行相称：预谋事故、盗窃和胁迫。这场真正戏剧的动机透明无比，就是黑尔对显赫地位的渴求。

His speeches borrowed **arthurian** honor while concealing **persecution** of the valley. A **jocund** porter, suddenly **savvy** to the danger, supplied a plain **narrative** of the station abduction. Hale tried a racial **slur** against the clerk, making the passengers **excitable**. He hoped to **subvert** their sympathy with his old **verve**, but the interruption was brief. In that **interlude**, the young fireman managed to **gladden** everyone by locking the rear brake.

他的演说借用了亚瑟王式的荣耀，却掩盖对山谷的迫害。一名原本快乐的搬运工突然看清危险，讲述了车站绑架的朴素经过。黑尔对文员作出种族侮辱，激得乘客愤慨。他希望靠旧日的激情颠覆他们的同情，但这段打断很短。在那片刻间，年轻司炉锁住后制动器，让众人欣慰。

The horizon turned **incarnadine**. Hale's **tightfisted** brother, a silent **sibling** on the board, tried to **rumple** the genuine deed. The clerk recognized him from a **pediatrics** charity account, where missing funds had bred public **mistrust**. An **advertiser** had praised that charity in a **sumptuous** pamphlet, beside a **votive** picture of a hospital. In fact, the money had paid for anti-inflammatory medicine while the children suffered **inflammation** untreated.

地平线转为血红。黑尔吝啬的兄弟，也是董事会中沉默的一员，试图揉皱真正的契约。文员从一笔儿科慈善账目中认出他，失踪的资金曾引发公众不信任。一名广告商在华丽小册子中赞美那家慈善机构，旁边还有医院的献奉图画。事实上，钱买了消炎药，孩子们的炎症却没有得到医治。

Nera read the account aloud, preserving proper **observance** of the evidence. It described an **intramural** dispute in which Hale was called a **blowhard**. The accusation had caused much **chaffing**, but the witness confirmed it. Hale caught the cook's **bodice** and made a **derisive** threat. She told him he was a **nonentity** before the law. The **aforesaid** assault made his **commitment** to violence unmistakable.

内拉朗读账目，恰当地遵守证据程序。账目描述了一场内部争执，其中黑尔被称作吹牛大王。这个指责招来许多戏谑，但证人证实了它。黑尔抓住厨师的上衣，作出嘲弄的威胁。她告诉他，在法律面前他微不足道。这起前述袭击使他诉诸暴力的意图再清楚不过。

The conductor moved to **chastise** him, but Nera stopped him; a broken **vaporizer** tube had filled the passage with steam, making any **offhand** strike dangerous. The clerk traced an **afferent** wire from the brake alarm to a cut switch. The engine chief had to **unyoke** the tender before its **foetid** water reached the firebox. The emergency lever was **reflexible** only with a heavy **anvil** counterweight, stored in the dining **refectory**.

列车长要惩罚他，内拉却拦住了他；破裂的蒸汽管让过道充满蒸汽，任何随手一击都很危险。文员顺着一根输入线，从制动警报追到被切断的开关。机车长必须在臭水进入火箱前脱开煤水车。紧急杠杆只有配上沉重的铁砧配重才可回弹，配重存放在餐厅。

The coal-heaver carried it by the **fringe** of its sling, trusting its **tensile** rope. They were **latish** by minutes, and the **asymmetrical** load pulled sideways. An **elastic** strap from the luggage cart let them **smuggle** the weight through the crowded corridor. Their arrival would **coincide** with the last chance to stop. Through an **airy** gap in the clouds, Nera saw the station's distant signal.

铲煤工抓着吊带边缘搬运它，相信绳索的抗拉力。他们已晚了几分钟，不对称的负载向一边拉扯。一条来自行李车的弹性带让他们把重物偷偷穿过拥挤走廊。他们到达时将正好赶上最后的停车机会。透过云层中空旷的缝隙，内拉看到了车站远处的信号。

She issued a formal **requisition** for the tool. Hale delayed with a verbal **filibuster**, hoping they would **dally** until the train rounded the gorge. But the passengers formed a **quorum** and voted by **expectancy**, not panic. The porter cut a **trim** strip from his cap's **plumage**. Nera used its red-and-white **dichotomy** to flag the engine. The **gangly** fireman ran it forward amid a **multiplicity** of swaying shadows.

她正式征用这件工具。黑尔以冗长的言语阻挠拖延，希望他们会磨蹭到列车绕过峡谷。但乘客组成法定人数，带着期待而非恐慌投票。搬运工从帽饰羽毛上剪下一条整齐布带。内拉利用它的红白双色向机车示警。高瘦的司炉在无数摇晃的影子中将它跑着送往前方。

The clerk explained the **juridical** proof while Nera made the **ironic** discovery that Hale had signed it himself. The **benighted** gorge opened beneath them, its **spinous** pines guarding the valley's **heritage**. Hale answered with an **epithet**, then tried a final **manoeuver** toward the door. The coal-heaver caught him with an **indestructible** grip. "No," the heaver said, **curt** and final. The evidence had become a **synthesis** no **biased** tribunal could ignore.

文员解释法律证据时，内拉讽刺地发现黑尔本人签过字。昏暗的峡谷在他们脚下展开，带刺的松树守护着山谷遗产。黑尔回以辱骂，随后作最后一次动作，朝门口冲去。铲煤工用不可挣脱的力道抓住了他。“不。”他简短而决绝地说。证据已形成任何偏颇法庭都无法忽视的整体。

At that moment Nera became the train's **cynosure**, though she hated attention. Hale made a cruel **genital** insult at the cook, and the conductor's loyal **factotum** recorded it. In Hale's pocket they found a **counterfeit** seal, wet with **condensation**. The directors had tried to **institutionalize** fraud, but their lack of **forethought** now ruined them. Hale remained **obnoxious** even as the porter applied a **plaster** to the clerk's wrist.

那一刻，内拉成了列车众人瞩目的中心，尽管她讨厌受关注。黑尔对厨师作出残酷的性侮辱，列车长忠实的助手记录了下来。他们在黑尔口袋里发现一枚沾着冷凝水的假印章。董事们曾想把欺诈制度化，但缺乏远见如今毁了他们。即使搬运工给文员手腕贴上膏药，黑尔仍然惹人厌恶。

The switchman, no **malingerer**, used a rail spike to **scarify** Hale's forged stamp. A survivor from the local **peerage** confirmed that the grant had never expired. The air outside was **frigid**, and the invoices proved illegal **importation** of explosives. Hale began to **brag** that no court would believe a night witness. "You **guess** wrong," Nera said. His **pretension** to immunity had met a **peerless** body of evidence.

扳道工并非装病者，他用铁轨钉划坏黑尔的伪造印章。一名当地贵族遗族证实，授予从未到期。外面空气刺骨，发票证明有人非法进口炸药。黑尔开始吹嘘没有法庭会相信夜间证人。“你猜错了。”内拉说。他自以为能免责的妄想，遇上了一组无与伦比的证据。

The **actuality** was simple: the forged deed sought to **cleave** the valley from its people. The clerk showed that a changed **synonym** in the contract had reversed the owner and tenant. Hale tried to **assail** him, but the porter held him fast. The churchmen offered **alms** for the wounded fireman, who was now **reliant** on the cook's care. Before dawn, the guard radioed ahead to **obtain** police at the next siding.

事实很简单：伪造的契约企图把山谷从其人民手中割裂。文员指出合同中一个被替换的同义词颠倒了所有者和租户。黑尔试图攻击他，但搬运工牢牢制住了他。教士们为受伤司炉提供施舍，他现在依赖厨师照顾。黎明前，警卫通过无线电联系前方，在下一处侧线请求警察。

The train stopped short of the washed-out bridge, denying Hale his planned **rapine**. The tunnel behind them gave a **sepulchral** sigh, as though it remembered the **preexistence** of every lie. At its base, a natural **cavern** held the stolen original and the demolition charges. Finch confessed the **wile** that had hidden them. By the first gray **quarter** of sunrise, officers placed Hale and Finch under arrest.

列车停在被冲毁的桥前，挫败了黑尔计划中的掠夺。身后的隧道发出坟墓般的叹息，仿佛记得每个谎言早已存在。在隧道底部，一个天然洞穴藏着被盗原件和爆破炸药。芬奇坦白了用来藏匿它们的诡计。日出最初的一片灰光中，警员逮捕了黑尔和芬奇。

No **grandiose** speech followed. Nera gave a **monitory** account to the officer, then accepted warm **nectar** from the cook. Dawn turned the rain **opalescent** on the rails. The recovered seal, stamped in twenty-four **karat** gold, lay beside the feed **trough** where Hale had dropped it. His **opprobrium** was complete. The clerk, once a **prominent** official, watched light restore **variegation** to the soaked hills.

之后没有华丽的演说。内拉向警员作了警示性的陈述，随后接过厨师递来的温热甜饮。黎明把铁轨上的雨水映成乳白色光彩。找回的印章以二十四开黄金制成，躺在黑尔丢下它的饲料槽边。他的耻辱已然彻底。那名曾显赫一时的文员看着阳光让湿透的山丘恢复斑斓色彩。

At the terminus, the **laundress** returned his torn coat, still **meager** protection against the cold. Finch began another **tirade**, but Nera refused its **prolixity**. She boarded the waiting **sleeper** for home, carrying certified copies of the deed and the knowledge that the valley would see another dawn.

在终点站，洗衣女工还给他破损的外套，仍只能勉强抵御寒冷。芬奇又开始一通辱骂，内拉拒绝听那冗长废话。她登上等候回家的卧铺车，带着经过认证的契约副本，也带着山谷将迎来又一个黎明的确信。
