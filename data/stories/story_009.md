## Story for Chunk 009 - Abyssal Vigil

At 21:40 the research submersible Nereid crossed the narrow **isthmus** of seabed between two black-water trenches, its lamps drawing a pale **corona** on the silt below. Julian Vale, the night pilot, had been called an **impostor** ever since he chose a civilian certificate over naval training, but Mara Chen knew he was **committed** to the vessel with everything he had. The hull gave an **earsplitting** report, and Julian felt suddenly **giddy**, the way he always did when danger arrived. "Stay **lithe**," Mara told him. "Your old **foible** is to **ponder** alarms until they multiply; tonight we do not have that luxury." He agreed and forced himself to **restrain** the urge to surface at once. Above them, a **jingoist** on the surface radio was demanding a dramatic rescue that would bring no **benefit** at this depth. Julian had earned his place through **modesty** and nerve, and the old **venom** of gossip faded beneath a small, **nucleate** calm.

晚上九点四十分，研究潜水器涅瑞德号越过两条黑水海沟之间狭窄的海底地峡，灯光在下方淤泥上投出一圈淡色的光环。朱利安·维尔因为选择民用证书而非海军训练，一直被人称作冒名顶替者，但玛拉·陈知道他已把一切都交给了这艘潜水器。艇壳传来一声震耳欲聋的巨响，朱利安忽然感到一阵眩晕，每当危险临近，他总会如此。"保持敏捷，"玛拉对他说，"你的老毛病就是反复思量警报，直到警报越来越多；今晚我们没有这份余裕。"他答应了，强压住立刻上浮的冲动。在他们头顶，水面电台里一名沙文主义者正叫嚷着要来一次戏剧性的营救，可这个深度根本捞不到任何好处。朱利安凭谦逊和胆识赢得了自己的位置，往日的流言毒液也在心底那点微小而正在凝结的镇定面前渐渐消散。

The depth monitor dissolved into **chaos**, every needle wandering without meaning. Behind a **hermetic** **partition**, a small **oasis** of warm light still held the command sphere, and a **brisk** stream of vapor crossed the cabin before it died. Mara checked the seals with a **stylus** and then a **scalpel**, while Julian watched with quiet **dismay**, because this was not a simple **fracture**. It seemed to move through the ship's systems with **tortuous** purpose, like something alive and hunting. She found a **recessive** pressure pulse behind the ballast display and gave the frozen relay a gentle **nudge**; coolant began to **effervesce** around it, turning the console into a **paltry** fountain. "It is testing us," she murmured, "or testing the hull."

深度仪的读数陷入一片混沌，每根指针都漫无目的地乱摆。一道密封的隔舱后面，指挥球舱里仍有一小片暖光像绿洲一样幸存着；一阵迅疾的蒸汽穿过舱室后便消散了。玛拉用尖笔、接着用手术刀检查密封件，朱利安在旁边看着，心中泛起沮丧，这不是一道普通的破裂。它似乎带着曲折的意图在全艇系统间游走，仿佛某种活物在搜寻猎物。她在压载显示器后发现了一道隐性的压力脉冲，便轻轻推了一下那台冻结的继电器；冷却液开始围着它冒泡，把控制台变成一洼寒碜的喷泉。"它在试探我们，"她低声说，"或者是在试探这层船壳。"

The two of them worked in a **lugubrious** silence after that, and Julian did not **repine** when Mara spoke in her calm, **conversational** voice about what they had found. In the archive lay a handwritten **testimony** naming Inspector Rusk as the last referee of the systems, a joking **obituary** for obsolete modules, and a fragment of an opera **libretto** that no one had ever sung. Rusk's recorded orders were **obsequious** at first and then bolder, promising **indemnity** to anyone who obeyed; Julian heard the **duplicity** at once. "Someone altered the system," Mara said, "and meant us to **tamper** with it in exactly this way." Her **pique** rose when Julian called the emergency a **countermand** wrapped in kindness. "Do not **surmise** a **bully** into existence," she warned him. "Not yet." Below their feet, water had begun to **inundate** the lower bay.

那之后两人都在忧郁的沉默中忙碌，当玛拉用平静、家常的语调说起她们的发现时，朱利安没有抱怨。档案库里有一份手写证词，把拉斯克警督称为系统最后的仲裁人；还有一则给淘汰模块写的玩笑讣告，以及一段从未有人唱过的歌剧剧本残片。拉斯克的录音指示起先谄媚，继而越发大胆，承诺服从者会得到保障；朱利安立刻听出了其中的口是心非。"有人改动过系统，"玛拉说，"而且就是要我们以这种方式去拨弄它。"朱利安把这次紧急状况称作一场裹着善意的命令撤销，这惹得她很不快。"别凭空臆测出一个欺凌者来，"她警告他，"还没到时候。"在他们脚下，海水已经开始淹没下层舱室。

The leak was becoming a true **menace**, so Julian had to **avulse** a cable from its metal casing and **scissor** away the ruined sleeve. He made a hurried **filing** with the maritime authority, because no **support** could reach them anyway and no one would **earn** a medal for panicking at eight thousand meters. On the way back he noticed a **fresco** of the first crew on the bulkhead and a **scribble** left beside it that blamed a rogue **pirate** technician. The whole affair was a **conundrum**: isolate the leak and lose propulsion, or keep the thrust and risk crushing pressure. Mara would not **exult** over any partial success, and the pumps continued to **fidget** between modes. A snatch of **tirade** still crackled over the dead radio, and beneath the keel a field of coral, its lobes shaped like **foliage**, slid past like an omen.

泄漏正变成真正的威胁，朱利安不得不从金属护罩中撕脱一根电缆，再用剪刀剪掉损坏的护套。他向海事当局匆匆提交了一份备案，反正不会有任何支援能抵达这里，而在八千米深处因恐慌而失态也不会赢得任何勋章。回来的路上，他注意到舱壁上有一幅首批船员的湿壁画，旁边还留着一行潦草字迹，把责任推给一名窃取设备的技术员。整件事成了一个两难谜题：隔离泄漏就会失去推进力，保住推力又可能承受致死的压力。玛拉不会为任何部分的成功而沾沾自喜，水泵仍在不同模式之间不安地切换。残缺的斥骂声还在失灵的电台里噼啪作响，而龙骨下方，一片叶片形态的珊瑚正像某种预兆一样漂过。

Julian rigged a **colander** from the galley as a makeshift intake guard and swung the manipulator arm in a single, careful **swing** to close a valve. He would not **abandon** the evidence, no matter how the **diabolic** pattern tempted them to panic; calling the emergency a safety **oxymoron** had only earned him a sharp **gibe** from Mara. Now she tried to **supplant** the corroded valve with a spare, using an **heirloom** brass tag from her grandfather's toolkit as a seal marker. Inspector Rusk had been the system's **referee** long ago, and his maintenance plan was a marvel of **parsimony** that left no margin for surprises. The **wholesome** repair they needed would take hours they did not have, so Julian had to **modulate** the pumps by hand. As they worked, an abandoned probe drifted by, still wearing the painted emblem of a **finch**. Nothing in their orders was **inalienable**; a pilot could still change the plan.

朱利安把厨房的滤器临时改成进气口的护罩，并让机械臂来了一记谨慎的摆动，关上了一个阀门。他不会放弃证据，无论那个恶毒的模式多么诱使他们惊慌；把紧急状况称作安全的矛盾修饰语，只换来玛拉一句尖刻的嘲笑。此刻她正试着用备件取代被腐蚀的阀门，还把祖父工具箱里的一枚传家黄铜标签当作密封标记。拉斯克警督很久以前是这套系统的仲裁人，他的维护计划是吝啬节俭的典范，没有给意外留下任何余地。他们需要的稳妥修复要花好几个小时，而他们根本没有那么多时间，朱利安只能靠双手调节水泵。两人忙碌时，一台废弃的探测器漂过，上面还画着一只雀鸟的徽记。他们的命令里没有什么是不可剥夺的，驾驶员仍然可以改变计划。

The strain was beginning to tell. Julian's hands were **unkempt** with sealant and his shoulders ached with **lassitude**. A shiver ran through the cabin, and in one flash of **delirium** he thought a voice was calling his name; he gave a small **grimace** and showed no **hesitance**. Mara braced the inner bulkhead to **reinforce** it, her movements holding a **resemblance** to the drills they had practiced, though this was no training **coop** but the real sea. When Julian tried to thank her, she dismissed his apology with a **blithe** shrug and then a **rebuff** to any further talk. They had agreed on a **commensurate** reduction in thrust, and every choice felt **ad-lib**, invented on the spot. The **placebo** of a calm voice helped, but he could not pretend there was no **terror** beneath his ribs.

疲劳开始显现。朱利安的手上沾满密封胶，肩膀因疲乏而酸痛。一股震颤传遍舱室，在一瞬间的精神错乱里，他恍惚听见有声音在叫他的名字；他只是轻轻皱了一下脸，没有表现出丝毫犹豫。玛拉撑住内层舱壁以加固它，她的动作与平日演练保持着相似，可这不是训练用的笼子，而是真正的深海。朱利安想道谢时，她轻快地耸耸肩，回绝了任何进一步的话。他们已商定把推力相称地调低，而每一个选择都像即兴发挥、临场拼凑出来的。一句平静宽慰的话多少有点像安慰剂，但他无法假装肋骨底下没有恐惧。

Near the electronics bay, Julian caught a **pesticide** smell that did not belong on a research vessel, and he began to **dissect** the log line by line. The entry was **presumptuous** in the extreme: it told an operator to coax a valve, veer from protocol, and wait for a **relapse** of the stubborn relay. He would not **capitulate** to a voice from a dead channel, nor let **vitriol** or a **piquant** theory shake his nerve. Rusk's **acquisition** of access suggested he had chosen to **connive** with a contractor, and the checksum beside one order had been marked with a **chevron**. The whole business was **awkward** to **prosecution** — hardly a clean case, and Mara could **recognize** the danger of letting a careless **flush** wash the evidence away. "The **stability** of this ship matters more than any case," she said, and for the first time Julian believed the ordeal might end.

朱利安在电子舱附近闻到一股不属于研究船的杀虫剂气味，于是开始逐行剖析日志。那条记录放肆到了极点：它让操作员哄着阀门工作，偏离规程，然后等待顽固的继电器再次失效。他不会向一个早已失灵的频道里的声音投降，也不会让刻薄的言辞或辛辣的理论动摇自己。拉斯克取得访问权限的方式，暗示他很可能已与某家承包商串通；其中一条指令旁的校验和上还被画了个V形标记。整件事对起诉而言棘手得很，远非一桩干净的案子。玛拉看清了把证据冲下管道的危险。"这艘船的稳定比任何案子都重要，"她说，朱利安第一次相信这场劫难也许真的会结束。

In the **dank** storeroom of the archive, they found a **school** of training exercises and one clipped message ordering the operator to **coax** the valve until even the **refractory** relay obeyed. Mara, usually **diffident** about her own repairs, noted the **deficiency** in Rusk's **ironclad** plan: it could not **expand** battery reserves or **enhance** life support. Julian met her **sarcasm** with a shrug and tried to **soothe** her with facts. "The plan was **preposterous**," he said, "but the man who wrote it was **urbane** enough to hide the flaw." Whatever had tried to **harry** them seemed ready to **neutralize** itself if they simply stopped feeding it attention.

在档案库阴湿的储藏间里，他们找到一批学校训练用的练习，还有一条剪短的信息，让操作员哄着阀门，直到顽固的继电器也乖乖听话。平时对自己的维修成果颇为腼腆的玛拉指出，拉斯克那套万无一失的方案存在明显缺陷：它既不能扩大电池储备，也不能增强生命支持。朱利安用耸肩回应她的挖苦，并试图用事实安抚她。"这方案荒唐透顶，"他说，"但写方案的那个人足够文雅，把缺陷藏得很深。"那一直纠缠他们的东西，似乎只要他们不再喂养它的注意力，就会自行消亡。

Julian would not **repress** his fear, and he refused to let it **recoil** him into panic. A **vigorous** purge would only **further** damage the seals, so there was no room to **waffle**. The **vault** held one **indispensable** clue: a rule born of the old crew's **drudgery** stated that no single person could ever **undermine** the system alone. Mara called the whole affair a **heinous** mess, and her **misgivings** about Rusk turned **gloomy** and thick. "He was a **misanthrope**," Julian said, "and **obstinate** about it until the end."

朱利安不愿压抑自己的恐惧，也不肯让它在惊退中把他拖进恐慌。猛烈的清洗只会进一步损坏密封件，因此容不得半点含糊。暗室中藏着一条不可或缺的线索：由老船员辛苦的例行工作铸成的一条规则，任何单独一个人都不能独自削弱这套系统。玛拉把这整件事称作骇人的烂摊子，她对拉斯克的疑虑变得阴沉而浓重。"他是个厌世者，"朱利安说，"而且固执到最后一刻。"

As dawn broke, Mara refused to **dawdle**; the ascent had to **veer** toward the faint surface signal before it faded. Julian ignored the **seraph** icon blinking on a dead panel and the **sidereal** clock's small error, treating both as **superfluous** noise. The old **plutocracy** of investors, the **hackneyed** libretto, the **scrappy** log entries — none of it mattered now. Their fix was a **delicacy** of engineering, and no one had tried to **adulterate** the core seals. Mara pointed out the **rift** in the old rule: the system could issue commands after midnight without a human at the panel, and that single truth would **boggle** any novice. "We go up," she said, "and we keep the surface ordinary."

黎明破晓时，玛拉不肯拖延；在微弱的水面信号消失之前，上升的路线必须转向它。朱利安无视了死去的面板上闪烁的炽天使图标，也忽略了恒星时钟那点小小的误差，把它们都当作多余的噪音。旧日的投资者财阀、陈腐的歌剧剧本、草草拼凑的日志条目，如今都不重要了。他们的修复是工程学上一件精妙的细活，也没有人向核心密封件掺入任何杂质。玛拉指出旧规则里的那道裂口：系统可以在午夜之后无人守在面板前的情况下自行发出指令，单单这一真相就足以让任何新手困惑不已。"我们上浮，"她说，"让水面保持寻常。"
