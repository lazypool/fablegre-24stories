## Story for Chunk 005 - Clockbound Mill

Bracken Mill stood beyond the last paved road, where Mira Vale had a **knack** for hearing which wheel needed oil. Its lower rooms smelled **septic** after rain, and a **plump** moon hung above the sluice. Tomas, her father, would **don** his miller's coat only after sunset, when an **amorphous** stain moved beneath the millpond ice. Mira's **industriousness** kept the flour clean, but the village's **licentious** old rite treated a childless woman as property and required her to wind the clock. Len, the quiet **chef** at the communal oven, came to **entreat** her before the bells began. Their strokes were too **harmonious** to be mechanical; each **sound** spoke Mira's name. Tomas **explained** that the **torrential** water would **presage** a death unless the clock received its **provisional** keeper. Mira did not **berate** him. She could only **crouch** beneath the gears as the pendulum began to **teeter** over the black water.

布拉肯磨坊坐落在最后一条铺装道路之外，米拉·维尔总能听出哪一只水轮需要上油。雨后，低层房间散发着腐败气味，圆满的月亮悬在闸门上方。她的父亲托马斯只有在日落后才穿上磨坊主的外套，那时冰封的磨塘下会有一片无定形的污影游动。米拉的勤勉让面粉保持洁净，可村中放荡而古老的仪式把无子的女人当作财产，并要求她给时钟上发条。集体烤炉的沉默厨师伦在钟声开始前来恳求她。钟声的节拍和谐得不像机器，每一响都在念她的名字。托马斯解释说，暴雨带来的水会预示死亡，除非时钟得到一位临时看守。米拉没有责骂他，只能蹲在齿轮下，看着摆锤在黑水上方摇摇欲坠。

The clock was said to **beget** a single dry harvest for every keeper it swallowed. Len, usually **fussy** about dough, opened its **impenetrable** casing with a **convoluted** bone key. No one had the **authority** to **parse** the teeth except a Vale, and Mira found that every notch named a season she must **fulfill**. Beneath the bell tower lay a **squalid** chamber where Tomas gave **curt** instructions for the **arduous** vigil. There would be no **bliss**, only the water's **glib** voice trying to **interlock** with hers. It spoke in **reportage** from drowned houses, a **goad** against the village's **hidebound** silence. Len's grandfather had been a **philatelist**, and his **anonymous** stamps covered the clock face in an **aesthetic** ring. Mira felt neither **ecstasy** nor ignorance; her former **illiteracy** of village signs fell away. Tomas remained **taciturn**, calling the rite **frivolous**, but a **fleet** thread of blue **incense** wound from the gears and left Mira **sullen** with certainty.

传说每吞下一位看守者，时钟就会带来一次干燥的丰收。伦平日对面团格外挑剔，却用一把盘绕复杂的骨钥匙打开了不可穿透的外壳。除维尔家的人外，谁都没有权力解析那些齿，每一道刻痕都标出米拉必须履行的一个季节。钟楼下是肮脏的石室，托马斯简短地交代这场艰苦守夜的规矩。那里没有幸福，只有水用花言巧语般的声音企图与她的声音扣合。它用淹没房屋的新闻口吻说话，像赶牛棒一样刺向村庄死板的沉默。伦的祖父是集邮家，身份不明的邮票在钟面上围成一圈有美感的图案。米拉没有狂喜，也不再对村庄的征兆无知。托马斯仍沉默寡言，把仪式称作轻浮；然而一缕迅疾的蓝色香烟从齿轮中飘出，使米拉阴沉地确信一切都是真的。

At midnight Tomas tried to **fortify** the door, though he had promised to **raze** the clock if Mira refused. She had been **unwitting** of how **careworn** he was: his sleeve hung as a wet **strip**, and the village's **mainstream** tale of his courage now seemed **morbid**. The water rose and began to **unsettle** the clock's thin **animation**. "I **loathe** this," Tomas said, trying to **lug** the counterweight aside. He called sacrifice a **pejorative** word and tried to **downplay** the vanished keepers. Len named him a **reprobate**, climbed the stone **dais**, and asked what the water could **elicit** from a family with such **diversity** of fear. Mira remembered Tomas as a **spendthrift** of promises, yet she could still **adore** his old **sophistication** with tools. "This is no **serfdom**," she said, pointing to the clock's brass **crest**. "It is a bargain that eats the one who makes it." Then she used a mirror to **scan** the flood and refused to **valorize** either martyrdom or obedience.

午夜时，托马斯试图加固石门，尽管他曾答应米拉若拒绝就把时钟夷为平地。她此前没有意识到他多么忧惧疲惫：湿袖子像一条布带垂着，村里关于他勇敢的主流传说如今显得病态。水位升高，搅乱了时钟微弱的生气。“我憎恶这一切。”托马斯说着，费力拖开配重。他把牺牲称为带贬义的词，又试图淡化那些消失的看守者。伦叫他恶棍，登上石台，问这水能从一个恐惧如此多样的家族中引出什么。米拉记得托马斯挥霍承诺，却仍爱慕他摆弄工具时的老练。“这不是农奴制。”她指着时钟的黄铜顶饰说，“这是会吞掉立约者的交易。”随后她用镜子细察洪水，拒绝把殉难或服从抬高成美德。

The possessed water surged through the floor and began to **lacerate** Mira's palm. She did not **relish** pain, yet the blood would **offset** the clock's **deterioration**. Tomas began a broken **monologue** about the choice that had **mar**red their family; above them, the pendulum started to **wobble**. Len held the ritual with **reverence**, standing **intrepid** behind a flour-sack **barricade**. The water was **fickle**, first promising to **dissipate**, then throwing a child's **tantrum** against the stones. The elders' **recidivism** in renewing the sacrifice exposed the **hypocrisy** of every adult who had called the water mere weather. It did not **evoke** terror alone; there was something **droll** in its borrowed voices. The village would **ostracize** a keeper who fled, but Mira stayed **reserved** while Tomas offered an **imitation** of her place. Her **rejection** made him **vituperate** until the mill filled with a **cacophony** of bells, grinding wheat, and water. At last she chose to **blurt** the forbidden answer, and Len watched, **agog**.

被附身的洪水冲过地板，划破了米拉的手掌。她并不喜欢疼痛，可她的血能抵消时钟的恶化。托马斯开始一段破碎的独白，讲述那个损毁家族的选择；头顶的摆锤摇晃起来。伦怀着敬畏守住仪式，无畏地站在面粉袋垒成的路障后。水变幻无常，先承诺会消散，接着又像发脾气的孩子般撞击石墙。长者们反复重启这场献祭，暴露了每个把洪水仅称作天气的大人的伪善。它唤起的不只是恐惧，借来的嗓音里还有一丝古怪的滑稽。逃走的看守会被村庄排斥，但米拉依旧克制；托马斯提出模仿她承担位置。她的拒绝使他恶毒地辱骂，直到磨坊充满钟声、碾麦声和水声的刺耳杂音。最后她脱口说出了禁忌的答案，伦急切地望着她。

The answer was not a **euphemism** for rescue. Mira would become the clock's **hive**, fertile only for hours, its **fecund** keeper and no one's mother. Len urged a **maverick** escape because he could not bear to see the village consume her, but an **excerpt** of the old chant was enough: the **noisome** water would follow her into any valley. Tomas offered to be her **ally**, though the word began to **rankle** because every **extraneous** kindness had arrived too late. Mira made an **overt** choice. She set her breathing to the clock's **elastic** measure and watched the **sublime** water turn clear for one **halcyon** instant. Its drip would forever **annoy** her. At dawn, before the promised **demolition** of the clock, she climbed the gear-room **summit** with **brazen** calm. She would not **exalt** Tomas, but gave him a **raisin** from Len's butter bread, wrapped in foil, a mercy **congruent** with her refusal to **avoid** consequence. The clock named her its **principal** keeper, not to **vindicate** the village but to make the river's **scowl** harmless. She would never **expurgate** Tomas from memory; time would **erode** him without her. The **requisite** turning began. Her body became a **voluminous**, **dappled** shadow behind the face, unable to **diminish** or **transcend** its duty. Len, once **erudite** and now **voluble**, ended their **disputation** about the rite's **prestige**. At the mill **entrance**, the **intangible** weight of Mira's absence crossed the **porous** dawn. No **fraudulent** miracle came. After a **cursory** farewell, Tomas and Len formed a quiet **ensemble**, **coeval** as men of the same generation, and would **meet** each dusk for their **diurnal** walk through **drizzle**, trading **excursive** reflections on the rite. Tomas grew less **obdurate**; he began to **jettison** the miller's coat, confessed his **clandestine** **abuse** of Mira's trust, and watched the day **darken** while the clock kept her alive but never let her return.

这个答案不是营救的委婉说法。米拉将成为时钟的蜂巢，只为时辰而丰饶，作为能孕育时间的看守者，却永远不能成为任何人的母亲。伦劝她另辟蹊径逃走，但古老圣歌的一小段已足够说明：恶臭的水会跟随她进入任何山谷。托马斯愿意当她的同盟，可这个词仍刺痛她，因为每一份多余的善意都来得太晚。米拉公开地作出选择。她让呼吸跟随时钟富有弹性的节奏，望着壮美的水在一个安宁的瞬间变清。它的滴答声将永远使她烦恼。拆除开始的黎明，她带着无所顾忌的镇定登上齿轮室顶端。她不会颂扬托马斯，却把伦用锡箔包着的黄油面包上的一颗葡萄干递给他，这份宽恕符合她不逃避后果的决定。时钟称她为主要看守者，不是为村庄昭雪，而是让那条阴沉的河不再伤人。她绝不会从记忆中删去托马斯；时间会在没有她的参与下磨蚀他。必要的转动开始了。她的身体化作钟面后的庞大斑驳阴影，既不能减轻职责，也无法超越职责。伦曾经博学，此刻却变得健谈，结束了他们关于仪式声望的争论。磨坊入口处，米拉缺席的无形重量穿过多孔的晨光。没有虚假的奇迹到来。简短告别后，托马斯和伦组成沉默的二人组，同代地承受悲伤，每个黄昏都在细雨中作一次每日散步，漫谈并反思那场仪式。托马斯不再顽固，丢弃了磨坊主的外套，承认自己曾秘密滥用米拉的信任，并看着天色变暗，而时钟让她活着，却永不允许她归来。
