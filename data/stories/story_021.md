## Story for Chunk 021 - Salt Horizon

Dr. Leda Voss, a desert **podiatrist** who had learned to read a blister as other doctors read an X-ray, was three hours into a rescue flight over the Kharif **savanna** when the pilot's radio began to **fluctuate**. Below, a **galaxy** of salt pans caught the late light with **considerable** and false **grandeur**; beyond them stood the survey camp's **gaudy** water tower, its small **turret** bent by wind. The call had said that Arman, a cartographer, had collapsed after a **hike** along the irrigation line. Leda's **supposition** was heatstroke, but the **fateful** choice to land came when Noor, the **svelte**, **saturnine** **pilot**, pointed to smoke leaking from the engine cowling. “We can **divert** to the ranger shed,” Noor said in her **terse** way. A **whiff** of hot oil entered the cabin, then the engine answered with a **jarring** cough. Leda could **infer** neither their height nor the machine's remaining will; she only watched the salt flats **coalesce** into a pale, **uncanny** map beneath them.

莉达·沃斯医生是一名沙漠足病医生，她能从一个水泡里读出讯息，就像别的医生读X光片。她乘救援直升机飞越哈里夫热带草原三小时后，飞行员的无线电开始波动。下方，一片片盐田在迟暮的光中闪耀，形成一片规模可观而虚假的壮丽，远处是勘测营地俗丽的水塔，那座小塔楼已被风吹弯。求救讯息说，制图师阿尔曼沿灌溉渠徒步后倒下了。莉达猜测是中暑，但当飞行员努尔指着从发动机罩缝隙漏出的烟时，降落成了决定命运的选择。努尔身材纤细，神情阴郁而寡言。“我们可以绕到护林员的小屋，”她说。一股热油味钻进机舱，接着发动机发出刺耳的咳嗽声。莉达无法推断他们的高度，也无法判断机器还剩多少意志，只看见盐地在脚下汇合成一张苍白而离奇的地图。

They put down beside the shed at dusk. Its **perimeter** was a drift of **sawdust**, fish bones, and empty fuel tins; a **rodent** fled through a gap in the **rampart** of sandbags. Arman lay under an awning, his clothes in **disarray**, his bootlaces **slack**, the **epidermis** of his heel split open. He had tried to **malinger** through a foot injury, he admitted, because the expedition leader called caution a **farce** and mocked him as a **dolt**. “That leader is a **libertine** with a compass,” Noor said, her **raillery** too sharp to **mollify** him. The wound's **malodor** made Leda **bristle**. She could **identify** a long thorn embedded below the **superficial** cut, but its **protean** **shadow** on the portable scanner began to **vex** her. Arman offered an **anecdote** about a snakebite, then tried to **dupe** them into believing the pain was ordinary. Leda's rebuke did not **irritate** him for long. A jar of water held three **tadpole** larvae and an **aquatic** beetle, proof that the nearby channel still ran. While Noor used a towing strap to **hitch** the damaged helicopter to an anchor, Leda took the **crucial** step: she would **extract** the thorn before the swelling could **inflame** the whole foot.

他们在黄昏时降落在小屋旁。小屋周界散着锯屑、鱼骨和空燃料罐，一只啮齿动物从沙袋壁垒的缝隙里逃走。阿尔曼躺在遮棚下，衣服凌乱，靴带松弛，脚跟表皮裂开。他承认自己曾假装伤势不重，想硬撑过去，因为探险队领队把谨慎称作闹剧，又嘲笑他是个笨蛋。“那个领队是个拿着罗盘的放荡鬼，”努尔说，她的戏谑太尖锐，没能安慰阿尔曼。伤口的恶臭让莉达全身发紧。她能辨认出有一根长刺嵌在浅表伤口下，可它在便携扫描仪上的变形阴影开始让她烦恼。一只水罐里有一只蝌蚪和一只水生甲虫，证明附近水渠仍在流动。努尔用牵引带把损坏的直升机拴在锚点上时，莉达采取了至关重要的一步：在肿胀引起整只脚发炎前，她要取出那根刺。

Rain began, **inclement** and **incessant**, drumming a **barrage** against the corrugated roof. Leda heated forceps over a stove, then used **bracing** water to **irrigate** the wound; the man's pain rose with **vociferous** **fury**, but she did not yield. A **conspicuous** black thread emerged from the cut. It was not a thorn but the root of a poisonous grass, and the scanner's **discrepancy** made sense: the root had entered beside the bone and was curving **up** toward an artery. Noor, who had a **penchant** for old machinery and a **tenacious** respect for procedure, found a field **syllabus** in the emergency kit. Its **figurative** drawings were useless until she noticed the actual **order** of the blood vessels. “I can **decode** the image,” she said. Leda had an **inkling** that the grass was the same species Arman had been sent to **discover**. She refused to let the leader's **caprice** **victimize** him further. With a **cogent** instruction to hold still, she used a **backhanded** pull and drew out a **prodigious** root, black as a **grotesque** bristle. Arman did not **respire** for one terrible beat. Then he coughed, and the shed's stale **effluvium** gave way to rain.

雨落了下来，天气严酷，雨声不断，密集地敲打着波纹铁皮屋顶。莉达在炉子上加热钳子，又用清凉提神的水冲洗伤口。阿尔曼的疼痛化作喧闹的狂怒，但她没有退让。一根醒目的黑丝从伤口中露出。那不是刺，而是一种有毒草的根，扫描结果中的不一致也就说得通了：根部从骨旁进入，正向上弯向一条动脉。努尔偏爱老机械，对规程也有顽强的敬重，她在急救包里找到一份野外课程提纲。其中的比喻性图示毫无用处，直到她看出了血管真正的排列顺序。“我能解读这张图，”她说。莉达隐约觉得，这种草正是阿尔曼被派来发现的物种。她拒绝让领队的一时任性再使他受害。她用一句有说服力的指示叫他别动，以反手的拉法拔出一条长得惊人的根，黑得像一根怪诞的硬毛。阿尔曼有一拍没有呼吸。随后他咳了起来，小屋里陈旧的恶臭废气被雨的气息替代。

By morning, the antidote kit was empty except for a **cameo** vial of mineral paste. Leda could **procure** more only by reaching the abandoned pumping station two kilometers away, where the channel crossed a **mire**. Arman asked them to leave him, but that was the old **delusion** of a useful man. Noor said the helicopter could not fly until she replaced a cracked fuel line, and there were **scads** of reasons to stay: the storm had torn the map, damp cloth had failed to **insulate** the engine, and a **pitiless** flash flood had erased the track. Still, a **perceptible** blue gleam pulsed through the grass, a **harbinger** of water moving in the drainage ditch. Leda made an **inventory** of bandages, rope, and the **restorative** tablets left; she told Arman, with more **tact** than warmth, that he must sip, not **nibble**, the hard biscuit. “You cannot malinger your way out of this,” Noor said, then laughed at the wrongness of her own joke. The three of them took the **detour** along the rampart, where the flood had begun to **grind** shells into the mud.

到早晨，解毒包里只剩下一小瓶浮雕宝石般的矿物膏。莉达只有到两公里外废弃的泵站才能设法取得更多药物，水渠在那里穿过一片泥沼。阿尔曼叫她们留下他，可那只是一个自认为有用之人旧有的错觉。努尔说，在她更换开裂的燃油管之前，直升机无法起飞，而留下来的理由多得很：暴雨撕碎了地图，努尔还得用湿布给发动机隔热，残酷的山洪冲掉了小径。然而草丛里有一抹可察觉的蓝光在跳动，那是排水沟里水流的先兆。莉达清点了剩下的绷带、绳索和恢复体力的药片，又用多于温情的机智告诉阿尔曼，他必须小口喝水，不能只啃硬饼干。“你不能靠装病脱离这个困境，”努尔说完，随即因自己玩笑的不妥而笑了。三人沿着沙袋壁垒绕路前行，洪水已经开始把贝壳磨进泥里。

At the pumping station, they found no antidote but a locked cabinet and the survey leader's **prologue** to a failed paper, in which he had tried to **vaunt** himself as a **visionary** who would **vanquish** the desert. Leda felt no wish to **indict** a man absent from the plain; she had seen how easily a **juggernaut** of ambition could **abet** harm without a **knave** at its center. The leader had ordered the workers to **repeal** rest breaks, called a **sapient** technician a **sluggard**, and demanded **perjury** on the injury forms; his **aggressive** **reign** had left **untold** harm. Yet the cabinet held a hand pump, and Noor managed to **doff** its rusted cover, **hone** the valve with a file, and **amalgamate** two broken tubes into one. The water they raised was bitter but **tasty** after a day of salt, and its **plasticity** in the clay seal proved the well still had pressure. Leda mixed the mineral paste with charcoal for **detoxification**, then watched the swelling recede. A **clarion** call of cranes crossed the brightening flats. Arman whispered a **valediction** to the map he had ruined, then asked to **emigrate** from fieldwork, not from fear but because he wanted a life that did not mistake pain for proof. Noor said nothing **acrimonious**; she simply started the repaired engine. In a faded **chiaroscuro** mural, an old **prude** in the camp's first **melodrama** held a pump lever above a painted **skeleton**; the **portentous** image seemed to **reveal** nothing but the artist's fear. Leda did not **abominate** the leader, nor did she **decry** Arman's wish to leave; **esteem** for work could not excuse a **repugnant** system. Noor used the **lathe** in the shed to cut a **wax** plug for the fuel line, then told Leda with **concision** that the weather was **propitious**. As the rotors lifted them above the salt pans, Leda did not **hallow** survival or preach a **sermon** about it. She used the clean back of the syllabus to **jot** one line: “Never let **fervor** **supersede** **probity**, **objectivity**, or **equality**.” She would **instill** that rule in every trainee, not as **mesmerism** or a **fad**, but as a way to **discern** what no forecast could **predict**.

在泵站里，他们没有找到解毒剂，却发现一个上锁的柜子和勘测队领队一篇失败论文的前言，他在其中自夸是能征服沙漠的远见者。莉达并不想指控一个不在平原上的人，她见过野心这股不可阻挡的力量多么容易助长伤害，即使核心没有一个恶棍。领队曾命令工人废除休息时间，把一名明智的技术员叫作懒汉，并要求他们在伤情表上作伪证。他那咄咄逼人的统治留下了无法尽述的伤害。可柜子里有一台手泵，努尔设法卸下生锈的盖子，用锉刀磨利阀门，并把两根破管合并成一根。他们抽出的水虽苦，但在经历一天盐味后却很可口，黏土封口的可塑性证明井里仍有压力。莉达把矿物膏与木炭混合，用来解毒，然后看着肿胀消退。一群鹤发出清亮的鸣叫，飞过逐渐明亮的盐地。阿尔曼低声向他毁掉的地图告别，接着说想离开野外工作，不是出于恐惧，而是因为他想过一种不把疼痛误当成证明的人生。努尔没有说任何尖刻的话，只是启动了修好的发动机。褪色的明暗对照壁画中，营地第一出情节剧里的一位老古板站在画出的骷髅上方，手握泵杆。那幅不祥的画似乎只显露出画家的恐惧。莉达并不憎恶领队，也不谴责阿尔曼想离开的愿望，对工作的敬重不能为一个令人厌恶的制度开脱。努尔用小屋里的车床切出一枚蜡制塞子补燃油管，又以简洁的话告诉莉达，天气正适合起飞。旋翼把他们带离盐田时，莉达没有把幸存神圣化，也没有就此说教。她在课程提纲干净的背面匆匆记下一句：“绝不能让热忱取代正直、客观或平等。”她会把这条规则慢慢灌输给每一位受训者，不把它当成催眠术或一时的风尚，而把它当成一种辨明任何预报都无法预测之事的方法。
