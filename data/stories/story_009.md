## Story for Chunk 009 - Abyssal Vigil

At 21:40, the research submersible Nereid crossed the narrow **isthmus** of seabed between two black-water trenches, its lamps making a **corona** on the silt. Julian Vale, the night pilot, had been called an **impostor** after taking a civilian certificate instead of naval training, but Mara Chen knew he was **committed** to the vessel. The hull gave an **earsplitting** report. A **brisk** stream of vapor crossed the cabin, then stopped. Their depth monitor showed **chaos** in the pressure readings. Julian felt **giddy**, yet his **foible** was to **ponder** alarms until they multiplied; tonight he had to stay **lithe**. In the cramped command sphere, an **oasis** of warm light survived behind a **hermetic** **partition**. Mara checked the seals with a **stylus** and a **scalpel**, while Julian tried to **restrain** the urge to surface. The fault was not a simple **fracture**. It seemed to move through the ship's systems with **tortuous** purpose.

晚上九点四十分，研究潜水器涅瑞德号越过两条黑水海沟之间狭窄的海底地峡，灯光在淤泥上形成光环。夜班驾驶员朱利安·维尔曾因没有海军训练背景而被人视为冒牌者，但玛拉·陈知道他对潜水器尽心尽责。艇壳发出震耳欲聋的声响，一股迅疾的蒸汽穿过舱室后消失。深度仪的压力读数陷入混乱。朱利安感到眩晕，他过去总爱反复思量警报，直到警报越来越多，今晚却必须保持敏捷。狭窄的指挥球舱中，密封隔舱后的暖光像一片避难之地。玛拉用尖笔和手术刀检查密封件，朱利安努力克制立刻上浮的冲动。故障不像普通破裂，更像带着曲折意图在全艇系统中移动。

Mara found a **recessive** pressure pulse behind the ballast display, a latent diagnostic signature inherited from the controller's original design, and used a **nudge** to wake its frozen relay. Coolant began to **effervesce** around the relay, turning the console into a **paltry** fountain. Julian had to **avulse** a cable from a metal **coop** that protected the relay, then **scissor** away its ruined sleeve. He made a hurried **filing** with the maritime authority: no **benefit** could come from concealing a leak at eight thousand meters. The archive held an old script for emergency announcements, a joking **obituary** for obsolete modules, and a **fresco** of the first crew. Its handwritten **testimony** named Inspector Rusk as the last systems **referee**. Mara could only **surmise** why his authorization remained active. “Someone can still alter the system,” she said in a calm, **conversational** voice. Julian did not **repine**. He watched the pumps **fidget** between modes and began to **modulate** their output.

玛拉在压载显示器后发现一道隐性的压力脉冲，这是从控制器原始设计中继承下来的潜伏诊断特征，随后轻推唤醒了冻结的继电器。继电器周围的冷却液开始冒泡，把控制台变成小小的喷泉。朱利安从保护继电器的金属笼中撕脱电缆，再剪掉损坏的护套。他向海事主管部门匆忙提交备案，因为在八千米深处隐瞒泄漏不会带来任何益处。档案中有紧急广播脚本，给淘汰模块写的玩笑讣告，以及首批船员的一幅湿壁画。手写证词显示拉斯克警督是最后一位系统仲裁人。玛拉只能推测他的授权为何仍然有效。她平静地说，有人仍能篡改我们。朱利安没有抱怨，只看着水泵在不同模式间不安切换，并开始调节输出。

At midnight, the **diabolic** pattern became plain: a command had opened a valve, then ordered the computer to **countermand** its own warning. Rusk's recorded voice sounded **obsequious**, then **presumptuous**, promising **indemnity** to anyone who followed it. Julian heard **duplicity** in the recording, but Mara refused to name a saboteur. The transmission might be a **placebo** planted to reassure the crew while directing suspicion elsewhere, or Rusk might be the **bully** hiding behind a dead channel. Her **pique** rose when Julian called the order a **heinous** **oxymoron** of safety. “Do not **abandon** the evidence,” she said. They would not **capitulate** to a voice, nor let its **venom**, **gibe**, or **tirade** **undermine** them. Julian used a **colander** from the galley as a makeshift intake guard and a **chevron** marker to trace the coolant path. The water began to **inundate** the lower bay. Their only choice was a **conundrum**: isolate the leak and lose propulsion, or preserve thrust and risk crushing pressure.

午夜时分，恶毒的模式变得清楚，一道指令打开阀门，随后又命令电脑撤回自己的警报。拉斯克的录音起先谄媚，继而傲慢，承诺会给服从的人保障。朱利安从录音里听出欺骗，但玛拉拒绝断定谁是破坏者。那段传输可能是安抚船员、同时把怀疑引向别处的安慰剂，也可能拉斯克就是躲在失效频道后的欺凌者。朱利安把命令称为安全的骇人自相矛盾，她因此感到不快。玛拉说，不要放弃证据。他们不会向一个声音投降，也不会让它的恶意、嘲笑或斥骂动摇自己。朱利安用厨房滤器临时保护进气口，再用V形标记追查冷却液路线。海水开始淹没下层舱室。两人面临难题，隔离泄漏会失去推进力，保留推力则可能承受致命压力。

Julian chose a **commensurate** reduction in thrust and told Mara to **reinforce** the inner bulkhead. The work was **awkward** and **scrappy**: she used an **heirloom** brass tag from her grandfather's toolkit as a seal marker, while he tried to **supplant** a corroded valve. His hands were **unkempt** with sealant and his shoulders ached with **lassitude**. A **recoil** shook the cabin. Mara showed no **hesitance**, though a flash of **delirium** made her **grimace** at a voice she thought was Julian's. He used a **swing** of the manipulator arm to close the valve, then heard the pressure hull answer with a low groan. Their small act of **support** could not **neutralize** every **menace**, but it bought time. Julian would not **repress** his **terror** or let **misgivings** **harry** him. He could **recognize** the new **stability** only after the gauges stopped wandering.

朱利安选择相称地降低推力，并让玛拉加固内层舱壁。这项工作笨拙而临时，她把祖父工具箱里的一枚传家黄铜标签当作密封标记，朱利安则设法替换腐蚀阀门。他的手沾满密封胶，肩膀因疲乏而酸痛。舱室震动了一下。玛拉毫不犹豫，尽管短暂的精神错乱让她对自己以为是朱利安的声音皱起脸。朱利安摆动机械臂关上阀门，随后听见耐压舱发出逐渐减弱的呻吟。他们的支撑无法消除所有威胁，却争取了时间。朱利安不会压抑恐惧，也不会让疑虑纠缠自己。直到仪表停止游移，他才能确认新的稳定状态。

Near dawn, the Nereid drifted above a field of **foliage**-shaped coral, its cameras catching a lone **finch** emblem painted on an abandoned probe. Julian found a **pesticide** smell in the electronics bay, an **indispensable** clue that could belong to Mara, Rusk, or an earlier **pirate** technician. The evidence had no **resemblance** to a clean **prosecution**, and neither of them wanted one. Mara's **modesty** kept her from claiming the repair; Julian's **diffident** apology made her **soothe** him with a **blithe** shrug. Their argument over **parsimony** in Rusk's old maintenance plan remained unresolved. Was the failure an **acquisition** of access by a saboteur, a **preposterous** automatic test, or one of the system's flaws? They would **dissect** the log at the shore lab, not before. The archive had a **dank** **vault** full of **school** exercises, one **ad-lib** drill, and a **hackneyed** **libretto** fragment that Mara had already read. Its technician had left a **scribble** about a **nucleate** sensor, a **flush** of hydraulic fluid, and a **gloomy** note that the pressure model could **boggle** any novice. One clipped message told the operator to **coax** the valve, then to **veer** from protocol and **dawdle** until a **refractory** relay could **relapse**. Another called the emergency **ironclad**, though its **deficiency** was obvious: it could not **enhance** life support or **expand** battery reserves. Julian saw **adulterate** beside a checksum and wondered whether Rusk had chosen to **connive** with a **misanthrope** contractor. Mara rejected that **sarcasm** with a sharp **rebuff**, and his **vitriol** sounded **obstinate** even to him. The log described a **plutocracy** of investors, a **jingoist** captain, and an **urbane** consultant, but no confirmed culprit. A **vigorous** purge might **further** damage the seals; a **wholesome** repair would take hours they did not have. Julian refused to **waffle**. He would not accept a **superfluous** **seraph** icon, a **sidereal** clock error, or a **piquant** theory as proof. The crew's old **drudgery** had produced an **inalienable** rule: no one may **tamper** alone. Mara noted a **rift** in that rule, because the system could issue commands after midnight without a human. It was a **delicacy** of software design, not a verdict, and its failure filled them with **dismay**. For now, Julian could **earn** a slow ascent, Mara could **exult** at the first pale surface signal, and both could leave the **lugubrious** night without a public rescue, a hearing, or certainty about who had tried to sink them.

接近黎明时，涅瑞德号漂在一片叶状的珊瑚上方，摄像机拍到一台废弃探测器上的雀鸟徽记。朱利安在电子舱闻到杀虫剂气味，这是不可或缺的线索，却可能属于玛拉、拉斯克或更早的一名盗取设备的技术员。证据不像一场清晰的起诉，两人也不希望如此。玛拉的谦逊让她不愿独占修复功劳，朱利安怯生生地道歉，她轻快地安慰了他。关于拉斯克旧维护计划中过度节俭的争论仍无结论。故障究竟是破坏者取得访问权限、荒谬的自动测试，还是系统自身的缺陷，他们无法确定。他们会在岸上的实验室分析记录，不会在此刻。档案库阴湿，里面存着学校练习、一场即兴演练和玛拉早已读过的陈旧歌剧脚本片段。技术员留下潦草字条，提到成核传感器、液压油猛然涌出，并忧郁地写道，压力模型足以使任何新手困惑。一条剪短的信息要求操作员哄着阀门工作，再偏离规程并拖延，直到顽固的继电器重新失效。另一条把应急方案称为万无一失，可它的缺陷显而易见，既不能增强生命支持，也不能扩大电池储备。朱利安在校验和旁看见篡改一词，怀疑拉斯克是否与一名厌世的承包商串通。玛拉严词驳回这种讥讽，朱利安自己的刻毒话语听来都很固执。记录写有投资者财阀、沙文主义船长和温文尔雅的顾问，却没有确证的嫌疑人。猛烈清洗会进一步损坏密封件，稳妥的修复又需要他们没有的数小时。朱利安不肯犹豫。他不会把多余的炽天使图标、恒星时钟误差或刺激性的理论当作证据。前任船员辛苦的例行工作留下不可剥夺的一条规则，任何人不得独自篡改系统。玛拉指出，这条规则存在裂口，因为系统能在午夜后无人操作时发出指令。这是软件设计的精妙之处，不是定论，而它的失效使他们沮丧。眼下朱利安终于能慢慢上浮，玛拉为最初微弱的水面信号欢欣，两人离开忧郁的夜晚，没有公开救援、听证，也没有谁试图让他们沉没的确定答案。
