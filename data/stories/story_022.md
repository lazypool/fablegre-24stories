## Story for Chunk 022 - Borrowed Sonata

On the afternoon of the conservatory's winter recital, Mara Saye, its **personable** but chronically **dyspeptic** copy editor, climbed to the abandoned **observatory** above the concert hall with her sister Ione's score in a leather case. The dome's **opaque** shutters had been sealed **airtight** since the astronomy program had died, and now Ione used the room to compose. Her new **sonata**, written for a **trumpet** and a childless piano, lay beneath an **exquisite** wool **canopy**, waiting. Mara had come to **proofread** the program, not to **squabble**, yet the **foreword** on the first page tightened the familiar knot behind her ribs.

冬季独奏会当天下午，音乐学院那位待人亲切却长期胃气不和、消化不良的文字校对员玛拉·赛伊，带着姐姐艾奥妮的总谱皮箱，爬上音乐厅上方废弃的天文台。自从天文学项目停办，圆顶不透光的百叶窗便被密封得严严实实，如今艾奥妮就在那里作曲。她为小号和一架没有子嗣的钢琴写的新奏鸣曲，静静躺在一块精美的羊毛天篷下等待。玛拉本是来校对节目单的，不是来争吵的，可第一页的序言又让她肋骨后那个熟悉的结收紧了。

"'A **replicate** of Maestro Vale's **primordial** style,'" she read aloud, and the phrase made her **jitter**. Vale, a **dandy** with a **voluptuous** smile, had once taught them both. His **ubiquitous** portrait hung in the stairwell downstairs; his **hallmark** was an **exotic** runic motif that he claimed no woman could properly **enunciate**. "It is a **gaffe**, not a crime," Ione said, trying to **placate** her. "Do not let one **ellipsis** in a program **overshadow** the music."

"'它是对维尔大师原初风格的复制，'"她念出声，那句话让她紧张得坐立不安。维尔是个穿着浮华、笑容妖娆的花花公子，曾教过她们两人。他无处不在的肖像挂在楼下楼梯间；他的标志性风格是一种异国的如尼纹样，他声称没有女人能把它清晰地表达出来。"这只是失言，不是罪，"艾奥妮说，试图安抚她，"别让节目单里的一个省略号遮蔽了音乐。"

But Mara heard the old **discord** beneath her sister's light **banter**, and she knew that quiet had never meant **quiescence** between them. Later, alone, she opened the score. Its **script** was **shipshape**, every **hem** ruled in red, yet a hidden page had been fastened into the binding with an **awl**. It contained Vale's contract: Ione would receive his **approbation** only if she signed a **submission** renouncing authorship. The proposed **bargain** was **pernicious** precisely because it sounded **venial**.

可玛拉从姐姐轻松的玩笑里听见了旧日的不和，她知道，她们之间的安静从来不是真正的静止。后来，独自一人时，她打开总谱。手稿整整齐齐，每一道页缘都用红线划过，可有一页纸被一把尖锥固定在装订里。那是维尔的合同：只有艾奥妮签字放弃著作权，才能得到他的正式认可。这笔交易之所以有害，恰恰因为它听起来像一件可以原谅的小事。

Vale would **elevate** her, he promised, and she would **consolidate** his legacy; her own melodies would become **synonymous** with his name. Mara's first impulse was to **condemn** him, to **spread** the page among the students, to **banish** his portrait and **pulverize** every engraved motif. Ione stopped her with a hand on the page. "Your **ethics** are not the same as my **emancipation**." She admitted she had almost signed; their mother had called refusal **ingrate** behavior, and Ione's numbness had begun as a defense against that relentless judgment.

维尔答应会提拔她，而她将巩固他的遗产，她自己的旋律将与他的名字同义。玛拉的第一冲动是谴责他，把这页纸在学生中传播开来，赶走他的肖像，再把每件刻着纹样的东西磨成粉末。艾奥妮一只手按住那页纸，拦住了她。"你的伦理并不等于我的解放。"她承认自己差一点签了字；母亲把拒绝称作忘恩负义，而艾奥妮的麻木正是从对这种无情评判的防御开始的。

"I used to **revere** him," she said. "Now his praise can still **excruciate** me." Mara saw, with sudden clarity, that Vale's charm was not an **insentient** mechanism but a practiced **demagogue**'s art, a **chicanery** designed to make a gifted woman mistake obedience for **triumph**. Below them, Vale had begun to **bluster** before the audience about "**fairness** in tradition," while his **obliging** assistant tried to **temporize** with a **prolix**, **monotonous** speech.

"我曾经敬仰他，"她说，"现在他的赞美仍能折磨我。"玛拉忽然看清楚了：维尔的魅力不是一台没有知觉的机器，而是一名煽动者训练有素的本事，一种让有天赋的女人把服从误认成胜利的欺骗术。楼下，维尔已经在对观众大放厥词，谈什么"传统中的公平"，而他乐于效劳的助手则用一篇冗长、单调的讲话拖延时间。

At the **fringe** of the stage, a young **connoisseur** of old instruments used a **blunt** **scythe** as a theatrical prop in a tasteless **travesty** of harvest music, and Ione's hands began to **fluster** her over the keys. "It's only noise," she whispered, but Mara knew better. She found the original program file on the stage **console**, where a **glitch** had erased Ione's name and left an **irreducible** blank. She could have sent the contract by instant **dispatch**, but publicity would not **resolve** the immediate harm. Instead, she used a pencil to **ravel** the false attribution from the printed **summary**.

舞台边缘，一位年轻的旧乐器鉴赏家把一把钝镰刀当作戏剧道具，拙劣地模仿着收获音乐，艾奥妮的手在琴键上慌乱起来。"不过是些噪音，"她低声说，但玛拉知道不是。她在舞台控制台上找到了原始节目文件，一个故障抹掉了艾奥妮的名字，只留下一个无法再缩减的空白。她本可以立即派发合同，但公开曝光解决不了眼前的伤害。于是她用铅笔从印刷摘要中拆开错误的署名。

She asked the registrar to **calibrate** the microphone and let Ione speak. Vale made a quick **dodge** toward the orchestra pit; Mara caught his sleeve. "This concerns **equity**, not your reputation." His face hardened; he called her a **callow** clerk, accused her of **pilfer**ing private papers, and tried to **besmirch** Ione as a **rabid** admirer. The accusation was an **exaggeration**, yet it **discompose**d Ione enough to nearly **bungle** the opening. Then she looked at the **fender** of the old piano, at the bruised **blemish** in its varnish, and began.

她请注册员校准麦克风，让艾奥妮开口。维尔想迅速闪向乐池，玛拉抓住了他的袖子。"这关系到公正，不是你的名声。"他的脸沉下来，叫她是个没有经验的文书，指控她偷取私人文件，又试图诋毁艾奥妮，说她是狂热的崇拜者。这指控当然是一种夸大，却让艾奥妮不安到几乎搞砸了开场。接着她看向旧钢琴的挡板，看向漆面上一块受伤的瑕疵，开始演奏。

The first movement did not **repel** the room; it seemed to **captivate** it, a slow **drone** broken by a sudden **jolt** of brass. A **runic** pattern seemed to **transmit** itself through the hall, then underwent a musical **abscission** when Ione cut it cleanly away. The audience began to **applaud**, not from **apathy** but from **awe**; the response was **appreciable** even to Vale, whose expression had become **inscrutable**.

第一乐章没有排斥这间屋子，反而迷住了它，一段低沉的持续音被突如其来的铜管震动打破。一种如尼纹样仿佛在音乐厅里传递开来，随后当艾奥妮把它干净利落地切开时，经历了一次音乐上的切断。观众开始鼓掌，不是出于冷漠，而是出于敬畏；就连维尔也能感到这份回应，他的表情变得难以捉摸。

No one could mistake its **verisimilitude** for Vale's work. After the applause, which Ione accepted without **delight**, Vale asked for a private **appeal**. In the observatory he offered to **rescind** the contract, claiming a **vagary** of his secretary had caused the error. Mara nearly laughed, but Ione said the mistake was not the page; it was his **inherent** conviction that a woman must be grateful for being borrowed. She would not **cultivate** a scandal, nor **trespass** on the students' recital by turning it into a trial. She asked only that the board **acknowledge** her authorship, remove the foreword, and end his **retainer**.

谁也不会把它误认成维尔的作品。掌声过后，艾奥妮并没有感到喜悦，维尔请求私下申诉。在天文台里，他提出取消合同，声称是秘书一时反常造成了错误。玛拉几乎笑出来，可艾奥妮说，错误不在那页纸，而在他固有的信念，他认定女人必须感激自己被借用。她不会培养一场丑闻，也不会侵扰学生的独奏会，把它变成一场审判。她只要求董事会承认她的著作权，删除序言，并终止付给维尔的顾问费。

Vale called that demand a **debacle** for the school and warned that the music world was **erratic** and **tightfisted**. His **dilatory** excuses began to **infuriate** Mara, but Ione could **outwit** them with silence. "Then let it be," she replied. "I will not **prolong** your lesson through **irresolution**." She said the contract did not **pertain** to music at all, but to an old, **carnal** hunger for possession. Vale called her language **profane** and **infelicitous**. She answered that his apparent kindness had been a **mongrel** thing, part patronage and part theft, and that his **persistence** had let the injury **fester**.

维尔说这个要求会成为学院的一场大失败，又警告音乐界变幻无常而且吝啬。他拖延的借口开始激怒玛拉，但艾奥妮能用沉默智胜它们。"那就如此吧，"她回答，"我不会靠优柔寡断拖长你的课程。"她说这份合同根本不涉及音乐，而是涉及一种古老的、肉体性的占有欲。维尔说她的措辞亵渎且不恰当。她回答，他表面上的善意是个杂种般的东西，一半是恩惠，一半是偷窃，而他的执拗已让伤口溃烂。

When he tried to **digress** into the school's finances, the registrar made him **disgorge** every marked copy of Ione's score. A **disaffected** former pupil left a **dolorous** note beside the **mash** of stale refreshments, saying Vale had tried to **primp** her for his publicity portraits. The old heater's faulty **gasification** filled the room with a mineral smell, yet the prospect of open air seemed to **exhilarate** Ione. Mara watched her sister **amble** to the window and **scour** a fingerprint from the glass. Outside, snow began to **exude** from the dark sky in small wet grains.

当他试图离题谈学院财政时，注册员逼他交出每一份标注过的艾奥妮总谱。一名对他不满的前学生把一张悲伤的纸条留在一堆陈旧点心旁，说维尔曾想把她打扮起来供自己的宣传肖像使用。老暖炉故障造成的气化让房间满是矿物气味，可想到露天，艾奥妮却似乎振奋起来。玛拉看着姐姐从容走到窗边，擦洗掉玻璃上的一个指纹。外面的黑暗天空开始渗出细小潮湿的雪粒。

Ione said that **pediatrics** had taught their mother to console frightened children, not to hear adult daughters; that was a **sequela**, not an excuse. They did not **condole** over the **extinct** version of their family, and they did not **pacify** each other with praise. They left the score open beneath the dome, while below the students held a **raffle** for instrument repairs and the **abundant** city lights made the old observatory seem less a **mystery** than a room with a door. Beyond the glass, a winter **arbor** stood black against the snow; it did not need to **bolster** their resolve. In that moment, leaving was **paramount**—the **abundance** of lights across the city did not promise a kinder world, but it gave the sisters enough brightness to see the icy steps. Mara held the door while Ione went first.

艾奥妮说，儿科训练教会母亲安慰害怕的孩子，却没有教会她倾听成年的女儿；那是一种后遗症，不是借口。她们没有为那个已经消失的家庭版本吊唁，也没有用赞美互相安抚。她们把总谱摊开在圆顶下，楼下学生正为乐器维修举办实物抽彩，城市灯光的丰盛让这座旧天文台看起来不再是谜团，而像一间有门的房间。玻璃外，一座冬日藤架黑沉沉地立在雪中；它不需要支撑她们的决心。此刻，离开是最重要的事。整座城市灯火丰盛，并不承诺一个更善良的世界，却给了姐妹俩足够的亮光看清结冰的台阶。玛拉扶着门，让艾奥妮先走。
