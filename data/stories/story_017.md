## Story for Chunk 017 - Tidal Mercy

Mara Leto had spent twelve years as the sole veterinarian on Vesper Key, where a sandbar road ended at her low, **rustic** quarantine barn and the sea began. Its peeling **molding** and abandoned **harrow** made it less **imposing** than a mainland clinic. Her **ken** was animals, not people: she could **diagnose** a collapsed lung by the **wan** color of a tongue, hear infection in a cough, and locate a blockage in the **lumen** of a newborn calf's intestine. Yet she had never learned how to refuse a frightened owner. At dusk on the first day, Eli Voss, a **hard-bitten** fisher with a **dapper** wool cap, arrived towing a crate that held Rose, his prized **filly**. A **surreptitious** animal broker had sold her to him cheaply at the mainland **terminus**, he admitted, and now she lay **prostrate**, sweating through the **frigid** wind. The inlet outside had become a **frothy** **maelstrom** around the **shoal**, cutting Vesper Key from the ferry. Mara found a **minuscule** puncture under Rose's jaw, a **maculate** swelling, and a faint **stench** beneath the horse's coat. Her **meticulousness** made her take blood before she touched the wound. Under the microscope, a translucent **membrane** enclosed a spiral larva whose **distal** hooks opened and closed. The sight did not petrify her; what did was Eli's **ingenuous** question: “Can you make her well before she reaches my **kinfolk**?”

玛拉·莱托在维斯珀岛上做了十二年唯一的兽医，沙洲公路在她低矮的乡村隔离马厩前终止，大海从那里开始。马厩剥落的装饰线脚和废弃的耙子，使它不如大陆诊所那般气派。她的见识在动物身上，不在人身上：她能从舌头苍白的颜色诊断肺部塌陷，从咳嗽中听出感染，还能在新生小牛肠道的腔内找到阻塞。然而，她始终没有学会如何拒绝一个害怕的主人。第一天傍晚，伊莱·沃斯来了，这位饱经世故的渔夫戴着一顶整洁时髦的羊毛帽，拖着一只箱子，里面是他珍爱的年轻母马罗斯。他承认，一个偷偷摸摸的牲畜经纪人在大陆终点站把她便宜卖给了他，现在她在寒风中大汗淋漓地平躺着。外面的海湾在浅滩周围变成了泡沫翻涌的大漩涡，使维斯珀岛与渡轮隔绝。玛拉在罗斯下颚找到一个极小的刺孔、一处有污斑的肿胀，还在马毛下闻到淡淡恶臭。她一丝不苟，先抽血，才触碰伤口。在显微镜下，一层半透明薄膜包裹着一条螺旋形幼虫，它末端的钩子一张一合。那景象没有把她吓僵，真正令她害怕的是伊莱天真的问题：“你能在她见到我的亲属之前让她好吗？”

The blood smear was **explicit**. The creature was a salt-borer, an organism believed extinct after it had **persecute**d coastal herds by turning muscle to brine. A salt-rich larval secretion could **fertilize** the eggs within warm mammalian tissue; its adult form could **capture** a host by scent and then **rend** the skin to release a chemical that would **induce** panic. Mara had a licensed serum, but the dose was **prohibitive** for a horse and its side effects might **demoralize** Eli, leaving him too dispirited to manage Rose's grueling recovery. Eli asked whether the old **diocesan** who blessed boats could help. Mara did not **opine** on miracles. She said the injection might save Rose but would **convert** her into a temporary reservoir, and that killing her now would prevent an outbreak. Eli's face, normally **rubicund** from wind and drink, went pale. “My wife is **pregnant**,” he said. “She is waiting across the inlet.” He offered Mara a **munificent** sum to keep quiet, then, with greater **temerity**, proposed they **sidestep** the reporting rule and use the barn's drainage channel. His **aboveboard** manner made the offer worse. Only a **concerted** response could contain the salt-borer: the rule required her to call the mainland **media**, notify every neighboring stable, and have the district **underwrite** a cull if the larva proved viable. The broker was an **addict** to easy commissions, but Eli's own **effrontery** had let him believe silence was a cure. The prospect of cameras and an **interminable** inquiry turned Eli into a **nonentity** in his own grief; he began to **cower** beside the crate while Rose gave a thin **caterwaul**. Mara could not **abate** the sound with pity, nor **embolden** him with false promises.

血涂片给出了明确结果。那生物是一种盐蛀虫，据说它在灭绝前曾迫害沿海牲畜，把肌肉变成盐水。只有在盐分丰富的幼虫分泌物它能使温暖哺乳动物组织内的卵受精，它的成虫能凭气味捕获宿主，再撕裂皮肤，释放一种会诱发恐慌的化学物质。玛拉有获准使用的血清，但给一匹马的剂量贵得令人无法承受，而且其副作用可能使伊莱意志消沉，无法承担罗斯艰苦的康复过程。伊莱问，为船只祝福的那位教区主教是否能帮忙。玛拉没有对神迹发表意见。她说，注射或许能救罗斯，却会把她转化为暂时的储存宿主，而现在杀掉她就能阻止疫情。伊莱那张平常被风和酒吹得红润的脸变白了。“我妻子怀孕了，”他说，“她正在海湾对面等着。”他拿出一大笔钱，要玛拉保守秘密，接着又更大胆地提议，他们绕开上报规定，利用马厩的排水沟。他这份坦率反而令提议更糟。只有协同行动才能控制盐蛀虫：规定要求她呼叫大陆媒体，通知每一间相邻的马厩，并在幼虫被证实可存活时，由该区承保扑杀行动。那个经纪人沉迷于轻易得来的佣金，但伊莱自己的厚颜无耻让他相信保密就是疗法。摄像机和没完没了的调查的前景，使伊莱在自己的悲伤里成了无足轻重的人物。他蜷缩在箱子旁，罗斯发出一声像猫叫春般的尖号。玛拉不能用怜悯减轻那声音，也不能用虚假的许诺助长他的胆量。

She told him to **hie** to the equipment shed and bring the copper **seine** used to **chase** escaped goats, a **sturdy** winch, a **bale** of clean straw, and the hand **implement** for cutting hoof grooves. While he ran, Mara began a plain call to the regional lab, but static **garble**d her words. The dispatcher heard “isolated horse” and answered with an **insipid** assurance that a boat would come when weather was **clement**. Mara looked at the black water and knew no boat could come. She used a **pen** to mark a **cardinal** circle around the puncture, then **carve**d a shallow channel in the hoof wall to relieve the pressure from the brine swelling there; the separate jaw wound still held the larva. Rose lurched upright. Her hindquarters shook with enough **torque** to tear the stall apart, and the larva's pulse began to **escalate** beneath her skin. Eli returned with the net and found Mara tying it across the door. “You mean to net my horse?” he said. “I mean to keep her from reaching the harbor.” The barn's old generator had been built to **purvey** power to incubators, but Mara could **combine** its heat coils with the saline pump and **lubricate** the winch bearings. She could also **randomize** the electrical pulses, confusing the parasite long enough to operate. It was a **formidable** plan, and it required her to **vaccinate** herself with the remaining serum first, then **disengage** Rose's halter at the exact moment the net tightened. Eli, who had treated every veterinary bill as a **malicious** trick, now watched her work with **solemnity**. “You **insist** on risking yourself,” he said. “I insist on not letting fear **exploit** your horse,” Mara said, though fear had already found its **nexus** in her throat.

她让他赶紧去器材棚，带来用来追捕逃跑山羊的铜丝大围网、一台结实的绞盘、一捆干净稻草，以及用来切开蹄槽的手工具。伊莱跑开后，玛拉开始直接拨打区域实验室的电话，但电流杂音把她的话弄得含混失真。接线员听成了“孤立的一匹马”，用乏味的口气保证，天气温和时就会有船来。玛拉看着漆黑的水，知道根本不可能有船来。她用钢笔在刺孔周围画出一个鲜红的圆圈，然后在蹄壁刻出一道浅沟，缓解那里积聚盐水造成的压力；下颌的另一处伤口仍困着幼虫。罗斯猛然挣扎着站起。她的后躯抖动，扭转力大得足以拆毁马厩，幼虫的脉搏也开始在她皮下增强。伊莱带着网回来，发现玛拉正把网系在门口。“你要用围网捕我的马？”他说。“我要阻止她抵达港口。”马厩那台旧发电机原本是给孵化器供应电力的，但玛拉可以把它的加热线圈和盐水泵结合起来，并给绞盘轴承上润滑油。她还可以让电脉冲随机化，扰乱寄生虫，为手术争取时间。那是个艰巨的计划，它要求她先给自己接种剩下的血清，再在网收紧的准确瞬间解开罗斯的缰绳。伊莱过去总把每一张兽医账单看作恶意的把戏，此刻却带着庄严的神情看她工作。“你坚持要让自己冒险，”他说。“我坚持不让恐惧利用你的马，”玛拉说，尽管恐惧已经在她喉咙里找到了连接点。

At midnight Rose broke the door, and the net held only because Eli had tied it with a fisherman's **prowess**. The winch screamed; its **groove** threw sparks, and the barn lights went **dim**. Mara ordered Eli to **jog** the emergency lever while she pressed a **mute** stethoscope against Rose's flank. The parasite had begun to **regress** from the blood toward the wound, exactly as the serum was meant to force it, but it had opened a second route along a vein. If Mara cut too shallowly, it would escape; too deeply, Rose would bleed out. Eli wanted an **impetuous** answer. “Tell me what to do.” “Stop making me your conscience,” she said. He answered with an **abject** little laugh, then admitted the broker had promised a **patent** cure and had told him to wait until the filly sickened. He had hoped to **obtain** both the cure and a heroic **epilogue** for the price of silence. The admission was not a **conspiracy**, only the kind of cowardice that can **encompass** two strangers in one bad choice. Mara could have reported him when the radio cleared. Instead, she told him to hold Rose's head and to **relent** when she pulled against him. Eli was **compliant**, not brave, but steady as he held her. She opened the vein, trapped the larva in a steel cup, and watched it **petrify** in the chilled serum. Rose collapsed into **slumber**. By morning, the tide was **equable**, the inlet no longer a threat, and the horse's breath had acquired an almost impossible **serenity**.

午夜时，罗斯撞破了门，围网之所以撑住，只因为伊莱用渔夫的高超本领打了结。绞盘尖叫，凹槽里迸出火花，马厩的灯光暗了下来。玛拉命令伊莱轻推紧急操纵杆，自己把一只无声听诊器压在罗斯的腹侧。寄生虫开始从血液退回伤口，正如血清本应迫使它做的那样，可它已经沿一条静脉开出了第二条路径。玛拉切得太浅，它会逃脱，切得太深，罗斯会流血而死。伊莱想要一个冲动而立即的答案。“告诉我该做什么。”“别再让我替你做良心，”她说。他发出一声可怜的小笑，随后承认，经纪人曾许诺一种有专利的疗法，并叫他等到母马发病。他本希望用沉默的代价，同时得到疗法和一段英雄式的收场白。这份坦白不是阴谋，只是那种能把两个陌生人包裹进同一个糟糕选择的怯懦。无线电恢复清晰后，玛拉本可以举报他。她却让他按住罗斯的头，并在罗斯挣扎时放松力道。伊莱很顺从，并不勇敢，但稳稳地按住了她。她割开静脉，用钢杯捕获幼虫，看着它在冰冷的血清中石化。罗斯倒下睡去。到了清晨，潮水平稳，海湾不再构成威胁，母马的呼吸里出现了一种近乎不可思议的安宁。

The rescue launch arrived after sunrise with a **rookie** inspector and a **smug** cameraman from the mainland media. They found Mara on the barn floor, too tired to **saunter**, preparing a full **explication** of the specimen. The inspector tried to **eavesdrop** on Eli's statement, then **smirk**ed when he heard that the fisher had paid a broker. Mara stopped him. “The **veracity** of the account does not make him a villain worth displaying.” The cameraman sought a **histrionic** angle, asking whether the key's **ecumenical** chapel would condemn the infected horse as **heretical**. Eli, whose face was still wan, told him to leave. Mara did not need to **rehabilitate** Eli's reputation or **gratify** his wish to be forgiven; she needed to **determine** whether Rose remained **vulnerable**, and to implement quarantine without punishing a man for asking too late. The lab's **lucid** report confirmed that the chilled larva could not revive, that the serum was **conducive** to Rose's recovery, and that the barn's **potable** water was clean. It further found no **profundity** in the broker's theory, only a **specious** sales **mimic**ry of science. Mara would **dispose** of the larva's carcass safely, **burnish** the steel cup for reuse, and submit the case without **doggerel**, a **torrid** plea for praise, or any **immaterial** claim of innocence. The inspector, an **indigent** trainee who had never seen a salt-borer, asked to **peer** into the cup; its dead **brilliance** did not **betoken** a new **renaissance** for the species. Outside, a **grueling** tide had left a **canyon** in the road and a **tumult** of broken reeds beneath the chapel's **colonnade**. Eli's small **croon** quieted Rose as she **lurch**ed toward the repaired gate. He asked Mara to **veto** his plan to sell her. “That is your **particular** burden,” she said. “The law asks for **rectitude**, not an **eternal** punishment.” The inspector's **favorable** report restored a thin **concord** to the key, though Mara felt only **vexation** at the cameraman's retreating van. At noon Eli led the filly, unsteadily but alive, across the reopened road. Mara watched until their figures became small beneath the cliff, then shut the barn door against the sea. The choice had not made her pure or wise. It had merely taught her the work of mercy: to cut where cutting was necessary, and to keep her hand from going farther.

日出后，救援艇带着一名新手检查员和一名来自大陆媒体、洋洋自得的摄影师抵达。他们发现玛拉坐在马厩地板上，累得无法闲逛，正在准备对标本作完整解释。检查员试图偷听伊莱的陈述，听见渔夫付过经纪人钱后便得意地笑了。玛拉制止了他。“这份叙述的真实，不会使他变成值得展示的恶人。”摄影师想找一个戏剧化角度，问岛上的普世教派小教堂是否会把受感染的马谴责为异端。脸色仍然苍白的伊莱叫他离开。玛拉不需要替伊莱恢复名誉，也不需要满足他想被原谅的愿望。她需要确定罗斯是否仍然脆弱，并实行隔离措施，而不因为一个人求助得太晚就惩罚他。实验室清晰的报告确认，冰镇过的幼虫不能复苏，血清有助于罗斯恢复，马厩的饮用水也很干净。报告还发现，经纪人的理论毫无深度，只是对科学的似是而非的销售模仿。玛拉会安全处置幼虫的尸体，把钢杯擦亮后再用，并提交这起病例，不写打油诗，不作热烈煽情的求赞陈词，也不提出任何无关紧要的无罪主张。那名从未见过盐蛀虫的贫困见习检查员，要求凝视杯子里的幼虫。它死去的光辉并不预示这种生物会再次复兴。外面，一场折磨人的潮水在路上冲出一道峡谷般的深沟，又在教堂柱廊下留下纷乱的断芦苇。伊莱轻轻哼唱，使罗斯在蹒跚走向修好的门口时安静下来。他请求玛拉否决他卖掉罗斯的计划。“那是你特有的负担，”她说，“法律要求正直，不要求永恒的惩罚。”检查员有利的报告让岛上恢复了一点和睦，尽管玛拉只因摄影师的面包车远去而感到烦恼。正午时，伊莱牵着这匹步子不稳却仍活着的年轻母马，沿着重新开放的道路离去。玛拉一直看着，直到他们的身影在悬崖下变小，随后关上马厩门，把大海挡在外面。这个选择没有使她变得纯洁或明智。它只是教会她仁慈的工作：在必须切开的地方切开，并让自己的手不要伸得更远。
