## Story for Chunk 001 - Buried Echoes

At the **commencement** of polar night, the research ship Nereid lay fast in a white **strait**, its hull pressed by ice against a black **plateau** of frozen sea. Dr. Elia Venn stood in the infirmary door and watched a crewman carry Mara Quill in after a drilling winch crushed her jaw. Mara's breathing was **labored**, and blood ran from a shattered **molar** into a steel **pan**; the cold made every exposed instrument **damp** and miserable to touch. "She is one of the best we have," the crewman said, his voice shaking. It was true. Mara was the ship's **proficient** acoustic engineer, normally **genial** and **vivacious**, but now her **gait** had become a dragging shuffle and her face began to **pucker** around the swelling. Elia felt **apprehension**, not panic: the nearest **haven** was twelve days away.

极夜开始之时，科研船"涅瑞德号"被困在一条白色海峡里，船体被冰压向漆黑的冻海高地。埃利娅·文恩医生站在医务室门口，看着船员把玛拉·奎尔抬进来，钻井绞盘压碎了她的下颌。玛拉呼吸吃力，血从碎掉的臼齿流进钢盘里；寒气让每件露出的器械都带着潮气，摸上去格外难受。"她是我们船上最出色的人之一。"船员的声音发着抖。这话不假。玛拉是船上熟练的声学工程师，平时亲切而活泼，如今却只能拖着步子挪动，肿胀让她的脸颊皱了起来。埃利娅感到的是忧虑，不是恐慌：最近的避难港还在十二天之外。

If the ice loosened, evacuation would be **inevitable**, and the ship's small oxygen reserve could not sustain a prolonged surgery. Elia remembered her **retrospective** notes from a former ice season, which warned that fear could **pervade** a ward faster than infection. Her **prudish** **predecessor** had filed a **ream** of rules that could not **motivate** a single decision. "Follow protocol, and you will be safe," the woman used to say—but protocol could not breathe for a patient. The danger of a narrowing airway was **immanent** in Mara's injury, and Elia had to decide what might **ensue** if she failed. The injury itself was an **imbroglio** of flesh, bone, and consent. A portable scanner showed a **vestigial** airway behind the fracture, while the wound's sweetly **redolent** antiseptic could not mask the smell of tissue beginning to **macerate**. Her former chief had left an **archaic** ventilation rig, **replaced** after inspection but never discarded.

一旦冰层松动，撤离便不可避免，而船上有限的氧气无法支撑一台长时间手术。埃利娅想起从前一个冰季写下的回顾笔记，上面警告说，恐惧渗入病房的速度比感染还快。她那过分规矩的前任留下了一大叠规程，却无法为任何一个决定提供推动力。"照规程走，你就安全。"那个女人常这么说，可规程无法替病人呼吸。玛拉伤势中潜伏着的危险，是不断狭窄的气道；埃利娅必须想清楚，若自己失手，接下来会发生什么。这处创伤本身就是皮肉、骨骼与知情同意的一团纠葛。便携扫描仪显示，骨折后方只剩一条残留的气道；伤口上甜腻的消毒剂气味，也盖不住组织开始浸软的腥味。她的前任主管留下了一台老式呼吸机，检修后已被替换，却一直没扔掉。

Its manual said the old rig could **potentiate** anesthetic gas, though no one aboard could **swear** it would not also **nullify** Mara's remaining reflexes. The captain arrived with a **hortative** speech about courage, a **saccharine** **platitude** that made Mara, still conscious, laugh once. "Do not **browbeat** me into heroism," she whispered. Her voice was **languid**, but the **nuance** was unmistakable: she wanted relief, not an experiment, and she asked Elia to **respect** that distinction. A remedy might **manumit** Mara from pain, Elia thought, yet it could also **reconstitute** her into someone permanently dependent on a machine. The captain, who regarded Elia's post as a **sinecure**, was not listening. He waved a hand at the old machine. "Use it if you must," he said, and turned away.

说明书上说，这台旧机器能增强麻醉气体的作用，但船上没人敢保证它不会同时令玛拉残存的反射全部失效。船长赶来了，发表了一通劝人勇敢的训导式讲话，那种甜腻腻的陈词滥调，让仍清醒的玛拉笑了一声。"别把我威吓成英雄。"她低语道。她的声音慵懒无力，其中的细微差别却清清楚楚：她要的是减轻痛苦，不是当实验品；她请埃利娅尊重这一区别。埃利娅心想，一种疗法或许能把玛拉从疼痛中解放出来，却也可能把她重塑成一个离不开机器的人。船长呢，一直把埃利娅的职位当成一个闲差，根本没在听。他朝旧机器挥了挥手。"要用你就用吧。"他说完便转过身去。

Before long he had gone below to **roister** within **earshot** of the door, drinking and laughing where the whole corridor could hear. His behavior was not merely **gratuitous**; it made the still **inchoate** plan harder to explain to anyone. Mara, meanwhile, gave Elia an **intimate** instruction: sedate her enough to repair the airway, but do not use the untested rig unless she lost consciousness by her own **volition**. The captain called that limit **reckless** when he heard of it, and tried to **admonish** Elia. He had a **propensity** to **harangue** whenever responsibility threatened his command. His **pompous** certainty could not **skirt** the central **issue**: a doctor may not **transgress** a lucid patient's boundary simply because the outcome is terrifying. "Then let her sign away her life," the captain snapped.

没多久他就下到下层去，在门外听得见的地方喧闹饮酒，喝喝笑笑，整条走廊都能听见。他的举动并非无缘无故，它让那个还没成形的方案更难向任何人说明。与此同时，玛拉给了埃利娅一条私密的指示：给她足够的镇静来修复气道，但除非她自己失去意识，否则别用那台未经验证的机器。船长听说这条界限后，称它鲁莽，还想训斥埃利娅。每当责任威胁到他的指挥权，他就忍不住长篇大论。他浮夸的自信绕不开那个核心问题：医生不能仅仅因为结果可怕，就去越界侵犯一位神志清醒的病人的界限。"那就让她签字放弃自己的命好了。"船长厉声说。

Elia asked the medic to **disinter** the old machine's cartridges from storage. A **forger** could have copied the label, and its neat numbers looked **smarmy**, but the chemistry was **recondite** rather than fraudulent. In the **interim**, Mara's blood oxygen fell. The medic, a habitual **skeptic**, urged Elia to **persevere** with the familiar drugs and forget the rig entirely. The captain demanded she **hasten**, threatening to **reproach** her in front of the whole crew. To him, **deportation** from the ice would be an **atrocious** disgrace, a stain on the mission he had promised the sponsor. His **servile**, **perfervid** deference to that sponsor had curdled into something **covetous**: he wanted the mission's success for himself.

埃利娅让医务员从储物间把旧机器的药筒翻出来。标签或许会被造假者轻易复制，上面整齐的数字看着也有点油滑，可其中的化学原理只是深奥，并非伪造。这期间，玛拉的血氧下降了。医务员一向是个怀疑论者，他劝埃利娅坚持用熟悉的药物，把那台机器彻底忘掉。船长则催她加紧，威胁要在全体船员面前申斥她。在他看来，从冰原上被撤离是一桩骇人的耻辱，会玷污他向资助方许诺过的任务。他对资助方那种奴性十足、狂热过头的顺从，已经变质为某种贪婪：他要的是把任务的成功据为己有。

That attitude could **engender** no useful **help**; it only made a pressure that seemed to **drench** the room. Elia chose a **moderate** dose and a local block instead—an **innovation** born less from confidence than from necessity. Mara's hands shook, yet she remained **unflappable** enough to answer each question put to her. Elia used a slender **strand** of warmed tubing to **infiltrate** the narrowed passage; the procedure made Mara **startle**, then retch. A **mangy** ship's dog scratched outside the door, and someone began a bad **dirge** in the mess, mistaking dread for **revelry**. The sound was **vulgar**, Elia thought, but it gave Mara an **offbeat** focus, something strange to hold on to while the world went grey.

这种态度生不出任何有用的帮助，只让一股压力像水一样浸透了整个房间。埃利娅改选了适量镇静和局部阻滞麻醉，这不是来自自信的创新，而是来自必然。玛拉的手在抖，却依然镇定，能回答每一个问题。埃利娅用一缕加温过的软管探进狭窄的通道；这一下让玛拉惊了一惊，接着干呕起来。一只长疥癣的船狗在门外抓挠，餐厅里有人哼起一首难听的挽歌，把恐惧错当成了狂欢。这声音很粗俗，埃利娅心想，可它反倒给了玛拉一个不合常规的焦点，让她的神志在一片灰暗中抓住了什么。

"Not because I am **prodigal** with my life," Mara said, "but because I have a **hankering** to finish the sonar map." She was asking for the old rig now. Elia rejected the captain's **canard** that Mara was invoking the map to coerce her; the wish was neither a **self-absorbed** **fantasy** nor a valid reason to override impaired consent. Still, to use the rig now would **entrap** Mara in a decision made while hypoxic, and to refuse might **stultify** the one chance to keep her breathing. Mara's **whimsical** maps had made the whole crew **exuberant**; she tried to **invoke** their beauty as an argument. Elia could see the mission's **decrepitude** beneath its polished reports: too many people would **engross** themselves in a **versatile** scientist's work, ready to **crab** about each risk instead of helping.

"不是因为我要挥霍自己的命，"玛拉说，"而是因为我心里一直盼着完成那张声呐地图。"她现在要求用那台旧机器。埃利娅驳回了船长的谣言，说玛拉拿地图来胁迫她；这个愿望既不是自我沉溺的幻想，也不是推翻受损同意的正当理由。然而，此刻用机器会把缺氧中的玛拉困在一项她做不了主的决定里，而拒绝又可能扼杀唯一能保住她呼吸的机会。玛拉那些异想天开的地图曾让全体船员兴奋不已；她试着用它们的美来说服埃利娅。而埃利娅能在光鲜的报告底下看见这趟任务正在衰败：太多人愿意沉迷于一位多才多艺的科学家的成果，却只会对每一分风险吹毛求疵，不肯真正帮一把。

It was like an **impenitent** **ichthyologist** who preferred data to a life, weighing every **segment** of risk as though it were a specimen. That **contempt** was a bitter **seed** in Elia's own judgment, not the **charm** or **solace** she needed; its **evasion** of the patient's present pain could not **salve** her conscience. When Mara finally fainted, Elia did not cite consent that had expired. She set the untested rig aside, let the operating lamp **extinguish**, and worked with the **resourceful** calm of a surgeon who knew that an **invincible** reputation was worthless. The narrow repair held. By dawn there came a **revival** of breath in Mara's chest, and outside, the ice's **iridescence** spread across a **lush** expanse of morning color. No rescue signal was needed. The captain's **condign** anger had nowhere to go.

这就像一位执迷不悟的鱼类学家，宁可要数据也不要一条命，把风险的每一段都掂来掂去，仿佛那是标本。那种轻蔑，是埃利娅自己判断中埋下的一颗苦涩种子，而不是她此刻需要的魅力或慰藉；它回避了病人当下的疼痛，无法抚平她的良心。当玛拉终于昏过去，埃利娅没有援引已经失效的同意。她把未经验证的机器搁到一边，熄了手术灯，以一名足智多谋的外科医生才有的沉稳继续操作，她很清楚，无敌的名声一文不值。狭窄处的修复撑住了。黎明时分，玛拉胸中重新有了呼吸；船外，冰面的虹彩铺开一片丰润的晨色。不需要求救信号。船长那份应得的怒火，突然没了去处。

His **venal** instincts remained intact. He offered to **remunerate** Elia with a **pittance** from his bonus, then made a **hangdog** apology that was both **gauche** and sincere. Elia declined it all. She would not **execrate** him, would not **absolve** him, would not **exonerate** herself. Medical **pathology** offered no **paragon** of clean choices: care could **ameliorate** suffering while leaving her with a **spleen** against his pressure. Her **nerve**, she reflected, did not come from any **laudatory** speech or prize. It came from the plain work of asking questions and listening to the answers—nothing more, and nothing less.

他那套贪财的本能一点没变。他提出从奖金里拿出微不足道的一笔来酬谢埃利娅，然后做了一番垂头丧气的道歉，既笨拙，又还算真诚。埃利娅全都拒绝了。她不会诅咒他，不会赦免他，也不会为自己开脱。医学病理学里没有无可挑剔的典范：照护可以减轻痛苦，却仍让她对船长施加的压力心怀怨气。她的勇气，她思忖着，不来自任何颂扬性的致辞或奖项，而是来自发问与倾听这些再平常不过的工作，仅此而已，不多也不少。

No **soliloquy**, no ward **shrine**, no research **subsidy** had made her steady; it was the **prosaic** **bedrock** of honesty that held her up. The captain was no **foil** against which to measure her virtue, no **vicarious** guilt could **flummox** her, and no **anathema** would empty the ship's **hoard** of uncertainty. She did not **covet** a decisive ending, would not **superimpose** doctrine on an individual, and refused to mistake the ship's **curriculum** for a clinical order. Mara woke, touched the dressing, and said, with dry **humor**, "You let me lose." Elia answered, "I let you remain yours." Outside, the ship's **inertia** finally yielded; Nereid moved on through the bright water without a public verdict or an **everlasting** lesson. That, Elia decided, was enough.

让她站稳的不是什么独白、病房里的圣龛，也不是研究补助金，托住她的是诚实这块平实如床岩般的基石。船长不是用来衬托她美德的陪衬，没有替人承担的负罪感能迷惑她，也没有任何诅咒能掏空船上囤积的不确定性。她不贪求一个明确的结局，不会把教条硬加在某个具体的人身上，也拒绝把船上的课程误当成临床命令。玛拉醒了，摸了摸敷料，带着干涩的幽默说："你让我输了。"埃利娅答道："我让你仍然属于你自己。"船外，惯性终于让步，"涅瑞德号"穿过明亮的水面继续前行，没有公开的裁决，也没有永恒的教训。埃利娅心想，这样就够了。
