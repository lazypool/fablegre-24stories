## Story for Chunk 020 - Midnight Orchard

At midnight Juniper Bell found the **forgery** beneath the old pear press: a new deed, made to look ancient, giving Midnight Orchard to Calder Rusk. It was **unwise** to touch it before the constable came, yet she asked her brother to **interrogate** no one until he had seen the boundary marks. Moonlight lay in a silver **dispersion** across the river, where a stolen **dinghy** rocked with an oil-blue **glow**. Her late father had trusted a village **chiropodist** named Bell, an **inglorious** man with a talent for listening; Bell had once caught a boy trying to **filch** apples. The orchard was no **exhaustible** mine, Juniper knew. Its roots were **entrenched** in land thick with storm **flotsam**, and a sharp smell of **acid** rose from the irrigation ditch.

午夜，杜松·贝尔在旧梨压榨机下发现了伪造物：一份做得像古物的新契据，把午夜果园交给考尔德·拉斯克。警员来前触碰它并不明智，但她请哥哥在看过边界标记前不要盘问任何人。月光以银色散射横过河面，一艘失窃小艇在油蓝色光泽中摇晃。已故父亲信赖一名叫贝尔的乡村足病医师，一个善于倾听却无名的人；贝尔曾抓住一个偷苹果的男孩。杜松知道，果园不是可耗尽的矿山。它的根深扎在布满风暴漂浮残骸的土地里，灌溉沟升起刺鼻的酸味。

After three years of **privation**, the family could not lose the harvest. The ruin seemed **inscrutable**: leaves were **ridden** with black spots, their edges **biting** the night air. A trapped bird struck its **pinion** against a wire. Bell offered an **extenuating** thought about frost, but Juniper led him to the family **crypt**, where her father had kept notebooks on how to **botanize** by lantern. In a stunned **trance**, she read his praise of the orchard and the **plaudits** it had won. He had tried to **conciliate** and **embroider** peace with a neighboring **clan**, she remembered, into every bargain. As a **precaution**, he had made the tenant copies **acquiescent** to no change of title without the parish **commissariat**.

历经三年困苦，这家人不能失去收成。毁坏令人费解：叶片布满黑斑，边缘咬噬夜空气。一只困住的鸟用翅膀撞击铁丝。贝尔提出关于霜冻的宽解想法，但杜松带他来到家族墓室，父亲曾在那里保存提灯辨识植物的笔记。她震惊恍惚地读着他对果园的赞美及它赢得的喝彩。她记得，他曾试图把与邻近家族的和解与和平织入每笔交易。作为预防，他使租户的副本认可：未经教区专员署，不得改变产权。

Someone had tried to **defoliate** the lower rows with a liquid that could **fuse** bark to soil. Rusk had demanded **fealty** from every picker, though he was too **engaging** in public to seem a threat. Juniper, an **assiduous** keeper of accounts, took **control** of the search. She watched a shadow **creep** between trunks and tried to **reassure** Bell when the pumps began to **emit** a metallic hiss. A **prohibitory** sign at the sluice had been freshly moved. Rusk had claimed merely to be on a **sojourn**, but the false deed gave him a private **ecstasy** whenever he spoke of land. Juniper could **visualize** his possession of it, and saw in his eyes a small **pyromania** toward anything living. Bell said the disease might be **curable** if the roots were washed; Juniper heard no **acclaim** in that hope, only the first pale **efflorescence** of a plan.

有人试图用一种能把树皮熔合进土壤的液体使低处树行落叶。拉斯克要求每位采摘者效忠，尽管他公开场合太有亲和力，不像威胁。杜松是一名勤勉账目保管人，接管了搜查。她看见阴影在树干间爬行，水泵开始发出金属嘶声时，她试图安慰贝尔。水闸旁的禁止标志刚被移动过。拉斯克声称只是短暂逗留，但假契据使他每逢谈到土地便暗自狂喜。杜松能想象他占有果园，看见他眼中对一切活物的一点纵火癖。贝尔说，如果冲洗根部，病害或可治愈；杜松从那希望中听不到欢呼，只看见计划最初淡白的萌发。

The poison was the orchard's **bane**, not a blight. A sample retained its **purity** in a jar weighted with **ballast**. At dawn the parish **tribunal** could hear them, but Juniper worked **tireless** beneath a **pine** lamp while Rusk tried to **blackball** her witnesses. Bell, whose childhood **sobriquet** was Sparrow, waited in the courthouse **anteroom** with a purple **bruise** on his wrist. The **inevitability** of harvest failure had caused an **irruption** of buyers, but Juniper called Rusk's oath **perjury**. It was not **inevitable**, she argued, that deceit should inherit an orchard.

毒药是果园的祸根，不是病害。一份样本在用压舱物压住的罐中保持纯净。黎明时教区法庭可以审理他们，但杜松在松木灯下不知疲倦地工作，拉斯克则试图排斥她的证人。贝尔儿时绰号麻雀，手腕带着紫色淤伤，在法院前厅等待。收成失败的必然性引来一批买家，但杜松称拉斯克的誓言为伪证。她辩称，欺骗继承果园并非不可避免。

An **ethnology** of old planting customs, read in a **tepid** archive, drew **disapprobation** from the clerk, yet it proved the family **weal** lay in communal water rights. Rusk made a **gaffe** by claiming the sluice aided the deed's **furtherance**. Juniper produced iron **pincers**, a **nomic** survey map, and a strip of damaged **epidermis** from the culprit's glove. The handwriting expert had **studied** the deed. From the gallery came a **catcall**, then silence as she showed a **treacly** imitation of her father's hand copied from a **decameron** volume. A childish **scribble** in its margin had become the forged signature. With **deference**, the magistrate asked Bell to **immortalize** his observations in a statement.

一份在温吞档案馆读到的旧种植习俗民族学研究引来文书的不赞同，却证明家族福祉在于公共水权。拉斯克声称水闸促进契据的推进，犯了失言。杜松拿出铁钳、一张法理测绘图以及从凶手手套上取下的受损表皮。笔迹专家已研究过契据。旁听席传来嘘声，随后安静下来，因为她展示了一份从十日谈卷册抄来的、父亲笔迹的甜腻模仿。页边儿童涂鸦成了伪造签名。治安官恭敬地请贝尔把观察永久记录在陈述中。

The water channel had a **breach** near its **terminus**, and the damage was **intrinsic** to the poisoning scheme. Rusk asked for **leniency**, saying **fashion** had made him reckless; his **lewd** joke about Juniper proved he had **wittingly** chosen cruelty over **longevity** for the trees. Bell could **limn** the culprit's route, though Rusk called the inference **illogical**. Juniper's father's **memoir** described a **sinewy** gardener whose work was **telling**, able to **knit** wire into a **stockade**. Rusk owned the same **cement**-stained boots, and his **libidinous** steward had seen him at the **distal** gate after the warning **signal**.

水道末端附近有一道裂口，损害正是投毒阴谋的内在部分。拉斯克请求宽恕，说时尚令他轻率；他对杜松的猥亵笑话证明，他明知故犯地选择残酷，而非树木的长寿。贝尔能勾画凶手路线，尽管拉斯克称推断不合逻辑。杜松父亲的回忆录描述一位筋骨强健的园丁，他的工作很有说明性，能把铁丝编成栅栏。拉斯克拥有同样沾水泥的靴子，他好色的管家在警报信号后曾在远端大门见到他。

That steward, a frightened **whelp**, gave a **profound** account of Rusk's **actuarial** calculations: poison the trees, **overleap** the family claim, buy the land at a **serene** auction. A **vestige** of dye in the deed matched the insurance **indemnity** form. The paper's **elasticity** was not **prescriptible** parchment but a modern fiber. Village **mores** had kept such knowledge quiet, until Juniper's **galvanic** certainty filled the **grandstand**. Her evidence would **bolster** the case. The magistrate could **enjoin** Rusk from the property, and Bell offered a root **tonic** whose **centripetal** wash would **outstrip** the poison's **dissipation**.

那名害怕的小崽子般的管家深刻讲述拉斯克的精算计划：毒害树木，跳过家族主张，以平静的拍卖买下土地。契据中的一丝染料与保险赔偿表相符。纸张的弹性不是可按规定开具的羊皮纸，而是现代纤维。村庄习俗使这些知识保持沉默，直到杜松电击般的确信充满看台。她的证据会增强案件。治安官可禁令阻止拉斯克进入地产，贝尔提供一种根部补剂，其向心冲洗会快过毒药的消散。

Rusk's **sportive** smile vanished. He called Juniper an **adversary**, but the **percipient** constable had found the boat during **reconnaissance**. In it were papers proposing a **secession** of the river meadow. Rusk looked **nonplussed** and tried to **coax** the clerk with a legal **lexicon** learned in **psychotherapy** sessions. His **timidity** before the evidence turned **insensitive** when he mocked the dead. The magistrate deemed the bargain **irreligious** and **repugnant**, then directed the clerk to **truncate** its false clauses from the record. He paused to **commend** Juniper's calm and named the plot **subversive**.

拉斯克嬉戏般的笑容消失了。他称杜松为对手，但敏锐的警员在侦察中找到了小艇。艇内有提议分割河畔草地的文件。拉斯克显得困惑，试图用心理治疗时学来的法律词汇哄骗文书。他面对证据时的怯懦，在嘲笑死者时变成冷酷。治安官认为交易不敬神且令人厌恶，命文书从记录中截去其虚假条款。他停下来称赞杜松的冷静，并定性阴谋具有颠覆性。

Behind the sluice, a clay **protuberance** began to **teem** with worms as clean water returned. The sight did not **affect** Juniper's resolve. A lone **cygnet** crossed the channel while villagers **bustle** into the yard, Rusk's former **retinue** now fearing **durance**. Bell remained **humble**, though he had slept in no **slumber** for a **quarterly** season. The magistrate promised a **forthcoming** inquiry and invited an **epistemologist** from the college to **chasten** the document examiner. Juniper's **betrothed** arrived, caught his breath, and gave Rusk a single **jab** of accusation: the sabotage had been **perpetual** in intent, meant to turn the orchard into a **landfill** and the river into a **defile**.

水闸后，清水回流时，一处黏土隆起开始爬满虫子。这景象没有影响杜松的决心。一只天鹅幼鸟穿过水道，村民忙碌地涌进院子；拉斯克原来的随从如今害怕遭受监禁。贝尔依旧谦卑，尽管他整整一个季度没睡过安稳觉。治安官承诺即将展开调查，并邀请学院一名认识论学者训诫文件鉴定人。杜松的未婚夫赶到，喘过气，对拉斯克发出一记指控：破坏意图是永久的，旨在将果园变为垃圾场、河流变为污水沟。

Rusk tried to **misconstrue** the charge as an **abhorrent**, **undulating** rumor. Bell called him a **quack** and held up the poisoned **draught**. Its facts could not **obtrude** on a man trained to **antipathize** with mercy. A broken **incisor** found by the hose belonged to the steward, who had carried the **culinary** corrosive from Rusk's house. The old **tiller** admitted he had heard them **eavesdrop** beside the shrine of a local **protomartyr**. He asked the court to **compensate** the pickers, whose hands needed **balsam** after a **neglectful** season. Juniper did not **commiserate** with Rusk; he had tried to **fritter** away their inheritance through an **inefficient** terror.

拉斯克试图把指控误解为一种可憎而起伏不定的谣言。贝尔称他为庸医，举起有毒饮剂。事实无法强加给一个受过训练、厌恶仁慈的人。软管旁找到的一颗折断门齿属于管家，他从拉斯克家带来烹调用的腐蚀剂。老耕作者承认，他曾在当地殉道先驱神龛旁听到他们偷听。此人请求法庭赔偿采摘者，他们的手在被忽视的一季后需要香膏。杜松不怜悯拉斯克；他试图以低效的恐怖挥霍掉他们的遗产。

The gardener showed how to **cauterize** the wounded trunks and **demonstrate** a **scrupulous** remedy. The court would **expunge** the deed and publish a **laudatory** account of Bell's service. Rusk answered with a **piquant** sneer, attempting to **denigrate** a red **blotch** on the sample label. In the estate **fernery**, however, the constable found bottles, an **orgy** of invoices, and a **feint** map leading to the river. Rusk had planned to **demolish** the press after winning an **aldermanship** seat. His **confinement** was therefore **drastic** but **solvent** under the law, its proof **rigorous** and its conclusion **tenable**.

园丁展示如何烧灼受伤树干，并示范一种严谨补救法。法庭将删除契据，并发表赞扬贝尔服务的报告。拉斯克以尖刻冷笑回答，企图贬低样本标签上的红斑。然而，警员在庄园蕨类温室发现瓶子、大量发票和一张通往河边的假地图。拉斯克计划在赢得市议员席位后拆除压榨机。因此，他的监禁在法律下严厉但可执行，证明严格，结论站得住脚。

At the courthouse **rotunda**, Rusk tried to **deviate** **athwart** a **multitude** of witnesses by offering **largesse** from an **usurious** lender. He meant to **fleece** the pickers, an **aberrant** act against the orchard's **homeostasis**. Juniper rose to **expound** the sequence, refusing to let rhetoric **befog** it. At the old **abbey** she had seen his wet **posture** at the sluice; he had tried to **deify** his greed, an **impiety** against an **elysian** place. Her account was **explicit** enough to **impeach** every claim. The deed was **defunct**, Rusk's defense **vain**, and even his lawyer looked **flustered**.

在法院圆形大厅，拉斯克试图以高利贷主的大笔施舍，穿过众多证人改变方向。他打算榨取采摘者，这一反常行为破坏果园稳态。杜松起身阐明经过，拒绝让修辞使之蒙蔽。在旧修道院，她曾看见他在水闸处湿漉漉的姿态；他试图神化贪婪，这是对天堂般地方的不敬。她的陈述足够明确，可以弹劾每项主张。契据已失效，拉斯克的辩护徒劳，连他的律师都显得慌乱。

A **swarm** of bees returned over the rows as Rusk's **hegemony** ended. The ruling was **pronounced** after a **tempestuous** hearing, but its remedy was **pragmatic**: restore the channel, preserve each **lineament** of the orchard, and pay its keepers. Juniper's **loneliness** eased; she would not **repine** beneath a **pneumatic** sky made strange by dawn. They would not **antiquate** the old ways, but **incorporate** new tests and **champion** the workers. Rusk's **crass** accounts faced an **audit**, while the valley regained its **serenity**. No one would again **guzzle** power from a crooked **delineation** on a deed.

拉斯克的霸权结束时，一群蜜蜂重返树行上方。风暴般听证后判决宣告，其补救却很务实：恢复水道，保存果园的每一处特征，并支付管理者报酬。杜松的孤独缓和了；她不会在被黎明变得奇异的气动天空下抱怨。他们不会废弃旧方式，而会纳入新检测并维护工人。拉斯克粗鄙的账目面临审计，山谷重获宁静。再也没人能从契据弯曲的图界中狂饮权力。

By evening, a last **spangle** of sun met the river. Rusk's written **rebuttal** sounded **treasonable** to those who had watched the trees **fledge** new leaves from a **noisome** ordeal. Bell's practical **charm** exposed its **tautology**: a lie did not become true by being copied. The court ordered a **disconnect** between Rusk and the estate, calling the sabotage **blatant**, **pernicious**, and beyond any **constraint** of ordinary debt. His seized funds would **defray** the treatment. The **recurrent** danger was gone; the old **barricade** at the ditch could come down.

到傍晚，最后一片日光碎闪与河流相接。对那些看着树木从恶臭考验中长出新叶的人，拉斯克的书面反驳听来近乎叛逆。贝尔务实的魅力揭穿其同义反复：谎言不会因被抄写而成真。法庭命令切断拉斯克与庄园的联系，称破坏行为明目张胆、有害，并超出普通债务的任何限制。查扣资金将支付治疗费用。反复出现的危险已消失，沟渠旁旧路障可以拆除。

Juniper woke from her **stupor** to see an **elephantine** moon sinking behind the hill. Bell's **facile** hands set the pumps, and their steady rhythm began to **reverberate** through the roots. The **gist** of the mystery was simple at last: theft had worn the mask of **chivalry**. Saving the trees would be **demanding**. They diluted the **herbicide** under the cool **clime**, set aside Rusk's **conceit**, and **particularize** every affected row. The court named his act a **transgression**, and the proof remained **disquieting** despite Juniper's brief **hesitation** before the recovered **bomb** of chemical concentrate.

杜松从昏沉中醒来，看见一轮巨象般的月亮沉到山后。贝尔灵巧的手启动水泵，稳定节奏开始穿过树根回响。谜团要旨终于简单：盗窃戴着骑士精神的面具。拯救树木将很艰难。他们在凉爽气候下稀释除草剂，抛开拉斯克的自负，并逐行具体列出受影响区域。法庭称其行为为违法；尽管杜松在找回的浓缩化学物炸弹前短暂犹豫，证据仍令人不安。

The river's **buoyancy** carried the little vessel free. A small **imp** of relief made Bell laugh as workers began the **salvage**. Nothing could **flummox** Juniper now: a **sonic** alarm, the **triad** of false papers, poisoned water, and paid silence, had forced Rusk to **equivocate**. Under a wet **frond**, the constable found a painted **viper** emblem from his ring. Juniper thanked **providence**, though the **magisterial** ruling, not heaven, had stopped the **despot**. It seemed **fantastic** that the town **machinist** could **unravel** the pump's **nucleus** of valves, yet he did, quieting the workers' **hysteria** without **circumlocution**.

河水的浮力让小船脱困。工人开始打捞时，一点顽皮的轻松令贝尔发笑。此刻没有什么能让杜松慌乱：声响警报、三份假文件、有毒水和收买来的沉默，已迫使拉斯克含糊其辞。在一片湿叶下，警员找到他戒指上绘制的毒蛇徽记。杜松感谢天意，尽管阻止暴君的是治安官的裁决，而非天堂。镇机械师竟能拆解水泵阀门核心似乎不可思议，但他做到了，毫不绕弯地平息工人的歇斯底里。

With **vigilance**, they kept watch through an **unearthly** week. The repair had a **thematic** elegance, said the village **prima**; every root joined the same recovery. Bell's **insightful** notes earned him no **sufferance** from Rusk's allies, who tried to **antagonize** him. His usual **diffidence** gave way when he published a short **monograph** and made a **beeline** for the press. The lesson was **universal**: while the soil looked **quiescent**, it still held an **option** for ruin. A first green shoot was the **intimation** that patience, **tolerance**, and **germinal** care could restore **continuity** with the **bygone** orchard.

他们警惕地守望，度过不可思议的一周。村里的女主角说，修复具有主题性的优雅；每条根都加入同一场复原。拉斯克的盟友不容忍贝尔富有洞见的笔记，试图激怒他。他发表一篇短专论并直奔媒体后，平常的羞怯消失了。教训具有普遍性：土壤看似静止，仍保有毁灭的可能。第一根绿芽暗示，耐心、宽容和萌芽般的照护能恢复与昔日果园的连续性。

The damage remained **discernible**, but not permanent. In the **pristine** dawn, a robin became a **harbinger** and sang a plain **ditty** from the gate. The parish **moralist** arranged an **oratorio** for the harvest, but Juniper preferred Bell's quiet **insight** at the hill **summit**: let rain **lave** the roots, then guard them against **recidivism**. A new **ordinance** made water tests public and barred any owner who might **diverge** from it. The old **feud** dissolved. Even the town **connoisseur**, formerly **uninhibited** in gossip, praised the careful **craft** of the growers and asked to **affiliate** with their cooperative.

损害仍可辨认，却非永久。纯净的黎明中，一只知更鸟成了先兆，从门口唱起朴素小调。教区道德家为收获安排清唱剧，但杜松更喜欢山顶上贝尔安静的见解：让雨洗涤树根，再防范故态复萌。新条例公开水质检测，并禁止任何可能偏离它的所有者。旧怨消散。就连镇上的鉴赏家，以前八卦时毫不克制，也称赞种植者细致技艺，并请求加入合作社。

No one could **vaunt** that recovery was easy. Juniper's first meeting as steward felt **gauche**, but she became **methodical**. Rusk's debt had tried to **hamstring** every **tract** of the estate; now each row entered a **metamorphosis**. The lawyer's **unctuous** speeches earned only **infamy**. A **covey** lifted from the grass while he offered legal **rigmarole** about a lost **masterpiece** of penmanship. Whether he wrote the deed was no longer **moot**. The evidence could **countervail** any tale, and the outcome was more than **tolerable**: the harvest's **culmination** arrived with an **impassioned** procession of **migrant** hands, each paid a **sufficient** wage instead of Rusk's **measly** promise.

没人能夸耀复原很容易。杜松作为管理人的第一次会议感到笨拙，但她变得有条理。拉斯克的债务曾试图削弱庄园每一块地；如今每一行都进入蜕变。律师油滑的讲话只赢得恶名。一群鸟从草中飞起，他还在围绕一幅失落书法杰作提供法律废话。他是否写了契据已不再有争议。证据可抵消任何说辞，结果不止可接受：收获的高潮伴随一队热情的流动工人到来，每人得到足够工资，而非拉斯克微薄的承诺。

The village midwife, trained in **obstetrics**, led a **cortege** of lanterns to the first picking. Bell could **diagnose** leaf disease better than any **rhetorician** could **integrate** the facts of fraud. The poisoned branches went to controlled **decomposition**, not the river, and a rule of **adjacency** kept every chemical store far from water. The magistrate refused to **retard** payment merely because a **complicit** banker asked delay. He would **mete** full compensation. On the final order, Juniper noticed an **apostrophe** in the forged name, an **obsessive** flourish that broke Rusk's own **formula** for disguise.

受过产科学训练的村中助产士带领一列灯笼队伍前往首次采摘。贝尔诊断叶病比任何修辞家整合欺诈事实都强。有毒枝条进入受控分解，而非河中；邻接规则使每处化学储存远离水源。治安官拒绝仅因一名同谋银行家请求延期便拖延付款。他会发放全额赔偿。杜松在最终命令中注意到伪造姓名有一处撇号，一个强迫性的花饰破坏了拉斯克自己的伪装公式。

That error voided a supposed **treaty** with the lender. A **finch** began to **babble** in the eaves as the clerk read the decision. It did not merely **depict** loss; it gave every worker a **stimulus** to remain. Rusk tried to **deride** the finding, but the court was not **receptive**. The orchard's **tutelar** physician, a **psychiatrist** with a **peculiar** love of grafting, told Rusk not to **belabor** his grievance. At the **acme** of the hearing, the magistrate called him a **hypocrite** and used the sentence to **embolden** the town.

那个错误使与放贷人的所谓条约无效。文书宣读决定时，一只雀鸟在屋檐下啁啾。决定不只是描绘损失；它给每名工人留下的刺激。拉斯克试图嘲笑裁定，但法庭并不接受。果园的守护医师是一名特别热爱嫁接的精神科医生，他告诉拉斯克不要反复诉说不满。听证达到顶点时，治安官称他是伪君子，并用判决鼓舞小镇。

Warm air began to **waft** through the rows. Someone played a **harp** in the lane, and even grief proved **repressible** beneath that clear sound. Juniper wore no ceremonial **toga**; the orchard was her **citadel**, and her duty was **filial**. She chose to **disclose** all accounts to the pickers, trusting their **perceptive** eyes. Their work became **pleasurable**, not because it forgot the danger, but because it could **rhapsodize** without fear of future **want**.

暖空气开始飘过树行。有人在巷中弹竖琴，清澈声音下连悲伤也可压抑。杜松没穿仪式长袍；果园是她的城堡，她的职责源于孝心。她选择向采摘者披露所有账目，信任他们敏锐的眼睛。他们的工作变得愉悦，不是因为遗忘危险，而是因为能无惧未来匮乏地欢歌。

At last, the **prologue** of suspicion gave way to the harvest itself. Juniper did not **juggle** ownership with justice: both must **appertain** to those who tended the ground. In the **foreground**, children gathered windfalls while she sought to **ascertain** that every sluice was sealed. The old threat of the **gallows** belonged to no one; evidence under a **microscope** had made Rusk's lie **minuscule**, unable to **stifle** the orchard's return. The season had been **debilitating**, and Rusk a **knave** whose **wanton** sabotage had tested Juniper's **poise**.

终于，怀疑的序幕让位于收获本身。杜松不在所有权与正义间玩弄把戏：二者都必须属于照料土地的人。前景中，孩子们收集落果，她则确认每个水闸都已封闭。旧日绞刑架威胁不属于任何人；显微镜下的证据使拉斯克的谎言微不足道，无法扼杀果园回归。这个季节令人虚弱，而拉斯克是个恶棍，其恣意破坏考验了杜松的镇定。

By the first market day, the trees had regained their **trimness**. The court found that Rusk had tried to **tamper** with both deed and water, and ordered **redress**. Baskets of fruit stood **magnificent** beneath the press. Bell, ever **benevolent**, tasted one and said its **marrow** held more promise than any **millennium** of paper claims. Juniper answered with **ruth**, remembering the workers, the trees, and even the frightened steward. She tied back her **coiffure** and understood the **subliminal** warning her father had **derived** from years of watching men **debauch** a landscape with **contrived** hunger.

第一个集市日到来时，树木恢复了整齐。法庭认定拉斯克企图篡改契据和水源，并命令补偿。压榨机下摆着华美的水果篮。贝尔一如既往地仁慈，尝了一颗，说其果肉比纸面主张的千年更有希望。杜松带着怜悯回答，记起工人、树木，甚至害怕的管家。她束起发髻，理解父亲从多年看人们以人为制造的饥饿放纵糟蹋景观中得出的潜意识警告。

His faith in **monotheism** could never **garble** the practical lesson: land was not an **insentient** **diorama**. The court would **disbar** the lawyer who prepared the false deed, because honesty was **imperative**. Juniper's final **thesis** was plain: ownership must **revert** to care, whatever **medium** bore the proof. With the harvest safe and the river clear, Midnight Orchard had reason to be **leery** of strangers, but none to fear the night.

他对一神论的信仰绝不会混淆这条实际教训：土地不是无知觉的立体模型。法庭会吊销准备假契据律师的资格，因为诚实势在必行。杜松最终的论点很清楚：无论证明承载于何种媒介，所有权都必须回归照料。有了安全的收成和清澈河流，午夜果园有理由警惕陌生人，却没有理由害怕夜晚。
