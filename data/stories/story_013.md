## Story for Chunk 013 - Memory Latch

Julian Merrow woke on a cold tiled floor with the brass **gauge** above him ticking softly, Mara Fenn kneeling at his side and pressing a **ragged** sleeve against the **gash** in his temple. The signal chamber's only window faced the **glacier** wall, and its frame had been painted shut with black **glaze**, so the room depended on a single humming vent for air. He knew her name, but not why he had come — a **misfortune** that made every object feel like an accusation, from the child's **eaglet** charm on her keyring to the **chromatic** wheel turning above the vent. "We came to inspect the **extant** emergency transmitter," Mara explained, "and then you collapsed." The air smelled **pungent**, and the **minute** hand on the gauge crept toward the red zone. Julian tried to **reflect** on the missing hour, but his thoughts slid into **murky** gaps. "Someone drugged us," Mara said quietly. "We will **suffocate** unless we solve the lock."

朱利安·梅罗在冰冷的瓷砖地板上醒来，头顶的黄铜仪表轻轻滴答作响，玛拉·芬恩跪在他身旁，用一截破袖子按住他太阳穴上那道深长的伤口。这间信号室唯一的窗户正对着冰川壁，窗框被黑釉封死，屋里只能靠一台嗡嗡作响的通风口换气。他记得她的名字，却不记得自己为何而来，这场不幸让每件东西都像在指控他，从她钥匙扣上的小鹰护符，到通风口上方转动的彩色轮盘。"我们是来检查那台仍可用的紧急发射器的，"玛拉说，"然后你就倒下了。"空气里气味刺鼻，仪表上的分针正缓缓爬向红区。朱利安试图回想那段缺失的时光，思绪却坠入浑浊的空白。"有人给我们下了药，"玛拉轻声说，"除非解开门锁，否则我们会窒息。"

The lock held four slots marked with a **cipher** Julian had studied years ago, back when he was preparing to **matriculate** in forensic linguistics at the institute. Mara spread a waterproof **glossary** across the floor, but its **abstruse** symbols seemed designed to **misrepresent** any direction. Then he noticed a **crease** in one page that matched a **tangent** scratched into the edge of the wheel, and the two marks together proved that the colors did not **constitute** a sequence at all — they were an instruction to run it backward. Julian's old **mastery** of codes returned in painful flashes, and as it did he became **cognizant** of a faint, **arrhythmic** tapping behind the wall. Being naturally **circumspect**, he caught Mara's wrist before she touched the final lever. "Wait," he said. "That lever is a **snare** — a spring-loaded needle meant to **incinerate** the oxygen filter."

锁上有四个凹槽，刻着朱利安多年前研究过的密码，那时他正准备进入学院攻读法庭语言学。玛拉把一份防水术语表摊在地板上，但那些深奥的符号像是存心要歪曲方向。然后他注意到一页上的折痕与轮盘边缘刻下的切线相吻合，两条痕迹共同证明这些颜色根本不是排列顺序，而是一道让它倒转的指令。朱利安对密码的掌握在痛苦的片段中恢复，而随着记忆归来，他也察觉到墙后有一下微弱而不规则的敲击。他天生谨慎，在玛拉触碰最后一根拉杆之前抓住了她的手腕。"等等，"他说，"那根拉杆是个陷阱，一枚弹簧针会焚毁氧气滤芯。"

"This is deliberate **sabotage**," Julian said, and Mara, moving with practiced **stealth**, drew a thin wire out from under the control panel. It ran toward the vent, where an unseen hand could **manipulate** the mechanism from the service passage outside. Yet when Mara played the tapping back through the pipe, she recognized it as their own signal, delayed by an acoustic **cavern** under the floor — a **vicissitude** in sound that gave Julian the answer. There was no attacker. During his blackout he had set the trap himself, under the influence of a **tonic** prescribed after his memory episodes, and then erased the act. The shame made him **slouch** against the wall in a dazed **stupor**. "No," Mara said, refusing his **acquiescence** to despair. "A drug can **enervate** judgment; it cannot determine it." She traced the wire to a valve while Julian used the wheel to **rarefy** the gas, and together they watched the chamber **constringe** as the vents began to close. The supposedly **slippery** tile bore a fresh scrape, and behind the chair Julian had dragged to the window sat an **indigenous** switch, original to the station.

"这是蓄意破坏，"朱利安说。玛拉以训练有素的隐蔽手法从控制面板下抽出一根细线，它通向通风口，一只看不见的手本可以从外面的维修通道操纵机关。可当玛拉把敲击声沿管道放回去听时，她认出那是他们自己的信号，被地板下的声学空腔延迟了，声音的这一变化让朱利安恍然大悟。外面并没有袭击者。昏迷期间，他受记忆发作后所服补剂的影响亲手设下了陷阱，随后又把那段行为抹掉了。羞愧让他靠着墙、神情恍惚地垂下身子。"不，"玛拉说，拒绝他对绝望的默认，"药物会削弱判断，却不能注定你的选择。"她顺着细线找到阀门，朱利安用轮盘稀释气体，一起看着房间随通风口关闭而渐渐收紧。那块看似湿滑的瓷砖上有道新磨痕，而在朱利安拖到窗下的椅子后面，藏着一个这座站点原有的本土开关。

Mara threw the switch, and the bolt released, but the transmitter erupted into a **frenzy** of pulses that projected Julian's missing hour onto the wheel in **euphonious** tones. He saw himself enter alone, pursued by a former clinic aide named Vale, a man who had once tried to intercept Mara in the corridor. Julian had fought him off; Vale swung a **crook** at his head and fled through the service hatch, a **hybrid** of spite and panic that did **appall** him. "Your memory invented a sentimental villain in the vent," Mara said, without a trace of a **grin**, "because it could not bear the simpler version." Julian answered with a **succinct** plan: open the hatch, keep the lock engaged behind them, and use the transmitter to summon help. "We must not **succumb** to the fear he planted," he insisted. They crawled into the passage, where Vale had arranged an **ambush** beside the door of a worker's **domicile**; when he lunged, Julian caught his wrist before he could **smother** Mara with a cloth. Vale spat that a **clique** of **gregarious** patients had made him irrelevant, that their **egalitarian** treatment room had become unbearable.

玛拉扳下开关，门闩松开了，但发射器爆发出一阵疯狂的脉冲，以悦耳的声响把朱利安缺失的那小时投映到轮盘上。他看见自己独自进门，身后跟着一个名叫维尔的诊所前职员，那人曾在走廊里想截住玛拉。朱利安击退了他；维尔用钩杖击中朱利安的头部，从维修舱口逃走了，那是怨恨与惊慌的混合，这个真相令朱利安不寒而栗。"你的记忆在通风口里编造了一个煽情的恶徒，"玛拉说，脸上没有一丝笑容，"因为它承受不了更简单的版本。"朱利安提出一个简洁的计划：打开舱口，让身后的锁保持闭合，用发射器呼叫援助。"我们不能屈服于他种下的恐惧，"他坚持道。他们爬进通道，维尔在工人住所门旁设下伏击；当他扑来时，朱利安抓住他的手腕，没让他用布闷住玛拉。维尔唾沫横飞，说一群爱交际的病人组成的小圈子让他变得无关紧要，说他们平等的治疗室已让他无法忍受。

Vale raved that the treatment room was a **riot**, an insult to every orderly mind, and Julian felt a chill as he traced the **overlap** between these threats and his false memories. The drug had made a **cornucopia** of borrowed fears, yet each one pointed back to the same assault, so Julian did not **deride** the man or **badger** him into a confession. Instead he turned the transmitter's **ascendant** beam onto the passage, and Vale's own shadow betrayed the wrench hidden in his coat as the **inexorable** alarm carried through the rock. Inside Vale's **decrepit** kit lay a **subpoena** for Julian's records, a **snobbish** note calling him an **unlettered** **scion**, and an **anthology** that contained a crude **lampoon** of the clinic's patients.

维尔叫嚷着说治疗室就是一场骚乱，是对每个讲秩序之人的侮辱，朱利安感到一阵寒意，他看清这些威胁与自己虚假记忆之间的重叠。药物制造出一大堆借来的恐惧，可每一种都指向同一次袭击，因此朱利安没有嘲弄他，也没有纠缠着逼他招供。他只是把发射器向上的光束对准通道，让维尔自己的影子暴露了藏在外套里的扳手，而无情的警报声穿过岩层。维尔破旧的工具包里有一张调取朱利安记录的传票，一张势利的便条称他为没有受过教育的后裔，还有一本选集，收着一篇粗劣讽刺诊所病人的文字。

The lampoon was meant to **provoke** a **throng** of frightened families into protest. A **dodder** vine had been wound around the hatch as a silent **germ** of obstruction, and its **burgeoning** tendrils had begun to **inhibit** the mechanism. Mara poured a **vessel** of solvent over the fibers while Julian used the manual to **abridge** the reset sequence to its essential steps, and there, in the **original** wiring, he saw the **palatable** answer: a **tear** in the insulation had made the lock **simulate** a **coma** signal. "You could have fixed this in minutes," he told Vale, who tried to **reprove** Mara for tampering — the accusation died in his throat.

那篇讽刺文原本要激怒一大群受惊的家庭来抗议。一株菟丝子藤缠在舱口上，作为无声的阻碍萌芽，不断生长的卷须已经开始妨碍机关运转。玛拉把一容器溶剂倒在藤蔓纤维上，朱利安则把复位流程压缩成关键几步，在原有的线路里，他看到了那个可行的答案：绝缘层的一处裂口让门锁模拟出昏迷信号。"你本来几分钟就能修好，"他对维尔说。维尔想责骂玛拉"多管闲事"，可那句指责还没出口就噎住了。

Vale tried to **ensconce** the wrench beneath his **sumptuous** **apparel** and to **fawn** his way back into favor, but his **jape** landed flat and his **rancor** left him at a **nonplus**. "Your **potentate** is a **buffoon**, and you are a **daft** **apostate** to your own clinic," he hissed at Julian. Julian stayed **composed**, reading the **substantive** maintenance marks aloud: a missing **augmentation** symbol had preceded a coded note about a "meticulous reset" that would help a patient recover — and would also multiply the emergency pulses. The contradiction was written in plain language.

维尔试图把扳手藏进华贵的衣服里，还想摇尾乞怜地挽回颜面，但他的玩笑落了空，一腔怨恨只让他不知所措。"你的统治者是个小丑，而你是个愚蠢的叛教者，背叛了自家诊所，"他朝朱利安嘶声说。朱利安保持镇定，大声读出实质性的维修标记：一个缺失的增补符号后面跟着一段编码注释，提到一次"精细的复位"，既能帮助病人康复，也会让紧急脉冲成倍增加。矛盾就明明白白写在字面上。

Mara found prairie grass ground into Vale's boot, proof he had entered from the outer shaft, and she used the transmitter to **disinfect** the chamber and **obviate** any chance the lock could seize again. "No bit of certainty should **predestine** how we read a person," Julian said, thinking of the clinic's director, a man who had offered no **amenity** to patients except fear. That **reactionary** doctrine had promised **eminence** to anyone willing to call a frightened mind a **pandemic** of disobedience; it had made obedience **imperative** and doubt a kind of **coven** treason. Understanding that finally lifted Julian's **doldrums**. The director's own records would have to **convalesce** with him, or rather he with them — and this locked room would never be a **sanctuary** for blame. The **denouement** was not arrest but a shared **consensus**.

玛拉在维尔靴子里发现磨进鞋底的草原草，证明他从外侧竖井进来，她又用发射器给房间消毒，排除门锁再度卡死的可能。"没有任何一点确定性能预先注定我们如何解读一个人，"朱利安说，他想到了诊所那位主任，一个除恐惧之外不曾给病人任何便利设施的人。那条反动的教条承诺，只要肯把一个受惊的心灵称作普遍蔓延的不服从，就能换来显赫地位；它把服从变成当务之急，把怀疑说成女巫集会般的背叛。想通这一点，朱利安的低迷情绪终于散去。主任的记录需要与他一同恢复，而这间锁室永远不会成为指责的避难所。结局不是逮捕，而是两人达成的一致。

A clinic **gadfly** had tried to **reprehend** Mara for trusting a man who could not trust his own memory, and now Julian saw Vale **prowl** the far tunnel while a colleague **lurk**ed near the hatch, waiting to see who was **impotent** and who was not. Vale had expected an **ingratiating** technician to **deracinate** the vine for him at an **opportune** moment; instead the man heard Julian **scuff** his boots, saw the **prairie** grass, and abandoned the plan. It was a **hodgepodge** of tricks that had been **begrudged** even by its author, and Julian, **leery** of melodrama, found the whole confession almost **bathetic**.

诊所里一只好事者般的牛虻曾试图责骂玛拉，说她不该信任一个连自己的记忆都不可信的人；如今朱利安看见维尔在远处的隧道里徘徊，一个同事则潜伏在舱口旁，等着看清谁软弱无力、谁不是。维尔原指望一个讨好的技术员在合适的时机替他连根拔掉藤蔓；结果那人听见朱利安拖着脚步走过，看见草原草，便放弃了计划。这是一堆连作者自己都勉强容忍的杂乱伎俩，而朱利安对夸张戏码心存警惕，只觉得这场招供几乎有些陈腐可悲。

Vale's **minion** had intended to **waylay** them at the outer hatch, but Julian used the transmitter's **rostrum** mode to **blazon** their route through the rock, and the man withdrew rather than face the light. The pulses, left running, would **proliferate** across the frequency — a **favor** the station manager would not see as **inimical** once Mara's report let the clinic **extrapolate** every fact that was **germane** to the case. Julian refused the **sop** of an easy apology, refused to **embark** on blame, refused to **build** a case against a frightened man; a **fastidious** account of what happened was enough. There would be no **applause** to **eulogize** the rescue. They simply walked out together, Mara reset the gauge to zero, and for the first time the missing hour no longer felt missing.

维尔的爪牙本想在外侧舱口伏击他们，但朱利安用发射器的讲台模式把他们的路线醒目地标在岩层中，那人面对灯光退了回去。脉冲继续运行，会沿着频率扩散开去；一旦玛拉的报告让诊所能够推断出与案情相关的一切事实，站方经理便不会把这看作敌意。朱利安拒绝一份轻松的道歉作为安抚，拒绝踏上指责之路，拒绝给一个受惊的人罗织罪名，一份一丝不苟的事实陈述就够了。没有人鼓掌来颂扬这次救援。他们只是并肩走了出去，玛拉把仪表重新归零，而那缺失的一小时第一次不再显得缺失。
