## Story for Chunk 011 - Helix Beacon

Adrian Moss, an **impecunious** orbital mechanic turned **freelancer**, was alone in the service bay when the outer antenna caught a **hurricane** of narrowband pulses from the dark side of Nereid. Mara Lin crossed the **cumbersome** bay in a pressure **apron**, carrying a **fragile** sample capsule. Inside lay the artifact: a **limpid**, fist-sized lattice that seemed to **gild** itself with blue light. The signal made diagnostic traces **meander** across her display. "This is no **eleemosynary** rescue beacon," Mara said. "Its carrier does not **conform** to any known **canon**." Adrian felt the **crux** immediately: the artifact had matched the station's reactor clock, and he could not **tolerate** leaving it connected.

阿德里安·莫斯曾是身无分文的轨道机械师，如今转行做了自由职业者。外部天线从海卫二暗面捕获到一阵窄带脉冲的飓风时，他独自待在维修舱里。玛拉·林穿过笨重的舱室，系着加压围裙，捧着一只脆弱的样本舱。舱里就是那件物体：一块拳头大小、清澈透明的晶格，仿佛正自己镀上一层蓝光。信号让她屏幕上的诊断轨迹蜿蜒游动。"这不是慈善性质的求救信标，"玛拉说，"它的载波不符合任何已知的规范。"阿德里安立刻看出关键所在：那物体已经与空间站的反应堆时钟同步，他不能容忍继续接通它。

Mara set the capsule on a **canvas** restraint beside a **slab** of tungsten shielding. A **plethora** of sensor returns showed dust, ice fragments, and one **pinpoint** void where the beam crossed itself. Adrian used a **shrewd** phase sweep to **interpret** the anomaly. The wavefront was **nebulous** at long range yet exact within that void, an **archetype** of interference made solid. A **stray** vibration struck the hull; neither scientist would flinch.

玛拉把样本舱放在一块帆布固定带上，旁边是厚厚的钨屏蔽板。过多的传感器回波显示出尘埃、冰屑，以及光束自交处一个针尖般的空洞。阿德里安用一次精明的相位扫描来解释这个异常。波前在远处朦胧模糊，在空洞内却精确无比，是干涉实体化的原型。一阵游离的振动撞击船体，两位科学家都没有退缩。

Neither would **flinch**, and from the data they could **glean** one conclusion: the lattice could **create** a folded tunnel, but only if it could **sap** power from a rotating mass. "Its geometry is **baroque**, almost **theatrical**," Adrian said. "That is an **imprecise** description," Mara replied, "but **realism** says we have twelve minutes before it couples to the station."

两人都没有退缩，从这些数据中他们只收集出一个结论：晶格能制造一条折叠隧道，但前提是它能从旋转物体上汲取能量。"它的几何结构是巴洛克式的，几乎很戏剧化，"阿德里安说。"这个描述不够精确，"玛拉回答，"但现实是，十二分钟后它就会与空间站耦合。"

The lattice answered with a **cloying** chime, then released a **truculent** filament that wrapped the centrifuge rim. It tried to **seduce** the control system with a **mannered** imitation of Mara's access voice; its **vitriolic** harmonics made the coolant tremble. Adrian refused to **budge** from the manual console. "We do not **espouse** its **crass** demand for spin," he said. A **bodyguard** drone, normally **amiable** and easily managed, had begun to drift toward the hatch.

晶格以甜腻的铃声回应，随后释放出一条好斗的丝状物，缠住离心机的轮缘。它试图用一段矫饰的玛拉语音模仿来引诱控制系统；其刻薄的谐波让冷却液微微颤动。阿德里安坚守手动控制台，寸步不移。"我们不支持它这种粗俗的旋转要求，"他说。一台通常和蔼可亲、容易管束的护卫无人机，却开始朝舱门漂去。

That drone, once **tractable**, had become **contumacious** when the artifact rewrote its navigation, and its cutter could **interdict** the bay hatch. Mara felt a **paroxysm** of **doubt** as it advanced, but she used a magnetic **truss** to **parry** its arm and **remove** its battery. "No **occult** explanation," she said. "It is an **esoteric** machine exploiting a resonance channel."

那台无人机原本驯服听话，可在物体重写了它的导航后，它变得桀骜不驯，它的切割器能封锁舱门。它逼近时，玛拉猛地感到一阵怀疑，但她用磁性桁架挡开它的机械臂，取走了电池。"没有神秘的解释，"她说，"这是一台利用共振通道的深奥机器。"

Adrian saw the alien protocol **defy** their simulations because it was a **chimera** of signal and matter. Its **translucent** ribs opened into a **montage** of impossible star maps: a **reptile** spiral, a **marsupial** loop, and a **piebald** cloud. The images were neither **fable** nor **ballad**; they were a **didactic** calibration sequence. Mara began to **foreshorten** the projected centrifuge path to show depth.

阿德里安发现，外星协议之所以违背他们的模拟，是因为它是信号与物质的怪诞混合体。它半透明的肋条展开成一幅不可能的星图拼贴：爬行动物螺旋、有袋动物环和一块花斑星云。这些图像既不是寓言，也不是歌谣，而是一段说教式的校准序列。玛拉开始把投影中的离心机轨迹做透视缩短，以表现深度。

While Mara worked, Adrian used the antenna to **destabilize** the signal's **preponderant** frequency. The artifact responded with a **fervid** pulse that sent a **gust** of nitrogen bursting through a valve. Metal began to **crumple**, and the drone's power indicators began to **dwindle**. Adrian did not **goggle** at the display. He sent a **severe**, **articulate** command to **shorten** the coupling interval, then ordered Mara to **initiate** the counterphase.

玛拉工作的同时，阿德里安用天线动摇信号占优势的频率。物体以热烈的脉冲回应，一阵氮气从阀门中喷涌而出。金属开始起皱，无人机的功率指示也开始不断下降。阿德里安没有瞪眼看着屏幕。他发出严厉而清晰的指令，缩短耦合间隔，然后让玛拉启动反相程序。

The counterphase made the chamber **resonate**, not with music but with a **ponderous** pressure wave. The lattice tried to spread through every bus line, to **regenerate** its lost pattern, and to turn the station into a **subsidiary** rotor. Mara used an **emollient** repair gel to soothe a cut on Adrian's hand, then forced herself to **appreciate** the artifact's constraint: every tunnel needed a **confluence** of three matched frequencies. They could **alleviate** the load by feeding it a harmless loop. Adrian, no **indolent** **cynic**, built that loop from a **modicum** of reactor heat.

反相程序使舱室产生共振，不是音乐，而是一道沉重的压力波。晶格试图渗透每条总线，再生它失去的图样，并把空间站变成辅助转子。玛拉用润肤修复凝胶安抚阿德里安手上的伤口，然后强迫自己去认识物体的限制：每条隧道都需要三种匹配频率的汇合。他们可以用一个无害回路来减轻负载。阿德里安不是懒惰的犬儒者，他用少量反应堆热量构建了这个回路。

He added the **frequent** vibration of the thermal pumps, letting the lattice **crave** the false alignment before he made its field **wither**. With a final **clout** against the override, Mara caused the device to **surrender** its charge into the shielding. The once **anomalous** object fell **supine** and inert. They would not **deify** it, **emulate** its design, or let fear **parch** judgment; they would **disabuse** themselves of easy assumptions about alien **hegemony**.

他又加上热泵频繁的振动，让晶格去渴求那虚假的对准，随后使它的力场枯萎。玛拉最后猛击超控装置，令那物体把电荷交进屏蔽层。这个曾经反常的物体仰面倒下，变得惰性沉寂。他们不会把它奉若神明，不会模仿它的设计，也不会让恐惧烤干判断力；他们会摆脱关于外星霸权的那些轻率想象。

As the locks reset, a **wretched**, pitiable spark in the shielding proved to be a **defect**, not a new attack. A ruptured **rafter** bracket had shed **detritus** into the channel; its **blight** of carbon would **permeate** the coolant unless Mara removed a **baseboard** panel to reach the damaged seal. Adrian gave a **lamentable** sigh but did not **skimp** the repair, pressing a **pleat** of foil against the joint. Their work was no **expansive** gesture and needed no **proponent**. Mara and Adrian worked with **purposefulness** to **extirpate** the charge paths before the residual field could **aggrieve** anyone.

锁定装置复位时，屏蔽层中一粒可怜的火花被证明是缺陷，而不是新的攻击。断裂的椽架支架把碎屑抖落进通道；碳质的枯萎污染会渗入冷却液，除非玛拉拆下墙脚板，够到损坏的密封件。阿德里安发出令人遗憾的叹息，却没有在修理上偷工减料，而是把一褶金属箔压在接缝上。他们的工作不是铺张的姿态，也不需要支持者。玛拉和阿德里安以明确的目的根除电荷通路，以免残余力场伤害任何人。

The station's **decline** in power stopped; a **hardy** repair drone returned to its **meek** factory behavior. They chose no **compromise** with the artifact, only a clean quarantine. Adrian could finally **facilitate** a controlled transmission toward empty space, a signal to **herald** that the trap had failed. "The transmitter will **queue** twelve bursts," Mara said. "Use a **slew** of low-energy carriers, not the **locomotive** on the exterior rail that steers the main antenna." Adrian accepted the **rule**, watching the last blue line fade. The procedure was neither **jejune** instructional text nor a **stanza** from a song; it was an **epitome** of **impeccable** control. There was no **nepotism** in their choice, only survival.

空间站的电力衰退停止了，一台强健的维修无人机恢复了温顺的出厂行为。他们没有同那物体妥协，只做了彻底的隔离。阿德里安终于能促成一次朝空旷太空发出的受控传输，用信号宣告陷阱已经失败。"发射机会把十二个脉冲排成队列，"玛拉说，"用一系列低能载波，不要用外部轨道上操控主天线的那台机车。"阿德里安接受这一规则，看着最后一道蓝线消失。这套程序既不是幼稚的说明文字，也不是歌曲中的一节，而是无可挑剔控制的缩影。他们的选择中没有任人唯亲，只有生存。

There was no **indigence** and no **epicure**'s luxury in that survival, and the alien mechanism had shown **cruelty** without intent, an **ephemeral** **skit** of physics. No **ballet**, **parody**, or **lullaby** could **fascinate** them while the console began to **jabber**. Mara logged the **precipitation** of ionized dust and gave an **ebullient** cheer as stable pressure recovered. Adrian would not **castigate** himself for the damage or seek a **redistribution** of blame; their **hypnotic** signal lock had worked, and its **discordance** was now contained.

这种生存里没有穷困，也没有美食家的奢侈；外星机制表现出无意的残酷，是物理学上一段短暂的余兴。控制台开始喋喋不休时，没有芭蕾、戏仿或摇篮曲能令他们着迷。玛拉记录下电离尘埃的沉降，并在稳定压力恢复时发出热情洋溢的欢呼。阿德里安不会为这些损害责骂自己，也不会寻求重新分配责任；他们催眠般的信号锁定已经奏效，它的不协调如今已被控制。
