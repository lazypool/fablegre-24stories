## Story for Chunk 014 - White Maelstrom

Martin Vey and Mara Quill crossed the **insular** ice shelf during a **sporadic** whiteout, hauling an **unpretentious** sled out from the **seminary** where they had trained as polar weather observers. Martin, a **novice** field engineer, kept a **naive** faith in the map, while Mara, an **avid** glaciology **practitioner**, trusted the **tangible** tremor beneath her boots. Ahead, a **gargantuan** pressure ridge had begun to **bulge**, its **precipitous** face shedding blue shards into the wind. "The pattern is **pertinent**, Martin," she said in her **laconic** way.

马丁·维和玛拉·奎尔在零星的白障中穿过那座孤立的冰架，拖着一架朴素的雪橇，离开他们受训成为极地气象观测员的训练学院。马丁是名新手野外工程师，对地图抱有天真的信任；玛拉则是一位热忱的冰川学从业者，只相信靴底下能感到的真实震颤。前方，一道巨大的压力脊正在鼓起，陡峭的冰壁不断把蓝色碎屑抖进风里。"这种模式很中肯，马丁，"她用一向简洁的语调说。

"Those pulses of **irregularity** **portend** a slab release," Mara added. Martin checked their **fail-safe** beacon, the only **defender** against being swallowed by the storm, and watched its battery **deplete** faster than the manual promised. The **baleful** sky and a low **sensation** in his ribs made him **muddle** the colors of two cables, and he would have wired them backward if Mara had not used old **mnemonics** to **concatenate** the readings. "We'll **suspend** the drill," she said, and he understood the choice was **deliberate**: they could **preempt** the slide by cutting a narrow release trench, or **traverse** the ridge and dare its collapse. Martin looked at her, then at the map. "Which one risks less?" he asked, already knowing her answer.

"那些异常脉冲预示着雪板会滑落，"玛拉补充道。马丁检查了他们的故障保护信标，这是防止被风暴吞没的唯一保障，眼睁睁看着电池耗得比手册承诺的更快。阴险的天空和肋骨间一股低沉的异样感让他弄混了两根电缆的颜色，要不是玛拉用旧记忆法把读数连成链，他险些就把线接反了。"暂停钻机，"她说，而他明白这个选择是慎重的：要么先切开一条窄泄压沟抢先引发滑落，要么横越山脊去赌它的崩塌。马丁看看她，又看看地图。"哪个风险更小？"他问，心里其实已有答案。

Neither option felt safe, and the ridge itself seemed **implacable**, so Mara took the **ductile** cutter while Martin rigged a **sling** to lower the charge into a **cramped** crevasse. A gust stripped snow from a **seamy** **covert** crack, exposing **antediluvian** ice shot through with black grit and signs of slow **decay**. "No **flippancy** now," Martin said, for the work was **demanding** and the wind could **exacerbate** every error. Mara answered with a **mordant** smirk and set the charge with **cunning** fingers, and from the drift ahead came a distant **shriek**.

两条路都不让人安心，山脊本身更显得无情难驯，于是玛拉拿起可延展的切割器，马丁用吊索把炸药放进一条狭窄的冰缝。一阵风掀开一道隐秘而裂纹斑驳的裂缝上的积雪，露出布满黑色砂砾和缓慢腐化痕迹的远古冰层。"现在别轻浮，"马丁说，因为这项工作要求很高，风又会让每个失误进一步恶化。玛拉以尖刻的冷笑作答，用灵巧的手指安放好炸药，而飘雪深处传来一声遥远的尖叫。

Out of the blowing snow stepped a pale **ewe**, and behind it padded a hungry snow leopard with a **prepossessing** coat and the reckless gait of a **rapscallion**. Martin raised the flare, but its flame would **desiccate** their last fuel cell, so Mara fit a **supple** hose to the sled's canister and made a **provisory** jet of vapor. The cat recoiled; the **somatic** shock, though, left Martin **frail**, and he began to **stammer** as he read the marker half buried in the ice. Still he could **hack** it free, and the engraved **incantation** of an earlier expedition pointed a route **away** from the unstable shelf. They took it down a **sinuous** ravine where every footfall made the snow **squelch**.

雪雾中走出一只苍白的母羊，后面跟着一只饥饿的雪豹，它皮毛讨喜，步伐却像个鲁莽的恶棍。马丁举起信号火焰，但火光会耗干他们最后一块燃料电池，于是玛拉把一根柔软的软管接到雪橇气罐上，喷出一道临时的蒸汽。雪豹退开了；然而这次身体上的冲击让马丁虚弱无力，他读着半埋在冰里的路标时开始结巴。好在他还能把它从冰里砍出来，上面刻着的早期远征队咒语指向一条远离不稳定冰架的路线。他们沿它走进一条蜿蜒的峡谷，每一步都让积雪发出挤压声。

Martin wanted to **douse** the flare, but Mara insisted its **lustrous** light could **deter** the predator. Her **epistemology** was practical: what they could measure mattered more than what they feared. Yet the leopard stayed **indifferent** to vapor and glare, while the ridge behind them began to **disintegrate** in groaning slabs. In a snowbank Mara found a **rudimentary** repeater, its **on** switch stiff with frost and its **periodical** signal too faint to summon help. "We cannot **quibble**, Martin," she said. "We must **outmaneuver** it." He used the repeater's **probe** to **rein** the charge circuit and let the line **mingle** with the beacon lead.

马丁想浇灭火焰，玛拉却坚持说有光泽的亮光能威慑捕食者。她的认识论很务实：能测量到的东西比恐惧更值得信赖。可雪豹对蒸汽和强光都毫不在意，身后的山脊则开始成片瓦解。玛拉在雪堆里发现一台初级中继器，结霜的开启开关僵得按不动，那一道定期信号也微弱得无法呼救。"不能吹毛求疵，马丁，"她说，"我们必须智胜它。"他用中继器的探针控制炸药回路，让线路与信标导线接合。

The signal was barely **intelligible**, yet it could **marshal** the sled's emergency rotor; the rotor's controller began to **babble** in bursts, a **verbose** electronic **peroration** of faults, and Martin had to **mediate** between its competing safety locks. He refused to **abdicate** control, even as **somnolence** pulled at his eyelids and the **depressant** cold dulled his breathing. Then the rotor spun and failed with a **break** that sent the sled sideways. Martin could **inveigh** at the **remiss** maintenance crew, but blame had the **pointlessness** of shouting into wind, so he helped Mara gather the gear and they agreed to **barter** what they had left for information.

信号勉强可以理解，却足以调度雪橇的应急旋翼；旋翼控制器开始断续地胡言乱语，像一段冗长的故障结语，马丁不得不在互相冲突的安全锁之间调停。他拒绝放弃控制，即使困倦拉扯着眼皮、抑制性的严寒让呼吸变得迟钝。随后旋翼转动一圈便因一次断裂失效，把雪橇甩向一侧。马丁本可以痛骂失职的维修人员，但责备如同对着风喊叫一样毫无意义，于是他只是帮玛拉收拾装备，两人决定用仅剩的东西去换取消息。

Under a rock overhang they found a shepherd sheltering with the pale ewe, and from him they traded their remaining flare for a covered route past the animal. There they found an old **charlatan**'s tourist cache, **replete** with useless **lottery** tickets, but its metal case held a **sage** guide's emergency **protocol**. The **politic** instructions told them to **enact** a ground pulse, **quell** panic, and **restore** the rotor by warming its intake; Martin's **disinclination** vanished. He would not let a technical failure **destroy** them, nor let the predator **subvert** their escape. Mara, usually **reticent**, took an **oath** in a clear voice.

在岩石突檐下，他们找到一名牧羊人正带着那只苍白的母羊避风，便用剩下的信号火焰换来一条绕过雪豹的有遮蔽路线。那里存着一只老骗子留下的游客储物箱，里面装满无用的彩票，但金属盒里放着一份贤明向导的紧急规程。精明的说明要求他们制造地面脉冲、平息恐慌、通过加热进气口恢复旋翼，马丁的不情愿随之消散。他不会让技术故障毁掉他们，也不会让捕食者颠覆逃生。平日沉默的玛拉用清晰的声音起了一个誓。

It was a **paean** to their **amity** — each would carry the other through the **skirmish** ahead — and that rare **altruism** gave Martin sudden **relief**. He packed snow around the casing to **intensify** the pulse, then used the charge to **emancipate** the jammed fan. The leopard lunged; Mara made a **sneer** and a **taunt** at it, and Martin drove the rotor blast between them and the animal. In the quiet afterward they could **achieve** the far moraine, and Martin could only **marvel** at Mara's **astute** timing, which she dismissed with a **stolid** nod. Their survival was no **punch line**, no accident to laugh off.

那誓言是对他们友好的赞歌，两人都要带着对方穿过前方的小冲突，这份罕见的利他主义给了马丁莫大的宽慰。他在外壳周围堆雪来增强脉冲，再用炸药解放卡住的风扇。雪豹扑来；玛拉朝它冷笑、嘲弄它，马丁把旋翼气流打在人与雪豹之间。风平浪静之后，他们得以抵达远处的冰碛，马丁只能惊叹玛拉时机判断之敏锐，而玛拉只是木然点头，不接受称赞。他们的生还不是一句笑点，不是可以一笑置之的意外。

No **neologism** could describe it, either, and there was no **ignominy** in being tired. It was a **content** conclusion earned by an **onerous** night. Martin used the last battery charge to **annul** the damaged drill's automatic restart, while Mara logged the **enormity** of the fault and the **depravity** of the contractor who had knowingly concealed the dangerous wiring. Their **provident** repairs would **nurture** a safer route for future travelers rather than **withhold** the warning data. A **philistine** contractor tried to **depose** Martin from the repair assignment, but Mara's brief report proved his work was **integral**, and the contractor's **autocracy** ended the moment the crew saw the evidence. Nobody needed a **pedestal** for the night's work.

也没有哪个新词能形容它，疲惫也并不丢人。这是艰难一夜换来的满足结局。马丁用最后一格电池取消了受损钻机的自动重启，玛拉则记下故障的严重程度，以及那个明知线路危险却刻意隐瞒的承包商的邪恶。他们有远见的修理将为后来的旅行者培育出一条更安全的路线，而不是扣留警报数据。一个庸俗的承包商想把马丁从维修任务中罢免，玛拉简洁的报告却证明他的工作不可或缺；工作人员看到证据的那一刻，承包商的独裁便结束了。没有人需要为这夜的辛劳立一座基座。

There would be no **derision** and no grand **tribute**, and Martin did not **dally** or **obsess** over who was at fault. The **quandary** had taught him to **abide** by Mara's calls without a **jaunty** pose or **indulgent** optimism. A **gangly** radio mast carried their signal to a **legion** of nearby camps, where a communications **maven** could **accelerate** the evacuation. Mara, a **neophyte** rescuer only two winters before, would not **embarrass** a frightened survivor; she planned against fuel **deprivation** instead. Under the returning **illumination** of the storm lamps, they made the beacon reliable again, ready for the next traveler.

不会有嘲笑，也不会有盛大的颂辞，马丁没有拖延，也没有执迷于追问谁对谁错。这个困境教会他遵从玛拉的判断，既不摆出轻快的姿态，也不抱持放纵的乐观。一根细长的无线电桅杆把信号传给附近众多营地，那里的一位通信行家能够加快撤离。仅仅两个冬天前还是新手救援者的玛拉，不会让受惊的幸存者难堪，而是为燃料匮乏预作打算。在风暴灯重新亮起的照明下，他们把信标重新修得可靠，等待下一位可能需要它的旅行者。
