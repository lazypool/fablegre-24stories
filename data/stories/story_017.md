## Story for Chunk 017 - Tidal Mercy

Mara Leto had served twelve years as the only veterinarian on Vesper Key, where a sandbar road dead-ended at her low **rustic** quarantine barn and the sea began beyond it. The peeling **molding** and the abandoned **harrow** rusting against the fence made the place far less **imposing** than any mainland clinic, and she liked it that way. Her **ken** was animals, not people. She could **diagnose** a collapsed lung from the **wan** color of a tongue, hear infection in a cough, and find a blockage in the **lumen** of a newborn calf's intestine. Yet she had never once learned to refuse a frightened owner, and that was how Eli Voss found her. The **hard-bitten** fisher arrived at dusk in a **dapper** wool cap, towing a crate that held Rose, his prized **filly**. A **surreptitious** animal broker had sold her to him cheaply at the mainland **terminus**, he admitted, and now she lay **prostrate**, sweating even through the wind.

玛拉·莱托在维斯珀岛上做了十二年唯一的兽医，沙洲公路在她低矮的乡村隔离马厩前终止，大海从马厩外开始。剥落的装饰线脚和靠在栅栏边生锈的废弃耙子，让这里远不如大陆诊所那般气派，而她正喜欢这一点。她的见地在动物身上，不在人身上。她能凭舌头苍白的颜色诊断肺部塌陷，从咳嗽中听出感染，还能在新生小牛肠道腔内找到阻塞。然而她始终学不会拒绝一个受惊的主人，伊莱·沃斯就这样找到了她。这位饱经世故的渔夫在黄昏时到来，戴着一顶整洁的羊毛帽，拖着一只箱子，里面是他珍爱的年轻母马罗斯。他承认，一个偷偷摸摸的牲畜经纪人在大陆终点站把她便宜卖给了他，如今她平躺着，即使在风中也浑身冒汗。

The inlet beyond the yard had churned into a **frigid**, **frothy** **maelstrom** around the **shoal**, cutting Vesper Key off from the evening ferry. Mara found a **minuscule** puncture beneath Rose's jaw, a **maculate** swelling beside it, and a faint **stench** rising under the horse's coat. Her **meticulousness** made her draw blood before she ever touched the wound. Under the microscope, a translucent **membrane** enclosed a spiral larva whose **distal** hooks opened and closed in a slow, rhythmic grip. The sight did not petrify her. What rattled her was Eli's **ingenuous** question, asked with the whole of his worry in it: "Can you make her well before she reaches my **kinfolk**?" She looked at the **explicit** smear of brine-flecked cells and thought of the herds the salt-borer had once **persecute**d into ruin.

院子外的海湾已经在浅滩周围翻腾成一片冰冷而泡沫翻涌的大漩涡，把维斯珀岛与傍晚的渡轮彻底隔绝。玛拉在罗斯下颚找到一处极小的刺孔、旁边一块有污斑的肿胀，还闻见马毛下升起的一股淡淡恶臭。她一丝不苟，先抽血，然后才触碰伤口。在显微镜下，一层半透明薄膜包裹着一条螺旋形幼虫，它末端的钩子一张一合，节奏缓慢而有力。那景象没有把她吓僵。真正令她不安的是伊莱那个天真的问题，里面装着他全部的忧虑："你能让她在见到我的亲属之前好起来吗？"她看着涂片上那些明显闪着盐花的细胞，想起盐蛀虫曾把沿海畜群迫害成一片废墟。

The creature was a salt-borer, an organism believed extinct. Its larval secretion could **fertilize** eggs inside warm mammalian tissue, the adult could **capture** a host by scent alone, then **rend** the skin open and release a chemical that would **induce** panic in the whole herd. Mara held a licensed serum, but a horse's dose was **prohibitive**, and the side effects might **demoralize** Eli at the very hour he needed steadiness. Eli asked whether the old **diocesan** who blessed boats could help. Mara would not **opine** on miracles. She said plainly that the injection might save Rose but would **convert** her into a temporary reservoir, and that putting her down now would **sidestep** nothing, because the danger was already loose in the wind. Eli's face, usually **rubicund** from weather and drink, turned pale. "My wife is **pregnant**," he said. "She is waiting across the inlet." He offered a **munificent** sum for her silence, then, with greater **temerity**, proposed they dodge the reporting rule together.

那生物是一种盐蛀虫，一种被认为早已灭绝的生物。它的幼虫分泌物能在温暖哺乳动物的组织里使卵受精，成虫则能单凭气味捕获宿主，撕开皮肤，释放一种会让整群牲畜陷入恐慌的化学物质。玛拉手上有获准使用的血清，但给一匹马的剂量贵得吓人，而其副作用也许会在伊莱最需要镇定的当口令他意志消沉。伊莱问，那位为船只祝福的教区主教能不能帮忙。玛拉不愿对神迹发表意见。她直白地说，注射也许能救罗斯，却会把她变成暂时的储存宿主，而此刻把她杀掉并不能回避任何问题，因为危险早已散在风里。伊莱那张一向被海风和酒气染得红润的脸白了。"我妻子怀孕了，"他说，"她正在海湾对面等着。"他为她的沉默开出一大笔钱，接着又更大胆地提出，两人一起绕开上报规定。

His **aboveboard** manner made the offer worse, because only a **concerted** response could contain the salt-borer. The rule required her to call the mainland **media**, notify every neighboring stable, and have the district **underwrite** a cull if the larva proved viable. The broker had been an **addict** to easy commissions, Mara knew, but Eli's own **effrontery** had let him believe that silence was a cure. The thought of cameras and an **interminable** inquiry turned Eli into a **nonentity** in his own grief; he began to **cower** beside the crate while Rose gave a thin **caterwaul** through the planks. Mara could not **abate** that sound with pity, and she would not **embolden** him with false promises. Instead she told him to **hie** to the equipment shed and bring the copper **seine** they used for escaped goats.

他这份坦率反而令提议更糟，因为只有协同行动才能控制住盐蛀虫。规定要求她呼叫大陆媒体，通知每一家相邻马厩，并在幼虫被证实可存活时，由地区承保扑杀。玛拉知道，那个经纪人是个沉迷于轻松佣金的人，但伊莱自己的厚颜无耻让他相信沉默即是疗法。一想到摄像机和没完没了的调查，伊莱就在自己的悲伤里变成了无足轻重的人。他蜷缩在箱子旁，罗斯隔着木板发出一声声尖细的号叫，像猫叫春。玛拉既无法用怜悯减轻那声音，也不愿用虚假的许诺给他壮胆。她只让他赶紧去器材棚，把平日用来抓逃跑山羊的铜丝大围网拿来。

He would need a **sturdy** winch, a clean **bale** of straw, and the hand **implement** for cutting hoof grooves; the copper net they used to **chase** escaped goats would not be enough by itself. While Eli ran, Mara tried a plain call to the regional lab, but static **garble**d her words into noise. The dispatcher heard only "isolated horse" and answered with an **insipid** assurance that a boat would come when the weather turned **clement**. Mara looked at the black water and knew no boat would come. She took a **pen** and marked a **cardinal** circle around the puncture, then **carve**d a shallow channel in the hoof wall to relieve the pressure of the brine swelling there. Rose lurched upright, her hindquarters shaking with enough **torque** to tear the stall apart, and the larva's pulse began to **escalate** beneath her skin. The barn's old generator had been built to **purvey** power to the incubators; Mara could **combine** its heat coils with the saline pump and flood the yard with light.

他需要一台结实的绞盘、一捆干净的稻草和一把用来切开蹄槽的手工具；那张平日里用来追赶逃跑山羊的铜网，光靠它自己是不够的。伊莱跑开后，玛拉试着拨打区域实验室的电话，可电流杂音把她的声音搅得支离破碎。接线员只听见"孤立的一匹马"，便用乏味的口气保证，等天气温和时就会有船来。玛拉看着漆黑的水面，知道根本不会有船来。她拿起钢笔，在刺孔周围画出一个鲜红的圆圈，又在蹄壁上刻出一道浅沟，缓解那里肿胀的盐水带来的压力。罗斯猛然挣扎着站起，后躯抖动的扭转力大得足以拆毁马厩，幼虫的脉搏也开始在她皮下不断增强。马厩那台旧发电机本是给孵化器供应电力的，玛拉可以把它的加热线圈和盐水泵结合起来，让整个院子亮如白昼。

She could **lubricate** the winch bearings with the pump's grease, **randomize** the electrical pulses to confuse the parasite long enough to operate, and trust a plan that was **formidable** precisely because it was unproven. It required her to **vaccinate** herself with the remaining serum first, then **disengage** Rose's halter at the exact moment the net tightened across the door. Eli, who had always treated a veterinary bill as a **malicious** trick, now watched her work with a kind of **solemnity**. "You **insist** on risking yourself," he said. "I insist on not letting fear **exploit** your horse," she answered, though fear had already found its **nexus** in her throat. The net across the door held only because Eli, back from the shed, had tied it with a fisherman's **prowess**. The winch screamed, sparks flew from the **groove**, and the barn lights went **dim**. Mara ordered Eli to **jog** the emergency lever.

她可以给绞盘轴承涂上油，把电脉冲随机化，扰乱寄生虫，为手术争取时间，并相信一个正因为未经检验而显得艰巨的计划。这个计划要求她先用剩下的血清给自己接种，再在围网收紧的瞬间解开罗斯的缰绳。伊莱过去总把每一张兽医账单看作恶意的把戏，此刻却带着一种庄严看着她。"你坚持要让自己冒险，"他说。"我坚持不让恐惧利用你的马，"她答道，尽管恐惧已经在她喉咙里找到了连接点。围网之所以撑住，只因为伊莱从棚里回来后，用渔夫的高超本领打好了结。绞盘尖叫起来，凹槽里迸出火花，马厩的灯光暗了下去。玛拉命令伊莱轻推紧急操纵杆。

She pressed the **mute** stethoscope to the mare's flank and listened hard. The parasite had begun to **regress** from the blood toward the wound, exactly as the serum was meant to force it, but it had opened a second route along a vein. If she cut too shallowly, it would escape; too deeply, Rose would bleed out. Eli wanted an **impetuous** answer. "Tell me what to do," he said. "Stop making me your conscience," she answered. He laughed a small **abject** laugh, then admitted the broker had promised a **patent** cure and told him to wait until the filly sickened; he had hoped to **obtain** both the cure and a heroic **epilogue** for the price of silence. The admission was no **conspiracy**, only the kind of cowardice that can **encompass** two strangers in a single bad choice. Mara could have reported him. Instead she told him to hold Rose's head and **relent** when she pulled against him. He was **compliant**, not brave, but steady. She opened the vein, trapped the larva in a steel cup, and watched it **petrify** in the chilled serum. Rose collapsed into **slumber**, her breathing slowing to an **equable** rhythm by the time the tide turned.

她把无声听诊器贴上母马的腹侧，凝神倾听。寄生虫已经开始从血液退回伤口，正如血清本应迫使它做的那样，但它沿着一条静脉另开了一条路径。切得太浅，它会逃走；切得太深，罗斯会流血而死。伊莱想要一个冲动干脆的答案。"告诉我该怎么做，"他说。"别再让我替你做良心，"她回答。他发出一声可怜的小笑，随后承认，经纪人曾许诺一种有专利的疗法，并让他等到母马发病；他本指望用沉默的代价同时得到疗法和一段英雄式的收场白。这坦白谈不上阴谋，只是那种能把两个陌生人包裹进同一个糟糕选择的怯懦。玛拉本可以举报他，却只让他按住罗斯的头，在罗斯挣扎时放松力道。他很顺从，并不勇敢，但稳稳地按着。她割开静脉，用钢杯接住幼虫，看着它在冰冷的血清中僵死石化。罗斯倒下睡去，到潮水转向时，她的呼吸已平缓成均匀的节奏。

By morning, the horse's breath had acquired an almost impossible **serenity**, and the rescue launch arrived with a **rookie** inspector and a **smug** cameraman from the mainland media. They found Mara on the barn floor, too tired to **saunter** anywhere, preparing a full **explication** of the specimen for the record. The inspector tried to **eavesdrop** on Eli's statement, then **smirk**ed when he learned the fisher had paid a broker. Mara stopped him. "The **veracity** of the account does not make him a villain worth displaying." The cameraman hunted a **histrionic** angle, asking whether the island's **ecumenical** chapel would condemn an infected horse as **heretical**. Eli, still pale, told him to leave. Mara did not need to **rehabilitate** Eli's reputation or **gratify** his wish to be forgiven; she needed to **determine** whether Rose remained safe, and to run the quarantine without punishing a man for asking too late.

到清晨，母马的呼吸里出现了一种近乎不可思议的宁静。日出后，救援艇载着一名新手检查员和一名来自大陆媒体、洋洋自得的摄影师抵达。他们发现玛拉坐在马厩地板上，累得连闲逛的力气都没有，正为存档准备对标本的完整解释。检查员想偷听伊莱的陈述，听到渔夫付过经纪人钱后便得意地笑了。玛拉制止了他。"这份叙述的真实，不会使他变成值得展示的恶人。"摄影师四处寻找戏剧化的角度，问岛上那座普世教派的小教堂会不会把受感染的马斥为异端。脸色仍白的伊莱叫他离开。玛拉不需要恢复伊莱的名誉，也不需要满足他想被原谅的愿望；她需要确定罗斯是否已经安全，并在不惩罚一个求助太晚之人的前提下执行隔离。

The lab's **lucid** report confirmed that the chilled larva could not revive, that the serum was **conducive** to Rose's recovery, and that the barn's **potable** water was clean. It found no **profundity** in the broker's theory, only a **specious** sales **mimic**ry of science, and it cleared the filly of further risk, for she was no longer **vulnerable** to a new bloom. Mara would **dispose** of the larva's carcass carefully, **burnish** the steel cup for reuse, and file the case without **doggerel**, without a **torrid** plea for praise, and without any **immaterial** claim of innocence. The inspector, an **indigent** trainee who had never seen a salt-borer, asked to **peer** into the cup, and what he saw there made him go quiet.

实验室清晰的报告确认，冰镇过的幼虫无法复苏，血清有助于罗斯康复，马厩里的饮用水也很干净。报告在经纪人的理论里找不到任何深度，只有一套对科学似是而非的销售模仿，并判定母马不再有进一步风险，因为她对新一轮发作已不再脆弱。玛拉会妥善处置幼虫的尸体，把钢杯擦亮以便复用，并在呈交病例时不写打油诗，不作热烈的求赞陈词，也不提出任何无关紧要的无罪主张。那名从未见过盐蛀虫的贫困见习检查员请求凝视杯中的标本，他看见的东西让他沉默下来。

The larva's dead **brilliance** did not **betoken** any **renaissance** for the species. Outside, a **grueling** tide had cut a **canyon** in the road and left a **tumult** of broken reeds beneath the chapel's **colonnade**. Eli's small **croon** quieted Rose as she **lurch**ed toward the repaired gate. He asked Mara to **veto** his plan to sell her. "That is your **particular** burden," she said. "The law asks for **rectitude**, not an **eternal** punishment." The inspector's **favorable** report restored a thin **concord** to the key, though Mara felt only **vexation** at the cameraman's retreating van. At noon Eli led the filly, unsteadily but alive, across the reopened road. Mara watched until their figures grew small beneath the cliff, then shut the barn door against the sea. The choice had not made her pure or wise. It had merely taught her the work of mercy: to cut where cutting was necessary, and to keep her hand from going farther.

幼虫死后那份光辉并不预示这个物种会再度复兴。外面，一场折磨人的潮水在路上冲出一道深沟，又在教堂柱廊下留下一堆纷乱的断芦苇。伊莱轻轻哼唱，让蹒跚走向修好门口的罗斯安静下来。他请求玛拉否决他卖掉罗斯的计划。"那是你特有的负担，"她说，"法律要求正直，不要求永恒的惩罚。"检查员有利的报告让岛上恢复了一点和睦，尽管玛拉只为摄影师远去的面包车感到烦恼。正午时，伊莱牵着这匹步子不稳却仍活着的年轻母马，沿着重新开放的道路离去。玛拉一直看到他们的身影在悬崖下变小，然后关上马厩门，把大海挡在外面。这个选择没有使她变得纯洁或明智。它只教会她仁慈的工作：在必须切开的地方切开，并让自己的手不要伸得更远。
