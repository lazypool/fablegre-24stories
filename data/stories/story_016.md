## Story for Chunk 016 - Glass Brood

On the first noon of her field **sabbatical**, Nera Vale worked alone in an insectary above Drybone **creek**, hired to **conserve** a desert moth whose larvae could **propagate** only in blue **flax** roots. She was a **scrupulous**, **diligent** entomologist, able to **discriminate** one wing vein from another, but **vacillation** overcame her whenever care demanded harm. The moths made an **asymmetrical** **display**, their **pigment** silver and rust, their antennae **spiny**. Her **compendium** lay beside **immaculate** tools, but Seven was **robust**, **pliant**, and covered in a **myriad** of pores that exhaled **viscid** mist. It made the light **diffuse** and nearly **insensible**. Its **racy** scent altered the usual **bouquet** of soil. This was no **piddling** anomaly. Her measurements verified fertile eggs, and a **resonant** click came from the cage like a **husky** throat beginning to **rouse**.

休假第一天的正午，内拉·维尔独自在枯骨溪上方的昆虫饲养室工作。她受雇保护一种沙漠蛾，这种蛾的幼虫只能在蓝亚麻根部繁殖。她是一位一丝不苟、勤勉的昆虫学家，能分辨每一条翅脉，但每当保护需要伤害时，犹豫不决便会压倒她。蛾子构成不对称的陈列，色素呈银锈两色，触角多刺。她的概要放在一尘不染的工具旁，可七号标本强健而柔韧，身上有无数微孔，正呼出黏稠的雾气。雾气使光线散开，几乎难以察觉。它浓郁的气味改变了泥土原有的香气。这不是微不足道的异常。她的测量证实了有生育力的卵，笼中传出洪亮的咔嗒声，如同一副沙哑的喉咙开始苏醒。

Milo Quist came from the weather mast in a **tawdry** scarf. The **affable**, **consummate** technician was **ham-handed** with forceps but proud of his **foolproof** irrigation **gadget** and its **construct**. “Your miracle has a **fault**,” he said in a **drawl**, a **facetious** salute following. A **tornado** had left **debris**, a **spate** of dust, and one **permeable** cracked panel. Nera’s **languor** vanished. Though the wind would **wane**, a **transient** gust could carry eggs past the mesh, whose broken **tether** no longer held it to the frame, toward pasture where a herder’s **cow** would eat the flax. Milo urged her to **defer** action. She saw a **substantial** breach: the brood’s **puissance** was not **absolute**, but it could **impede** every plan. “Do we **cosset** it because it is rare?” he asked. By night, larvae made a **welter** of roots, pausing to **masticate** flax with **voracious** patience. One gave Milo a **lash**; Nera applied a **tourniquet**, had him **splice** the mesh, and began to **excavate** trays. His **feeble** apology made her **testy**, but she did not **excoriate** him. Their **mobility** allowed a sudden **dart**, and their skins began to **mottle** the floor. The manual’s **lacunae** offered no answer.

米洛·奎斯特围着一条俗丽的围巾，从气象塔回来。这位和蔼可亲、技艺高超的技术员拿镊子却笨手笨脚，他为那套万无一失的灌溉小装置及其构造感到自豪。“你的奇迹有个故障，”他拖着长腔调说，随后开玩笑般敬礼。一场龙卷风留下残骸、大量尘土和一块可渗透的裂面板。内拉的倦怠消失了。即使风会减弱，一阵短暂的阵风也能把卵吹过网罩；固定网罩与框架的系绳已经断裂，卵会飘向牧场，而牧民的母牛会吃掉亚麻。米洛劝她推迟行动。她看见一次实质性的泄漏，这窝生物的力量并非绝对，却能阻碍所有计划。“我们是因为它稀有就溺爱它吗？”他问。夜里，幼虫把根系搅成一团混乱，贪婪而耐心地咀嚼亚麻。一只抽伤了米洛，内拉给他绑上止血带，让他接合网罩，自己开始挖出托盘。他无力的道歉令她烦躁，但她没有严厉责骂他。它们惊人的移动性使其能突然猛蹿，皮肤把地板染成斑驳颜色。手册中的空缺没有给出答案。

Milo, a **gourmand** even over canned beans, proposed fire. Nera would not **violate** the **obligatory** rule whose **foundation** was the desert’s **purity**, though she had never tested that belief. An **addendum** described a chamber to **subjugate** breeding stock. They could **fabricate** a hose from **limber** tubing and **deploy** nitrogen, but not trust its controls. At dawn, a **stentorian** alarm brought no **retinue**, only the **abiding** brood. Milo’s **kinfolks** had farmed there. A **devout** neighbor would call the moth **impiety** made flesh; his **diehard** grandmother would call its death a **sin**. Nera could not **adopt** either answer. The larvae had reached the chamber’s **hub**. Release vapor and risk the strain, or let the uncontained swarm’s dazzling, toxic display **ravish** the valley cattle, holding them spellbound until they wandered into the poisoned flax. Milo’s **persiflage** ceased. “Your **qualification** is knowing them. Mine is knowing this station.” A **discretionary** bypass could **catalyze** a controlled coolant reaction, lowering the chamber’s temperature slowly and perhaps offering Seven **succor**. Nera rejected the **illusion** of a merciful choice. She told him to **broach** the vent; together they assembled a foil funnel and began the **travail**.

米洛即使面对罐装豆子也是个美食家，他提议放火。内拉不会违反那条强制规则，它的根基是沙漠的纯净性，尽管她从未检验过这种信念。一则补遗说明有一间舱室可用来制服繁殖种群。他们能用柔软的管材制造软管并部署氮气，却不能信任它的控制装置。黎明时，洪亮的警报没有带来随从，只有持续不息的幼虫群。米洛的亲属曾在这里务农。一位虔诚的邻居会把蛾说成人类不敬神明的化身，他顽固守旧的祖母会把它的死亡称作罪。内拉无法接受任一种答案。幼虫已经到了舱室的核心。释放蒸汽就可能危及这支种群，不释放则会让失控虫群炫目而有毒的展示迷住山谷里的牛群，使它们走进有毒的亚麻地。米洛的插科打诨停止了。“你的资格是了解它们。我的资格是了解这个站点。”一条可自行启用的旁路能够催化受控的冷却剂反应，使舱室温度缓慢下降，也许能救援七号。内拉拒绝把任何选择称作仁慈的幻觉。她让他钻开通风口，两人造出锡箔漏斗，开始辛劳的工作。

The bypass held. Nera chose containment, not **liberation** or annihilation, and made a **contrite** admission that she had delayed too long. Repairs exceeded the **honorarium**, but they could **reap** several days for quarantine without any need to **alienate** herders whose **wealth** was forage. She would **cede** no specimen or **divulge** the site before quarantine could **qualify** it. Her **abstract** would **verify** the damage did not **incriminate** Milo. She would not **profess** **logicism** or write a **lachrymose** **elegy**. At the county hearing, their opponents had used a **filibuster** to delay a vote on emergency containment, concealing the **barbarity** of easy fire. A **stalwart** Milo used an axe to **hew** a **twig**-sized wedge free from a crate and brace the bypass; the valve’s **strength** failed, but the wedge held the line. They would not **enfranchise** Seven by granting it release into certain ruin, nor **feign** mercy in a **masquerade**. A **rabble** gathered, but Nera’s **imperturbable** stare sent them away. She felt no **envy** for their certainty. She would **pore** over data until her **contention** could earn a defensible review **score**, then let the station **rejoice** when new filters could **distill** mist and keep it from **encumber**ing the vents. A **mischievous** larva gave one final **leer**. Milo gave praise a **snub**, then asked with **wit** whether a moth was an **underdog**. “A **puerile** question,” Nera said; no answer could **tantalize** her now. Their **camaraderie** was hard won. Outside, the **preeminent** female **kangaroo** rat, the known dominant female of the local breeding pair, passed beneath the flax. Nera watched its shadow **forge** a line across the sand as it burrowed and felt no need to **sanctify** what they had saved, only to **honor** the boundary she had nearly failed to keep.

旁路撑住了。内拉选择隔离，而不是解放或消灭，并悔悟地承认自己拖延了太久。维修费超过酬金，但他们可以争取数天隔离时间，又不至于疏远那些牲畜就是其财富的牧民。在隔离团队确认此处合格之前，她不会割让标本，也不会泄露地点。她的摘要会证实损坏不能证明米洛有罪。她不会宣称逻辑主义，也不会写一首伤感的挽歌。在县里的听证会上，对手曾用冗长演说阻挠紧急隔离表决，掩盖轻易放火的残酷。坚强的米洛用斧头从木箱上砍下一块细枝大小的楔块来支撑旁路，阀门的强度失效了，但楔块撑住了管线。他们不会以授予七号自由的名义把它放进必然毁灭的环境，也不会假装他们的处理是一场仁慈的伪装。一群人聚来，但内拉镇定自若的一瞥让他们离开。她并不嫉妒他们的确信。她会仔细研读数据，直到自己的论点能在评审中获得站得住脚的评分，随后等新的过滤器能蒸馏雾气并阻止它堵塞通风口时，再让站点欢庆。一只调皮的幼虫投来最后一个斜眼。米洛冷落了赞扬，接着机智地问一只蛾是否算作弱者。“幼稚的问题，”内拉说，没有答案能再令她干着急。他们的友谊是在艰难中得来的。外面，那只当地繁殖配对中公认占主导地位的雌性袋鼠鼠从亚麻下经过。内拉看着它钻洞时投下的影子在沙地上划出一条线，并不需要把他们救下的一切神圣化，只需尊重那条自己险些没能守住的界限。

By evening, the chamber was sealed and the emergency report was on its way. Nera marked the surviving moths for observation, while Milo set a quiet watch beside the repaired mesh.

傍晚时，舱室已经封闭，应急报告也在发送途中。内拉为存活的蛾子作上观察标记，米洛则在修好的网罩旁安静值守。
