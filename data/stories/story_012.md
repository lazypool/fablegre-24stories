## Story for Chunk 012 - Abyssal Lattice

When a **squall** struck the black inlet, Elias Wren and Mara Senn crossed it in a **rickety** cabin-boat, a floating **hovel** whose compass had begun to **discharge** blue sparks. Elias, an **aspirant** **apothecary**, carried a **lethal** vial of brine stolen from no person, while Mara kept the vessel steady with unexpected **stamina**. A **paunchy**, **rakish** moon hung above the water. With each thunderclap, the **decibel** reading on the compass rose, and its blue sparks made the moon appear nearer. The sea itself gave a shrieking cry from below, and Elias saw that its rising light was no **fluke** but a **forerunner** of something ancient. His **prescience** offered only one warning.

暴风雨袭击漆黑海湾时，伊莱亚斯·雷恩和玛拉·森乘着一艘摇晃的舱船横渡海面；这艘漂浮的简陋小屋，其罗盘已经开始放出蓝色电火花。身为药剂师学徒的伊莱亚斯携带着一小瓶致命的盐水，并非从任何人手中偷来；玛拉则以惊人的耐力稳住船身。一个肥胖而潇洒的月亮悬在水面上。每次雷鸣时，罗盘上的分贝读数都会升高，随之迸出的蓝色火花使月亮仿佛更近。海底传来尖厉的呼号，伊莱亚斯明白那涌起的光绝非偶然，而是某种古老之物的前兆。他的预感只给出一个警示。

That **insight** came too late to waste: they must **deflect** the current before it could **precipitate** the thing beneath them into the waking world. Mara's **predilection** for clear action made the choice **tenable**. She poured a **coagulant** into the **gossamer** net Elias had woven around a brass **gear**, then lowered it through a **sparse** aperture in the hull. Below, a **seascape** of impossible shelves unfolded: every **dune** stood vertical, every trench seemed to **contort** its own course in a **serpentine** knot.

这份洞察来得恰到好处：他们必须转开洋流，赶在海下之物被推进清醒世界之前。玛拉偏爱明确的行动，这让选择依然成立。她把凝结剂倒入伊莱亚斯围住黄铜齿轮编成的薄网中，再从船壳上一处稀疏的孔口放下去。船下展开一片不可能的海景：每座沙丘都直立着，每道海沟都像要扭曲自己的轨迹，绕成蛇形的结。

A **fictitious** horizon folded around their boat, and the geometry seemed a **pastiche** made by a mad **gourmet** arranging courses of coral and bone. A drowned **spelunker** drifted past, still clutching a **wrangler**'s hook; his open mouth formed a **bawdy** grin. "Do not **gloat**," Mara told the darkness when it answered in a **jocular** chorus. "We did not come to **woo** you." The chorus became a **diatribe**, a **trivial** yet hateful **gripe** that tried to make them **acquiesce** to the deep. Soon the water itself began to **block** their oars.

虚假的地平线折回船的周围，那种几何构造像发狂的美食家把珊瑚和骨头摆成的一道杂烩。一名溺亡的洞穴探险者漂过，仍抓着牧马人的钩子；他张开的嘴形成一副猥亵的笑容。"别幸灾乐祸，"当黑暗以诙谐的合唱回应时，玛拉对它说，"我们不是来讨好你的。"合唱化为一场抨击，一句琐碎却充满憎恨的抱怨，试图让他们向深海默认。很快，海水本身也开始封住他们的船桨。

Yet the net began to **congeal** the shining fluid into a **clot** around the turning mechanism. Elias set the **filter** over the intake and watched the **motile** threads within it begin to **dilute**, thinning into pale dust. Mara used a **clasp** to tether them together, because an **errant** angle could **shackle** either body to a wrong direction. Their route made them **perambulate** across a wall that had become a floor. A **striate** arch opened toward an **infinite** void, and the entity tried to **hoodwink** Elias with an **illusory** **daguerreotype**.

然而网开始把发光液体凝成围住转动机关的一团凝块。伊莱亚斯把滤器罩在进水口上，看着其中会蠕动的细丝开始稀释，逐渐变成苍白的尘埃。玛拉用扣环把两人拴在一起，因为一个偏离的角度可能把任一人的身体锁到错误方向。他们沿着已变成地板的墙面行走。一道有条纹的拱门通向无穷的虚空，那实体试图用一张幻影般的银版照片蒙骗伊莱亚斯。

The picture showed a **patrician** banquet, all **savory** smoke and **mint** wine, but Elias saw the lure for what it was and would not let its **insouciant** beauty **irk** his resolve. At the void's center, the ancient entity rose as a **commodious** lattice of fins, mouths, and eyes. It offered him a **bribe** of **perpetual** calm, then threatened to **discredit** Mara as a **quixotic** intruder and make her a **subject** of its hunger. Its **harsh** voice ordered them to **scold** one another, awaken old **enmity**, and turn their fears into division.

画面里是一场贵族宴席，飘着可口的烟，盛着薄荷酒，但伊莱亚斯看穿了诱饵，没有让其漫不经心的美感动摇自己的决心。在虚空中心，古老的实体以由鳍、嘴和眼组成的宽敞格架升起。它用永恒的平静贿赂他，继而威胁要诋毁玛拉为不切实际的闯入者，并把她变成其饥饿的对象。它刺耳的声音命令他们彼此责骂，唤醒旧日敌意，把恐惧化为分裂。

They refused the **scission** it demanded, and Elias answered with **clarity** rather than **repartee**. "Your **egoism** mistakes terror for command." Mara crouched into a **squat** stance and threw the mechanism through the lattice; the collision made space **implode**. The creature tried to **retaliate**, but its limbs could not **vary** fast enough, and the **responsive** brine made its entire form recoil. The lattice tore free with a **vicious** **eddy**, leaving the boat in a **turbid** but ordinary sea. Elias and Mara made no **maudlin** speeches and did not **pontificate** over their escape.

他们拒绝了它要求的割裂，伊莱亚斯以清晰而非机锋作答。"你的自私把恐惧误当成命令。"玛拉蹲低身子，将机关掷入格架；碰撞使空间向内坍缩。那生物试图报复，但它的肢体来不及变化，能感应的盐水令它的整个形体后退。格架伴着凶恶的漩涡撕裂脱离，船回到了浑浊却寻常的海面。伊莱亚斯和玛拉没有发表伤感的言辞，也没有高谈阔论他们的脱险。

They sealed the recovered device inside a **palatial** shell, then **cast** it beyond the reef, where it could not **coerce** another mind. The entity's **legacy** was only a fading stain, neither **valid** nor **virtuous**, and their **dogged** work had ended the **precarious** danger without any **half-baked** bargain. Mara's **panache** and **suppleness** had matched Elias's **adaptability**; together they refused every **condescending** lure and every **brattish** command.

他们把找回的装置封进一只宫殿般华丽的贝壳，随后把它抛过礁石，使它再也无法胁迫任何心灵。那实体留下的遗产只是一片逐渐消退的污痕，既不正当，也不善良。他们顽强的努力终结了岌岌可危的危险，没有达成任何草率的交易。玛拉的神采与柔韧配合了伊莱亚斯的适应力；两人一起拒绝了每一种居高临下的引诱，每一道幼稚的命令。

They also refused each **haphazard** **embargo** imposed on research by frightened authorities. The entity's last **antic** gesture left a **dent** in the rail and a **toy** moon in the foam, while Elias saw no **episode** of mercy in it and offered an absolute **refusal**. They would not **equivocate** over its **heresy**, or let its **plodding** pulse **ripen** inside the sea. Mara used the **serried** stars to guide their course, while Elias, a quiet **anarchist** of the abyss, saw the creature's own geometry **inculpate** it in the attack.

他们还拒绝了恐惧的当局对研究施加的每一次无端禁令。那实体最后一个滑稽动作在栏杆上留下凹痕，又在泡沫中留下一个玩具般的小月亮；伊莱亚斯从中看不出任何宽恕的迹象，便给出断然的拒绝。他们不会对它的异端含糊其辞，也不会让它迟缓的脉冲在海中成熟。玛拉借排列密集的星辰引导航向，而作为深渊中沉静的无政府主义者，伊莱亚斯看见那生物自身的几何结构使它在这场袭击中现出罪责。

The **edifice** of its angles had become **effete**, ornate and self-indulgent, unable to sustain itself. The **troupe** of mouths lost its rhythm as the **sanitation** of the surrounding water was restored. They flushed its residue with a **bland** **milk** of lime, enough to **compensate** for the lost alkalinity, until the entity's residual voice became **penitent** and **temperate**. Elias used **apophasis**, saying he would not name the entity while clearly referring to it: its **caricature** remained only to **gerrymander** irregular containment zones around its old currents, and both knew the task was **ticklish**. Yet their **accord** held; even a **patriot** defending a vanished nation could not have been more steadfast.

那座由角度构成的建筑已经衰微、装饰繁复而放纵，无法自持。随着周围海水的卫生状况恢复，那一群嘴失去了节奏。他们用淡性的石灰乳冲洗残渣，足以补偿流失的碱度，直到实体残余的声音变得悔悟而温和。伊莱亚斯使用了佯言不提的修辞，说自己不会点出实体的名字，却分明在指向它：只留下它的一幅讽刺肖像，用来不规则地划定围住旧日洋流的隔离区；两人都知道这项任务很棘手。然而他们的一致意见没有动摇，即使是保卫一个已消失国家的爱国者，也不会比他们更坚定。

As the wind began to **waft** clean salt across the deck, even the **dour** Elias felt **buoyant**. They shared water instead of **swill**, checked the cloud shaped like a **pancreas** above, and took a short **respite** while the **peripheral** stars regained their proper **orientation**. There was no **court** to **misconstrue** the result, no **calumny** to answer, no **fiasco** left to **renovate**. Their **preference** was simple: keep moving, **tinker** with the compass, and let the sea's **veneer** of normality remain unbroken.

清洁的盐味开始飘过甲板时，即使阴郁的伊莱亚斯也感到轻快。他们喝水而非劣酒，查看上方形似胰腺的云，并趁着外围星辰恢复正确方位时稍作喘息。没有法庭会误解结果，没有诽谤需要回应，也没有灾难尚待修整。他们的选择很简单：继续前行，调校罗盘，让海面寻常的表层保持完整。
