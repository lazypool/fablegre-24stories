## Story for Chunk 007 - Verdict Carriage

At midnight Julian Rowan and Leah Park boarded a train that had no platform, timetable, or living conductor. Its last carriage was a courtroom: green lamps burned without flame, benches held pale witnesses, and a stranger in a rain-dark **garment** stood in the dock. The presiding ghost announced an **elucidation** of one question: should the stranger be carried beyond the final station, or returned to the moment of his death? Julian, a railway **savant** who preferred exact mechanisms to moral riddles, felt a **qualm**. Leah, less **vague** in her sympathy, asked the stranger for his **origin**. He gave only a **barefaced** **claim**: he had pushed a child clear of the rails and awakened on this train. The judge called that story an **aberration** from every witness account, and a **querulous** bailiff warned them not to **obstruct** the hearing. Outside, a **cloudburst** bruised the windows while the wheels kept an impossible **melody**. Julian saw a **vestige** of blood on the stranger's cuff, but no wound.

午夜，朱利安·罗文和莉娅·帕克登上一列没有站台、时刻表或活人乘务员的火车。末节车厢是一间法庭：绿灯无焰燃烧，长凳上坐着苍白的证人，一名身穿被雨浸暗的衣袍的陌生人站在被告席中。主审幽灵宣布要阐明一个问题：这名陌生人该被送过终点站，还是送回他死亡的时刻。偏爱精确机械而不擅道德谜题的铁路专家朱利安心怀疑虑。莉娅的同情并不含糊，她问陌生人来自何处。他毫不掩饰地声称，自己把一个孩子推离铁轨后便在这列车上醒来。法官说这个说法与所有证词都不相符，一名爱抱怨的法警警告他们不要妨碍听证。窗外暴雨拍打玻璃，车轮却敲出不可能的旋律。朱利安看见陌生人袖口有一丝血迹，却没有伤口。

"We must **assess** the act, not admire its **garish** appearance," Julian said, and the ghost judge replied that no verdict could **abbreviate** a life to courage or guilt. A **recluse** in life, Julian knew how silence could **estrange** one person from another. Leah answered that **uncertainty** did not make the man a **dullard**, a villain, or a saint. The first witness was a ticket collector whose **hoary** face shone through a sheet of rain. She said the stranger had reached for the child, then frozen; his **complacency** had cost a second passenger her life. Her testimony was **tedious** only because it was precise. The stranger did not **concede** the death, but his **scorn** for the charge made Leah flinch. "Do not **understate** the loss," she said. "Yet do not let grief **stifle** thought."

"我们必须评估行为，而不是欣赏它浮夸的外表。"朱利安说，幽灵法官则回答，任何裁决都不能把一生缩略为勇气或有罪。朱利安生前是个隐居者，他知道沉默如何使人彼此疏远。莉娅说，不确定并不使这人变成愚人、恶人或圣人。第一名证人是个售票员，她灰白的脸透过一片雨幕发亮。她说陌生人曾伸手去够那孩子，随后却僵住了，他的自满让另一名乘客丧了命。她的证词之所以显得冗长，只因每个细节都准确。陌生人没有承认那起死亡，可他面对指控时的轻蔑让莉娅不由得退缩。"不要低估这份损失。"她说，"但也别让悲痛窒息思考。"

A second ghost, a boy with a paper cap, began to **preen** before the jury and said the stranger had tried to **hamstring** the child by cutting the tendon behind his knee with a suitcase strap. The accusation was so **absurd** that even the bailiff dropped his **genteel** pose. Julian asked the boy to **particularize** what he had seen, and under a few calm questions the boy's account began to **crumble**: a **fluctuation** in the carriage lights had blurred the platform, and fear had made every shadow **protrude** into menace. Leah said that careful attention, rather than flattery, was the only **remedy** for his fear, and he admitted that the stranger had shouted for help, then tried to **relinquish** his own place at the door. The judge said the court must **reconvene** after a **rehearsal** of the fatal minute, and the silent witnesses rose in a **chorus**. At the **zenith** of the train's climb, the walls became transparent.

第二个幽灵是个戴纸帽的男孩，他在陪审团前得意地整理自己，声称陌生人曾想用手提箱带子割断孩子膝后的腿腱，使他残废。这项指控荒谬至极，连法警都放下了那副文雅的姿态。朱利安请男孩详细说明他看见了什么，几句平静的提问就让男孩的陈述开始瓦解：车厢灯光的一次波动模糊了站台，恐惧则把每道影子都夸张成威胁。莉娅说，认真倾听而非奉承，才是化解他恐惧的唯一补救，男孩承认陌生人先呼救，随后还试图让出自己门边的位置。法官说，法庭必须在重演致命一分钟后再次开庭，沉默的证人们随即齐声起立。列车爬到最高点时，车壁变得透明。

A black **brook** ran below the rails, and above it hung a bleak moon. The court arranged a **patchwork** of remembered positions: the child, the stranger, the dead woman, and a porter with a brass claw on his watch chain. "This is not a **treaty** between memory and desire," Leah told Julian. "It is a choice." The stranger watched the scene with **depressed** stillness, as if **repose** were the only sentence he could bear, and Julian rejected that **mawkish** surrender. He asked whether the porter had tried to **retard** the train. The porter, an **extremist** for rules, said passengers must **comply** with the bell and never cross the yellow line. His **zeal** was nearly a **zealot**'s devotion to procedure. Leah saw the crucial fact: he had locked the gate to preserve order, then let panic **agitate** the platform. "Your rule did not **guarantee** safety," she said, and the porter answered with **vehement** **skepticism**.

铁轨下方流着一条黑色的小溪，上方悬着凄清的月亮。法庭排布出一幅由记忆拼接的场景：孩子、陌生人、死去的女人，以及一名表链上挂着黄铜爪饰的搬运工各就其位。"这不是记忆与欲望之间的条约。"莉娅对朱利安说，"这是一项选择。"陌生人以沮丧的静止看着那一幕，仿佛安息是他唯一承受得起的判决，朱利安拒绝了这种伤感的屈服。他问搬运工是否曾试图让列车减速。那名搬运工是规章的极端主义者，他说乘客必须遵从铃声，绝不能越过黄线。他的热忱几乎是狂热者对程序的信奉。莉娅看到了关键事实：他为了维持秩序锁上闸门，随后任由恐慌搅动站台。"你的规章并不能保证安全。"她说，搬运工则报以激烈的否认。

Julian noticed that the lock's **inanimate** latch was bent; no person had forced it, the train itself had shaken it loose. The ghost judge called this a **watershed**, and the stranger began to **rue** the breath he had spent blaming himself. Then the dead woman spoke. She wore a **turquoise** scarf and carried no grievance, only a **scabbard** of light at her side. "He did not kill me," she said. "He chose whom he could reach." Her words did not **sate** the court's hunger for certainty. A juror in a canvas-backed chair muttered that such mercy was **mediocre** justice; another called it **profligate** emotion and urged them to outlaw pity. Leah would not **flatter** their severity. "A verdict may be **impervious** to bribery and still be **immune** to compassion," she said. The stranger revealed that he had heard the child scream first, that the woman's hand had already slipped from the rail, and that he had wanted to **guzzle** the whisky offered after the accident. Julian felt his old **negligence** of human consequence turn to **remorse**, and the judge asked for the court's **roster**.

朱利安注意到那把锁无生命的锁扣已经弯曲：并非有人强行破坏，而是列车自身的震动将它摇松。幽灵法官称这为一个分水岭，陌生人开始后悔自己一直耗费气力地责怪自己。随后死去的女人开口了。她戴着一条青绿色围巾，身旁没有怨恨，只有一把光构成的剑鞘。"他没有杀我。"她说，"他选择了他能够够到的人。"她的话并未满足法庭对确定性的渴求。坐在帆布靠背椅上的一名陪审员咕哝说，这样的怜悯只是平庸的正义；另一人称它为放纵的情感，主张将同情列为禁令。莉娅不肯奉承他们的严厉。"裁决可以不受贿赂影响，却仍可能对同情毫无感受。"她说。陌生人透露，他先听见孩子的尖叫，女人的手那时已从栏杆上滑脱，事故后他曾想狂饮递来的威士忌。朱利安感到自己昔日对人类后果的疏忽转为悔恨，法官索要法庭名册。

Each witness placed a hand over an empty name, and the clerk began to **alliterate** the names in a chanted roll call. The dead woman asked Julian and Leah whether the stranger's sacrifice should **ennoble** him, or whether moral luck made praise a sham. "Neither," Julian said. "It should make us **conversant** with the limit of a hand in motion." Leah added that no **layperson** needs permission to recognize that limit. For the final examination, the judge made the carriage **denude** itself of benches and lamps. A **nascent** dawn seeped through the windows, but the train remained **quiescent** between stations. The stranger's fate lay before them like a **parquetry** board: many small pieces, no concealed mechanism. Julian refused the judge's invitation to **camouflage** the choice as **metaphysics**. "A person is not a **prop** for our fear," he said. Leah, with **brevity**, asked the stranger what he wanted. He said he wanted an **asylum** from the repeating moment, yet feared that peace would be a **surfeit** he had not earned.

每名证人都把手放在一个空白名字上，书记员在唱名时让这些名字形成头韵。死去的女人请朱利安和莉娅决定，这名陌生人的牺牲是否该使他高尚，还是道德运气使赞美沦为骗局。"两者都不是。"朱利安说，"它应让我们熟悉一只伸出的手所能达到的界限。"莉娅补充，任何外行都不需要许可就能认出这个界限。最后审问时，法官让车厢褪去座椅和灯盏。初生的黎明渗进车窗，可列车仍静止在两站之间。陌生人的命运像一块细木镶拼板摆在两人面前：许多小块，没有隐藏的机关。朱利安拒绝了法官把选择伪装成形而上学的邀请。"人不是承载我们恐惧的道具。"他说。莉娅简短地问陌生人想要什么。他说自己想要逃离这重复的时刻，却害怕安宁是自己不配享有的过量恩赐。

The judge's face went **crestfallen**. "Then sentence him," it said. Leah considered the **plentitude** of the dead who had no voice and the living child who did. She would not **aggrandize** herself with mercy, nor **calumniate** the porter for failing. "Let him remain aboard until he can face the memory without trying to **distract** from it," she ruled, and Julian added that the train must not **secrete** pain behind ritual; every passenger should hear the account. The ghost judge bowed, and the carriage's **astringent** air warmed. Julian recalled a railworker's warning: "When the dawn begins to **scintillate**, keep clear of the line." The wheels began to **flit** forward. At the next **inch** of light the stranger smiled, not **hale** but no longer alone. While the train gathered speed, the clerk would **convoke** the witnesses one final time, and a **prudent** woman set an **easel** beside the rail.

法官神情沮丧。"那你们来判吧。"它说。莉娅想到无声的众多死者，也想到仍能说话的活着的孩子。她不会以怜悯抬高自己，也不会诽谤失职的守门人。"让他留在车上，直到能直面记忆而不试图转移注意。"她裁定，朱利安补充说，列车不可借仪式把痛苦藏起来，每名乘客都应听到经过。幽灵法官鞠躬，车厢中凛冽的空气转暖。朱利安想起铁路工人的警告："当天色开始闪烁时，务必远离铁轨。"车轮开始轻快前行。在下一寸晨光里，陌生人微笑了，他尚未康复，却不再孤单。随着列车渐渐加速，书记员将最后一次召集证人，一名谨慎的女人在铁轨旁支起画架。

The **lumber** rail beside the easel would not be wasted; her sketch was not a **census** of souls but a way to show how the choices could **align**. Her **ingenuity** made the **exponent** of each risk clear without a **desultory** speech. The boy's **coltish** impatience gave way to calm; he did not **worship** the train's **stasis**, nor did he let a simple answer **infatuate** him. A **salient** bell began to **hammer** the roof, then to **rustle** like an **arboreal** canopy, while the boiler's steam began to cloud the glass.

画架旁那根木料不会被浪费；她的速写不是灵魂普查，而是说明各种选择如何对齐。她的巧思无需漫无目的的演说，便清楚呈现每种风险的指数。纸帽男孩孩子气的急躁平息下来；他既不崇拜列车的停滞，也不让一个简单答案使自己迷恋。醒目的钟铃先重击车顶，继而像树冠般沙沙作响，锅炉的蒸汽开始模糊玻璃。

The boiler sent a **geyser** of steam across the windows, and the moisture began to **creep** down the glass. The porter, once eager to **replace** judgment with **convenience**, had to admit his **malfeasance**. His **infamy** was not a sentence, and the court would not **rile** itself into a **turbulent** punishment. Leah told him to **rest** his hands on the rail; no verdict could **detract** from the dead, but honest listening might **infuse** the living with **beneficence**. Julian called the hearing a turning point rather than a reckoning. He would not **dump** blame onto a stranger, nor **proscribe** doubt.

锅炉喷泉似的蒸汽漫过车窗，水汽开始沿着玻璃向下爬。守门人曾急于用方便取代判断，如今不得不承认自己的渎职。他的恶名不是一种刑罚，法庭也不会把自己搅成动荡的惩罚。莉娅让他把手放在栏杆上休息；任何裁决都无法减损死者的损失，但诚实的倾听或许能把善意注入生者。朱利安称这场听证是一个转折点而非清算。他不会把责任倾倒给一个陌生人，也不会禁止怀疑。

Nor would he let **hedonism** make pity cheap. The clerk lifted a **recumbent** brass **talon** used to mark the verdict, but it could not **penetrate** the **obscure** glass. "That is no **disguise**," Leah said. "It is proof that consequences can be **barren** of certainty." The train's **bacteria**-green lamps began to **ignite** with a **zesty** glow, and a final **extraction** of breath passed through the car. The judge asked whether the stranger might become a **disciple** of remembrance; Julian answered that the court must **antedate** blame with care, not let rhetoric **clog** mercy, and never mistake a **spoof** for justice. The **delighted** child boarded at dawn, alive because the stranger had acted.

他也不会让享乐主义使怜悯变得廉价。书记员举起一只横卧的黄铜爪用来标记裁决，可它穿不透昏暗的玻璃。"那不是伪装。"莉娅说，"那恰好证明后果可能毫无确定性。"列车的细菌绿灯开始燃起鲜活的光芒，一阵最后的气息穿过车厢。法官问陌生人是否能成为记忆的信徒；朱利安回答，法庭必须让关怀先于责难，不能任修辞堵住怜悯，也绝不能把恶作剧误当正义。黎明时，那个安然的孩子登上了车，他活着，因为陌生人曾行动过。
