## Story for Chunk 005 - Clocktower Inquest

At dawn, Inspector Elian Venn called the fall of Aldren's clockmaster a municipal **coup** against time itself. His broad, **ursine** body lay under a painted ceiling of **cosmography**, while the stopped escapement held its **metronome** beat at eleven fifty-nine. Only the council's **eligible** repairmen had keys, and their **preparation** for the noon ceremony made the crime's **wickedness** more pointed. An **elliptical** smear of oil crossed the brass **avatar** of Saint Aldren; beside it crouched a **paunchy** clerk with a notebook of **technography**. Someone had used kitchen string to **baste** a wire around an **incandescent** lamp, pulling it **taut** enough to **constitute** a trap. A single **speck** of blood made Venn's pulse **palpitate**. The town kept three clockwork **species** of mechanism, and the dead man's receipt, an **acquittance**, disproved the old **myth** that he had been paid to abandon the tower.

黎明时，埃利安·文探长宣称，奥尔登钟表师的坠亡是一场针对时间本身的市政政变。他宽阔而如熊般的身躯躺在描绘着宇宙地理的彩绘天花板下，停住的擒纵机构把节拍器般的节奏定格在十一点五十九分。只有议会中符合资格的修理工有钥匙，而他们为中午仪式所做的准备，使罪行的邪恶更加刺目。一道椭圆形油迹划过圣奥尔登的黄铜雕像；旁边蹲着一个大腹便便的书记员，手边有一本技术志。有人用厨房细绳把电线缚在白炽灯周围，拉得足够紧，形成了一个陷阱。一小点血迹让文的脉搏悸动起来。镇上有三种发条机构，而死者的收据，即清偿凭证，推翻了那个说他收钱离开钟楼的旧神话。

Below, a rain **maelstrom** worried the square, where civic **intrigue** had left tempers **stagnant** as water in a tree's **bole**. The wire had struck an **intestine** of cables described in an ancient **treatise**, then vanished behind a **dormer**. The discovery was **discomfiting**: the killer had climbed from the **aery** bell chamber by a **perverse** route. Venn asked who might **connive** with the dead man. The clerk began to **indite** a name, then blamed **scurvy**, **socialism**, and every passing grievance. Lightning made the clock face **scintillate**; the tower's resident **grouch**, a watchman, called the memorial a **sham** and its projected **success** an **incomparable** insult to his predecessor.

楼下，暴雨形成的漩涡搅扰着广场；市政阴谋使人们的情绪像树干里的水一样停滞。电线击中了古老论著所描述的一束如肠道般盘绕的电缆，随后消失在老虎窗后面。这个发现令人不安：凶手沿着一条反常的路线，从高处的钟室爬了下来。文问谁可能与死者共谋。书记员开始写下一个名字，接着却把一切归咎于坏脾气、社会主义以及路过时遇到的每一桩怨事。闪电使钟面闪烁；钟楼里那个爱抱怨的守夜人称纪念仪式是骗局，并说它预想中的成功会成为对前任的无可比拟的侮辱。

Venn followed a narrow **passage** to records meant to **supersede**, **perhaps**, the gardener's account. The **florist** had seen a **recreant** councilor **stammer** under gentle **suasion**, then **luxuriate** in a **supposititious** alibi. Her movements had been **noiseless** as birds that **migrate** over the river **ravine**. Public **odium** made the councilor **restless**; he relied on the tower's old **glamor**, calling its angel an **archangel** whose gaze could **recapture** civic faith and **beatify** his campaign. Yet his **impuissance** before the locked door supported **reconciliation** with the evidence. Beyond a model **tundra** in the museum, Venn found **desperate** boot marks in **loam**, measured their **stature**, and heard a subterranean **rumble**.

文沿着狭窄的通道查找记录，这些记录或许可以取代园丁的说法。花商看见一个背叛议会的议员在温和劝说下结结巴巴，随后又沉溺于一个假想的不在场证明。她行动时悄无声息，如同飞越河谷的鸟。公众的憎恶让议员坐立不安；他倚仗钟楼昔日的魅力，称其中的天使为大天使，声称其目光能重新赢得民众信任并使他的竞选蒙受祝福。然而，他面对上锁的门时显出的无力，反而与证据相互印证。在博物馆的一座模型苔原之外，文在壤土中发现凌乱而绝望的靴印，量出它们对应的身高，并听见地下传来的隆隆声。

The sound was **harsh**, but it ended her **mania** for grand motives. The victim's **existence** had been threatened by a contractor's talk of **manliness**, an **inflexible** schedule, and **inexhaustible** funds supposedly **compensated** by a donor. A retired **dialectician** had drafted the **provision**; in its margin someone had made a childish **doodle** of a serpent pouring **venom** into a **phoenix**. That **genre** of warning urged Venn to **economize** her theories. The watchman's **torpor** broke when she showed him an **exhilarating** camera **swipe** from the security **compilation**: at an **unwonted** hour, a former **conscript** had led a **cow** through the square.

声音刺耳，却终结了她对宏大动机的狂热。承包商谈及所谓男子气概、不可变更的工期，以及由捐赠者据称补偿的取之不尽的资金时，受害者的生命已经受到威胁。一位退休的辩证家起草了那项条款；有人在页边画了孩子气的涂鸦：一条蛇把毒液倒进凤凰体内。这类警告促使文节制自己的推理。她给守夜人看安保汇编中一段令人振奋的摄像头滑动画面时，他的昏沉被打破了：在一个反常的时刻，一名退伍应征兵牵着一头牛穿过广场。

The man was an **androgynous** stagehand who refused to **demean** himself by explaining a **schematic** to a **layperson**. Venn's **intuition** rejected his **carnivorous** grin and the general **malaise** of his **bombastic**, **theatrical** account. He claimed an **obedient** **clothier** had delivered a **batch** of uniforms, though the cloth bore a stain he called **distain**. Inside its sleeve Venn found a copper **conduit**, **tangible** proof that someone had used **flax** fibers to **procure** a current from the church **offertory** box. The stagehand had tried to **bowdlerize** the ledger, but two resin seals still **osculate** on its fold, proving the clerk's **competence** and a date that would **antedate** the festival. The mayor's **unilateral** order had routed the current through a drainage **culvert** to **transcend** the old **breaker**.

那人是个雌雄同体的舞台工，却拒绝通过向外行解释示意图来贬低自己。文的直觉不接受他肉食动物般的笑容，也不接受他那番浮夸而戏剧化叙述中透出的普遍萎靡。他声称一名顺从的制衣商送来了一批制服，尽管布料上有一处污渍，他称之为轻蔑。文在袖子里发现一根铜导管，这是有人用亚麻纤维从教堂奉献箱取得电流的确凿证据。舞台工试图删改账簿，但两枚树脂封印仍在折痕处相吻，证明书记员有能力，也证明日期早于节庆。市长的单方面命令让电流经过排水涵洞，越过旧断路器。

That **outmoded** alteration was too **indiscriminate** for the **raucous** operator Venn imagined. A **predator** of contracts, he had left a **reek** of burned insulation meant to **exacerbate** the panic. The wire was **sterling** work, yet its installation had been **arduous**. A **hortative** notice ordered workers to apply **wax**, never **relent**, and seek **afflatus** in the tower's history; it was designed to **sidetrack** inspection. At the parish **altar**, Venn learned that delayed stone had incurred **demurrage**, while an **ancillary** invoice showed the mayor planned to **calcify** opposition. She watched the contractor **dismount** from his carriage, cite **precedent**, and pocket a small **hammer**.

那项过时的改造对于文所想象的喧闹操作员来说过于不加区别。他是合同的掠夺者，留下烧焦绝缘层的臭味，意在加剧恐慌。电线做工精良，但安装过程十分艰苦。一张劝诫性的通知命令工人涂蜡，绝不要退让，并从钟楼的历史中寻求灵感；这是为了把检查引向旁途。在教区祭坛处，文得知延误的石材已经产生滞期费，而一张附带发票显示市长打算让反对意见僵化。她看着承包商从马车上下来，援引先例，并把一把小锤放进口袋。

He called the dead man a **goldbrick** who would not **promote** the new project until the restoration was **finished**. His map was **intricate**, showing how the gears would **rotate** beside a hidden **confederate** switch. The plan looked **valid**, but Venn sought **solitude** with an **expository** maintenance diary. Its **tenacious** author had spilled **mead** over a page naming a local **soothsayer**, whose **remediable** error had begun the fraud. A secret **pact** made the contractor's promised safety **illusory**; the resulting charge was **maleficient**, not merely **malicious**. He had staged a public **embrace** with the clockmaster to **inure** witnesses to their quarrels.

他称死者是个金砖骗子，修复完成前不肯推动新项目。他的地图错综复杂，标出了齿轮如何在隐藏的同谋开关旁旋转。计划看似有效，但文带着一本说明性的维护日记寻求独处。那位坚韧的作者把蜂蜜酒洒在一页上，页中写着一名当地预言家的名字；预言家那个可以补救的错误开启了这场欺诈。一项秘密协定使承包商承诺的安全成为幻象；由此产生的指控是有害的，而不仅仅是恶意的。他曾与钟表师公开拥抱，想让目击者习惯他们之间的争吵。

The clockmaster, habitually **dutiful**, had used a sheep cutter to **shear** the live line. A **drip** from the roof then helped it **demagnetize** the governor, making a failure plainly **impliable** from the scene. Venn found his final **stamp** on a letter to a **sympathetic** judge. The mayor, **vainglorious** and eager to **proscribe** dissent, had arranged a three-part **triplet** of false reports. The clockmaster, **founder** of the restoration society, had called the closure **temporary**; the mayor branded him **litigious**, **blithe**, and unable to **neutralize** a rumor. Yet the diary said a soaked fuse would **impregnate** the wood with gas, **nauseate** any worker, and invite public **derision** after a **tortious** collapse.

钟表师一向尽职，曾用剪羊毛的工具剪断带电线路。屋顶滴下的水随后帮助它使调速器退磁，使故障显然可以从现场推断出来。文发现了他写给一位同情他的法官的最后印章。市长虚荣自大，急于禁止异议，安排了由三部分组成的一组假报告。作为修复协会创办人的钟表师称关闭只是暂时的；市长却说他好诉讼、轻率，而且无法消除谣言。然而日记写道，浸湿的保险丝会让木头充满气体，使工人恶心，并在一次侵权性的坍塌后招来公众嘲笑。

Venn did not **malign** the mayor until the **morose** clerk said he had seen him enter. "I **believe** you," she said, though the tale of a **morganatic** ancestor hidden behind the clock **facade** was a **hindrance**. Her **diligent** search made each mystery **reducible** to a fact. The councilor did not **espouse** the theory; his study of **philology** showed that an **untempered** phrase in the diary was written by another hand. Venn could **invert** the sheet over a lamp and see an **oasis** of **original** ink beneath. It contained a **timely** warning: a blue **blaze** would ignite in the adjoining **arboretum** unless the **distaff** cable was cut.

在那个阴郁的书记员说他看见市长进去之前，文没有诋毁市长。“我相信你，”她说，尽管关于一位藏在钟面后的贵族婚生旁系祖先的故事成了障碍。她勤勉的搜查把每个谜团都化约为事实。议员没有接受这个理论；他对语言文字学的研究表明，日记中那句未经调和的话出自另一只手。文可以把纸张倒转放在灯上，看见下面一片原始墨水的绿洲。纸上有一条及时的警告：除非剪断母系电缆，否则相邻的植物园会燃起蓝色火焰。

The gardener brought **succor**, identifying the disguised **chameleon** who had asked the council to **prorogue** its vote. An **inept** forger had made two initials **assonate**, but the **inclement** weather had blurred only one. An **ichthyologist** visiting the museum supplied the missing **preoccupation**: at the **start** of the ceremony, he had seen the mayor **flush** when the **convivial** crowd cheered. The ground near the tower was **alluvial**, loose enough to **provoke** a slide. His speeches against **chauvinism** drew **raillery** at an artist's **atelier**, where a **freelancer** heard him **prate** about **goosebumps**, an **invincible** city, and the **ineffable** beauty of controlled ruin.

园丁带来了援助，认出了那个伪装的变色龙；正是他请求议会延期表决。一个拙劣的伪造者让两个首字母押韵，但恶劣天气只模糊了其中一个。来博物馆参观的鱼类学家补上了缺失的关注点：仪式开始时，他看见市长在人群欢呼时脸红。钟楼附近的地面是冲积土，松软得足以引发滑坡。他反对沙文主义的演讲在一位艺术家的工作室招来嘲弄；一名自由职业者听见他夸夸其谈地说起鸡皮疙瘩、不可战胜的城市，以及受控废墟不可言喻的美。

The wire's **hexangular** bolts left Venn **agog**. She watched it **meander** through a false wall, past a card of **chiromancy**, a **trencherman**'s lunch, and a rack the mayor could **wield** as a lever. The method was **merciless**: in the **chiaroscuro** of the bell room, it targeted the victim's **anatomy** without reference to **liberalism** or faction. Smoke would **debilitate** him; an **elizabethan** pulley would supply **intimidation** by forcing the weight to **detrude** through the floor. Even a bold witness would **quail** in that **instant**, while a trained accomplice could **mimic** an accidental failure.

电线的六角螺栓让文目瞪口呆。她看着它蜿蜒穿过假墙，经过一张手相术卡片、一个大胃王的午餐，以及一架市长可以拿来当杠杆使用的架子。这种方法毫不留情：在钟室明暗交错的光影中，它不顾自由主义或派系，直指受害者的身体。烟雾会使他衰弱；一只伊丽莎白时代的滑轮会迫使重物穿过地板，从而制造恐吓。即使勇敢的证人也会在那一瞬间畏缩，而训练有素的同谋可以伪装成意外故障。

The mayor had chosen a **seasonable** festival because its noise concealed **collusion**. Venn's **instructive** reconstruction began with a **primeval** oak planted by a **forefather**, then noted the mayor's **sartorial** mud, a **premonition** of the **bucolic** route he used. His **aggression** showed in a **bureau** order that made porters **scurry** and caused the wooden platform to **warp**. A **theologian** had seen him **animate** the crowd with warnings of an **avalanche** of debt and **nihilism**. His strained **bronchus** betrayed him when Venn explained how the sabotage had begun to **gestate** months earlier, one **increment** of bribery after another, with mayor and contractor paid **respectively**.

市长选择了时令合宜的节庆，因为喧闹掩盖了勾结。文以一棵祖先种下的原始橡树开始她有启发性的重构，随后注意到市长衣着上的泥，那是他所走田园路线的预兆。他的侵略性体现在一份办公室命令中：它让搬运工奔忙，也使木台变形。一位神学家看见他用债务雪崩和虚无主义的警告煽动人群。当文解释破坏行为如何早在数月前就开始孕育、贿赂一点接一点，而市长和承包商分别收钱时，他紧绷的支气管暴露了他的心虚。

The clockmaster had been struck by a concealed **bludgeon**, not the falling gear. Venn traced its **mountainous** force to an **unbearable** tension in a bell **cord**, then used its frayed end to **whet** the clerk's memory. He remembered a file marked **retention**, invoices for **apiculture**, a campaign at the **hustings**, and the mayor's **prognostication** that no witness would speak. The mayor tried to **dissemble**, blaming an **erratic** current, but a polished shoe had left a fresh **burnish** on the ladder. Venn used that detail to **enkindle** the council's courage. The contractor had used the bell's **butt** as a hiding place; its **capacity** made the scheme **primal** in simplicity.

钟表师是被藏起来的棍棒击中的，并非被坠落的齿轮击中。文把那山一般的力量追溯到钟绳难以承受的张力，然后用绳子的磨损端头唤醒书记员的记忆。他想起一份标有保留字样的文件、养蜂发票、一次竞选集会，以及市长关于不会有人作证的预言。市长试图掩饰，把责任推给不稳定的电流，但一只擦亮的鞋在梯子上留下了新的磨光痕。文利用这一细节点燃了议会的勇气。承包商把钟的底部当作藏身处；它的容纳空间让这个计划简单得近乎原始。

When questioned, he let out a donkeyish **bray**, but Venn, **sagacious** in interviews, laid a **ream** of papers before him. The contractor, a strict **herbivorous** man who loved to **orate**, had bought the adjacent **lot** before the tower's **arrival** on the preservation register. His accounts were not **reconcilable**: he had funded a **boycott** of repairs, bribed an **apostle** of reform, and used the clockmaster's **descendent** as an unwitting **louse** in the archive. A garden **rake** carried his prints. The route was not **impracticable** after all; its **odorous** tunnel ran beneath the square, where rats would **nibble** at insulation beside a small **cataract**.

受审问时，他发出驴叫般的叫声，但善于审讯的文把一沓文件摊在他面前。这个严格的食草主义者承包商喜欢演说，在钟楼列入保护名录之前就买下了相邻地块。他的账目无法相互吻合：他资助了抵制修复的行动，贿赂了一名改革使徒，还把钟表师的后裔当成档案中不知情的寄生虫。一把园艺耙上有他的指纹。那条路线终究并非不可行；它那条有气味的隧道从广场下方穿过，老鼠会在一处小瀑布旁啃食绝缘层。

The clerk, usually **sullen** among his **kith**, admitted he was a **recidivist** forger whom the mayor had tried to **compel** into signing the permit. Venn switched the tower lights **off** and asked the town **cartographer** to locate the drainage mouth. A **remonstrant** neighbor described a **selective**, **extravagant** convoy that arrived after the **nascent** reform **caucus**. In a **compressible** oilskin bag lay a **decaliter** can, enough fuel to **synthesize** a fire report. Its **libelous** labels promised to **reciprocate** investor trust, beside a child's **terrapin** toy, a **gossamer** thread, and a mayoral **pendant**.

书记员平时在亲族中总是阴沉，承认自己是个屡犯的伪造者，市长曾试图强迫他签署许可证。文关掉钟楼的灯，请镇上的制图师找出排水口。一名抗议的邻居描述了一支挑选而出、铺张浪费的车队，它在新生改革核心小组成立后抵达。一个可压缩的油布袋里有一只十升罐，燃料足以编造一份火灾报告。罐上诽谤性的标签声称会回报投资者的信任，旁边还有孩子的水龟玩具、蛛丝般的线和市长的垂饰。

The evidence made a **bibulous** contractor's workshop a **shambles**. Its **receivable** ledger listed a victory **bacchanal** and the supposed **efficacy** of a **populist** lie: call the clockmaster a **ragged** drunk, **fuddle** the jury, and let them grow **weary** of facts. His **anglophobia** was mere **contrivance**, violating the simple **axiom** that theft leaves a trail. Venn gave him no **stint** of attention to his **fallacy**, then heard him **remonstrate** that a merely **theoretical** charge could not **confound** a man. She answered that starving a witness with smoke was **inhuman**, not an argument about **euthanasia**. The waiting **lout** at the door had heard every **transitory** word.

证据使这个嗜酒承包商的工场变成一片狼藉。可收账的账簿列着一场胜利后的酒宴，以及民粹谎言所谓的效力：把钟表师说成衣衫褴褛的醉汉，迷惑陪审团，让他们厌倦事实。他的仇英情绪只是人为安排，违背了盗窃必留下痕迹这一简单公理。文没有给他的谬论丝毫注意，随后听见他抗议说，仅仅理论性的指控不可能使一个人陷入混乱。她回答说，用烟雾让证人挨饿是不人道的，并非关于安乐死的论证。门口等候的粗人听见了每一个短暂的字句。

At the inquest, his **testimony** exposed the mayor's **regime** of **iciness** toward the old tower. The judge recited its **antiquity**, called the scheme **abominable**, and said no **aeon** of civic **euphony** could hide the **influx** of stolen money. The defense raised an **abdominal** objection about **epistemology**, but Venn opened the clock's **aperture** and produced the **redoubtable** fuse.
